# Orbital Game

Orbital game research analyzes strategic behavior, deterrence, inspection, proximity operations, and competitive dynamics among spacecraft in Earth orbit and cislunar space.

## Research Focus

- Space domain awareness and behavior interpretation
- Rendezvous and proximity operations
- Inspection, deterrence, and escalation management
- Cislunar security governance and rules of the road
