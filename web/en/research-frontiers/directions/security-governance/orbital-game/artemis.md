# Artemis Program

The Artemis Program is NASA's crewed lunar exploration architecture and a major driver of cislunar transportation, communications, surface operations, and governance requirements.

## Connection to Orbital Game Research

Artemis creates high-value cislunar infrastructure and operational patterns around the Moon, including Gateway, lunar landing systems, logistics flights, and surface assets. These activities make space domain awareness, traffic coordination, proximity-operation norms, and escalation management increasingly important.
