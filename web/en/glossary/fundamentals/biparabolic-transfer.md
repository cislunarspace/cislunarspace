# Biparabolic Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A theoretical Earth-Moon transfer method. The spacecraft departs Earth on a parabolic trajectory toward infinity, where a small maneuver raises the perilune altitude, then returns to the Moon on another parabolic trajectory. This reduces the hyperbolic excess velocity at lunar perilune but requires extremely long flight times. It is the limiting case of a bi-elliptic transfer as the intermediate orbit radius approaches infinity.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
