# Verification and Validation, V&V

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Systematic methods for evaluating the performance and robustness of guidance and control algorithms, including design metrics and corresponding verification methods.

## Application Value

This concept is applied in cislunar space: 该基础概念是地月空间轨道力学和任务分析的理论基础，正确理解其内涵对于进行轨道设计、任务规划和性能评估.
