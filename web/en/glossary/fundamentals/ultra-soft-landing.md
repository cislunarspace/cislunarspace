# Ultra-Soft Landing

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A landing technique for crewed lunar missions using extremely low touchdown velocity. The core challenge lies in the trade-off among touchdown speed, landing impact load, plume heat protection, and propellant consumption: lower speed reduces impact and buffer mass, but extends flight time and increases propellant use and plume heating. An optimal touchdown speed is determined through multidisciplinary optimization.
