# Ion Propulsion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An electric propulsion technology that ionizes a noble gas (typically xenon) and accelerates it with electric fields. Exhaust velocities reach 20-50 km/s, far exceeding chemical rockets, but thrust is in the millinewton range. Validated by missions including DAWN, SMART-1, and PROCYON.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
