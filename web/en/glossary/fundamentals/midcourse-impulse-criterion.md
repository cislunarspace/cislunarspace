# Midcourse impulse criterion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Along a trajectory, adding a midcourse impulse lowers cost when p>1. The impulse is placed at the time of maximum p, with position offset chosen so Δv is parallel to p. This criterion derives from analyzing violations of the p≤1 necessary condition.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
