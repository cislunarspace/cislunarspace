# Zenith Distance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angular distance between the direction to a celestial body and the observer's zenith direction. A zenith distance of zero means the body is directly overhead, and 90° means it is on the horizon. The paper uses zenith distance to determine whether a ground station can observe the spacecraft: when the spacecraft's zenith angle α relative to a station is less than the station's maximum observable zenith distance αm, the spacecraft is within observable range. The geometric relationship between zenith distance and Earth-central angle is key to converting TT&C constraints into orbital design parameters.

## Application Value

在天顶距的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
