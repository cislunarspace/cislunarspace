# Composite Representation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A statistical visualization method for simultaneously comparing characteristic parameters across multiple orbit families. For each family, mean, range, and standard deviation are computed and displayed as dot plots (mean at center, radius scaled to range) or bar charts (showing upper and lower bounds), facilitating rapid screening from a large set of candidate families.
