# Equivalent Radius

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The radius of a sphere with the same cross-sectional area as the actual irregularly shaped target, used for collision discrimination and probability calculation. The sum of equivalent radii forms the geometric threshold for collision.
