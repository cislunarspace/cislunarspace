# Verification and Validation (V&V)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Systematic methods for evaluating the performance and robustness of guidance and control algorithms, including design metrics and corresponding verification methods.

## Application Value

V&V is critical for ensuring the reliability of guidance and control algorithms in cislunar space missions. Verification confirms that algorithms correctly implement design requirements, while validation proves that algorithms truly satisfy mission needs. In practice, V&V typically includes analytical verification (such as Lyapunov stability analysis), numerical simulation verification (such as Monte Carlo robustness assessment), and hardware-in-the-loop verification. Thorough V&V can identify algorithm failure modes under extreme conditions and is an important guarantee of mission safety.
