# Stationary Plasma Thruster

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of Hall-effect electric thruster that ionizes the propellant (typically xenon) via the Hall current generated by crossed radial magnetic and axial electric fields, then accelerates the ions electrostatically to produce thrust. It achieves a specific impulse above 1600 s with low thrust, making it well suited for long-duration orbit transfer missions.
