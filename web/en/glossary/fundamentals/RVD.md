# Lunar Orbital Rendezvous and Docking, RVD

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The process by which the orbiter-return combination in lunar orbit rendezvous and docks with the ascent module. Used in Chang'e-5 and Chang'e-6 missions; after docking, the return capsule separates and re-enters Earth with lunar soil samples.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
