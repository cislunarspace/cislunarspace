# Antenna Main Lobe

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The beam region of maximum power density in an antenna radiation pattern, where transmitted energy is concentrated. GNSS satellite antenna main lobes are directed toward the Earth's center to serve terrestrial users, so in cislunar space the main lobe signal is easily blocked by the Earth and available to receivers only during specific arcs.
