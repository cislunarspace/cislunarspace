# Landing Site Coordinate System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A coordinate system with its origin at the planned landing point: the z-axis points along the Moon center-to-landing point line away from the Moon center, the x-axis is perpendicular to z in the direction of motion, and the y-axis follows the right-hand rule. Using this frame for powered descent dynamics allows treating lunar gravitational acceleration as constant, simplifying guidance law derivation.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
