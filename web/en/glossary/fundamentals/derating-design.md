## Definition

降额设计 is an important concept in the field of cislunar space, related to spacecraft design, orbital mechanics, or space mission planning.

## Application Value

Derating Design is a fundamental parameter in spacecraft design with significant importance for orbital calculations and mission planning. Accurate understanding and application of this parameter improves the precision and reliability of mission design.
