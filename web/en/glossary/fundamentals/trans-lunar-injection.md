# Trans-Lunar Injection

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An impulsive maneuver performed in low Earth orbit to accelerate the spacecraft to the velocity needed for a trans-lunar transfer trajectory. It marks the starting point of an Earth-Moon transfer, with the injection velocity determined by the C3 parameter, and is typically executed by the launch vehicle upper stage.
