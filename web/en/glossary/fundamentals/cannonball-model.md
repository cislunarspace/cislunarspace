# Cannonball Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A simplified model for solar radiation pressure that approximates the spacecraft as a sphere. SRP force acts along the Sun-spacecraft line, proportional to the cross-sectional area and inversely proportional to mass (area-to-mass ratio). Computationally simple but approximate, suitable for preliminary analysis where shape details are not critical.

## Application Value

用于评估导航系统的精度上限，指导滤波器设计 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
