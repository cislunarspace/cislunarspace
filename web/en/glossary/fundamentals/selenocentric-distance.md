# Selenocentric Distance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The distance from a spacecraft to the Moon's center of mass. In cislunar orbit prediction, selenocentric distance is a key indicator of the force environment: when the distance is small, lunar non-spherical gravity becomes the dominant perturbation, and a distance less than the lunar radius indicates impact.
