# Characteristic Energy

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter describing the kinetic energy remaining after escaping the gravitational well of a central body, defined as the square of velocity minus the square of escape velocity. C3 = 0 means a parabolic escape, C3 > 0 indicates excess velocity for deep-space travel, and C3 < 0 means insufficient energy. In this paper, a typical rideshare C3 to the Sun-Earth L1 point is about -0.66 km^2/s^2. Commonly used interchangeably with v_infinity in interplanetary trajectory design.

## Application Value

Characteristic energy is a key parameter describing orbital energy state; C3>0 indicates capability for deep space missions, core to matching launch vehicle capacity with mission requirements.
