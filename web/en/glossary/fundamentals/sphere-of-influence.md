# Sphere of Influence

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The spherical region dominated by a celestial body's gravity, within which that body's gravity is the primary perturbation. Earth's SOI is approximately 927,000 km and Moon's SOI is approximately 66,000 km. SOI boundaries determine which celestial body is used as the central body in orbital mechanics models.
