# Mean-Earth/Mean-Rotation System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A definition of the Moon-fixed reference frame. Uses the mean rotation axis as the polar axis and the meridian pointing toward the mean Earth direction as the prime meridian. Selenographic coordinates and low lunar orbit inclinations are commonly expressed in this system.

## Application Value

在航天器动力学建模中，需要准确描述引力场、摄动力和坐标系变换。该概念是进行轨道力学分析、姿态控制和任务设计的基础。
