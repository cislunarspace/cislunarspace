# Lunar Euler Angles

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Three angles (Ω′, is, Λ) describing the rotation between the lunar body-fixed coordinate system and the lunar-centered celestial coordinate system, provided by JPL ephemerides. They are required for transforming probe positions from the lunar celestial frame to the lunar body-fixed frame and for computing the Moon's non-spherical gravity potential. In force model simplification, approximate formulas can replace ephemeris lookups to improve computational efficiency.

## Application Value

These coefficients form an important tool in lunar theory, essential for high-precision orbit calculations in lunar missions.
