# Stable Lunar Surface Ascent

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The technique for a lunar module to take off vertically from the lunar surface. Multiple orbit-control engines are arranged circumferentially around the main structure. The key challenge is attitude control under coupled disturbances: lunar slope causes propellant centroid offset in tanks, which coupled with thrust deviations, produces disturbance torques exceeding attitude control capability, requiring rapid engine fault diagnosis and differential thrust control for safety.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
