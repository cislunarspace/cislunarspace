# Gaussian Mixture Model, GMM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A probabilistic density model that represents data as a weighted sum of multiple Gaussian distributions. In cislunar orbit determination, it represents multimodal probability distributions of orbital states, enabling sustained tracking even when measurement data is partially missing.

## Application Value

The 高斯混合模型 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
