# Runge-Kutta-Fehlberg 7(8) Integrator

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An adaptive step-size Runge-Kutta numerical integration method that employs both 7th and 8th order formulas simultaneously, automatically adjusting the step size by comparing their differences. In the referenced paper, it is used for satellite orbit integration with a 1-minute step size.

## Application Value

Based on the functionality and characteristics described in the definition, this term holds significant application value in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. During the orbit design phase, the related dynamical properties can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization. In navigation and control, it can improve the precision and reliability of mission execution. In systems analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission concept development and risk assessment.
