# Synodic Rotating Frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A reference frame rotating with the line connecting two primary bodies. Its origin is at the Earth–Moon barycenter, the X-axis points toward the Moon, and the Z-axis aligns with the system's angular momentum. In this frame the two primaries are stationary and the circular restricted three-body problem takes an autonomous form.

## Application Value

在轨道力学建模和分析中，该问题或模型提供了简化分析方法，用于理解航天器在多体引力场中的运动特性，支撑轨道设计和任务规划.
