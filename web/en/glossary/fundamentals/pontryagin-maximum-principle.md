# Pontryagin Maximum Principle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A fundamental theorem of optimal control theory stating that the optimal control must minimize the Hamiltonian globally, yielding the control law and costate equations. Unlike the calculus of variations, it handles inequality constraints on control and serves as the theoretical basis for fuel- or time-optimal trajectory design.

## Application Value

庞特里亚金极大值原理是地月空间研究的基础概念，为理解更复杂的航天器动力学问题提供了理论支撑 在实际工程应用中，庞特里亚金极大值原理的准确计算和分析对于任务成功至关重要 掌握庞特里亚金极大值原理的概念有助于工程师进行轨道设计和任务规划 庞特里亚金极大值原理为航天器轨道力学研究提供了基本框架，是进一步学习高级概念的基础
