# Debye Length

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The characteristic distance over which an electrostatic potential decays to 1/e due to shielding in a plasma. In Coulomb force formation, the Debye length determines the effective range of inter-spacecraft electrostatic forces: at distances much larger than the Debye length, the Coulomb force decays sharply due to plasma shielding. The Debye length in near-Earth space ranges from hundreds to thousands of meters; this paper uses 60 m as a simulation parameter.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
