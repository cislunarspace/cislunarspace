# JPL DE430 Ephemeris

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Planetary ephemeris model published by JPL providing high-precision position and velocity data for solar system bodies. Used in this paper to determine positions of Earth, Moon, Sun, and major planets.

## Application Value

The JPL DE430 Ephemeris provides high-precision position and velocity data for solar system bodies. It is used to determine positions of Earth, Moon, Sun, and major planets in cislunar mission design.
