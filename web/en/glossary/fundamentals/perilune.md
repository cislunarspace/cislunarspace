# Perilune

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The closest point of a spacecraft's orbit around the Moon. Used as a terminal constraint in the third search layer, requiring the altitude to reach exactly 200km.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
