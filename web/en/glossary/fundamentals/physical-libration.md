# Physical Libration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Periodic small oscillation of the Moon's actual spin axis relative to its mean position described by geometric libration. Physical libration causes small angular offsets between the Moon's true equator and the mean equator of epoch, affecting the accuracy of the transformation between the lunar body-fixed and lunar-centered celestial coordinate systems. Its magnitude is approximately 10⁻² degrees, with limited impact on short-term libration point orbit prediction.

## Application Value

Physical libration causes small angular offsets in lunar coordinate transformations, with magnitude approximately 0.01 degrees, affecting precision orbit prediction.
