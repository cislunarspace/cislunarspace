# Moon-Centered Rotating Frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A coordinate frame centered at the Moon, rotating with the Earth-Moon line. The X-axis points from the Earth center to the Moon center, Z-axis aligns with the Moon's orbital angular momentum, and Y-axis completes the right-handed system. In the CR3BP, Earth and Moon remain stationary in this frame, making it the fundamental reference for studying periodic orbits such as DROs.


## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
