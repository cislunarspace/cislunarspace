# Real-Valued Stability Index

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A pair of indices nu1 and nu2 used to assess the stability of a periodic orbit, defined as the moduli of the non-unity eigenvalues of the monodromy matrix. When both indices are real-valued with absolute value at most 1, the orbit is linearly stable and perturbations do not grow exponentially. If complex or exceeding unity in modulus, the orbit is unstable and requires active control.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
