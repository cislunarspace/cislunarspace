# Retrograde-Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit with inclination 90°<i≤180°, where the spacecraft moves opposite to the central body's rotation. Retrograde elements use cot(i/2) to eliminate the singularity at i=180°, though small-divisor issues arise near i=0°. In practice, retrograde elements can be used together with prograde elements, switching based on inclination range.

## Application Value

Retrograde orbits with inclinations 90 to 180 degrees offer unique geometrical properties for certain mission scenarios, particularly for coverage of polar regions.
