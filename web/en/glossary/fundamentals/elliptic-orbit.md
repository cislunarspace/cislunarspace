# Elliptic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A closed conic orbit with eccentricity e<1. The spacecraft orbits the central body periodically with semi-major axis a>0. In cislunar space, large-eccentricity ellipses can reach distances as far as the Moon and are the common geocentric representation of libration point orbits. Elliptic elements (e.g., Kepler elements) effectively describe such orbits.


## Application Value

椭圆轨道特别是大偏心率轨道可覆盖远至月球的区域，是地月转移轨道和月球卫星轨道的常见形式。
