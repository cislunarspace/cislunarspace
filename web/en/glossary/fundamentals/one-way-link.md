# One-Way Link

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An inter-satellite measurement link where signals propagate in one direction only. One-way link measurement biases include clock errors from relativistic effects at both transmitter and receiver, producing modeling differences of up to 10 m between reference frames. BCRS or similar deep-space frames are required for proper handling.

## Application Value

为目标跟踪和状态估计提供关键技术支撑 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
