# Kolmogorov-Arnold-Moser Tori, KAM Tori

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Fundamental invariant structures in Hamiltonian dynamics, guaranteed by the Kolmogorov-Arnold-Moser theorem: under small non-integrable perturbations, invariant tori sufficiently far from resonance conditions persist. KAM tori act as barriers in phase space, confining the extent of chaotic orbits.

## Application Value

The KAM环面 concept provides technical support and analytical methods for cislunar space research and lunar exploration missions.
