## Definition

The classic star catalog published in 1979, forming the basis of the IAU-76/FK5 J2000 reference system. Has been replaced by ICRF.

## Application Value

Fundamental Katalog 5 is a fundamental parameter in spacecraft design with significant importance for orbital calculations and mission planning. Accurate understanding and application of this parameter improves the precision and reliability of mission design.
