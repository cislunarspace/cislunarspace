# Solar Activity Cycle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Approximately 11-year periodic variation in solar activity indicators such as sunspot number, serving as the key metric for quantifying solar activity levels.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
