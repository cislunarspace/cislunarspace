# Root Mean Square Error
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A statistical measure of estimator deviation from true values, computed as the square root of the mean squared error. Used in orbit determination analysis to quantify the estimation accuracy of an Extended Kalman Filter.

## Application Value

The Root Mean Square Error plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
