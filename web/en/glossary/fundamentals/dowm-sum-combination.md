# DOWM Sum Combination

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A data processing method in dual-one-way measurement (DOWM) that eliminates clock errors by summing the two pseudo-range measurements from satellite A to B and from B to A. The clock error terms largely cancel, while equipment hardware delays, coordinate time versus proper time differences, ionospheric delays, and the sum of geometric transmission distances remain, all solvable as estimated parameters during orbit determination. After sum combination, the observation is sensitive to both satellites' position vectors, providing stronger constraints on orbits with greater gravitational asymmetry.
