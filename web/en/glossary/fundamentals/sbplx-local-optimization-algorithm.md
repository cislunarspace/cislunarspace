# Sbplx Local Optimization Algorithm

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A derivative-free local optimization algorithm in the NLOPT nonlinear optimization library, based on the Subplex method—an improved variant of the Nelder-Mead simplex method. It improves convergence for high-dimensional problems by partitioning variables into groups and performing simplex searches in lower-dimensional subspaces. The paper uses Sbplx for continuous variable local refinement after particle swarm optimization global search.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
