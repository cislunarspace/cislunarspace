# Noncontemporaneous variation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A variation in calculus of variations where the perturbed and nominal trajectories are evaluated at different times. Denoted d, it satisfies dx(t_o) = δx(t_o) + ẋ_o* dt_o, unifying trajectory perturbation and impulse time shift effects into one operator. Essential for deriving terminal coast gradients.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to understanding cislunar space dynamics and plays an important role in orbital mechanics modeling and mission analysis.
