# Primer vector equation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A second-order linear ODE p̈ = G(r) p governing the evolution of the primer vector along a trajectory, where G(r) is the gravity gradient matrix. Its transition matrix is identical to the state variational equation's transition matrix.
