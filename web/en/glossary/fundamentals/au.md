# Astronomical Unit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Standard astronomical unit of distance, equal to the mean Earth-Sun distance, approximately 1.496×10⁸ km.

## Application Value

Astronomical Unit is a fundamental parameter in spacecraft design with significant importance for orbital calculations and mission planning. Accurate understanding and application of this parameter improves the precision and reliability of mission design.
