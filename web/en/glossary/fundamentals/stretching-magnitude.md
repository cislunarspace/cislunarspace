# Stretching Magnitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The singular values σ_i from the singular value decomposition of the CGT or state transition matrix, representing the amplification factor of a unit initial perturbation along the corresponding direction. The maximum stretching magnitude σ_max = Σ_{11} reveals the maximum possible terminal state deviation caused by initial errors and serves as the core metric in sensitivity analysis.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in spacecraft dynamics analysis and design, and plays a vital role in mission success.
