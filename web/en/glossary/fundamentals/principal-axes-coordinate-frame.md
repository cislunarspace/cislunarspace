# Principal Axes Coordinate Frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A coordinate frame centered at the libration point (or lead satellite) with axes aligned to the principal axes of the elliptical orbit. Obtained by rotating the synodic frame through a specific angle, it decouples the in-plane equations of motion into independent directions, simplifying the solution.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
