# Runge-Kutta-Fehlberg 7(8) integrator

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An adaptive-step numerical integration method that simultaneously computes 7th-order and 8th-order solutions and adjusts step size by comparing their difference. The paper uses it with a 1-minute step to propagate satellite orbits.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
