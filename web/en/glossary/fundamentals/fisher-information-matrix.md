# Fisher Information Matrix

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A matrix that quantifies the amount of information an observation sequence provides about system states, and is the inverse of the CRLB. The paper computes the information matrix iteratively to evaluate the observability of different navigation schemes. Larger diagonal elements correspond to higher estimation accuracy for the corresponding state components.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in spacecraft dynamics analysis and design, and plays a vital role in mission success.
