# Radial-Normal-Cross-Track Frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A local orbital coordinate frame centered at the apogee. The radial axis points from Earth's center to the apogee, the normal axis aligns with the orbital angular momentum (cross product of position and velocity vectors), and the cross-track axis completes the right-handed triad. In this frame, velocity impulses are decomposed into independent in-plane and out-of-plane components, facilitating parameterized search.
