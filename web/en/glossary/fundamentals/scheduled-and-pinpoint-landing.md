# Scheduled and Pinpoint Landing

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital control approach that ensures a lander touches down at a designated lunar surface location at a scheduled time. First required by the Chang'e-4 mission; longitude error is eliminated jointly through circumlunar orbit lowering and powered descent phases.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
