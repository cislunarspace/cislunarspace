# Multilayer Perceptron, MLP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A feedforward neural network consisting of an input layer, hidden layers, and an output layer with full connections between adjacent layers. Used in cislunar awareness to correct TLE data for LEO targets, reducing errors by at least 30% for over 85% of targets.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
