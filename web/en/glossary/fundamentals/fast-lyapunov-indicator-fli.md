# Fast Lyapunov Indicator, FLI

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A numerical diagnostic tool for distinguishing between regular and chaotic regions in phase space. It measures the exponential divergence rate of nearby initial conditions: low FLI values correspond to regular motion, while high values indicate chaos. FLI maps on surfaces of section reveal the spatial distribution of periodic, quasi-periodic, and chaotic orbits.
