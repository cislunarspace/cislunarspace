# Time of Flight, TOF

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The time required for a spacecraft to travel from departure orbit to arrival orbit. In the paper, low-energy exterior transfers have a flight time of approximately 162 days, longer than high-energy transfers (typically dozens to within 100 days), but with significantly reduced velocity increment.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
