# Earth-Central Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angular distance between two objects as seen from the center of the Earth. The paper uses the Earth-central angle to establish visibility criteria between ground stations and the spacecraft: when the spacecraft's zenith angle relative to a station is less than the maximum observable zenith angle, the corresponding Earth-central angle satisfies a condition enabling observation. In the near-lunar-point TT&C analysis, the Earth-central angle determines the geometric relationship between a station's observation cone and the lunar sub-satellite track, enabling calculation of continuous tracking duration.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design, involving orbit design, navigation control, or mission planning.
