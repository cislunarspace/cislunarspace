# Mahalanobis Distance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A weighted distance measure that accounts for covariance structure, measuring the statistical distance between two points under uncertainty. In collision probability computation, it is the key parameter in the Rice distribution integral, characterizing the deviation between two targets' positional uncertainty distributions.

## Application Value

马氏distance考虑协方差结构, is 衡量碰撞概率中两个target不determine性分布偏差 of keyparameter. In 空间碎片碰撞预警中, 马氏distance is Rice 分布积分 of core输入.
