# Simulation-based Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

One of the methods for cislunar constellation design, selecting initial solutions based on prior experience within the feasible design space, then iteratively adjusting parameters through performance simulations until further performance improvements become challenging, suitable for assessing constellation feasibility and application potential.
