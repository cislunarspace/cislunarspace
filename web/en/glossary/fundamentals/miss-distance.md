# Miss Distance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The minimum center-to-center distance between two targets at closest approach, computed from the B-plane components. A collision is declared when the miss distance is less than the sum of the two targets' equivalent radii.


## Application Value

质心坐标系以地月系统质心为原点，简化了限制性三体问题的数学描述。
