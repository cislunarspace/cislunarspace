# LP100K Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar gravity field model derived from Lunar Prospector mission data, providing zonal, tesseral, and sectoral harmonic coefficients up to degree and order 100. Terms with coefficients exceeding |J_lm| > 5×10⁻⁶ are considered to have a practical effect on orbital motion.

## Application Value

The LP100K模型 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
