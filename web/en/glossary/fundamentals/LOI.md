# Lunar Orbit Insertion, LOI

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A deceleration maneuver performed near the first perilune passage to gradually convert a hyperbolic lunar approach trajectory into a circular lunar orbit. Sampling and return missions typically perform three to four LOI burns, each near perilune.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
