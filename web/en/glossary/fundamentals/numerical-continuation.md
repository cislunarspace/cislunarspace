# Numerical Continuation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A numerical method that traces a curve of solutions by incrementally varying a system parameter. In trajectory design, starting from one known solution, prediction and correction steps alternate to continuously follow a family of periodic orbits or transfers along a parameter direction such as the Jacobi constant.
