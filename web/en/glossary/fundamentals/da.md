# Differential Algebraic Method, DA Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that maps functions into equivalence classes and propagates derivative information of all orders through algebraic operations. Used to efficiently compute high-order state transition tensors, avoiding the expression explosion of symbolic differentiation beyond second order.

## Application Value

The 差分代数方法 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
