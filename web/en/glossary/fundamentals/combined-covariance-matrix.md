# Combined Covariance Matrix

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The sum of the two targets' individual covariance matrices in the B-plane, describing the joint uncertainty distribution of their relative position. It directly determines the shape of the uncertainty ellipse and the magnitude of collision probability.

## Application Value

This concept is used in orbital analysis and has significant impact on mission design.
