# Gravitational Asymmetry at Libration Points

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The gravitational field near libration points in the Earth-Moon three-body system lacks symmetry. This asymmetry endows orbits with unique size, shape, and absolute orientation, forming the physical basis of the LiAISON autonomous orbit determination method.
