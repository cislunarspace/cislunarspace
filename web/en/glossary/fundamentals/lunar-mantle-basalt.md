# Lunar Mantle Basalt

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Basalt formed when the lunar mantle partially melts and erupts to the lunar surface. Its mineral and chemical composition reflects the material characteristics of the lunar mantle, providing an important window into the Moon's deep structure and evolution. Von Karman crater has elevated contents of thorium, iron oxide, and titanium dioxide with anomalous spatial distributions potentially related to lunar mantle basalt, making it significant for studying the origin of mantle basalts.
