# Landing Stability

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ability of a lunar module to remain upright and avoid collision after landing. Related to lunar surface bearing capacity, slope, friction characteristics, and initial landing conditions (velocity, attitude, angular rate, center-of-mass position). Assessment metrics include distance from center of mass to tip-over wall, strut stroke, maximum center-of-mass acceleration response, and minimum clearance to lunar surface. Sensitivity of these metrics to initial conditions is analyzed through multibody dynamics simulation.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to understanding cislunar space dynamics and plays an important role in orbital mechanics modeling and mission analysis.
