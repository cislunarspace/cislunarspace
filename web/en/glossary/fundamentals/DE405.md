# DE405 numerical ephemeris

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-precision numerical ephemeris developed by JPL providing position and velocity data for the Sun, Moon, and planets with centimeter-level accuracy. The paper uses it to supply accurate solar and lunar orbits in the realistic force model.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
