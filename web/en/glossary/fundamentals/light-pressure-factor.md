# Light Pressure Factor

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dimensionless parameter characterizing the propulsion capability of a solar sail, defined as the ratio of solar radiation pressure force to solar gravitational attraction. A larger β indicates stronger thrust per unit sail area. Its value depends on sail area, spacecraft mass, and reflectivity properties. β reaches its maximum when the sail normal vector points toward the Sun. This paper uses β=0.035.

## Application Value

在航天器系统设计和任务分析中，该概念用于描述航天器的运动特性或任务模式，为任务方案论证和系统设计提供理论基础.
