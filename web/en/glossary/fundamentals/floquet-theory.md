# Floquet Theory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A mathematical framework for analyzing local stability of periodic orbits. Stability is determined by eigenvalues (characteristic multipliers) of the monodromy matrix: the orbit is locally stable when all multipliers lie inside the unit circle in the complex plane, and unstable otherwise.

## Application Value

Floquet theory is the theoretical foundation for orbital stability analysis. In engineering applications of halo orbits, Lissajous orbits, and other libration point orbits, determining whether these periodic orbits will diverge under perturbations is the first priority. Computing the monodromy matrix and analyzing its characteristic multipliers enables quantitative assessment of orbital stability margins. For unstable periodic orbits, spacecraft require active orbit maintenance; for stable periodic orbits, spacecraft can operate long-term without active control. In lunar navigation satellite design and deep space mission orbital design, Floquet theory is an essential tool for assessing orbital feasibility and selecting control strategies.
