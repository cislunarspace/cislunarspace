# Whole-Course Orbital Control

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that jointly plans and designs orbital control across all flight phases from Earth-Moon transfer through powered descent. Control parameters are no longer solved independently for each phase but are jointly optimized with final landing and takeoff targets as constraints.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
