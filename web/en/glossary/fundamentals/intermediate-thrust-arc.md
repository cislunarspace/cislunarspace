# Intermediate thrust arc

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A Lawden term for a trajectory segment where the switching function S(t)≡0, so the thrust acceleration magnitude is not determined by the minimum principle and can take any value between zero and maximum. Known as a singular arc in optimal control theory.
