# JPL DE430 Ephemeris

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Planetary ephemeris model published by JPL providing high-precision position and velocity data for solar system bodies. Used in this paper to determine positions of Earth, Moon, Sun, and major planets.

## Application Value

JPL's high-precision planetary ephemeris for orbit determination and propagation analysis in deep space missions — one of the baseline ephemeris models for deep space trajectory design.
