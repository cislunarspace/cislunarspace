# Tangential Escape
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An escape maneuver where the spacecraft accelerates along the orbital velocity direction, making the escape direction perpendicular to the position vector. This maximizes propulsion efficiency by eliminating the normal velocity component, representing the optimal escape strategy from LEO into a transfer trajectory.

## Application Value

The Tangential Escape plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
