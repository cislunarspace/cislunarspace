# Earth Observation (EO)

Earth Observation (EO) is a systematic technical framework that uses sensors aboard satellites, aircraft, and unmanned aerial vehicles to observe the Earth's surface, atmosphere, and oceans. EO data plays a critical role in climate change monitoring, resource management, urban planning, and disaster early warning, representing one of the core applications of space technology serving society.
