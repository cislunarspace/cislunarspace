> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A non-singular attitude error parameter, related to modified Rodrigues parameters and Gibbs vector

## Application Value

Generalized Rodrigues Parameters is a fundamental parameter in spacecraft design with significant importance for orbital calculations and mission planning. Accurate understanding and application of this parameter improves the precision and reliability of mission design.
