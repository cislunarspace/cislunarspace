# Rodrigues Rotation Formula

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An analytical formula for rotating a vector about an arbitrary axis. In safe transfer formation design, it rotates the initial direction along the normal of the plane spanned by two position vectors toward the target direction, constructing a smooth reference trajectory from the initial point to the target.

## Application Value

Rodrigues Rotation Formula provides theoretical support for fundamental research in cislunar space.
