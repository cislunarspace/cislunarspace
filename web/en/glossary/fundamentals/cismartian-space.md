# Cismartian Space
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The region beyond cislunar space in the Moon-to-Mars corridor and the space under Mars' gravitational influence. While cislunar space is bounded by Earth-Moon gravitational dominance, cismartian space extends to Mars' gravitational sphere, and together they form the operational environment for Moon-to-Mars missions.

## Application Value

The Cismartian Space plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
