# Generalized Cocycle Conditions

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The second-order generalization of the cocycle property for state transition tensors. It allows the second-order STT over a long time interval to be decomposed into products of STMs and STTs over two subintervals. This provides the mathematical basis for the precomputation and interpolation algorithm, enabling STT composition from stored subinterval data.

## Application Value

Generalized Cocycle Conditions provides theoretical support for fundamental research in cislunar space.
