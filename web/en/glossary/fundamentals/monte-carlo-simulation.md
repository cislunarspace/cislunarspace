# Monte Carlo simulation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A numerical simulation method that performs statistical analysis through repeated random sampling. Used in relative orbit determination research to evaluate statistical properties of orbit determination accuracy and convergence time.

## Application Value

Monte Carlo simulation has practical applications in cislunar space mission design and analysis.
