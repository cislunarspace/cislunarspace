# Argument of Periapsis

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angular distance from the ascending node to the periapsis (the closest point of the orbit to the central body), measured along the direction of motion within the orbital plane. One of the six classical orbital elements that, together with the longitude of ascending node and inclination, describes the spatial orientation of the orbit. It determines the direction of the line of apsides within the orbital plane.

## Application Value

The argument of periapsis determines the orientation of the orbital ellipse within the orbital plane, essential for describing and designing spacecraft trajectories in cislunar space.
