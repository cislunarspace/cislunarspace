# Canonical Units

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Dimensionless system where the initial orbit radius is the distance unit, initial circular velocity is the velocity unit, and initial mass is the mass unit, resulting in gravitational constant μ=1.

## Application Value

This unit system provides a standardized measurement basis for cislunar mission dynamics analysis.
