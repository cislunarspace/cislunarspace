# Polar Motion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Small movement of Earth's instantaneous rotation axis relative to the crust, causing periodic displacement of the North Pole on the surface. In coordinate transformation, it is handled by the polar motion matrix W(t) for precise geocentric celestial to Earth-fixed coordinate conversion. Its magnitude is very small and can be neglected in force model simplification.
