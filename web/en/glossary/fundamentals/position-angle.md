# Position Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angular position of a spacecraft relative to the line connecting the two primary bodies in the synodic coordinate system. The triangular libration point L₄ corresponds to a position angle of 60°, and the deviation Δλ characterizes the spacecraft's drift from the libration point.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
