# Collinear Lagrange Point

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The three collinear equilibrium points of the restricted three-body problem, lying on the line joining the two primary masses. At these points the Hessian of the effective potential is indefinite (saddle-type): repulsive along the line joining the primaries and attractive perpendicular to it. The Jacobi constant at a collinear point makes the Hill's region develop a 'neck', enabling orbits to transit between the two primaries. Conley's paper provides the first systematic topological analysis of orbits near these points.
