# Plume Heating Protection

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Thermal protection design against megawatt-level heat density produced by the lander's orbit-control engine exhaust in confined space. Longer landing flight time increases plume heat and thermal protection mass cost. Directly related to touchdown velocity: low-speed landing reduces impact but increases flight time and plume heat load, requiring trade-off between thermal protection mass and landing impact.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
