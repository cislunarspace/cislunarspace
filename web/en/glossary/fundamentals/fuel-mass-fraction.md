# Fuel Mass Fraction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of a spacecraft's fuel mass to its total mass, a core parameter measuring the spacecraft's maneuver capability. A higher fuel mass fraction provides greater available velocity increment but reduces the payload fraction. It must be determined in mission design by balancing maneuver requirements and launch vehicle capacity.

## Application Value

在航天器动力学建模中，需要准确描述引力场、摄动力和坐标系变换。该概念是进行轨道力学分析、姿态控制和任务设计的基础。
