# GL0660B Lunar Gravity Field Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar gravity field model derived from GRAIL probe data, truncated to degree and order 20. Used for computing lunar non-spherical gravitational perturbation in orbit propagation.

## Application Value

The GL0660B月球重力场模型 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
