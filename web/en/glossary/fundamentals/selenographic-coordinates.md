# Selenographic Coordinates

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A coordinate system describing positions on the lunar surface, using the Moon's equatorial plane as the reference plane and the prime meridian as the reference longitude. Analogous to Earth's geographic coordinates, selenographic longitude ranges from 90°W to 90°E (Earth-facing hemisphere) and latitude from 90°S to 90°N. The paper uses selenographic coordinates to describe near-lunar-point positions, the Sun's lunar ground track position, and to determine illumination-constrained near-lunar arrival windows.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
