# Normal to Orbital Plane

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The direction perpendicular to the spacecraft's orbital plane, determined by the cross product of radial and tangential unit vectors (ŵ=r̂×t̂). The perturbation acceleration component W along this direction primarily causes rotation of the orbital plane (changes in RAAN and inclination), making it the key direction for out-of-plane perturbation analysis.
