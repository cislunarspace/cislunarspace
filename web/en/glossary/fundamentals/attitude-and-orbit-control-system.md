# Attitude and Orbit Control System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The subsystem performing attitude estimation, guidance, and control when orbit guidance is not performed on board, typical for LEO, MEO, and GEO missions.

## Application Value

AOCS (Attitude and Orbit Control System) is the core subsystem for spacecraft on-orbit operation, responsible for attitude estimation, guidance, and control. In cislunar missions, autonomous AOCS capability is essential for flight safety and mission independence.
