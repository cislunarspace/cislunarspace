# Analytical Solution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An approximate closed-form expression for a transfer orbit derived directly through algebraic and geometric methods. Based on B-plane targeting and patched conic concepts, this paper uses the analytical method to determine the initial transfer orbit that targets the Moon at the arrival time while satisfying constraints, serving as the initial guess for subsequent numerical iteration. The analytical solution has lower precision but fast computation, playing a 'cold start' role in the staged search strategy.

## Application Value

解析解是问题的闭式数学表达式，计算速度快且物理意义明确，是轨道力学理论分析的重要工具。
