# GGM05C Earth Gravity Field Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An Earth gravity field model jointly derived from GRACE and GOCE satellite data, truncated to degree and order 20. Used for computing Earth's non-spherical gravitational perturbation in high-precision orbit propagation.

## Application Value

The GGM05C地球重力场模型 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
