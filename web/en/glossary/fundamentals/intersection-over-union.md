# Intersection over Union

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of intersection area to union area of two regions, measuring the degree of overlap between two bounding boxes. IoU = 0 means no overlap, IoU = 1 means perfect overlap. In multi-object tracking, IoU matching serves as a fallback association method when cascade matching fails.

## Application Value

This term has important application value in the design, analysis, and control of cislunar space missions, providing theoretical support for research and engineering practice in related technical fields.
