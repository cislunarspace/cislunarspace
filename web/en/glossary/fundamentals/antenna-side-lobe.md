# Antenna Side Lobe

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Radiation regions in an antenna radiation pattern outside the main lobe, with power much lower than the main lobe. GNSS satellite side lobe signals are not blocked by the Earth and can be received throughout cislunar space arcs, but their lower power demands higher receiver sensitivity (typically around -190 dBW).
