# Jacobi Field

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A nontrivial solution of the variational (linearized Hamiltonian) equation along a reference extremal. A Jacobi field is said to be vertical at a time t if its state component vanishes. A positive time tc is conjugate to t=0 if there exists a Jacobi field vertical at both times. Numerically, conjugate times are detected by evaluating the rank of four Jacobi fields, forming the standard algorithm for local optimality verification.
