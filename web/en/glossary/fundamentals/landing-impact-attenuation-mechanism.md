# Landing Impact Attenuation Mechanism

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A mechanical device installed at the bottom of the lunar module to absorb landing energy. The buffering energy depends on touchdown kinetic and potential energy, related to vertical landing velocity and lunar surface slope. Crewed missions require adaptability across all lunar terrains. Buffer mechanism mass is inversely related to touchdown velocity.

## Application Value

该概念在地月空间任务设计中具有重要应用价值，涉及轨道设计、导航控制或动力学分析等多个方面，为地月空间探测任务提供理论支撑.
