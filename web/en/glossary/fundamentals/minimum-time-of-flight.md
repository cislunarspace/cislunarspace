# Minimum Time of Flight, Min TOF

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The shortest flight time from the minimum parking orbit to the lunar surface for a given Jacobi energy. Shows stepped increase with energy: ~3 days at C=2.08 (direct-transfer-like), jumps to ~5 days at C=2.78, sharply rises to ~46 days at C=2.98, and exceeds 67 days at C=3.18. The stepped growth results from discontinuous intersections between stable manifolds and the minimum parking orbit.

## Application Value

The theoretical shortest flight time from the minimum parking orbit to the lunar surface at a given Jacobi energy, representing the lower bound of cislunar low-energy transfer time for efficiency evaluation.
