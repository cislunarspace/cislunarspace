# JPL DE430 Planetary Ephemeris

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A precision planetary and lunar ephemeris published by JPL, providing positions and velocities of solar system bodies. Compared to DE405 and DE421, DE430 incorporates GRAIL lunar gravity data and lunar laser ranging measurements.

## Application Value

The JPL DE430行星历表 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
