# Magma Ocean

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A hypothesis about early lunar evolution proposing that a global magma layer existed on the Moon's surface shortly after its formation. The magma ocean formed the Moon's layered structure through fractional crystallization: lighter plagioclase floated up to form the crust while heavier ferromagnesian minerals sank to form the mantle. The plagioclase highlands preserved in the South Pole-Aitken basin are considered products of magma ocean crystallization; Chang'e-4's investigation of this region is expected to test and refine this hypothesis.
