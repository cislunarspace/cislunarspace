> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The system that oversees the malfunctioning of the GNC system with the goal of detecting the anomaly, isolating it, and computing recovery action to maintain mission operability in contingent situations.

## Application Value

Failure Detection, Isolation, and Recovery is a fundamental parameter in spacecraft design with significant importance for orbital calculations and mission planning. Accurate understanding and application of this parameter improves the precision and reliability of mission design.
