# Nuri (KSLV-II)

Nuri, officially designated KSLV-II, is an orbital launch vehicle independently developed by South Korea under the leadership of the Korea Aerospace Research Institute (KARI). It is South Korea's first fully domestically developed orbital rocket, capable of delivering 1.5-ton-class payloads to low Earth orbit, marking the country's entry into the league of nations with autonomous space launch capabilities.
