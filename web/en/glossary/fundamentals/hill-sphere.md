# Hill Sphere

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The approximate region where a celestial body's gravity dominates, with a radius approximately equal to its distance from the primary multiplied by the cube root of the mass ratio. The Moon's Hill sphere in the Earth-Moon system has a radius of about 60,000 km. Within this region, a spacecraft is primarily governed by the Moon's gravity, allowing two-body selenocentric dynamics to approximate flyby trajectories, providing a simplified model for preliminary flyby analysis.
