# Measurement noise

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Random errors in measurement data, modeled as Gaussian white noise. Dominated by thermal noise in inter-satellite ranging links. The noise standard deviation determines measurement accuracy and directly affects orbit determination convergence speed and accuracy.
