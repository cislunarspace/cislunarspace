# Unscented Kalman Filter, UKF

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A nonlinear filtering method that captures nonlinear transformations of mean and covariance through deterministic sampling points (sigma points), avoiding the need for Jacobian computation required by the Extended Kalman Filter. Applied to highly nonlinear cislunar orbit determination problems.
