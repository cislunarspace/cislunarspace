# Phase Space
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A multidimensional state space jointly spanned by position and velocity (or momentum). In debris cloud analysis, the phase space has dimension d, including position coordinates and velocity components, with the complete debris state represented by a single point in phase space. The probability density distribution of the debris cloud in phase space describes the probability of debris appearing at a specific position-velocity combination.

## Application Value

The Phase Space plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
