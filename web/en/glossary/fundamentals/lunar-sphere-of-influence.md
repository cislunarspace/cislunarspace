# Lunar Sphere of Influence

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A spherical region centered at the Moon within which the Moon's gravitational influence on a spacecraft dominates over that of the Earth. In the Earth-Moon system, this sphere has a radius of approximately 66,100 km (about one-sixth of the Earth-Moon distance). The patched conic method switches to a Moon-centered two-body model inside the sphere and uses an Earth-centered model or restricted three-body model outside. Qi and Xu demonstrated that the BCP model should replace CR3BP when the spacecraft enters this region.

## Application Value

Lunar Sphere of Influence is valuable for dynamics modeling and analysis in cislunar space research.
