# Transfer Period

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The time from Earth launch to insertion into the target orbit. A key trade-off parameter in transfer design: shorter periods enable faster mission response but typically require more delta-V. PRM transfer period is approximately 16 days, between LFM (about 7 days) and WSB trajectories (over 100 days).
