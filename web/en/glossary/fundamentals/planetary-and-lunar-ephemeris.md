# Planetary and Lunar Ephemeris

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-precision numerical dataset describing the positions and velocities of solar system bodies (including the moon) over time. This study uses JPL DE421, maintained by NASA/JPL. The ephemeris model captures lunar orbital eccentricity, inclination, and perturbations from bodies like the sun.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
