# Continuous Low Thrust

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A micro-newton to milli-newton level thrust continuously applied throughout a transfer trajectory using high-specific-impulse electric propulsion. Unlike impulsive chemical burns, continuous low-thrust trajectory design requires solving an optimal control problem for thrust direction and magnitude at every instant.

## Application Value

The control curve describes the range of attainable low-thrust Hamiltonian values, providing a basis for optimal trajectory design.
