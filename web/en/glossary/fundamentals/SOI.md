# Sphere of Influence

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The boundary around a celestial body where its gravity predominantly governs spacecraft motion. In the Earth-moon system it lies approximately 66,000 km from the moon. The paper matches the forward-integrated Earth-escape segment and backward-integrated moon-capture segment at the SOI to reduce numerical sensitivity of the three-dimensional problem.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
