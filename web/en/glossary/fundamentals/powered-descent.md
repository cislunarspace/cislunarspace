# Powered Descent

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The flight phase in which a spacecraft ignites its engines near perilune, decelerates, and gradually descends to the lunar surface. Autonomously executed by the lander-ascent combination; the range, flight duration, and landing point are linked through analytical geometric relations.

## Application Value

在动力下降的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 在任务规划阶段用于评估不同推进方案的优劣。
