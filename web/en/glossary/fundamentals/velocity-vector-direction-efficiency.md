# Velocity Vector Direction Efficiency

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of the velocity-vector-aligned component of delta-V to the total delta-V. PRM achieves approximately 75% along the velocity vector, while LFM achieves only about 60%. A higher ratio means more propellant is used for speed change rather than direction change.

## Application Value

推进系统参数直接影响航天器的机动能力和任务持续时间。
