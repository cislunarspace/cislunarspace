# Solar Incidence Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle between the Sun's illumination direction and the local surface normal at a point on the lunar surface, equivalent to the solar zenith angle at that point. The paper identifies the optimal solar incidence angle as 45°±10°, with low-latitude lunar regions offering the best illumination and high-latitude regions the worst. The solar incidence angle constraint at the near-lunar point (less than 75°) directly affects the quality of lunar surface photography and the performance of the spacecraft's solar energy equipment.
