# Hermite Interpolation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A polynomial interpolation method that matches both function values and first derivatives at given nodes, producing a polynomial of degree 2n+1 from n+1 nodes. It offers smoother interpolation than Lagrange interpolation and is used in this paper to replace Chebyshev polynomial fitting for computing solar and lunar positions.
