# Observability index

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The minimum eigenvalue of the Gramian matrix. Used to measure the degree of system observability; values above 10^-3 indicate good observability. Independent of the specific estimation method or process.

## Application Value

The 可观测性指数 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
