# Circular Convolution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An operation that multiplies and sums elements of two equal-length sequences under cyclic shifts. In constellation optimization, the circular convolution of the accessibility profile and constellation pattern vector equals the coverage timeline; its cyclic nature arises from the periodicity of the observer orbit.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
