# In-Space Assembly and Manufacturing, ISAM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technology paradigm for fabricating and assembling large structures directly on orbit. Unlike the traditional constraint of fitting components into a rocket fairing, ISAM leverages the microgravity environment to build structures at the 100–1,000 m scale, fundamentally removing launch-based size limitations for sensors and other space systems.

## Application Value

Breaking free from launch vehicle fairing size constraints, it enables in-orbit manufacturing of hundred-meter-scale structures, providing a technical pathway for future space solar power stations and ultra-large telescopes.
