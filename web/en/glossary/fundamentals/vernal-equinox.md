# Vernal Equinox

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Point where the ecliptic crosses the celestial equator from south to north, used as reference direction in inertial space.

## Application Value

The vernal equinox is a fundamental concept in cislunar space research, providing theoretical support for understanding more complex spacecraft dynamics. In practical engineering applications, accurate calculation and analysis of the vernal equinox is crucial for mission success. Mastering the concept of the vernal equinox helps engineers with orbit design and mission planning. The vernal equinox provides a fundamental framework for spacecraft orbital mechanics research and serves as a foundation for advanced concepts.
