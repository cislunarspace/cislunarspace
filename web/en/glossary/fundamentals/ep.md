# Electric Propulsion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A propulsion technology that uses electrical energy to accelerate propellant and generate thrust. Specific impulse is much higher than chemical propulsion (thousands of seconds), but thrust magnitude is small (typically millinewton to newton level). Suitable for long-duration continuous acceleration in orbital transfers, significantly improving payload mass fraction.

## Application Value

Electric propulsion offers high specific impulse with low thrust, suitable for deep space mission orbit transfer.
