# Eccentric Anomaly

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An auxiliary angle describing position in an elliptical orbit, zero at periapsis. Defined as the angle on the auxiliary circle corresponding to the true anomaly when viewed from the ellipse center. It establishes the relationship between position and time and is the core variable in Kepler's equation.
