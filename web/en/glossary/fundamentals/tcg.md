# Geocentric Coordinate Time, TCG

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The relativistic coordinate time defined by IAU with origin at the Earth's center of mass, excluding the gravitational potential effect of Earth on time rate. TCG converts to TT via the defining constant LG and forms the theoretical basis for time systems within GCRS.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
