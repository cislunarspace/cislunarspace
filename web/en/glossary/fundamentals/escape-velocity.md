# Escape Velocity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The minimum speed required for a spacecraft to escape a celestial body's gravitational pull. The lunar escape velocity is approximately 2.4 km/s. In manifold-based lunar departures, the departure velocity is close to the lunar escape velocity, a consequence of the Jacobi integral constraint.
