# Osculating Keplerian Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A fictitious two-body Keplerian orbit that is tangent to the true trajectory at a given instant, sharing the same position and velocity. Its orbital elements vary with time under perturbations. The paper uses the eccentricity and pericenter distance of osculating Keplerian orbits to parametrize initial conditions for WSB computation.
