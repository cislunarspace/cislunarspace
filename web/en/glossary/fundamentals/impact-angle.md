# Impact Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle between the velocity vector and the surface normal as a trajectory reaches a celestial body's surface. A vertical impact has 0 deg impact angle; the LCROSS mission used 4 deg (86 deg from the surface) for scientific objectives.
