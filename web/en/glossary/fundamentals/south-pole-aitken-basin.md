# South Pole-Aitken Basin

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The largest and oldest known impact basin on the Moon, located near the lunar south pole on the farside, with a diameter of about 2,500 km and depth of about 13 km. It preserves the Moon's oldest crustal rocks; its plagioclase highlands may have formed from fractional crystallization of the lunar magma ocean. The basin has elevated thorium, iron oxide, and titanium dioxide contents, and anomalous spatial distribution of material composition may provide clues about volcanic and crustal activity, with significant implications for studying the origin of lunar mantle basalts. Chang'e-4's landing site was selected within Von Karman crater in the SPA basin.
