# Hyperbolic Excess Velocity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The velocity of a spacecraft relative to a celestial body after escaping its gravitational influence, i.e., the residual velocity beyond the gravity sphere. In Tisserand parameter analysis, v_∞ is the key variable connecting different resonance bands, with its contour lines plotted on the Tisserand-Poincaré graph.

## Application Value

This orbit type has important applications in cislunar mission design, useful for constructing stable observation platforms.
