# Measurement Noise Covariance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The covariance matrix R in the measurement equation of a Kalman filter, characterizing the statistical properties of sensor measurement errors. When measurement data contain outliers, a fixed R cannot accurately reflect measurement reliability, necessitating adaptive estimation.
