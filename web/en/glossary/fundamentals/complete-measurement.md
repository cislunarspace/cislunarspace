# Complete measurement

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A measurement combination that can uniquely determine the estimated parameter states from geometric measurements. In DRO close formations, inter-satellite ranging combined with optical angle measurement, or ranging combined with angle and velocity measurement, constitutes complete measurement and converges rapidly to high accuracy.

## Application Value

该基础概念是理解地月空间系统行为的出发点。
