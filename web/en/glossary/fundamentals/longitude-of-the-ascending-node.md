# Longitude of the Ascending Node

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The longitude, measured in the reference plane, of the ascending node where the spacecraft crosses from the southern to the northern side of the reference plane. Called right ascension of the ascending node (RAAN) in the equatorial frame, and ecliptic longitude form in the ecliptic frame. One of the six classical orbital elements that, together with inclination, determines the orientation of the orbital plane in space.
