# Shadow Cone Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A mathematical model describing the geometric shadow cast by an occluding body (Earth or Moon) blocking sunlight. Composed of the umbra cone (total shadow) and penumbra cone (partial shadow), its dimensions are determined by the radii of the Sun and the occluding body and the distance between them. The paper employs this model to compute shadow passages along trajectories, forming the geometric basis for the eclipse avoidance constraint.

## Application Value

阴影锥模型描述天体遮蔽光源形成的暗区形状，航天器进入阴影锥时失去太阳能供给，热控和电源系统设计师必须考虑阴影锥的影响。
