# Earth-centered inertial frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An inertial coordinate system centered at the Earth's center of mass, using the J2000.0 mean equator and mean equinox as reference. In the paper, libration point orbit initial states are transformed to this frame to consistently account for solar and other third-body perturbations.
