# Signal Attenuation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The phenomenon of wireless signal power decreasing during propagation due to factors such as increased distance, medium absorption, and scattering. Cislunar navigation faces severe signal attenuation: libration point orbit satellites are distant from both Earth and the Moon, with path loss exceeding 20 dB. To compensate, satellites require high-power transmitters and high-gain antennas, significantly increasing power consumption, thermal control demands, and development costs, constituting a key bottleneck for engineering three-body orbit navigation constellations.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
