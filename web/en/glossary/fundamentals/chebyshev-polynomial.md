# Chebyshev Polynomial

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orthogonal polynomial system used to approximate the position and velocity of periodic orbits. The paper uses Chebyshev polynomials to express the position and velocity components on the LEO parking orbit and DRO target orbit, serving as constraints for tangential patching between the transfer trajectory and the parking/target orbits.

## Application Value
