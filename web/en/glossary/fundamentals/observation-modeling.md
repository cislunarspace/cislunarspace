# Observation Modeling

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The process of computing theoretical values of various observables (range, range rate, delay, delay rate, etc.) based on spacecraft orbital states and measurement instrument characteristics. In high-precision cislunar orbit determination, observation modeling differences between reference frames directly affect orbit accuracy.

## Application Value

观测建模将物理观测转化为数学模型，是导航系统设计和轨道确定的基础。
