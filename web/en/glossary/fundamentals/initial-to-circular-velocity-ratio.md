# Initial-to-Circular Velocity Ratio

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Ratio of initial velocity to local circular orbital velocity; β=1 gives circular velocity, β=√2 gives escape velocity.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in spacecraft dynamics analysis and design, and plays a vital role in mission success.
