# EGM2008 Earth Gravity Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Spherical harmonic coefficient model describing Earth's gravitational field, with maximum degree and order of 2190. This paper uses EGM2008 in orbit analysis to calculate the effects of Earth's non-spherical gravitational field on spacecraft trajectories.

## Application Value

A 2190-degree-and-order spherical harmonic model of Earth's gravitational field for calculating the effects of Earth's non-spherical gravitation on spacecraft trajectories.
