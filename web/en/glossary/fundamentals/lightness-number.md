# Lightness Number

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dimensionless parameter quantifying the effect of solar radiation pressure on a spacecraft, defined as the ratio of the acceleration experienced by a reflective surface normal to the sun line to the Sun's local gravitational acceleration. At 1 AU, lambda equals the characteristic acceleration divided by approximately 5.93e-3 m/s^2. A higher lightness number indicates greater influence of solar radiation pressure, as relevant for solar sails or high area-to-mass ratio spacecraft.

## Application Value

Lightness Number provides theoretical support for fundamental research in cislunar space.
