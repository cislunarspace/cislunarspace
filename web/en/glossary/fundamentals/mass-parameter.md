# Mass Parameter

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The sole parameter characterizing a circular restricted three-body problem, defined as the ratio of the secondary body's mass to the total mass of both primaries. It determines Lagrange point locations, manifold structures, and zero-velocity curve topology. In the paper, the five Uranian moons span a wide range of mass parameters (Oberon ~3.48e-5 to Miranda ~7.61e-7), producing significantly different manifold geometries and transit neck widths.
