# Normalized Units

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Dimensionless units defined to simplify calculations in the restricted three-body problem. For the Earth-Moon system: unit length is the Earth-Moon distance (~390,877 km), unit time is derived from Kepler's third law (~384,715 s), and unit velocity is length divided by time (~1.016 km/s).
