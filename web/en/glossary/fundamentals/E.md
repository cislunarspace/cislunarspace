# Two-Body Energy with Respect to the Moon

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The two-body orbital energy of a spacecraft relative to the Moon at its perilune. E < 0 is a necessary condition for ballistic capture; E > 0 permits only lunar flybys without orbital insertion. Low-energy transfers have negative E values while direct transfers have positive E values.

## Application Value

A key parameter for determining whether a spacecraft can undergo lunar ballistic capture. E<0 enables natural capture while E>0 permits only flyby without orbital insertion, distinguishing low-energy from direct transfers.
