# Two-Body Energy with Respect to the Moon

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The two-body orbital energy of a spacecraft relative to the Moon at its perilune. E < 0 is a necessary condition for ballistic capture; E > 0 permits only lunar flybys without orbital insertion. Low-energy transfers have negative E values while direct transfers have positive E values.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
