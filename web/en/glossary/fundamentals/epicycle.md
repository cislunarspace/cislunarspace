# Epicycle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Geometric model in ancient Greek astronomy to describe planetary apparent motion: a planet moves uniformly on a small circle (epicycle) while the center of the epicycle moves on a larger circle (deferent) around the Earth.

## Application Value

在轨道力学建模和分析中，该问题或模型提供了简化分析方法，用于理解航天器在多体引力场中的运动特性，支撑轨道设计和任务规划.
