# Conical Shadow Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An eclipse geometric model representing the shadow of an occulting body as a conical volume. Compared to the cylindrical shadow model, it more accurately captures penumbra and umbra geometry, which is important for precise eclipse duration estimation in low-thrust trajectory design.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
