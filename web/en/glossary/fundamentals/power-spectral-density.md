# Power Spectral Density

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Power Spectral Density; a function describing the distribution of signal power in the frequency domain, representing signal power per unit frequency bandwidth, an important tool for analyzing clock noise in the frequency domain.

## Application Value

PSD is an essential tool for analyzing clock noise characteristics in the frequency domain, critical for precision timing and navigation systems in deep space missions.
