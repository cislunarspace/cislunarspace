# Penumbra

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The outer portion of an occluding body's shadow where only part of the solar disk is blocked. Unlike the umbra (total shadow), a spacecraft in the penumbra still receives partial sunlight. In eclipse avoidance design, keeping the spacecraft outside the penumbra generally satisfies thermal and power constraints. The penumbra boundary is defined by the occluding body radius, solar radius, and relative geometry of the three bodies.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to understanding cislunar space dynamics and plays an important role in orbital mechanics modeling and mission analysis.
