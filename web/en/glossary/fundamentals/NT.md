# Null-thrust arc

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A coasting arc where S<0 (p<1) and thrust is zero, alternating with maximum-thrust arcs to form the bang-bang optimal trajectory.

## Application Value

This propulsion technology is important for deep space missions, serving as a key means for efficient orbital transfers.
