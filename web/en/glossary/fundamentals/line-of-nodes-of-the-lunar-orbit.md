# Line of Nodes of the Lunar Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The intersection line between the lunar orbital plane and the ecliptic, with ascending and descending nodes. In the tilted patched three-body approach, patching is performed on the line of nodes to ensure the two planar CRTBPs connect in three-dimensional space. The direction of the line of nodes, defined by angle γ, precesses with a period of 18.6 years.
