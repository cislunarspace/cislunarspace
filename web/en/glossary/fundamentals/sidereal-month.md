# Sidereal Month

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The period for the Moon to complete one revolution relative to the stars, which is 27.3215 days for the Earth-Moon system. Unlike the synodic month (approximately 29.5 days, the Moon phase cycle), the sidereal month is the intrinsic orbital period of the two primaries about their barycenter in the restricted three-body problem, serving as the fundamental time unit TU = T/2π in nondimensionalization.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
