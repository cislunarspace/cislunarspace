# Circumlunar Orbit Lowering, DM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital maneuver that reduces perilune altitude in lunar orbit to prepare for powered descent. In sampling and return missions, typically performed twice: the first with fixed burn duration, the second targeting the altitude and argument of periapsis at the powered descent initiation point.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
