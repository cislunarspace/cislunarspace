# Lunar Gravitational Potential

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The scalar potential function of the Moon's gravitational field, expanded in Legendre polynomials into zonal harmonics (longitude-independent axially symmetric components) and tesseral/sectoral harmonics (longitude-dependent asymmetric components). The expansion coefficients are derived from tracking data of lunar probes such as Lunar Prospector.
