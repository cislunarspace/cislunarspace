# Relativistic Four-Dimensional Space-Time

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The physical framework unifying three-dimensional spatial coordinates with time into a four-dimensional manifold, described by the metric tensor of general relativity. Both GCRS and BCRS are defined within this framework, and precise conversion between frames requires four-dimensional space-time transformation mechanisms.
