# Line-of-sight direction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The unit vector from the observing satellite to the target satellite, obtained by dividing the difference of their position vectors by its magnitude. A fundamental geometric quantity for inter-satellite ranging and angle measurement models.

## Application Value

The 视线方向 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
