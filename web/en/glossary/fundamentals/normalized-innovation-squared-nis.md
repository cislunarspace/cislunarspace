# Normalized Innovation Squared, NIS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The squared Mahalanobis distance of the innovation vector with respect to its theoretical covariance matrix. Under optimal Kalman filter conditions, NIS follows a chi-square distribution; deviation from this distribution indicates system or measurement anomalies. It serves as the test statistic for chi-square tests.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in spacecraft dynamics analysis and design, and plays a vital role in mission success.
