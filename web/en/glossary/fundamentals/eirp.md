# Effective Isotropic Radiated Power, EIRP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The product of transmit antenna gain and transmit power (dBW), representing the equivalent isotropic radiated power in the maximum radiation direction. In inter-satellite link budget analysis, EIRP together with receiver figure-of-merit (G/T) and free-space path loss determines the received carrier-to-noise ratio, serving as the core parameter for assessing whether the link meets ranging requirements.

## Application Value

The 有效全向辐射功率 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
