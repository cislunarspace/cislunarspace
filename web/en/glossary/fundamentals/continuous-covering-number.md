# Continuous Covering Number

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A metric measuring the number of simultaneously visible satellites over a lunar surface region. At any given moment, the covering number at a grid point equals the count of satellites visible to that point. The lowest covering number is the minimum across all grid points at a time instant. Navigation constellations require continuous quadruple coverage, meaning at least 4 satellites visible at all times and locations.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to understanding cislunar space dynamics and plays an important role in orbital mechanics modeling and mission analysis.
