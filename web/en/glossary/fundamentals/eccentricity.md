# Eccentricity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter describing orbital shape, defined as e = (r_a - r_p)/(r_a + r_p), where r_a and r_p are apoapsis and periapsis distances. In WSB computations, eccentricity is held constant along the radial segment.
