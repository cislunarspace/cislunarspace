# Propellant Mass Fraction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of consumed propellant mass to the initial mass of the spacecraft, used to assess the fuel efficiency of an orbital transfer. Low-thrust interior transfers achieve around 10%, while single-impulse exterior transfers around 66%.

## Application Value

该方法在轨道转移设计中被用于分析转移通道和能量最优轨迹，为低能量转移方案提供理论支撑。
