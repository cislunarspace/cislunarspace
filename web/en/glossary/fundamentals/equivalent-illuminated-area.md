# Equivalent Illuminated Area

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A key parameter in solar radiation pressure models representing the total effective area of a spacecraft illuminated by the Sun at a given attitude. For spacecraft with complex geometry (such as the Magpie Bridge relay satellite with a dish antenna), this area varies continuously with the angle between the Sun vector and the spacecraft body axes, requiring facet-by-facet projection based on the satellite's structure and real-time attitude.
