# Non-Spherical Gravitational Potential

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Additional gravitational potential caused by a celestial body's oblateness and non-uniform mass distribution, after removing the point-mass gravity. The J₂ terms of the Earth and Moon are the dominant components. In libration point orbit prediction, although these perturbations are small (on the order of 10⁻⁷), neglecting them introduces systematic bias and alters the error divergence pattern.
