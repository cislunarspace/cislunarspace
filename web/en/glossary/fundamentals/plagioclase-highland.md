# Plagioclase Highland
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Ancient highland terrain on the lunar surface composed predominantly of the mineral plagioclase. Plagioclase highlands are believed to have formed during fractional crystallization of the Moon's early magma ocean: lighter plagioclase floated upward to form the primordial crust while heavier ferromagnesian minerals sank to form the mantle. The lunar farside preserves the oldest plagioclase highlands; in-situ investigation of their material composition helps reveal the Moon's early evolutionary history.

## Application Value

The Plagioclase Highland plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
