# Multi-Body Dynamical Environment

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The spacecraft operating space where gravitational fields of multiple celestial bodies significantly affect motion, distinct from two-body and restricted three-body problems. In cislunar space, the combined gravitational influence of Earth, Moon, and Sun governs orbital evolution. Near libration points and Sun-Earth weak stability boundaries, multi-body coupling produces far more complex orbital structures than two-body dynamics, yet simultaneously provides pathways for low-energy transfers.
