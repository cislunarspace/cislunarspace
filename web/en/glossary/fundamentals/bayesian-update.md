# Bayesian Update

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A statistical inference method that uses new measurement information to correct the prior probability distribution and obtain the posterior. The core formula states that the posterior is proportional to the product of the prior and the likelihood function. In non-cooperative target search, the negative information from failing to detect a target is also used in Bayesian update to progressively shrink the reachable domain and search scope.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
