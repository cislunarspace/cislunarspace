# launch C3

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The characteristic energy at launch, defined as the square of the hyperbolic excess velocity when the spacecraft leaves Earth's sphere of influence. The paper sets the Earth-Moon launch C3 upper limit at 20 km²/s² as one of the constraints for launch window filtering.

## Application Value

This术语在地月空间任务的设计、分析和控制中具有important应用价值，为相关技术领域的研究和工程实践provides理论支撑。
