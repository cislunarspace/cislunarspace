# Relativistic Effect

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The effects of general relativity on spacecraft measurements and motion. In cislunar navigation, relativistic effects manifest primarily as: the difference between proper time and coordinate time (tens of microseconds per day), gravitational light deflection (increasing signal travel distance), and coordinate transformation differences between reference frames (position differences on the order of 10 meters). These effects must be considered in high-precision navigation modeling.
