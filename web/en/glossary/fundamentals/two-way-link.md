# Two-Way Link

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An inter-satellite measurement link with signals propagating in both directions. The sum combination effectively cancels relativistic clock errors. The study shows two-way link modeling biases remain at centimeter level between frames, causing less than 1 m impact on orbit determination.
