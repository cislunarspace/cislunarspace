# Grid Search Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that searches for feasible trajectories by uniformly discretizing initial conditions across the parameter space. In this paper, Jacobi energy C, initial y-coordinate, and velocity angle θ are discretized with steps of ΔC=0.01, Δθ=0.005°, Δy₀=0.01, producing 120×360×100 grid points propagated to obtain global trajectory distributions.
