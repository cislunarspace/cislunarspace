# Richardson Third-Order Analytical Solution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A third-order approximate expansion of Halo orbits in the circular restricted three-body problem, developed by Richardson in 1980 using the Lindstedt-Poincaré method.

## Application Value

The Richardson third-order analytical solution provides closed-form initial guesses for Halo orbit design without numerical iteration. By expanding the equations of motion around the libration point to third order and eliminating secular terms via the Lindstedt-Poincaré perturbation method, designers obtain analytical expressions for in-plane and out-of-plane amplitudes. These expressions directly yield initial conditions for numerical differential correction, making them the standard first step in Halo orbit computation for cislunar mission design.
