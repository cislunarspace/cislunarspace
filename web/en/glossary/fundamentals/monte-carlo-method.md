# Monte Carlo Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition
A numerical computation method based on large-scale random sampling. In debris cloud evolution analysis, the Monte Carlo method generates a large number of random debris samples (3000 in this paper), propagates the orbit of each sample, then statistically determines the debris distribution within a specified region. The method is intuitive and reliable but computationally expensive, and its results depend on sample count, unable to directly provide probability density values at specific locations. This paper uses it as a validation benchmark.

## Application Value
This term has application value in the design and analysis of cislunar space missions, supporting trajectory design, mission planning, and system optimization. Researchers can analyze its physical mechanisms and engineering applicability based on specific mission requirements to advance cislunar space exploration technology.
