# Vacuum Perigee

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The point of minimum geocentric distance on the Earth-return segment of a free-return trajectory. Located outside the atmosphere, its geocentric distance has an approximate geometric relationship with the reentry point distance and reentry angle. Reentry constraints on the free-return trajectory (such as reentry altitude and angle) can be equivalently expressed as vacuum perigee constraints, simplifying constraint handling in trajectory design.
