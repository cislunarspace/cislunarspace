# Analytic Hierarchy Process

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A multi-criteria decision method that decomposes a complex problem into goal, criteria, and alternative layers, determines criterion weights through pairwise comparison, and computes a weighted composite score for each alternative. The paper uses it to evaluate constellation configurations across four dimensions: satellite count, quadruple continuous coverage rate, mean GDOP, and minimum GDOP.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
