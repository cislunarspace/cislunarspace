# Robust Factor

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A coefficient matrix that scales the measurement noise covariance R, with values between 0 and 1. A larger normalized innovation yields a smaller robust factor, which effectively inflates R to down-weight the corresponding measurement in the filter update, thereby suppressing the influence of measurement outliers.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in spacecraft dynamics analysis and design, and plays a vital role in mission success.
