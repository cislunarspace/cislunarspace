# Differential Algebraic Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that maps functions into equivalence classes and propagates derivative information of all orders through algebraic operations. Used to efficiently compute high-order state transition tensors, avoiding the expression explosion of symbolic differentiation beyond second order.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to understanding cislunar space dynamics and plays an important role in orbital mechanics modeling and mission analysis.
