# Lunar True Anomaly, LTA

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The Moon's angular position in its orbit around Earth, measured from perigee. Different LTAs correspond to varying Earth-Moon distances and directly affect transfer trajectory flight times. Studies show that when the Moon is at apogee (LTA=180°), PLF transfers to DRO require the longest duration and may encounter the 13-day time constraint.
