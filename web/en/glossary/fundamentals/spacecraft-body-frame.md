# Spacecraft Body Frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A coordinate frame fixed to the spacecraft body with its origin at the center of mass, with axes typically aligned with the spacecraft's principal inertia axes. Unlike orbital frames, it rotates with spacecraft attitude and is used for attitude description and equipment orientation. In cislunar orbit determination, measurement directions must be transformed from the body frame to the inertial frame.

## Application Value

This concept is applied in analysis and design, with important implications for mission performance.
