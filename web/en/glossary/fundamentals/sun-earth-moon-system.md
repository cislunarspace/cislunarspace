# Sun-Earth-Moon System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The full dynamical model including gravitational effects of the sun, Earth, and moon. Compared to the Earth-moon CRTBP, it introduces the sun as a fourth-body perturbation source, producing transfer trajectories that exploit sun-Earth libration point dynamics or solar gravity to reduce delta-V.

## Application Value

In spacecraft dynamics modeling, the gravity field, perturbing forces, and coordinate-frame transformations must be described accurately. This concept provides the foundation for orbital mechanics analysis, attitude control, and mission design.
