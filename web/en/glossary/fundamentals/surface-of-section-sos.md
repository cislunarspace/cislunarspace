# Surface of Section, SOS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A hyperplane that reduces a continuous dynamical system to a discrete map. In the circular restricted three-body problem, the SOS is commonly defined by y=0 with ẏ<0. Each crossing of the hyperplane yields a point; repeated integration produces a Poincaré map that reveals the distribution of regular and chaotic regions in phase space.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
