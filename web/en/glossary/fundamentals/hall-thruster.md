# Hall Thruster

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An electric propulsion device that uses electric fields to accelerate plasma to generate thrust. The PPS-1350 Hall thruster used in the SMART-1 mission is a solar electric propulsion system with thrust 73.19 mN and specific impulse 16.434 km/s. Hall thrusters are steady-state plasma propulsion devices with high thrust density and simple structure.
