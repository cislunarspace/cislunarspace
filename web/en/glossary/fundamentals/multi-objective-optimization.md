# Multi-Objective Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A mathematical approach that simultaneously optimizes multiple conflicting objectives. In cislunar navigation constellation design, it seeks balanced solutions among contradictory metrics such as coverage, positioning accuracy, fuel consumption, fault tolerance, and cost. Common algorithms include NSGA-II and MOEA/D, which generate Pareto frontiers to reveal multi-dimensional trade-offs. Compared with single-objective optimization, multi-objective methods provide decision-makers with a complete set of non-dominated solutions adaptable to different mission priorities.
