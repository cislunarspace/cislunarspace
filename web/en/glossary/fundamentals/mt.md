# Maximum-thrust arc

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An arc where S>0 (p>1) and the engine operates at maximum thrust T_max, alternating with null-thrust arcs to form the bang-bang optimal trajectory.

## Application Value

Maximum-thrust arc is a fundamental concept in cislunar mission design, playing an important role in orbital design and tracking planning.
