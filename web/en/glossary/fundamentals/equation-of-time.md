# Equation of Time

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The difference between apparent solar time and mean solar time, caused by the non-uniform apparent annual motion of the Sun and the obliquity of the ecliptic.

## Application Value

该基础概念是理解地月空间系统行为的出发点。
