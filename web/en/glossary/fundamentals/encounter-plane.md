# Encounter Plane

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The cross-sectional plane at closest approach between two space targets, lying in the B-plane. Collision probability is obtained by projecting the uncertainty ellipsoid onto this plane and integrating, bridging 3D uncertainty to a scalar collision probability.
