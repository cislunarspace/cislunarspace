# Argument of Perigee

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angular distance of the perigee from the ascending node along the direction of the spacecraft's motion in an elliptical orbit. The argument of perigee determines the spatial orientation of the ellipse's major axis within the orbital plane. In this paper, the argument of perigee values for the three solutions are 157.30°, 138.41°, and 138.36° respectively. When searching transfer orbits within one month for a fixed insertion point, the argument of perigee varies in a sinusoidal pattern.

## Application Value

在轨道设计和转移轨道优化中，该方法用于确定最优转移时机和轨道形状，以最小化燃料消耗或飞行时间. 通过数值仿真和迭代优化，可获得满足任务约束的可行轨道方案.
