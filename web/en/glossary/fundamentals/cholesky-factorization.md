# Cholesky Factorization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method to decompose a symmetric positive definite matrix into the product of a lower triangular matrix and its transpose. In horizon-based optical navigation, it transforms horizon observation vectors from the camera frame into factorization space and normalizes them, simplifying the least-squares solution. Also used to generate correlated Gaussian random noise.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
