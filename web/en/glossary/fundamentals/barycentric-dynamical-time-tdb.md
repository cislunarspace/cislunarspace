# Barycentric Dynamical Time, TDB

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A time scale derived from TCB via a linear transformation that removes the secular drift relative to TT, keeping the two close. TDB and TT differ by an annual oscillation of approximately 1.66 ms amplitude. The paper uses TDB as the time coordinate under the BCRS framework.

## Application Value

TDB 经线性变换消除 TCB 相for TT of 长期漂移, and TT 之间存In 约1.66毫秒 of 年周期振荡. TDB is BCRS 框架下 of 标准When 间坐标.
