# Saros Period

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The period for the Sun-Earth-Moon system to return to approximately the same configuration, approximately 18 years. The paper selects event epochs every six months over an 18-year range to cover the full range of Sun-Earth-Moon configurations.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in spacecraft dynamics analysis and design, and plays a vital role in mission success.
