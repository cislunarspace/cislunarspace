# Apse

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The point on an elliptical orbit closest to (periapsis) or farthest from (apoapsis) the central body. In optimal two-impulse transfers, thrusts are typically applied at apses because the velocity is perpendicular to the radial direction there, yielding maximum maneuver efficiency.

## Application Value

拱点(近拱点、远拱点) is velocity方向垂直于径向 of 位置, In 此施加冲量efficiency最high. In optimal双脉冲transfer中, 出发脉冲和到达脉冲usuallyIn 拱点处施加.
