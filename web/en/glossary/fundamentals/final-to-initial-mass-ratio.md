# Final-to-Initial Mass Ratio

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of spacecraft mass at mission end to initial mass, reflecting propellant consumption efficiency. A ratio closer to 1 indicates less propellant consumed. The planar optimal transfer yields a ratio of 0.931; the three-dimensional polar transfer requires only 0.6% more propellant.

## Application Value

在终始质量比的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 在任务规划阶段用于评估不同推进方案的优劣。
