# Ecliptic Plane

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The orbital plane of Earth's revolution around the Sun, serving as a natural reference plane for Sun-Earth trajectory design. In this paper, DROs lie approximately in the ecliptic plane; the first transfer maneuver nullifies the out-of-plane velocity component to keep subsequent trajectory segments in this plane.

## Application Value

在轨道设计阶段，可usingThis轨道类型构建候选轨道池，为星座部署和任务轨道选择provides参考。在轨运行时，This轨道特性可used for轨道维持策略设计，降低推进剂消耗。在轨道转移规划中，其稳定流形结构可指导低能量转移走廊的搜索。
