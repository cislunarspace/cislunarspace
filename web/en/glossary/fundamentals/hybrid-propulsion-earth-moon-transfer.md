# Hybrid Propulsion Earth-Moon Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An Earth-Moon transfer scheme that combines two or more propulsion modes (e.g., chemical plus electric) within a single mission, achieving a better tradeoff between trip time and propellant consumption than any single-propulsion-mode approach.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to understanding cislunar space dynamics and plays an important role in orbital mechanics modeling and mission analysis.
