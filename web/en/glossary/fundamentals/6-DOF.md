# Six-Degree-of-Freedom, 6-DOF

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A motion state descriptor that simultaneously captures position (3 translational DOFs) and attitude (3 rotational DOFs) of a spacecraft.

## Application Value

Six degrees of freedom describing complete spacecraft motion state: three position coordinates and three attitude angles, the fundamental model for flight simulation.
