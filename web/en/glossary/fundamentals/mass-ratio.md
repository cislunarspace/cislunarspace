# Mass Ratio

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The fundamental dimensionless parameter of the circular restricted three-body problem, defined as the ratio of the secondary's mass to the total mass of both primaries: u = M_2 / (M_1 + M_2). The Earth-Moon mass ratio is approximately 0.01215. It determines the dynamical structure of the system, including positions of all five libration points, the shape of zero-velocity curves, and characteristics of periodic orbits.

## Application Value

During the orbital design phase, this orbit type can be used to construct candidate orbit pools, providing references for constellation deployment and mission orbit selection. During orbital operations, the orbit characteristics can be applied to station-keeping strategy design, reducing propellant consumption. In orbital transfer planning, its stable manifold structure guides the search for low-energy transfer corridors.
