# Geostationary Transfer Orbit, GTO
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An intermediate orbit used by launch vehicles to deliver spacecraft to geostationary orbit, with perigee around 200-400 km and apogee near 35,786 km. Most launch vehicles are optimized for GTO insertion, making it a practical departure orbit for Earth-Moon transfers.

## Application Value

The Geostationary Transfer Orbit, GTO plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
