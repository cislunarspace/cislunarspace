# Terminal Constraints

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The geometric and kinematic conditions that a spacecraft must satisfy upon arriving near the target body in transfer trajectory design. Typical terminal constraints include altitude constraint (position vector magnitude equals target orbit radius) and tangential entry constraint (position-velocity dot product equals zero). Terminal constraints transform the transfer problem into a two-point boundary value problem.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
