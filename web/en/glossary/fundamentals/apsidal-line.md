# Apsidal Line

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A straight line connecting the periapsis and apoapsis of an orbit, passing through the central body. In the WSB criterion, the apsidal line serves as the reference: the third body departs from this line, orbits the second primary, and returns; the sign of Keplerian energy upon return determines whether the motion is stable or unstable.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in spacecraft dynamics analysis and design, and plays a vital role in mission success.
