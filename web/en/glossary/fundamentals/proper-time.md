# Proper Time

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The time interval measured by a clock co-located with the observer, dependent on the gravitational potential and velocity at the clock's location. Its relationship with coordinate time involves gravitational redshift and velocity-induced time dilation, and it is critical for high-precision inter-satellite link modeling.

## Application Value

在航天器导航和定位中，该方法用于从传感器数据中提取有效信息，实现自主定轨和轨道预报. 支持地月空间探测任务的实时导航需求.
