# SPICE, SpiceyPy

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A spacecraft mission toolkit developed by JPL and widely used in NASA planetary missions. Provides ephemeris computation, time conversion, and coordinate transformation services. The Python wrapper is SpiceyPy. In this paper, SPICE is used to obtain solar phase angle histories for determining observer-target geometry in the CR3BP frame.

## Application Value

SPICE, SpiceyPy is a fundamental concept in cislunar mission design, playing an important role in orbital design and tracking planning.
