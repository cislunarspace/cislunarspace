# Coefficient of Reflectivity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dimensionless parameter describing a spacecraft surface's reflectivity, ranging from 0 (fully absorbing) to 2 (fully specular reflection). The Cr value directly affects solar radiation pressure acceleration magnitude. The ARTEMIS mission demonstrated that updating Cr from navigation data during the stationkeeping phase significantly improved trajectory prediction accuracy and reduced fuel costs.

## Application Value

Coefficient of Reflectivity is a fundamental concept in cislunar mission design, playing an important role in orbital design and tracking planning.
