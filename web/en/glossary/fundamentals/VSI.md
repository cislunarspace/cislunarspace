# Variable-specific-impulse engine

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Also called power-limited engine. An electric propulsion engine with adjustable thrust and exhaust velocity, with a separate power source (e.g., solar panels) bounded by a maximum. The optimal thrust acceleration vector equals the primer vector: Γ(t) = p(t), coupling the equations of motion with the primer vector equation into a fourth-order ODE in position.

## Application Value

This propulsion technology is important for deep space missions, serving as a key means for efficient orbital transfers.
