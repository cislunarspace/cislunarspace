# Local Vertical Local Horizontal Frame, LVLH

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A local coordinate frame centered on the target spacecraft's orbit. R-bar points toward the primary body, H-bar is perpendicular to the orbital plane (negative normal direction), and V-bar completes the right-handed system. Widely used for describing relative motion between chaser and target.
