# Thrust Vector Control, TVC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique that generates control torques by changing the direction of engine thrust. During lander main engine operation, the engine nozzle is gimbaled to change thrust direction, simultaneously achieving deceleration and attitude control.
