# Pseudo-Potential

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The effective potential function U in the rotating frame of the circular restricted three-body problem. Comprising gravitational potentials of both primaries and the centrifugal potential, it governs the non-Coriolis accelerations and its level surfaces form the zero-velocity surfaces.
