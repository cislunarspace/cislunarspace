# Canonical Units

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A nondimensional unit system used in the restricted three-body problem. The distance unit (DU) equals the distance between the two primaries (approximately 384,400 km for the Earth-Moon system), and the time unit (TU) is chosen so that the orbital period of the primaries equals 2π (approximately 375,190 s for Earth-Moon). Under canonical units the sum of the gravitational parameters of the two primaries equals 1 DU³/TU², yielding compact equations of motion.

## Application Value

该基础概念是理解地月空间系统行为的出发点。
