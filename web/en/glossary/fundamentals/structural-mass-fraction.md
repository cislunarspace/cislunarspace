# Structural Mass Fraction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of structural mass to total mass of a launch vehicle, a key parameter in vehicle preliminary design. A smaller structural mass fraction indicates higher vehicle efficiency. Different vehicle types and propellant combinations have different typical ranges: LOX-methane first stage approximately 0.06, second stage approximately 0.08; orbital transfer stage approximately 0.38; lunar lander/ascender approximately 0.23.
