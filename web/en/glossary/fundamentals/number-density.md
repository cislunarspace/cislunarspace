# Number Density

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The number of debris per unit spatial volume, satisfying the condition that the integral over all space equals the total debris count Nf. The relationship to probability density is: number density equals probability density multiplied by the total debris count. In debris cloud evolution analysis, probability density can be converted to obtain the expected number of debris in a specified region for quantitative collision risk assessment.
