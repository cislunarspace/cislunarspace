# DE440 Ephemeris

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Planetary ephemeris model published by NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, used for precise calculation of solar system body positions and velocities at any time. This paper uses DE440 in orbit propagation analysis to account for gravitational effects of Earth, Moon, and Sun.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
