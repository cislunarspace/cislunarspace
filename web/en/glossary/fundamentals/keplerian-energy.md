# Keplerian Energy

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The two-body energy integral of the third body relative to the second primary, equal to kinetic energy minus potential energy. Negative energy corresponds to an elliptical (bound) orbit; positive energy corresponds to a hyperbolic (escaping) orbit. In WSB and temporary capture analysis, the sign of Keplerian energy is the key indicator of stability and capture status.

## Application Value

在航天器动力学建模中，需要准确描述引力场、摄动力和坐标系变换。该概念是进行轨道力学分析、姿态控制和任务设计的基础。
