# Apsidal Motion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Progressive rotation of the line of apsides (connecting perigee and apogee) in the direction of orbital motion, with a period of approximately 8.85 years for the Moon.

## Application Value

Apsidal Motion provides theoretical support for fundamental research in cislunar space.
