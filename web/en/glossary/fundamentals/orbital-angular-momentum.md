# Orbital Angular Momentum (h*)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The cross product of position and velocity vectors of a spacecraft orbiting a central body (H = r × v). It is a constant of motion in a central force field, with its direction perpendicular to the orbital plane and magnitude related to semi-latus rectum by h*² = μp. The three components of angular momentum are used to determine the orbital plane orientation (h and k elements).

## Application Value

该基础概念是理解地月空间系统行为的出发点。
