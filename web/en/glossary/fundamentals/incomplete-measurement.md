# Incomplete measurement

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A measurement combination that cannot uniquely determine the estimated parameter states from geometric measurements. In DRO close formations, ranging-only or ranging-plus-velocity-only measurements are incomplete, with poor observability, long convergence times, and sensitivity to initial values.
