# Semi-Major Axis

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Half the major axis of an elliptical orbit, determining the orbit's size and period. In this paper, the semi-major axes of the three solutions (analytical, simple force model, and precise force model) are approximately 202185 km, 194832 km, and 195187 km respectively, with the corresponding orbital periods determining flight time. The semi-major axis is one of the six classical orbital elements describing an Earth-Moon transfer orbit, jointly determining the orbit's shape and size with eccentricity.

## Application Value

In low-thrust trajectory optimization, minimum-time trajectories serve as a prerequisite, first determining the minimum flight time, then using it as the terminal time constraint for the fuel-optimal problem. This hierarchical optimization strategy significantly reduces the computational complexity of multi-objective trajectory design.
