# Taylor Series Expansion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A mathematical tool that represents a smooth function as a power series of derivatives evaluated at a reference point. High-order state transition tensors use Taylor expansion to approximate the nonlinear relationship between orbital deviations and initial perturbations; higher order yields better accuracy.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
