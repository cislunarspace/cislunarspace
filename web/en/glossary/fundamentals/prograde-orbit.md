# Prograde Orbit
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit with inclination 0°≤i<90°, where the spacecraft moves in the same direction as the central body's rotation. In the unified elements framework, prograde orbits define h, k using sin(i/2), avoiding a singularity at i=90° but encountering small-divisor issues as i approaches 180°.

## Application Value

The Prograde Orbit plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
