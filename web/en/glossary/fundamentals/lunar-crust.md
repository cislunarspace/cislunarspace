# Lunar Crust

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The outermost solid rocky layer of the Moon, approximately 60 to 100 km thick, formed by fractional crystallization of the early magma ocean. The lunar crust is primarily composed of plagioclase, and the farside crust is thicker than the nearside. One of Chang'e-4's scientific objectives is to investigate the shallow structure of the lunar farside roving area, obtaining composition and layering information of the shallow crust.

## Application Value

月壳是地月空间研究的基础概念，为理解更复杂的航天器动力学问题提供了理论支撑 在实际工程应用中，月壳的准确计算和分析对于任务成功至关重要 掌握月壳的概念有助于工程师进行轨道设计和任务规划 月壳为航天器轨道力学研究提供了基本框架，是进一步学习高级概念的基础
