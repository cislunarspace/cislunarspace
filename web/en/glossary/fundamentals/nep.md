# Nuclear Electric Propulsion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A propulsion concept using a nuclear reactor to generate electricity for electric thrusters. Offers specific impulse an order of magnitude higher than chemical propulsion, but very low thrust (T/W ~10⁻⁴), suited for long-duration low-thrust transfers of large cargo spacecraft.

## Application Value

Nuclear Electric Propulsion is a fundamental concept in cislunar mission design, playing an important role in orbital design and tracking planning.
