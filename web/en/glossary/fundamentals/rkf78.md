# Runge-Kutta-Fehlberg 7(8) Integrator

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An adaptive step-size numerical integrator that estimates local truncation error by comparing 7th- and 8th-order solutions and automatically adjusts step size. Widely used in orbit prediction for balancing accuracy and efficiency.

## Application Value

This integrator achieves high-precision numerical integration through adaptive switching between 7th and 8th order methods.
