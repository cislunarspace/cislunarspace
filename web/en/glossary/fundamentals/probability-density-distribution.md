# Probability Density Distribution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A function describing the probability of a continuous random variable taking values at spatial locations. In debris cloud analysis, the probability density function characterizes the probability of debris appearing at a specific position in space, satisfying the normalization condition that the integral over all space equals 1. It differs from number density only by a normalization constant (total debris count) and the two are interconvertible. This paper directly obtains the probability density distribution of debris clouds by solving boundary value problems on Eulerian grids rather than through statistical sampling.


## Application Value

This optimization method has wide applications in cislunar space trajectory design, suitable for scenarios such as trajectory optimization and mission planning.
