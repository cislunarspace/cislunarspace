# Geocentric Distance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The distance from a spacecraft to the Earth's center of mass. In cislunar space, geocentric distance indicates the orbital region: near Earth, terrestrial gravity dominates; at greater distances, lunar or solar gravity may surpass Earth's gravity. The switching of dominant forces directly affects step size selection in element-based integration.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in spacecraft dynamics analysis and design, and plays a vital role in mission success.
