# Lunar Regolith

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A loose debris layer covering the lunar bedrock surface, formed by long-term fragmentation of surface rocks through meteorite impacts and micrometeorite bombardment, solar wind and cosmic ray radiation, and thermal cycling. The physical and chemical properties of lunar regolith (particle size distribution, mineral composition, thermal conductivity, etc.) are fundamental parameters for landing safety, surface equipment design, and lunar scientific investigation. One of Chang'e-4's lunar penetrating radar scientific objectives is to detect regolith thickness along the roving path.
