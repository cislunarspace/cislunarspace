# Energy Minimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An optimal control problem that minimizes the L2-norm of the control (squared control energy). Compared to the minimum-fuel problem (L1-norm), the energy minimization problem has a strictly concave and smooth Hamiltonian, yielding a continuous control law that is much easier to solve numerically. It typically serves as the starting point for homotopy continuation toward the minimum-fuel problem.
