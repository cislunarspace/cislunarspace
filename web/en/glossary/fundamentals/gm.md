# Gravitational Constant

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The product of the universal gravitational constant and a body's mass, determining the strength of its gravitational field.

## Application Value

The Gravitational Constant GM is one of the most fundamental physical parameters in orbital mechanics calculations. For Earth, GM is known to be approximately 398600.44 km³/s² with extremely small error; for the Moon, GM is approximately 4902.80 km³/s². In orbital maneuver calculations, transfer trajectory design, and orbit prediction, GM value precision directly affects result accuracy. Earth-Moon transfer missions require considering both Earth and Moon gravitational fields simultaneously. High-precision GM values are critical for ensuring correct orbital control commands.
