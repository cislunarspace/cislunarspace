# Selenocentric Instantaneous Earth-Moon Plane Coordinate Frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A reference frame with origin at the Moon's center and axes aligned with the barycentric synodic frame. Since its axes do not rotate with the lunar body, the reachable domain characterized in this frame is time-invariant, revealing the general geometric features of transfer trajectory reachable domains.

## Application Value

该基础概念是理解地月空间系统行为的出发点。
