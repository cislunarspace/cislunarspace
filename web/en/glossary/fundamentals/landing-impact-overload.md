# Landing Impact Overload

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The impact acceleration transmitted to astronauts at the moment of lunar touchdown. A critical medical constraint for crewed lunar landing; excessive impact overload may cause astronaut discomfort or injury. Controlled through touchdown velocity management and landing impact attenuation mechanism design.
