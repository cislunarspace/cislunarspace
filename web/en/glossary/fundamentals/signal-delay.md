# Signal Delay

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The time interval for a signal to travel from transmitter to receiver, serving as a core observable in VLBI measurements. The study demonstrates that delay modeling differences between GCRS and BCRS are less than 0.003 m (equivalent).

## Application Value

在航天器动力学建模中，需要准确描述引力场、摄动力和坐标系变换。该概念是进行轨道力学分析、姿态控制和任务设计的基础。
