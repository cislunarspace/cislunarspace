# Semi-Minor Axis

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The semi-minor axis of a hyperbolic orbit, denoted b. In B-plane targeting, the magnitude of the B-vector equals the semi-minor axis (|B| = b), characterizing the hyperbolic geometry.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
