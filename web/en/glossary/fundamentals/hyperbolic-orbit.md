# Hyperbolic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An open conic orbit with eccentricity e>1. The spacecraft follows one branch of the hyperbola and does not return. Traditional elliptic elements cannot describe this orbit; unified elements achieve hyperbolic applicability through semi-latus rectum and universal argument of latitude, though computational efficiency degrades when eccentricity becomes large.
