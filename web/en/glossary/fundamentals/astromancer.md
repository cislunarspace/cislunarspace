# Astromancer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory optimization and mission design software toolkit developed by the Space Situational Awareness and Space Sustainability (SSASSI) group at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. It supports high-fidelity n-body dynamical models, indirect optimal control methods, and shooting algorithms, used in this paper for cislunar transfer trajectory simulation.
