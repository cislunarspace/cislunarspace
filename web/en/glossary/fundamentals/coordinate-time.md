>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A uniform time parameter defined within a specific space-time reference frame, independent of any particular clock's motion or gravitational environment. Different frames use different coordinate time systems (e.g., TT for GCRS, TDB for BCRS), and their precise conversion is central to high-precision cislunar observation modeling.
