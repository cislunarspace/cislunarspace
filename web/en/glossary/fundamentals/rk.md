# Runge-Kutta Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A family of numerical integration methods for solving ordinary differential equation initial-value problems. The fourth-order Runge-Kutta method (RK4) evaluates the function four times per step, combines them with weighted averaging to produce a fourth-order accurate solution whose error scales with the fourth power of the step size. One of the most commonly used integrators in astrodynamics simulations, offering a good balance between accuracy and computational cost.

## Application Value

The Runge-Kutta method is a fundamental numerical method for solving ordinary differential equations.
