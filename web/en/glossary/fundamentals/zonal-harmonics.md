# Zonal Harmonics

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Coefficients in the lunar gravitational potential expansion that depend only on latitude, not longitude, denoted J_l. Zonal terms represent north-south asymmetries in the Moon's mass distribution; the J_2 term, the largest, captures the lunar oblateness effect.
