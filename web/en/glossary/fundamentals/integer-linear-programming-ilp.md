# Integer Linear Programming, ILP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An optimization method where decision variables are restricted to integers and both the objective function and constraints are linear. In constellation design, binary variables (0 or 1) indicate whether a satellite occupies a specific position at a given time step, minimizing satellite count to meet coverage demand. Solvable efficiently by commercial solvers such as Gurobi.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
