# Chebyshev Polynomial Fitting

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The standard representation method in JPL DE ephemerides for celestial body positions. Position data over 32-day segments are fitted to Chebyshev polynomial series, requiring 312 coefficients for the Moon and 66 for the Sun. While numerically stable, the parameter count is high for onboard storage-constrained applications.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
