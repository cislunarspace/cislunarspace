# Separation Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In orbital rendezvous problems, the central angle measured from the chaser spacecraft to the target spacecraft (with the primary body at the vertex). For moving-target rendezvous between coplanar circular orbits, this angle changes continuously as the target moves, and its magnitude directly determines the velocity increment cost. The cost is minimized when the separation angle equals zero or an integer multiple of 360 degrees.

## Application Value

分离角是地月空间研究的基础概念，为理解更复杂的航天器动力学问题提供了理论支撑 在实际工程应用中，分离角的准确计算和分析对于任务成功至关重要 掌握分离角的概念有助于工程师进行轨道设计和任务规划 分离角为航天器轨道力学研究提供了基本框架，是进一步学习高级概念的基础
