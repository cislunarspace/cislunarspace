# Autonomous Rapid Circumlunar Rendezvous

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Autonomous completion of rendezvous between the lander ascending from the lunar surface and the crewed spacecraft in circumlunar orbit within hours. Without navigation constellation support near the Moon, it relies on onboard autonomous navigation and long-range rendezvous sensors to autonomously generate rendezvous strategies that adapt to various initial conditions from different fault-triggered emergency ascents, subject to propellant, energy, and oxygen constraints.

## Application Value

This term has significant application value in cislunar space missions。In the orbital design phase, engineers use relevant theories for trajectory optimization；In navigation and orbit determination, it is used to improve measurement accuracy；In attitude control and orbit maintenance tasks, it ensures stable spacecraft operation。In practical applications, parameter optimization and algorithm adaptation can be combined with mission requirements to improve mission success rate and resource utilization efficiency。
