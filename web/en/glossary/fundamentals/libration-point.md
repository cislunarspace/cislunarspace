# Libration Point

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In the restricted three-body problem, five points where the gravitational pull of two massive bodies balances the centrifugal force in the rotating frame. L1, L2, L3 are collinear; L4, L5 form equilateral triangles. The invariant manifold tubes near L1 serve as critical pathways for low-energy Earth-Moon transfers.

## Application Value

In spacecraft dynamics modeling, the gravity field, perturbing forces, and coordinate-frame transformations must be described accurately. This concept provides the foundation for orbital mechanics analysis, attitude control, and mission design.
