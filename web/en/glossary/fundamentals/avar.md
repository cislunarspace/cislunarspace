# Allan Variance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Allan Variance; a statistical measure characterizing clock stability by computing the variance of the difference between average fractional frequency deviations of adjacent sampling intervals, used as a standard method for analyzing clock noise characteristics.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
