# Thrust Arc

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Segments of a trajectory where the engine is active, alternating with coast arcs. The paper's optimal solution contains three thrust arcs and two coast arcs.

## Application Value

In low-thrust trajectory optimization, the alternating structure of thrust arcs and coast arcs directly determines flight time and propellant consumption. Engineers can exploit this characteristic to cover greater transfer distances within a limited propellant budget by carefully designing thrust arc on/off timing. For example, in Earth-Moon transfer missions, rationally arranging the ratio of thrust to coast arcs enables reaching the Moon within several months using electric propulsion while saving substantial propellant. Multi-segment thrust arc designs can also leverage celestial gravity assist to improve transfer efficiency.
