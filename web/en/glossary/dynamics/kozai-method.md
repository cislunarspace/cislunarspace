# Kozai Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A perturbation method for solving averaged co-orbital resonance problems, commonly used to analyze long-period orbital evolution of celestial bodies.

## Application Value

The choice of method directly affects the efficiency and accuracy of problem solving, so an appropriate method should be selected according to the specific problem.
