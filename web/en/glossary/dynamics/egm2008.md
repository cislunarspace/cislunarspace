# EGM2008 Earth Gravitational Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Spherical harmonic coefficient model describing Earth's gravitational potential at 50×50 order/degree, used to compute non-spherical Earth gravity effects on spacecraft trajectories.

## Application Value

EGM2008 Earth Gravitational Model plays a critical role in trajectory optimization and multi-body dynamics analysis for solving complex trajectory design problems.
