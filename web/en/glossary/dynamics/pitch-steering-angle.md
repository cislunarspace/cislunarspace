# Pitch Steering Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle between the projection of the thrust vector onto the orbital plane and the local horizontal direction in the local orbital frame. Together with the yaw angle, it defines the three-dimensional thrust direction and serves as a fundamental control variable in low-thrust trajectory optimization.

## Application Value

推力俯仰角 is low推力轨迹optimize中 of fundamentalcontrol量, and 偏航角共同determine三维推力方向. In 螺旋orbitdesign中, 俯仰角control决定orbit of 提升efficiency.
