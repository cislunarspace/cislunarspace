# Lagrange-Jacobi Identity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An identity describing the relationship between the second derivative of the moment of inertia and the potential energy and total energy of the three-body system, fundamental to qualitative analysis of three-body motion.
