# Lagrange-Jacobi Identity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An identity describing the relationship between the second derivative of the moment of inertia and the potential energy and total energy of the three-body system, fundamental to qualitative analysis of three-body motion.

## Application Value

This term has important application value in the design, analysis, and control of cislunar missions, providing theoretical support for research and engineering practice in related fields.
