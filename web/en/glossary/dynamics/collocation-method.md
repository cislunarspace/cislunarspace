# Collocation Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of direct transcription method that enforces equations of motion between piecewise polynomial representations of states. Nonlinear constraint equations are constructed by satisfying differential constraints at the midpoint of each segment. Commonly uses Hermite cubic polynomials or Gauss-Lobatto collocation points.

## Application Value

Collocation methods enforce equations of motion at discrete points using piecewise polynomial state representations, enabling accurate trajectory optimization.
