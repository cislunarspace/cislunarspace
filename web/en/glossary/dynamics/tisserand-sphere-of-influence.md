# Tisserand Sphere of Influence

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A sphere of influence defined based on the Tisserand criterion, describing the relative dominance of gravitational forces from different celestial bodies on a spacecraft in a multi-body environment.

## Application Value

蒂瑟兰影响力球描述天体引力起主导作用的区域，是确定引力模型选择的重要参考。
