# Attention-Augmented Proximal Policy Optimization, A2PPO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A deep reinforcement learning framework that integrates a directional cross-attention mechanism into an actor-critic policy architecture for autonomous low-thrust trajectory optimization in cislunar space. The cross-attention enables the policy network to selectively attend to state features deemed important by the critic, improving learning stability and sample efficiency.

## Application Value

Based on its definition, a deep reinforcement learning framework that integrates a directional cross-attention mechanism into an actor-critic policy architecture for autonomous low-thrust trajectory optimization in cislunar s.
