# Measurement Covariance Matrix

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A matrix representing the statistical characteristics of measurement noise. Larger measurement error covariance means the filter trusts the corresponding measurement less.

## Application Value

Measurement covariance matrices represent noise statistics; larger covariance indicates lower filter trust in corresponding measurements.
