# High-Precision Torque Allocation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A strategy that allocates desired torques from the controller to thrust vector control devices and attitude thrusters. During the main braking phase, large torques are executed by TVC while small torques below its resolution are supplemented by thrusters, balancing control precision and propellant efficiency.
