# Homological Equation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An equation in normal form computation that determines the generating function; its denominators involve small divisor problems.

## Application Value

同调equationIn 标准型calculate中determine生成function, 其分母appear小除数problem. 小除数导致级数收敛性差, is 平动点邻域动力学analysis中 of main数学困难.
