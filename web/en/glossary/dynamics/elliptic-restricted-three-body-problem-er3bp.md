# Elliptic Restricted Three-Body Problem, ER3BP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dynamical model that extends the circular motion of the two primary bodies in the CR3BP to elliptic motion. Compared with the CR3BP, the ER3BP accounts for the eccentricity of the primary bodies' orbit and more accurately represents the dynamics of periodic orbits in real celestial systems. Periodic orbits near libration points still exist in the ER3BP framework, but their stability characteristics differ from those in the CR3BP.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
