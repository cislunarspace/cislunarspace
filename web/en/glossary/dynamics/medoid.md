# Medoid

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The member of a cluster that has the smallest sum of distances to all other members, serving as the motion primitive for that cluster.

## Application Value

Medoid plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
