# Bifurcating Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit within a family of periodic orbits where the eigenvalues of the monodromy matrix change (indicating a stability transition). Identified by examining eigenvalues of the monodromy matrix, bifurcating orbits can be perturbed to generate 3-D periodic orbits.
