# Shooting Constraint

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Equality constraints in the multiple shooting method that ensure state continuity of adjacent trajectory segments at discrete nodes. Includes state continuity constraints and boundary constraints.

## Application Value

该方法可用于精确修正轨道偏差，提高轨道预报精度。
