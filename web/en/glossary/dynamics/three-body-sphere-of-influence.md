# Three-Body Sphere of Influence

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A spherical region of approximately 159,200 km around the Moon where Earth-Moon three-body dynamics dominate.

## Application Value

The 3BSOI defines the region around the Moon where Earth-Moon three-body dynamics dominate, approximately 159,200 km in radius, critical for lunar mission trajectory design.
