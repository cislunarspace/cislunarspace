# Flyby Direction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The direction of spacecraft motion near perilune relative to the Moon in the Earth-Moon rotating frame. Clockwise is retrograde, counterclockwise is prograde. Retrograde flybys consume significantly less impulse than prograde flybys and are preferred for DRO transfers.

## Application Value

在绕月方向的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 在任务规划阶段用于评估不同推进方案的优劣。
