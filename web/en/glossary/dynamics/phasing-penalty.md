# Phasing Penalty

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A penalty term in transfer cost computation that penalizes deviations of transfer time from the ideal rendezvous time.

## Application Value

相位惩罚在优化目标中加入相位约束，确保轨道转移在合适的时机执行。
