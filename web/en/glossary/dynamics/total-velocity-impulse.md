# Total Velocity Impulse

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The sum of all velocity impulses required to complete a full orbital transfer. In the paper, the total velocity impulse is approximately 3.28 km/s, including the departure impulse from the parking orbit and the halo orbit insertion impulse.

## Application Value

在总速度增量的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
