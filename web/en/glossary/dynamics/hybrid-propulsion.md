# Hybrid Propulsion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technical scheme combining two or more different propulsion methods. In this paper, it specifically refers to the combination of solar sail propulsion and Coulomb force formation: Coulomb force provides a Sun-pointing force component to resolve the solar sail's underactuation problem, while the solar sail provides off-line-of-sight force components for steering, improving Coulomb formation's controllability. The two methods complement each other, and the entire process consumes no propellant.
