# Forward Predictive Propagation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In the saturation phase of hybrid predictive control, forward propagating the relative orbit dynamics by 30 minutes at fixed intervals to determine when to switch from thrust saturation to feedback linearization.

## Application Value

在前向预测传播的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 为航天器的精确控制提供理论依据，确保任务执行的可靠性 用于分析航天器在复杂引力场中的运动特性。
