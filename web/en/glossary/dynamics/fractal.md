# Fractal

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Geometric objects with self-similar structure whose dimension is non-integer. Strange attractors, Cantor sets, and Koch curves are examples of fractals. Fractal dimension can be measured by box-counting dimension, information dimension, correlation dimension, etc.
