# Quaternion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A four-parameter method for representing spacecraft attitude, consisting of a scalar and a 3D vector, avoiding singularity issues of Euler angles.

## Application Value

四元数是描述刚体姿态的参数化方法，避免欧拉角的奇异性问题，是航天器姿态控制系统的首选表示方法。
