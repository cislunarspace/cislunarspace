# Periselenium Rendezvous

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A maneuver executed at the periselenium (lunar periapsis) during a flyby transfer, which inserts the spacecraft from the flyby trajectory onto the transfer arc toward the libration point region.
