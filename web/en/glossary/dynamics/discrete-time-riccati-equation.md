# Discrete-Time Riccati Equation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A matrix algebraic equation for solving optimal gain in LQR controller design. Form: A^T S A - S - (A^T S B)(B^T S B + R)^{-1}(B^T S A) + Q = 0.

## Application Value

The discrete-time Riccati equation solves optimal gain in LQR controller design, fundamental for spacecraft attitude and orbit control.
