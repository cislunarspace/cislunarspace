# Hill Sphere Radius

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The radius of the largest spherical region within which a celestial body's gravity dominates satellite motion.

## Application Value

在分析月球卫星或地月空间物体的动力学边界时，该参数是重要参考。
