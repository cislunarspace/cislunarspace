# Spiral Manifold

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Manifold structure where flow approaches or departs the orbit along spiral trajectories when the orbit possesses complex eigenvalues with magnitude greater than unity.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
