# Phase Deviation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Position deviation of a spacecraft along the orbital direction relative to the reference trajectory on a periodic orbit. In cislunar libration point orbit station-keeping, single-maneuver control methods (e.g., XAC) can assign at most three of six translational state components per maneuver, causing the spacecraft's xz-plane crossing time to diverge from the baseline. The along-track position drifts ahead or behind, accumulating over time to the order of days.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
