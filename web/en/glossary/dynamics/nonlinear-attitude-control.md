# Nonlinear Attitude Control

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Attitude tracking feedback control law designed based on Lyapunov stability theory, enabling spacecraft attitude and angular velocity to track desired time histories with quasi-global stability.
