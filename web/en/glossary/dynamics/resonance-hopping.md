# Resonance Hopping

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A phenomenon where a celestial body transitions from one orbital resonance ratio to another through temporary capture by a planet. Classic examples are Jupiter's comets Oterma and Gehrels 3, which hop from 3:2 to 2:3 resonance under the control of libration point orbit manifolds. This demonstrates the key role of LPO manifolds in natural dynamical channels of the solar system.

## Application Value

This control method can be applied to spacecraft attitude stabilization, orbit keeping, and maneuver trajectory optimization in cislunar space missions, improving mission execution flexibility and reliability.
