# Trajectory Optimization with Sparse Optimal Control Software, TOSOCS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A 3 degree-of-freedom, open-loop trajectory design and optimization tool developed internally at Lockheed Martin, built around the commercial optimization engine SOCS, used for high-fidelity full-ephemeris DRO transfer trajectory optimization.

## Application Value

Trajectory Optimization with Sparse Optimal Control Software, TOSOCS plays a critical role in trajectory optimization and multi-body dynamics analysis for solving complex trajectory design problems.
