# Hamiltonian Increment (ΔH)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The difference between the Hamiltonian in the SBCM model and that in the CR3BP model, characterizing the magnitude of solar gravitational perturbation, being the main factor affecting the extremum of periapsis altitude and eccentricity of captured trajectories.

## Application Value

In the analysis of Hamiltonian increment, researchers must first establish mathematical models describing spacecraft motion, solve the corresponding dynamic equations through numerical integration or analytical methods, and then evaluate the spacecraft's motion characteristics under different initial conditions. In-depth research on Hamiltonian increment helps understand the complex dynamic environment of cislunar space, providing theoretical support and engineering reference for future mission design. In practical missions, numerical simulation and analytical methods must be combined to analyze and validate the Hamiltonian increment, ensuring the design meets mission constraints and performance indicators.
