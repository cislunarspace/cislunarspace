# Powered Lunar Swing-By, PLSB

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A maneuver where the spacecraft applies thrust at the lunar perilune to combine lunar gravity with active deceleration, reducing orbital energy from the transfer orbit to a lunar capture orbit. PLSB is about 173 m/s for IDT and about 271 m/s for PRM (due to the longer elliptical orbit requirement). PLSB is the critical maneuver linking the Earth-Moon transfer phase and the lunar orbital phase, and its ΔV error is the largest error source along the entire transfer trajectory.

## Application Value

Powered lunar swing-by expands the energy change range of lunar gravity assists.
