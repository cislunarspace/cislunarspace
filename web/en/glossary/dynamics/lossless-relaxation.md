# Lossless Relaxation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Relaxation of nonconvex constraints via slack variables that does not alter the optimal solution of the original problem.

## Application Value

Lossless Relaxation is significant for optimization in orbit design and trajectory planning.
