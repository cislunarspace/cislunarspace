# Calculus of Variations

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A branch of mathematics dealing with extremal problems of functionals, forming the theoretical basis for optimal control. Euler-Lagrange equations and necessary conditions can be derived through calculus of variations.

## Application Value

Calculus of Variations plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
