# Approaching Cone Angle Constraint

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Approaching Cone Angle Constraint; constraint on flight direction during final approach phase, defining maximum allowable deviation angle.

## Application Value

This constraint defines the maximum allowable flight direction deviation during the final approach phase of rendezvous, critical for safe docking operations.
