# Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A density-based clustering algorithm that discovers clusters as groups of points with sufficiently dense local neighborhoods that are overlapping, labeling points in sparse regions as noise or border points.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
