# Lunar Synodic Resonance, LSR

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A 9:2 monthly resonance where the Gateway NRHO orbital period forms a specific resonant relationship with the lunar synodic period, preventing Earth eclipses.

## Application Value

A 9:2 resonance between Gateway NRHO and lunar synodic period preventing Earth eclipses and ensuring continuous power supply, an important consideration in NRHO design.
