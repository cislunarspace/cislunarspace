# Lyapunov Stability

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A stability property of dynamical system orbits: after a small perturbation, the system state remains within a small neighborhood of its initial state without diverging. This paper verifies that small Sun-Earth DROs maintain Lyapunov stability in the ephemeris model by inspecting monodromy matrix eigenvalues, remaining bounded for years despite perturbations.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
