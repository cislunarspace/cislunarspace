# Transit Disk

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Two open disks on each bounding 2-sphere of the equilibrium region L, composed of transit orbits. One disk contains orbits crossing left-to-right; the other contains orbits crossing right-to-left. The boundary of each disk consists of asymptotic orbits (analytic circles by Moser's theorem). This topological structure is the key intermediate step in Conley's extension from linearized to nonlinear results.

## Application Value

In orbit design and optimization, this concept is used to analyze spacecraft motion characteristics in the cislunar multi-body gravitational field, providing a theoretical basis for low-energy transfer trajectory design.
