# Earth-Moon Phase

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The relative angle between the Earth-Moon line and the Sun-Earth line, determining the phase relationship between the Sun-Earth and Earth-Moon three-body coordinate systems and affecting the position of manifold connection points and the magnitude of velocity impulse.

## Application Value

Earth-Moon Phase plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
