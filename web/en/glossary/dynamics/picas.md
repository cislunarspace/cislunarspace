>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Maximum invariant set maintained by spacecraft under constraints, used for constraint-satisfying maneuver planning

## Application Value

Positively Invariant Constraint Admissible Set plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
