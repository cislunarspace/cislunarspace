# Convergence

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ability of an iterative algorithm to progressively approach and ultimately reach the optimal solution; in the two-layer optimization framework, the global convergence of the upper layer provides qualified initial values for the lower layer.

## Application Value

在轨道设计阶段，该方法可用于求解低能量转移轨道，减少推进剂消耗。
