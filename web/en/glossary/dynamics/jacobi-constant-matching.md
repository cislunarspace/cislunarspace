# Jacobi Constant Matching

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The condition in phase plots where Earth–Moon and Sun–Earth manifolds have equal Jacobi constant values, enabling intersection at the hyperplane projection.

## Application Value

When designing cross-system transfer trajectories, matching the Jacobi constant of Earth-Moon manifolds with that of Sun-Earth manifolds ensures intersection at the hyperplane projection, enabling zero-cost transfer channels from Earth to Moon.
