# Apollo Guidance Computer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Digital computer on Apollo spacecraft for guidance, navigation and control, featuring 16-bit word length and limited memory; the core of humanity's first fully digital onboard autonomous navigation system.

## Application Value

Apollo Guidance Computer plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
