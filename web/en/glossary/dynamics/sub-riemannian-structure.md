# Sub-Riemannian Structure

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A geometric structure defined on a manifold by a distribution of rank less than the manifold dimension together with a Riemannian metric. The control vector field distribution of the circular restricted three-body problem combined with the thrust direction field in the rotating frame constitutes a sub-Riemannian structure.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
