# Multi-Impulse Maneuver, MI

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Strategy using multiple impulses during transfer to increase flexibility, relax execution accuracy constraints, and accommodate navigation errors.

## Application Value

This maneuver type plays an important role in cislunar transfer and orbit adjustment missions, affecting energy consumption and flight time.
