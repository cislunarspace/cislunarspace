# Natural Parameter Continuation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method of obtaining families of periodic orbits by gradually varying system parameters, starting from a known periodic orbit and generating the entire family through continuous parameter variation.

## Application Value

Natural Parameter Continuation plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
