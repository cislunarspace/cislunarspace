# Elliptic Non-linear Equations of Relative Motion, ENERM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Non-linear relative motion equations derived in the ER3BP framework, accounting for gravitational forces from both Moon and Earth on the chaser and rotational acceleration of the reference frame. These serve as the reference equations for accuracy comparison in this paper, with zero error by definition.

## Application Value

Nonlinear relative motion equations in elliptical orbital frames, more accurate than CW equations, suitable for high-eccentricity relative orbits.
