# Resonant Gravity Assist

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Technique of using repeated flybys near a body to amplify or alter orbital energy.

## Application Value

Resonant gravity assist is a classical method for interplanetary mission spacecraft to utilize natural forces to extend mission capability. When a spacecraft performs multiple flybys of the same planet, each flyby changes the spacecraft's orbital energy; through carefully designed flyby timing and geometry, orbital semi-major axis and eccentricity can be progressively amplified. In multi-planet systems like Jupiter and Saturn, resonant gravity assist enables spacecraft to gain enormous velocity increments at minimal propellant cost. Famous examples include Cassini using two Venus flybys and one Earth flyby to reach Saturn. This technology plays an irreplaceable role in deep space mission planning and represents a cost-effective approach for achieving long-distance exploration missions.
