# Coulomb Force Formation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A formation technique that controls relative spacecraft positions using electrostatic forces between charged spacecraft (e.g., charged via electron guns). It offers high control precision, zero propellant consumption, and no plume contamination, making it suitable for ultra-close formations at distances of 100 m or less. Its limitations include: Coulomb forces are internal forces acting only along the line connecting spacecraft (unable to change angular momentum for steering), and effectiveness is constrained by the Debye length in the space plasma environment.
