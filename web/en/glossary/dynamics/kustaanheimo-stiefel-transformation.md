# Kustaanheimo-Stiefel Transformation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A regularization transformation for collision singularities in the spatial three-body problem, mapping points in four-dimensional space to three-dimensional space while preserving Hamiltonian structure. An extension of the Levi-Civita transformation to spatial problems.
