# DMOC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A direct local optimal control method based on direct discretization of the variational structure of the mechanical system (Lagrange-d'Alembert principle), using forced discrete Euler-Lagrange equations as equality constraints for nonlinear optimization solved by SQP.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
