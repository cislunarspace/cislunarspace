# Normally Hyperbolic Invariant Manifold

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A normally hyperbolic invariant manifold that is expanding in hyperbolic directions and neutral in center directions; a bottleneck structure in phase space.

## Application Value

Normally Hyperbolic Invariant Manifold plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
