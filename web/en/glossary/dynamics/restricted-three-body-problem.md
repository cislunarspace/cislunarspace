# Restricted Three-Body Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A three-body system where two massive bodies (primaries) revolve around each other in circular orbits, while a third body (of negligible mass) is attracted by the primaries but does not influence their motion. This problem occupies a central place in analytical dynamics, celestial mechanics, and space dynamics.
