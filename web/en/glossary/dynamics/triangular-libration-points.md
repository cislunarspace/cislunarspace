## Definition

Two libration points forming equilateral triangles with the two primaries, denoted L₄ and L₅. They are linearly stable when the mass ratio of the two bodies satisfies certain conditions.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
