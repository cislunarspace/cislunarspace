# Adaptive Trajectory Design Catalog

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory design reference catalog developed collaboratively by Purdue University and NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. It contains data for various periodic and quasi-periodic orbits in the Earth-Moon system, including libration point orbits and resonant orbits, enabling trajectory designers to rapidly query and combine orbit segments. The paper extracts 3:4 and 4:3 resonant orbits from this catalog for transfer design.

## Application Value

The 自适应轨迹设计目录 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
