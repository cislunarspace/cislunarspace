# Ballistic Arc

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A flight segment where the spacecraft is unpowered and subject only to gravitational forces. In the CR3BP, the Jacobi constant is conserved along a ballistic arc. Transfer trajectories consist of alternating ballistic arcs (following invariant manifolds) and powered arcs (adjusting energy so that manifolds from different systems match at a Poincare section).

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
