# Cavitation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Phenomenon of vapor bubble formation when pump inlet pressure falls below propellant vapor pressure

## Application Value

Cavitation is the phenomenon of vapor bubble formation when pump inlet pressure falls below propellant vapor pressure. It must be avoided in spacecraft propulsion systems to prevent pump damage and performance degradation.
