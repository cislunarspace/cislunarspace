# Forced Pendulum

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A pendulum subjected to external periodic forcing, a classic model for studying chaos, bifurcation, and mode locking. The equation is x''+ε[sin x+sin(x-t)]=0.
