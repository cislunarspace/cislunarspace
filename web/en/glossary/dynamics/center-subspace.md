# Center Subspace

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In linearized analysis near a collinear libration point, the subspace spanned by eigenvectors corresponding to eigenvalues with modulus equal to 1. Both periodic and quasi-periodic orbits exist within the center subspace. Unlike the stable and unstable directions of the saddle-type subspace, motions within the center subspace neither diverge nor converge, sustaining quasi-periodic oscillations.

## Application Value

In orbit design and transfer trajectory optimization, the method determines the optimal transfer timing and orbit shape while minimizing fuel consumption or flight time. Through numerical simulation and iterative optimization, feasible orbit solutions satisfying mission constraints can be obtained.
