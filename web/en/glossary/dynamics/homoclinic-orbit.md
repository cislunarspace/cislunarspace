# Homoclinic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit that is both alpha-asymptotic and omega-asymptotic to the same unstable periodic orbit, meaning it approaches the same periodic solution in both time directions. Conley notes in Section 3.6 that such orbits must exist for appropriate mass ratios (to be proven in a future paper). The existence of homoclinic orbits signals chaotic motion in phase space, connecting the stable and unstable manifolds of the periodic orbit.
