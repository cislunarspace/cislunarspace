# pseudostate sphere radius

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter defining the size of the pseudostate sphere in pseudostate theory, determined by the return time. Inside the sphere, precise dynamics or gravity-dominated state transition matrices are used; outside, conic approximations apply. The Moon's optimal return time of 65% of flight time corresponds to the optimal sphere radius where accuracy of the inner and outer computation methods is best matched.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
