# Euler-Lagrange Equations

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Differential equations that render a functional stationary in the calculus of variations, describing the time evolution of states and costates in optimal control problems.

## Application Value

为航天器的精确控制提供理论依据，确保任务执行的可靠性 描述系统状态随时间的变化规律，是轨道预报的基础。
