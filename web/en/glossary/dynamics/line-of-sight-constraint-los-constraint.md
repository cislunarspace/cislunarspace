# Line-of-Sight Constraint, LOS Constraint

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A geometric constraint in rendezvous and docking requiring the chaser spacecraft to remain within the sensor cone of the target docking port at all times. Formulated as a set of five planar inequalities (A_L x(t) <= b_L), it defines a conical visibility region. Violating the LOS constraint means the target leaves the sensor field of view, constituting mission failure. In NRHO rendezvous, the LOS constraint is the most critical path constraint.
