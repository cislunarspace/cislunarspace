# Zero-Velocity Curves

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The intersection of zero-velocity surfaces with the orbital plane, used for qualitative analysis of possible motion regions for the third body. Hill used this method to prove that the moon's distance from Earth must remain bounded.

## Application Value

零速度曲线是分析轨道可达域和设计转移方案的重要工具。
