# Reinforced Lyapunov Controller, RLC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A control framework combining Lyapunov control laws with reinforcement learning, where neural networks learn state-dependent weight parameters, retaining both the stability of Lyapunov control and the optimization capability of RL.

## Application Value

This control method is suitable for attitude and orbit control of cislunar spacecraft, enabling precise maneuvers and stationkeeping.
