> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Linear time-invariant or time-varying approximation of relative motion when target spacecraft is at or near a libration point periodic orbit.

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
