# Lagrange-d'Alembert Principle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Principle describing the variational structure of controlled mechanical systems, forming the theoretical basis of DMOC.

## Application Value

This term has significant application value in cislunar space missions. In orbit design, it can be used to optimize transfer trajectories and reduce mission fuel consumption. In attitude control and dynamics analysis, it helps understand spacecraft motion characteristics in complex gravitational fields, providing theoretical support for mission planning. In navigation and orbit determination, methods based on this term can improve orbit prediction accuracy and support the development of autonomous navigation algorithms.
