# NRHO Two-Impulse Phasing

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A phasing maneuver on a Near-Rectilinear Halo Orbit where the chaser spacecraft executes two impulsive burns to rendezvous with a co-orbital target. The first burn departs the nominal orbit to chase or wait for the target; after the transfer time elapses, the second burn restores the chaser to the target's orbital state. Compared with three-impulse strategies, two-impulse phasing is more suitable for large phase difference rendezvous.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
