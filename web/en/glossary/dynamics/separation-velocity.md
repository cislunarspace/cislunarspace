# Separation Velocity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The velocity of fragments relative to the pre-breakup spacecraft center of mass during a breakup event. In breakup models, the logarithm of separation velocity follows a normal distribution (lognormal distribution), and the velocity vector direction is uniformly distributed across all spatial directions. The probability density function of separation velocity is a fundamental input for calculating the initial state distribution of the debris cloud.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
