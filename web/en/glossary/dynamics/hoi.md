# Halo Orbit Insertion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The process of applying the second impulse to introduce a spacecraft from a transfer trajectory into a target halo orbit. The insertion location and velocity components are design unknowns, determined through differential evolution optimization.

## Application Value

The halo orbit insertion concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
