# Time-to-Go

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The remaining time required for the spacecraft to fly from its current state to the terminal state, a key parameter in explicit guidance laws. For time-optimal control problems, time-to-go is obtained analytically from the terminal condition of the Hamiltonian via a quartic equation, and its accuracy directly affects the correctness of guidance commands.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
