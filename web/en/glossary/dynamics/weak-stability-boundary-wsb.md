# Weak Stability Boundary (WSB)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In the planar circular restricted three-body problem, the set of points along a radial segment emanating from a small body where the trajectory transitions from n-stable to n-unstable. Within a specific energy range, these points lie on the stable manifold of a Lyapunov orbit and satisfy the conditions of zero radial velocity and negative Kepler energy relative to the small body. The Weak Stability Boundary is a fractal set that can be used to construct low-energy Earth-Moon transfer trajectories.

## Application Value

In orbital design and transfer trajectory optimization, this method is used to determine optimal transfer timing and orbital shape, minimizing fuel consumption or flight time. Through numerical simulation and iterative optimization, feasible orbital solutions satisfying mission constraints can be obtained.
