# Radial-Transverse-Normal Frame, RTH

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A local coordinate frame attached to the spacecraft's orbit. The R-axis points radially outward along the position vector, the T-axis lies in the orbital plane along the transverse direction (near velocity), and the H-axis is normal to the orbital plane along the angular momentum direction. It is commonly used in moon-centered dynamics to express thrust components and equations of motion.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
