# Momentum Wheel Desaturation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The process of using thrusters to produce counter-torque to desaturate reaction wheels whose angular momentum accumulates during long-duration attitude control operations. Desaturation imparts unexpected velocity impulse disturbances on the spacecraft. For long-duration NRHO station-keeping missions, this is a non-negligible source of orbital disturbance. In the paper, desaturation events are scheduled at specific true anomaly values.
