# Escape Motion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Motion in which at least one body tends toward infinity in a three-body system, with escape conditions determined by energy, Jacobi integral, and other parameters.

## Application Value

该模型是分析地月空间动力学、设计低能量转移轨道的基础工具。该矩阵在可观测性分析和滤波器设计中起关键作用。分析逃逸条件有助于评估物体是否会离开目标天体附近区域。
