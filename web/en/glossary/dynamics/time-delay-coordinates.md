# Time-Delay Coordinates

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method for reconstructing the attractor of a dynamical system from a time series. Embedding scalar time series {x(t), x(t+T), ..., x(t+(d-1)T)} in d-dimensional space can recover the geometric structure of the original system.
