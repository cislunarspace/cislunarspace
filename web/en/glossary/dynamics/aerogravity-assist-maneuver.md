# Aerogravity-Assist Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A maneuver using aerodynamic forces from a planetary atmosphere to change orbital energy or reduce braking delta-v. It can lower the braking delta-v for crew vehicles returning to an LEO space station, making the LEO-based lunar mode more feasible in terms of delta-v budget.
