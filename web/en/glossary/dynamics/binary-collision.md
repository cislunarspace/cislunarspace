# Binary Collision

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A collision event where the distance between two bodies becomes zero, extensible to past and future through Sundman regularization.

## Application Value

该分析在碰撞预警和轨道规避机动规划中具有重要应用。
