# Strange Attractor

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A chaotic attractor with fractal structure. Nearby orbits diverge exponentially while motion remains bounded; the correlation dimension is non-integer. Typical examples include the Lorenz attractor and Hénon attractor.
