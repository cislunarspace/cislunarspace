# Backward Time Integration
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The numerical method for computing stable manifolds. Starting from a point on the periodic orbit, a small deviation is added along the stable eigenvector direction, and the resulting state is integrated backward in time. The resulting trajectory traces the stable manifold from the Earth or Moon region back toward the periodic orbit, revealing the complete flow path.

## Application Value

The Backward Time Integration plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
