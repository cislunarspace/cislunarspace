# Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A deep reinforcement learning policy gradient algorithm that maintains training stability by clipping the surrogate objective function, well-suited for continuous control tasks.

## Application Value

This method can be applied to solve optimization problems in cislunar missions, such as orbit transfer, fuel consumption minimization, or task scheduling.
