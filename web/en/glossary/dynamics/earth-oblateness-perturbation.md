# Earth Oblateness Perturbation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Perturbation of a satellite orbit caused by the non-spherical gravitational potential of Earth, primarily the J2 term.

## Application Value

地球扁率摄动是由地球非球形引力位引起的轨道摄动，对低轨卫星的轨道寿命和交会对接精度有显著影响，设计师在任务规划中必须考虑。
