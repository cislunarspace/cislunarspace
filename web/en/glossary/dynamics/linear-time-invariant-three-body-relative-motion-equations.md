# Linear Time-Invariant Three-Body Relative Motion Equations

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The nonlinear relative motion equations in a three-body system linearized at a reference point with frozen coefficients, yielding a set of linear ordinary differential equations with constant coefficients. Suitable for guidance design of close-range relative motion over short durations, with accuracy comparable to complex models.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
