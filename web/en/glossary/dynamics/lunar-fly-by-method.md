# Lunar Fly-by Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The Earth-to-NRHO transfer method selected by NASA for the Orion crewed mission. After TLI, the spacecraft performs a powered lunar swing-by at perilune, followed by NRHO insertion at a phase angle of approximately 157 degrees. Transfer period is short (generally under 7 days), but the NRHO insertion maneuver has a large angle relative to the velocity vector, reducing propulsive efficiency.

## Application Value

During the lunar orbit insertion phase, this parameter determines the energy requirements for insertion maneuvers and mission feasibility, being critical to mission success.
