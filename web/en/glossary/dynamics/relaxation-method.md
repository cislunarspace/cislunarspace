# Relaxation Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A strategy within direct methods that relaxes nonconvex dynamics or operational constraints into convex ones, transforming the original problem into a tractable convex optimization problem. A typical application is second-order cone programming relaxation in Mars powered descent guidance.

## Application Value

In the orbit design phase, the method can solve for low-energy transfer trajectories while reducing propellant consumption. The optimization method is computationally efficient and suited to constrained trajectory optimization problems.
