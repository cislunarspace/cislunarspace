# Natural Hamiltonian, H_nat

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The Hamiltonian in CR3BP composed of kinetic and potential energy, H_nat = v²/2 - (x²+y²)/2 - (1-μ)/r_13 - μ/r_23, conserved in the unperturbed CR3BP.

## Application Value

The Hamiltonian in CR3BP composed of kinetic and potential energy, conserved in the unperturbed CR3BP, an important physical quantity for classifying periodic orbits and evaluating transfer energy.
