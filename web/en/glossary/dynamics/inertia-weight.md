# Inertia Weight

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter in particle swarm optimization that determines the tendency of a particle to retain its previous velocity; larger inertia weight favors global exploration while smaller favors local convergence.
