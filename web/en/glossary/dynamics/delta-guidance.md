# Delta Guidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An early guidance method invented by Laning and Battin, defining reference trajectories via a geometric constraint function F(r,v,rT)=0 for intercepting targets, later superseded by the Q system.
