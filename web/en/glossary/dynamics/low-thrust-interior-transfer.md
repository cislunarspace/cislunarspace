# Low-Thrust Interior Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer strategy using low-thrust propulsion to spiral outward from an Earth parking orbit until reaching the interior branch of the stable manifold of a distant periodic orbit. Thrust direction is tangential to velocity, with propellant mass fraction around 10%.

## Application Value

The 低推力内部转移 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
