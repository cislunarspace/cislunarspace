> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Phase space structure connecting different energy regions; spacecraft can travel along it with low energy.
