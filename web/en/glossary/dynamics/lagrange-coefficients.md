# Lagrange Coefficients

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Coefficient pairs (f, g, fdot, gdot) that linearly relate position and velocity at two points on an orbit, expressible in terms of change in true anomaly or time of flight in the two-body problem.
