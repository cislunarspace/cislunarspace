# Extremal Flow

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The family of trajectories satisfying Pontryagin's Maximum Principle. The extremal flow is driven by the co-state Hamiltonian equations, and splits into bang extremals (nonvanishing switching function) and singular extremals (switching function identically zero).
