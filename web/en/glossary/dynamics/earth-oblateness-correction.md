# Earth oblateness correction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A correction applied to orbital calculations accounting for Earth's non-spherical gravitational effect, primarily from the J2 oblateness term. The paper introduces this correction in free-return trajectory design, using an improved multiple-conic method to enhance accuracy under Earth's oblateness effects.

## Application Value

该概念是分析地月空间动力学行为的基础工具。在轨道设计和任务分析中具有重要应用价值。
