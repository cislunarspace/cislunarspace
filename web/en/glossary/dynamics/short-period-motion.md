# Short-period Motion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

One mode of relative motion near triangular libration points, with a period of approximately one month and an axis ratio of approximately 2. Pure short-period relative motion can be obtained by selecting specific initial velocity conditions to filter out the long-period component; its in-plane frequency is close to the out-of-plane frequency, allowing a planar approximation to achieve near-circular formations.

## Application Value

The short-period mode near triangular libration points has a period of approximately one month with an axis ratio of about 2, suitable for designing near-circular formation flying reference trajectories.
