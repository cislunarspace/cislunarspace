# Polynomial Chaos Expansion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An expansion that transforms nonlinear uncertainty mapping into a sum of orthogonal polynomials multiplied by coefficients, serving as a surrogate model approach in uncertainty quantification.

## Application Value

Polynomial Chaos Expansion plays an important role in orbital mechanics and mission planning, applicable for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics and optimizing transfer trajectories.
