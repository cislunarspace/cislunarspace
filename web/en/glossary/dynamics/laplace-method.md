# Laplace Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A classical initial orbit determination method. It estimates the target's position and velocity by computing time derivatives of the line-of-sight vectors from multiple angular observations. Effective under two-body dynamics, but for cislunar objects, lunar gravitational perturbations cause deviations that require formal modifications.

## Application Value

In orbit design and optimization, this concept is used to analyze spacecraft motion characteristics in the cislunar multi-body gravitational field, providing a theoretical basis for low-energy transfer trajectory design.
