# Newton-Raphson Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An iterative numerical method for solving systems of nonlinear equations. This paper applies it to multi-impulse maneuver parameter optimization in orbit design, linearizing the equations and iterating to satisfy constraints such as apogee altitude and orbital inclination.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
