# Reaction Control System, RCS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A system that controls spacecraft attitude by generating reaction torques through jet thrust. The paper assumes 16 thrusters of 10 N each distributed on the chaser edges for attitude maneuvers and position control.

## Application Value

This control method is suitable for attitude and orbit control of cislunar spacecraft, enabling precise maneuvers and stationkeeping.
