# Intermediate Equations

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A simplified model between the bicircular problem and the real equations of motion, retaining only the time-independent terms and periodic terms with the synodic period of the Sun, used for computing intermediate periodic orbits.

## Application Value

The intermediate equations are a simplified model between the bicircular problem and the real equations of motion, retaining only periodic terms with the synodic period of the Sun. They are applied in computing intermediate periodic orbits.
