# Intermediate Equations

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A simplified model between the bicircular problem and the real equations of motion, retaining only the time-independent terms and periodic terms with the synodic period of the Sun, used for computing intermediate periodic orbits.

## Application Value

middleequation is 双圆problem和真实motionequation之间 of 简化model, 仅保留Sunwill合周期 of 周期项. In middle周期orbit of calculate中has applicationvalue.
