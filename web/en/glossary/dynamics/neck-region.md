# Neck Region

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A narrow passage near a collinear libration point that serves as the sole connection between the Earth region and the Moon region in cislunar space. The neck barely opens when Jacobi energy is just below C₁, permitting low-energy transfers; a second neck opening to the exterior region appears when energy drops below C₂.

## Application Value

The Neck Region near a collinear libration point serves as the sole connection between the Earth region and the Moon region in cislunar space. The neck barely opens when Jacobi energy is just below C₁, permitting low-energy transfers. Understanding neck region dynamics is critical for trajectory design between Earth and Moon regions.
