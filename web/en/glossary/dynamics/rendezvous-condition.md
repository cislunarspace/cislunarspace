# Rendezvous Condition

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

State where both relative position and relative velocity of the chaser with respect to the target are zero; the two spacecraft meet at the origin of the relative phase space.
