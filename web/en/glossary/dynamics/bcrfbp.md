# Bicircular Restricted Four-Body Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dynamical model approximating the Sun-Earth-Moon-spacecraft four-body system where Earth and Moon revolve in circular orbits around their barycenter, which in turn orbits the Sun; all motions are coplanar with constant angular velocity. Compared to CRTBP, it incorporates solar gravitational perturbation.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
