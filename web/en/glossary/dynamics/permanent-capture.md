# Permanent Capture
>
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The physical state in which a spacecraft's Jacobi constant drops below the critical value at L1, confining its motion to the vicinity of the Moon. In the PCR3BP, this is a strict condition: once met, the spacecraft cannot leave the lunar region without thrust.

## Application Value

The Permanent Capture plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
