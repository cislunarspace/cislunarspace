> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-order perturbation method for solving celestial mechanics problems through canonical transformations and Poisson series expansion.

## Application Value

Meyer's Method plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
