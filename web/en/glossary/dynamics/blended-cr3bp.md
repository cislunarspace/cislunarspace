# Blended CR3BP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method to model a four-body system by overlapping two circular restricted three-body problems (Sun–Earth and Earth–Moon) at their common body.

## Application Value

混合 CR3BP through 叠加两个圆型限制性三体problem来建模四体system, 以共同天体for 叠加基准. is analysis复杂多体system动力学 of 近似method.
