# Bicircular Restricted Four-Body Problem, BCR4BP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A four-body model that introduces perturbation of a third body (such as the Moon or Sun) on the restricted three-body problem. In this model, two primary bodies orbit each other while a third body moves in a circular orbit around one primary, making the system non-autonomous. The paper uses two variants: the Sun-Earth-Moon BCR4BP and the Earth-Moon-Sun BCR4BP.

## Application Value

This provides a fundamental theoretical framework for cislunar dynamics modeling and analysis, serving as a mathematical tool for studying spacecraft motion.
