# Planar Bicircular Restricted Four-Body Problem, PBRFBP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A four-body dynamics model combining two planar circular restricted three-body problems, with the Sun, Earth, and Moon as primary bodies and the spacecraft as the infinitesimal fourth body.
