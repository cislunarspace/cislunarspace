# Clohessy-Wiltshire Equations

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Linearized equations describing relative motion of spacecraft in near-circular orbits, also known as Hill's equations. Based on assumptions of central gravitational field, circular chief orbit, and small relative distance.

## Application Value

Clohessy-Wiltshire Equations plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
