# Maximum Stretching Direction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The direction along which a velocity impulse yields the maximum change in combined position and velocity states at the final propagation time, enabling rapid departure from or arrival at an orbit.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
