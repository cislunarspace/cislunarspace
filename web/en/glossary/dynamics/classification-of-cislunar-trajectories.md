# Classification of Cislunar Trajectories

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A methodology that classifies cislunar transfer trajectories into direct and low-energy types based on Jacobi energy and time of flight. Direct transfers have Jacobi energy far below C₁ with 2–6 day flight times; low-energy transfers have Jacobi energy just below C₁ and above C₂, where the neck region barely opens, requiring tens of days.

## Application Value

在设计地月转移方案时，利用该轨道特性可降低任务总速度增量需求。该矩阵在可观测性分析和滤波器设计中起关键作用。
