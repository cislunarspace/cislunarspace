# Collision Avoidance Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Active maneuvers performed to prevent the follower from approaching or colliding with the leader. The paper embeds the collision avoidance constraint into the MPC quadratic programming framework, approximating the KOZ sphere as a tangent plane through linearization and expressing it as an inequality constraint.

## Application Value

This term has significant practical applications in cislunar mission planning and execution.
