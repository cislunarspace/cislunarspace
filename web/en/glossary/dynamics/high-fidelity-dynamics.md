# High-Fidelity Dynamics

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

High-accuracy orbital dynamics model incorporating non-spherical Earth/Moon gravity, third-body perturbations from Sun/planets, solar radiation pressure, and lunar solid tides, used in Monte Carlo simulations to validate navigation performance.

## Application Value

该模型是分析地月空间动力学、设计低能量转移轨道的基础工具。
