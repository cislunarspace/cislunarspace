# Relative Degree

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A structural index of the input-output relationship of a nonlinear system, defined as the minimum number of differentiations required for the output derivative to explicitly contain the control input. The continuous-time Earth-Moon three-body dynamics has relative degree (2,2,2), but it drops to (1,1,1) after single-rate sampling, giving rise to additional 3-dimensional unstable sampling zero-dynamics. This is the fundamental reason multi-rate sampling is needed.
