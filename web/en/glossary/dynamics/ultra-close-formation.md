# Ultra-Close Formation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A formation flying mode with inter-spacecraft distances at or below the 100-meter scale. It demands extremely high control precision, as minor errors can cause configuration loss or collision. Traditional ion and chemical propulsion are unsuitable due to plume contamination; Coulomb force formation is currently the most promising technical approach. Among existing libration point formation studies, large-scale formations (kilometers to thousands of kilometers) are more common, while ultra-close formation research is relatively scarce.

## Application Value

This propulsion technology is important for deep space missions, serving as a key means for efficient orbital transfers.
