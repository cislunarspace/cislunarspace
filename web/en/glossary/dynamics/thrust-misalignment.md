# Thrust Misalignment

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The deviation between the actual thrust direction and the nominal direction of a thruster. Includes internal errors (misalignment between thruster flange and nozzle axis) and external errors (misalignment between flange and master spacecraft reference), modeled with Gaussian noise and uniform distribution respectively.

## Application Value

This concept is relevant to analysis and design in cislunar space applications.
