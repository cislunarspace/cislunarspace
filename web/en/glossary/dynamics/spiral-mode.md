# Spiral Mode

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A motion pattern where invariant manifolds spiral around a periodic orbit while departing or arriving. When the state transition matrix of a periodic orbit possesses complex eigenvalues with magnitudes greater than one, unstable manifolds depart along spiral paths rather than straight asymptotic directions. Certain members of the P2HO₁ (butterfly orbit family) exhibit unstable manifolds with this spiral mode character.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
