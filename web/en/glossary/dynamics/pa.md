# Principal Axes

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar body-fixed coordinate system defined by the Moon's principal axes of inertia, used for lunar dynamics studies and lunar laser ranging.

## Application Value

Principal Axes plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
