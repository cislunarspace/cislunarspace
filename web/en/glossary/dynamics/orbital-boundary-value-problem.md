## Definition

The problem of finding an orbit given initial and terminal states; fundamental in trajectory design, including classic cases such as Lambert's problem.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
