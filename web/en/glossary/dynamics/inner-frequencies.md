# Inner Frequencies

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The proper frequencies of motion near triangular libration points, generalizing the vertical, short-period, and long-period modes of the circular RTBP.

## Application Value

This term has important application value in the design, analysis, and control of cislunar missions, providing theoretical support for research and engineering practice in related fields.
