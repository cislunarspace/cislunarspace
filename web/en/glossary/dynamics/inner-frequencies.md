# Inner Frequencies

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The proper frequencies of motion near triangular libration points, generalizing the vertical, short-period, and long-period modes of the circular RTBP.

## Application Value

This术语在地月空间任务的设计、分析和控制中具有important应用价值，为相关技术领域的研究和工程实践provides理论支撑。
