# Injection Point

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A selected initial point on a periodic orbit, denoted as Pref. Any point on a manifold corresponds to two parameters: the time ΔtP0 from Pref along the periodic orbit to the injection point, and the time ΔtM of flight on the manifold from the injection point. In the paper, the manifold grid is constructed based on 100 equally-spaced injection points.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
