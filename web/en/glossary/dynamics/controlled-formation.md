# Controlled Formation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A formation in which impulsive control is applied to the deputy to overcome natural dynamics constraints. Unlike natural formations, controlled formations enable fast fly-around and arbitrary configurations at the cost of propellant consumption.
