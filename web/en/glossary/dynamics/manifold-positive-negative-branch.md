# Manifold Positive/Negative Branch

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Each invariant manifold (stable or unstable) splits into a positive and a negative branch, corresponding to two distinct asymptotic directions in phase space. Both branches of a stable manifold approach the target orbit but occupy different regions of phase space, resulting in different accessible regions when used for transfers from the Moon or Earth.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space trajectory design and transfer planning, enabling analysis of orbital characteristics and design of low-energy transfer schemes.
