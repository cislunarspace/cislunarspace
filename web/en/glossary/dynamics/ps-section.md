# PS Section

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The Poincaré section at x=1-μs in the Sun-Earth+Moon synodic frame, used for patching invariant manifolds of LL2 Lyapunov orbit with EL1 (or EL2) Lyapunov orbit. A key tool for constructing WSB transfers.
