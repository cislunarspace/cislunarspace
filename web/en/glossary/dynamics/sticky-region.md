> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

## Definition

A stable barrier formed by the stable region near DRO and the sticky region near P3DRO, preventing spacecraft from deviating from or entering DRO. The sticky region has a longer lifetime, causing spacecraft to stay nearby for extended periods.
