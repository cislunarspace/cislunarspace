# Consensus Clustering

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that uses an ensemble of individual clustering results to form a single clustering solution, improving clustering quality and robustness.

## Application Value

共识聚类through 集成多个聚类结果提high聚类quality and 稳健性. In orbit模式识别和空间target分类中, 共识聚类可有效decrease单一聚类算法 of 偏差.
