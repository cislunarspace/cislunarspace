# Radiation Pressure Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Mathematical models describing the laws of solar radiation pressure effects on satellites. They are divided into empirical models (such as ECOM model), physical models (based on satellite attitude and surface characteristics), and semi-empirical/semi-physical models. High-precision radiation pressure models are the key to improving orbit determination accuracy.
