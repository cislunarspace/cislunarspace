# Practical Stability

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Stability characterized by very low station-keeping cost, meaning a spacecraft can naturally follow the orbit for extended periods without significant maneuvers. Unlike mathematical stability, practically stable orbits in the CR3BP have no asymptotic stable or unstable manifolds available for transfer design.
