# Marginal Stability

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The state where all characteristic multipliers of a periodic orbit lie exactly on the unit circle in the complex plane. Station-keeping impulses are so small that some can be skipped, though perturbations may destroy this property.
