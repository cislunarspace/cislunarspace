>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A four-body dynamical model describing two primary bodies moving in circular orbits around their common barycenter with a test mass moving in their combined gravitational field, used to simulate the real gravitational environment of the Sun-Earth-Moon system.
