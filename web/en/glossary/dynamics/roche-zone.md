# Roche Zone

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The distance within which an orbiting body is tidally disrupted by a planet, defined as the region where planetary gravity equals the body's self-gravity.

## Application Value

Spacecraft orbit design and orbital avoidance for Earth-observation satellites must consider the effects of planetary tidal forces on orbital stability. When a spacecraft enters a planet's Roche zone, it risks being tidally disrupted.
