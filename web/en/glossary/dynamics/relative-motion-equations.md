# Relative Motion Equations

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Dynamical equations describing the relative position, velocity, and acceleration between a chaser and target spacecraft. In the three-body problem framework, gravitational effects from both primary bodies must be considered, including Coriolis force, centrifugal force, and gravity gradient terms.
