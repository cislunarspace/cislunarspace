# Adaptive Patch Distance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Adaptively varying patch sphere radius during RL training, used to switch control authority between Earth-centered and Moon-centered legs, encouraging the spacecraft to rendezvous with the lunar sphere of influence.
