# Plume Impingement

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Effect of propellant plume directly hitting a target surface; may damage optical devices, generate force perturbations and large disturbance torques, and disrupt thermal blankets and coatings.

## Application Value

The analysis of plume impingement can be applied to trajectory propagation and maneuver design, helping engineers evaluate spacecraft motion characteristics in various dynamical environments. This concept provides a theoretical foundation for cislunar space mission design, particularly valuable for libration point orbit design and low-energy transfer analysis. Applying plume impingement analysis to trajectory optimization can effectively reduce mission propellant consumption and improve mission economics. In mission design, plume impingement analysis helps understand spacecraft behavior in complex gravitational fields and guides the development of station-keeping strategies.
