# Equilateral Triangle Libration Point

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Libration points forming equilateral triangles with Earth and Moon, i.e. L4 and L5 points, located 60 degrees from the Earth-Moon line on the Moon's orbit, which are stable equilibrium points.

## Application Value

The Equilateral Triangle Libration Points (L4 and L5) form equilateral triangles with Earth and Moon, located 60 degrees from the Earth-Moon line on the Moon's orbit. They are stable equilibrium points and potential locations for cislunar infrastructure.
