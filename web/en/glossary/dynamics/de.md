# Differential Evolution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Population-based evolutionary optimization algorithm driven by group differences, used in this paper to search for optimal costate initial values and transfer times.

## Application Value

Differential Evolution plays an important role in orbital mechanics and mission planning, applicable for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics and optimizing transfer trajectories.
