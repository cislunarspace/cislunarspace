# Moon's Exterior Sphere of Influence

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The external spherical region around the Moon where lunar gravity is dominant and Earth-Sun gravity acts as perturbation. Unlike the conventional lunar SOI, it reflects the outer gravitational influence zone of the Moon in multi-body dynamics models.

## Application Value

Within the Moon's exterior sphere of influence, lunar gravity is dominant. Unlike the conventional lunar SOI, this concept reflects the outer gravitational influence zone of the Moon in multi-body dynamics models and serves as a reference boundary for cislunar transfer trajectory design.
