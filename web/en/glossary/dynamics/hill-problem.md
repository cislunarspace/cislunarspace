# Hill Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The limiting case of the restricted three-body problem as the mass parameter approaches zero, where an integral of motion exists and periodic orbits organize into one-parameter families. Serves as the theoretical starting point for deriving and classifying fundamental periodic orbit families.
