# Poincaré Map

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dimension-reduction technique for studying high-dimensional dynamical systems through intersections of a section with the phase flow. Transforms continuous orbit problems into discrete maps, facilitating analysis of periodic orbits and invariant tori.
