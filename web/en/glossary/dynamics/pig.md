# Pendulous Integrating Gyro Assembly

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Gyroscopic device using pendulous integrating principle to measure thrust acceleration, with output proportional to velocity-to-be-gained components; key sensor in Q system guidance.

## Application Value

Pendulous Integrating Gyro Assembly plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
