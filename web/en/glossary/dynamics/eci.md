# Earth-Centered Inertial Frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Coordinate system with origin at Earth's center of mass, fundamental plane as Earth's equatorial plane, X-axis pointing to vernal equinox, K-axis pointing to North Pole. Also known as GCRF.

## Application Value

Earth-Centered Inertial Frame plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
