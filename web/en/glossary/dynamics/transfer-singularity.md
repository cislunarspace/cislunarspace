# Transfer singularity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A phenomenon in normal transfer problems where the required initial velocity becomes undefined or unbounded when an element of the state transition matrix equals zero, causing impulse spikes.
