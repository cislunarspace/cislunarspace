# Unstable Eigenvector

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An eigenvector of the monodromy matrix corresponding to an eigenvalue with absolute value greater than 1, indicating the direction along which perturbations grow exponentially. Traditional Floquet stationkeeping methods cancel the unstable component of errors along this direction, whereas OCS yields maneuvers more aligned with the stable direction.

## Application Value

In orbital design, analysis, and control, dynamics models are used to predict spacecraft trajectories, with equations of motion solved numerically or analytically. This concept underpins critical mission capabilities including orbital maneuver design, orbit improvement, and formation flying.
