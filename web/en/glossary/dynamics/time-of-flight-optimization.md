# Time-of-Flight Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique that searches for the optimal time of flight to minimize a performance metric in guidance problems. Burns and Scherock (2004) used Powell's method to optimize interceptor flight time and minimize velocity-matching fuel consumption. The mechanism: flight time determines the approach angle to the target trajectory; a shallower angle reduces the required velocity-matching increment.


## Application Value

In a linear quadratic optimal control framework, selecting appropriate weight matrices Q and R achieves a balance between tracking accuracy and control energy consumption, making it suitable for real-time control in cislunar orbit keeping.
