# Cylindrical Shadow Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A shadow model that approximates a celestial body's solar shadow as a cylinder aligned with the rotation axis. A spacecraft inside the cylinder receives no sunlight, causing SEP shutdown. It is more conservative than conical shadow models, easy to implement, and significantly affects propellant consumption in low-orbit multi-revolution missions.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
