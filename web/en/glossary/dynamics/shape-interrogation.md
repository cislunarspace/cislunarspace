# Shape Interrogation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Methods that focus on extracting geometric information about curves and surfaces, employed in disciplines such as computer graphics and computer-aided geometric design and modeling.

## Application Value

Shape interrogation methods extract geometric information from curves and surfaces, applied in computer graphics and computer-aided geometric design for trajectory shaping.
