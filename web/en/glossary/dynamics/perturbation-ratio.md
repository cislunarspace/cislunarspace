# Perturbation Ratio

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Perturbation Ratio; the ratio of third-body acceleration to the sum of all accelerations in the system, used to characterize the strength of third-body asymmetry in a three-body system; larger values indicate stronger asymmetry and better observability for LiAISON navigation.

## Application Value

The Perturbation Ratio characterizes the strength of third-body asymmetry in a three-body system; larger values indicate stronger asymmetry and better observability for LiAISON navigation. Understanding perturbation ratio helps designers optimize navigation performance.
