# Lunar Phasic Angle (θs)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The parameter measuring the included angle between the line from the Sun to the barycenter of the Earth-Moon system and the intersecting line of the ecliptic and lunar planes.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space dynamics modeling and analysis, and can be applied to trajectory design and mission planning.
