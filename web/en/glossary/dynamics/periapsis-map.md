# Periapsis Map
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A Poincaré section defined by the periapsis condition where the position and velocity vectors are perpendicular, used to design Earth escape stages that depart a circular LEO tangentially. Unlike traditional Poincaré sections, it guarantees a purely tangential escape from a circular orbit.

## Application Value

The Periapsis Map is significant in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. It can be used for transfer trajectory optimization during orbital design, navigation and control for improved mission precision and reliability, and system analysis to understand multi-body dynamical behavior and guide mission planning.
