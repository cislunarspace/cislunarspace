# High-fidelity Ephemeris Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A full solar system ephemeris model including gravitational attraction of all planets, the Moon, and the Sun using JPL planetary ephemerides (DE405), accurately reflecting the real orbital mechanics environment. Used in this paper to validate results from the simplified CRTBP model.

- Welch et al., 2015, Mission Considerations for Transfers to a Distant Retrograde Orbit
