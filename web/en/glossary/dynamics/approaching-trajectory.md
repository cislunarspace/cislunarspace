# Approaching Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The transfer arc from a working orbit toward the Moon. Approaching trajectories from NRHOs have perilune altitudes below 300 km and eccentricities near 1 (approximately parabolic).
