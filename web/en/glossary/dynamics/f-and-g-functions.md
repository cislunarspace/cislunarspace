# f and g Functions

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Functions expressing the position and velocity at any point on an orbit through linear combinations of initial position and velocity vectors: r = fr₀ + gv₀, v = ḟr₀ + ġv₀, where f, g, ḟ, ġ are time functions. Applicable to orbit propagation and initial orbit determination.
