# Apsidal Motion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The slow rotation of the orbital line of apsides in space, caused by the tangential component of perturbing acceleration. Closely related to the resonance argument in resonant systems.
