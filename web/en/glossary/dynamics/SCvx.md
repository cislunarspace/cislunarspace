# Successive Convexification

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An optimization method that iteratively convexifies non-convex constraints to solve trajectory optimization problems.

## Application Value

Solving landing guidance through convex optimization offers high computational efficiency suitable for real-time onboard computation, though with limited accuracy in handling nonlinear dynamics and constraints.
