> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An iterative approach for solving non-convex optimal control problems through convexification.

## Application Value

Approximating original non-convex optimization problems through sequential convex programming, a practical method for solving trajectory optimization problems in engineering.
