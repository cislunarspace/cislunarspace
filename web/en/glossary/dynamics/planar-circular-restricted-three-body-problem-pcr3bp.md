# Planar Circular Restricted Three-Body Problem, PCR3BP (PCR3BP)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The two-dimensional specialization of the CR3BP, assuming the two primaries move in uniform circular motion in a common plane while the massless third body is confined to that plane. In the Earth-Moon system this model neglects small variations in the lunar orbital inclination. Despite its simplifications, PCR3BP effectively captures fundamental low-energy transfer dynamics, especially the periodic orbit and invariant manifold structures near libration points.

## Application Value

该轨道类型可作为地月空间运输网络的中转站或任务的目标轨道。在设计地月转移方案时，利用该轨道特性可降低任务总速度增量需求。利用流形结构可设计低能量转移轨道，减少对推进剂的依赖。该模型是分析地月空间动力学、设计低能量转移轨道的基础工具。
