# Natural Formation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A formation flying approach that leverages the natural dynamical environment near a reference trajectory to maintain a multi-spacecraft configuration. By placing the formation within low drift regions, the relative distance and pointing direction between spacecraft remain approximately constant with minimal or no active control.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
