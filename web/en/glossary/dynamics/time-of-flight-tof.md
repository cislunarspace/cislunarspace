# Time of Flight, ToF

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The transfer time from initial to final position, one of the three boundary conditions in Lambert's problem.

## Application Value

Time of flight is one of the three boundary conditions in Lambert's problem, determining the transfer orbit shape and velocity increment. Mission planning requires trade-offs between transfer time and propellant consumption.
