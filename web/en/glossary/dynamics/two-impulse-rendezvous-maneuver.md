# Two-Impulse Rendezvous Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A rendezvous maneuver accomplished with only two impulses.

## Application Value

Two-impulse rendezvous is the most fundamental and classical form of orbital maneuver. For coplanar or nearly coplanar spacecraft, precisely calculating the timing and direction of two impulses enables rendezvous completion in the shortest time. In engineering, the first impulse changes orbit to align phases of the two spacecraft, and the second impulse eliminates relative velocity to achieve docking. This method is widely used in space station resupply, satellite servicing, and crewed lunar landing docking missions. Although two-impulse is theoretically optimal, actual missions must consider orbital control errors, ranging accuracy, and docking safety margins, possibly requiring additional fine-tuning impulses.
