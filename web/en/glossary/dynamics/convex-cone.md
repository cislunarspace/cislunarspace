# Convex Cone

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The geometric representation of the glide slope constraint: a cone with its apex at the landing point, vertical axis, and half-angle β, whose interior defines the safe flight region for the lander. The constraint itself is convex, but coupling with nonconvex dynamics makes the overall optimal control problem nonconvex.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
