# Chebyshev-Gauss-Lobatto Nodes

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A discretization scheme with denser distribution at interval endpoints, where node positions are given by zk = -cos(kπ/N). Compared to uniform distribution, it provides higher sensitivity at boundaries, suitable for handling problems with strong dynamic sensitivity at transfer trajectory endpoints.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space research and applications.
