# Elliptic Restricted Three-Body Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A restricted three-body problem model where the third body has zero mass and the primary bodies move in elliptical orbits rather than circular orbits around their barycenter.

## Application Value

The 椭圆限制性三体问题 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
