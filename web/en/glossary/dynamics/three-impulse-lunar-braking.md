# three-impulse lunar braking

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A braking and capture method when a spacecraft enters lunar orbit from an Earth-Moon transfer trajectory, involving three impulses at the perilune. The first impulse converts the hyperbolic trajectory into a large elliptical orbit at perilune; the second impulse adjusts the orbital plane at apolune; the third impulse achieves rendezvous with the target lunar orbit at perilune. This approach effectively reduces the velocity increment needed for lunar capture.

## Application Value

These coefficients form an important tool in lunar theory, essential for high-precision orbit calculations in lunar missions.
