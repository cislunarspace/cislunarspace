# Energy Reduction Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A retrograde burn that reduces a spacecraft's orbital energy, transitioning it from a higher-energy to a lower-energy trajectory toward Earth. In this paper, the first maneuver simultaneously performs energy reduction and plane change: lowering energy enables the spacecraft to return to Earth for a gravity assist.
