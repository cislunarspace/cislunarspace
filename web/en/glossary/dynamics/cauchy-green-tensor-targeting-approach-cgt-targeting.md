# Cauchy-Green Tensor Targeting Approach, CGT Targeting
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An impulsive station-keeping strategy developed specifically for NRHOs. It constructs the Cauchy-Green tensor from the state transition matrix, obtains the accessible region under linear approximation via eigenvector decomposition, and determines the optimal correction impulse satisfying orbit constraints. This approach exploits the geometric structure of three-body dynamics and effectively handles the weak instability of NRHOs.

## Application Value

The Cauchy-Green Tensor Targeting Approach, CGT Targeting plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
