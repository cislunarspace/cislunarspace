# π-Singularity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of switching point that appears when distributions on the switching surface are involutive. At this point, the switching angle is exactly π, meaning the control direction reverses without changing magnitude. The existence of π-singularities is related to the rank conditions of distributions and Lie brackets.

## Application Value

In attitude control and orbit control systems, this concept is used to design feedback laws for precise调节 of spacecraft motion. This concept has important application value in cislunar space missions and deserves in-depth study.
