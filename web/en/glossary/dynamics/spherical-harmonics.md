# Spherical Harmonics

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Orthogonal basis functions used to expand the gravitational potential of a non-spherical body in spherical coordinates. Higher degree and order capture finer gravitational anomalies at greater computational cost. This study uses GRAIL data truncated to degree and order 8.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
