# Pruning Technique

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique in global trajectory optimization that uses domain knowledge to eliminate infeasible or clearly suboptimal candidate solutions in advance, reducing search space and accelerating convergence. Myatt et al. proposed effective pruning for ballistic multi-gravity-assist trajectories, achieving polynomial search complexity. The paper proposes combining exponential sinusoids with pruning techniques to enable efficient global search for low-thrust multi-gravity-assist missions.
