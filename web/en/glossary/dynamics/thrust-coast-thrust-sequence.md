# Thrust-Coast-Thrust Sequence

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A pre-structured flight profile for Earth-moon transfers consisting of a continuous-thrust Earth-escape spiral, a thrust-free translunar coast, and a continuous-thrust moon-capture spiral. The three segments are joined via state-matching conditions. Compared to continuous-thrust profiles, the coast phase significantly reduces propellant consumption.

## Application Value

推力-滑行-推力序列是低推力转移的典型推力模式，设计师通过优化推力开关时机最小化燃料消耗。
