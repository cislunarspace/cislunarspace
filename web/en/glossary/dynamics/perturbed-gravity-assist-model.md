# perturbed gravity assist model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An approximate gravity assist model based on pseudostate theory that decomposes the three-body problem into analytically computable conic arc segments via the pseudostate sphere, avoiding direct numerical integration. It improves near-lunar trajectory accuracy by incorporating perturbation corrections such as lunar non-spherical perturbation. It achieves a better trade-off between computational efficiency and accuracy compared to traditional Lambert patching.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
