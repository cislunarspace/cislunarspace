# Inertia Ellipsoid

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Geometric ellipsoid describing anisotropy of rigid body rotational inertia, equation Ix·x²+Iy·y²+Iz·z²=1.

## Application Value

该几何表示可用于描述姿态机动中转动惯量的各向异性。
