# Relative Attitude Quaternion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A quaternion describing the relative attitude between the chaser body frame and the LVLH frame. The paper uses the form q=[cos(θ/2), e·sin(θ/2)], where e is the Euler rotation eigenaxis and θ is the rotation angle about that axis. Coupled with the quaternion kinematic equation and relative angular velocity.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
