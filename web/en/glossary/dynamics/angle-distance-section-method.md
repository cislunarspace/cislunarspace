# Angle-Distance Section Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method using angular and radial quantities as Poincaré section coordinates, which maintains geometric clarity regardless of section inclination angle, superior to traditional coordinate representations.

## Application Value

The Angle-Distance Section Method uses angular and radial quantities as Poincare section coordinates, maintaining geometric clarity regardless of section inclination angle. It is superior to traditional coordinate representations for trajectory analysis.
