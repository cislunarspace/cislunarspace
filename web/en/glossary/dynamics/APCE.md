# Adaptive Polynomial Chaos Expansion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An adaptive polynomial chaos expansion surrogate model that uses adaptive coefficient regression methods such as least angle regression to overcome the curse of dimensionality, enabling efficient solution of uncertain multiple-revolution Lambert problems.

## Application Value

Adaptive polynomial chaos expansion adaptively approximates uncertain system responses through online adjustment of orthogonal polynomial bases, suitable for uncertainty analysis.
