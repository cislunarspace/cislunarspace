# Double Powered Lunar Flyby

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer strategy that performs two consecutive powered lunar flybys. The first applies an impulse at perilune to change in-plane velocity components and inclination relative to the Earth-Moon orbital plane; the second applies another impulse at a subsequent perilune after returning to the plane, directing the spacecraft toward the target orbit. Double flybys save propellant compared to a single large maneuver because the Moon's gravitational steering effect is utilized twice.

## Application Value

在双次动力月球飞越的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 为航天器的精确控制提供理论依据，确保任务执行的可靠性 用于分析航天器在复杂引力场中的运动特性。
