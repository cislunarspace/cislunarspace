# Lunar Gravity Assist Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A maneuver applying a velocity increment at perilune to change the spacecraft's orbital energy and direction using the Moon's gravity. Unlike a ballistic flyby, this adds propulsive thrust during the flyby for greater orbital adjustment. Typical delta-V in this paper ranges from 180 to 200 m/s.

## Application Value

These coefficients form an important tool in lunar theory, essential for high-precision orbit calculations in lunar missions.
