# LP165P Lunar Gravitational Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Spherical harmonic coefficient model describing the Moon's gravitational potential at 50×50 order/degree, used to compute non-spherical lunar gravity effects on lunar orbits.

## Application Value

LP165P Lunar Gravitational Model plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
