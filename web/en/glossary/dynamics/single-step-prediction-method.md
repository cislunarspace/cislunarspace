# Single-Step Prediction Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A control optimization strategy for libration point orbit station-keeping. It exploits the property that the residual from the n-th revolution significantly affects the (n+1)-th revolution's control cost but the effect on the (n+2)-th typically exceeds the threshold. By optimizing only for the single-step-ahead control cost, it avoids the computational complexity and accumulated errors of multi-step prediction.
