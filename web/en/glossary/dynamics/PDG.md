# Powered Descent Guidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The technology that steers a vehicle to vertical landing through active deceleration and attitude adjustment during the final phase of spacecraft descent.

## Application Value

It actively controls spacecraft deceleration and attitude during the final landing phase, serving as the core technology for precise soft landing and directly determining landing accuracy and fuel consumption.
