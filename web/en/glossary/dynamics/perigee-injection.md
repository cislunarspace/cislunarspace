# Perigee Injection

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Orbit insertion applying impulse at transfer orbit perigee to enter large elliptical transfer trajectory.

## Application Value

Perigee Injection plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
