# Second-Order Cone Constraint, SOC Constraint

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A constraint form in second-order cone programming, used to formulate the delta-v minimization as a convex optimization problem. PC-SCoP replaces the two-norm objective with an SOC constraint to avoid high sensitivity at small delta-v magnitudes.

## Application Value

The standard constraint form in second-order cone programming, formulating two-norm minimization as convex constraints, suitable for engineering problems like low-thrust maneuver optimization.
