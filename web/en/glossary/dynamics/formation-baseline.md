# Formation Baseline

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The desired position distance of a follower spacecraft relative to the leader in formation flying. Baseline length directly affects observation resolution, control difficulty, and required thrust. A longer baseline enhances observation capability but increases control difficulty. The baseline in this paper is 50 m (ultra-close formation), with a baseline relative error within 1%.

## Application Value

在航天器姿态和轨道控制中，该方法用于实现高精度跟踪和稳定保持. 通过设计合适的控制律，可以有效抑制外部扰动的影响，保证航天器在复杂动力学环境中的可靠运行.
