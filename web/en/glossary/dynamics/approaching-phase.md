# Approaching Phase

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The first phase of sliding mode control system motion, where system states move from any initial position toward the sliding surface. The dynamic quality of the approaching phase is governed by the reaching law, which must balance speed and low chattering. This paper uses a fractional-power reaching law to ensure the approaching phase duration has an upper bound independent of initial conditions.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
