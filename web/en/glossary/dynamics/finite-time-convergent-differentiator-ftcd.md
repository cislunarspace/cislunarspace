# Finite-Time-Convergent Differentiator, FTCD

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A signal differentiation estimator based on singular perturbation techniques. Given a measurable signal, it reconstructs the first and second derivatives with high accuracy within finite time. Typically used to estimate velocity from position measurements when direct velocity sensing is unavailable.
