> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Universal functions can be evaluated via exponential functions e^(jx) and e^(-jx), eliminating trigonometric functions and improving computational efficiency.
