# Crossover Operator

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In differential evolution, the operation of generating a trial vector by mixing the target vector with the mutant vector at a certain probability. The crossover probability CR is one of the algorithm's key parameters.

## Application Value

The 交叉算子 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
