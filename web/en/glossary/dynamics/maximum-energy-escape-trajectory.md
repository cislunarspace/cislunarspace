# Maximum-Energy Escape Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The optimal escape spiral trajectory that maximizes total spacecraft energy within a fixed time. Used in the first stage of the three-stage optimization approach to provide an initial reference for the full transfer problem.

## Application Value

该概念在轨道能量分析中用于量化航天器的能量状态，是评估转移方案经济性的重要指标。
