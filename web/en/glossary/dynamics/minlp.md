# Mixed Integer Nonlinear Programming, MINLP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An optimization problem involving both integer and continuous variables with nonlinear constraints. The interplay of discrete choices and nonlinear dynamics makes the feasible space combinatorially vast. Cislunar architecture design problems naturally fall into this class, requiring advanced search methods such as MCTS to approximate global optima.

## Application Value

Mixed Integer Nonlinear Programming, MINLP plays a critical role in trajectory optimization and multi-body dynamics analysis for solving complex trajectory design problems.
