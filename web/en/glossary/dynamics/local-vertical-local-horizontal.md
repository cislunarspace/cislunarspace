# Local Vertical Local Horizontal

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A reference frame centered on the target spacecraft with R-bar pointing toward the Moon, H-bar perpendicular to the radial direction and velocity cross product, and V-bar completing the right-handed coordinate system.
