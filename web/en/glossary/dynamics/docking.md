> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Physical connection of two spacecraft in orbit, typically performed after rendezvous.


## Application Value

交会是地月空间任务中的关键环节，实现两个航天器在轨接近和特定相对位置形成，是空间站对接、燃料补加等任务的基础。
