# θ-r Section Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A Poincare section construction method that uses angle and distance as coordinates. θ is the angle between the velocity direction and the section normal, and r is the distance from the intersection point to Earth. This method uses coordinates (r, θ) instead of traditional (x, ẋ) or (y, ẏ), providing a more intuitive representation of the geometric relationship between the manifold and target orbit sections, facilitating the search for patching points on the Poincare section. In three-dimensional space, it extends to four coordinates (r̄, θ, β, γ).

## Application Value

θ-r Section Method plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
