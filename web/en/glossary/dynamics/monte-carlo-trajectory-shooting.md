> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that randomly samples parameter sets to propagate corresponding trajectory segments and construct databases.

## Application Value

Monte Carlo Trajectory Shooting plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
