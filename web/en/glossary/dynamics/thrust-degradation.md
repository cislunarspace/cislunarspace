# Thrust Degradation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A robustness testing method that progressively reduces deterministic thrust levels (e.g., 0% to 35%) to examine how a pretrained policy adapts to degraded propulsion performance. The A2PPO policy maintains successful transfer with up to 32% thrust degradation.

## Application Value

在低推力轨道优化中，该推进方式可通过长时间持续加速实现低能量转移，比高推力系统更具燃料经济性。
