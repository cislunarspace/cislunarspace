# Circular Non-linear Equations of Relative Motion, CNERM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Non-linear relative motion equations based on the CR3BP assumption, neglecting the eccentricity of the primaries. Has good accuracy near the aposelene with errors at centimeter level, sufficient for proximity rendezvous guidance requirements.

## Application Value

Circular Non-linear Equations of Relative Motion, CNERM plays an important role in orbital mechanics and mission planning, applicable for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics and optimizing transfer trajectories.
