# Shape-Based Feature Vector

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A finite-dimensional vector composed of tangent unit vectors at sampled states along a trajectory, used in clustering to capture the local shape of a trajectory.

## Application Value

Shape-Based Feature Vector plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
