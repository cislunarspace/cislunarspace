# Planar Bicircular Restricted Four-Body Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The planar form of the BR4BP, neglecting motion out of the orbital plane.

## Application Value

The Planar Bicircular Restricted Four-Body Problem is the planar form of the BR4BP, neglecting motion out of the orbital plane. It provides a simplified model for analyzing certain four-body dynamics scenarios.
