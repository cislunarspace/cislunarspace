# Two-Impulse Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer maneuver using two impulsive velocity changes: the first impulse alters the orbit to enter a target trajectory, and the second impulse completes orbit capture.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
