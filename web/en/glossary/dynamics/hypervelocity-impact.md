# Hypervelocity Impact

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-speed impact phenomenon where collision velocity far exceeds the material's speed of sound. Fragment collisions during spacecraft breakup events fall within the hypervelocity regime, with collision velocities reaching several to tens of kilometers per second. Under hypervelocity impact, materials behave fluid-like, and the resulting fragment size and velocity distributions follow specific statistical patterns, forming the physical basis for breakup model formulation.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
