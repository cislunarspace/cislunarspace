# Total Velocity Impulse

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The sum of all velocity impulses required to complete a full orbital transfer. In the paper, the total velocity impulse is approximately 3.28 km/s, including the departure impulse from the parking orbit and the halo orbit insertion impulse.

## Application Value

The 总速度增量 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
