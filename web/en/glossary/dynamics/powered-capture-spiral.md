# Powered Capture Spiral

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The flight phase where a low-thrust spacecraft spirals inward toward a target body, gradually decreasing altitude until achieving the target orbit. The NEP vehicle requires about 7 days to spiral from near-perilune to HLO.
