# Quasi-Periodic Solution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of solution for linearized relative motion derived from Floquet theory. Quasi-periodic solutions result from the superposition of two periodic components at different frequencies, oscillating quasi-periodically in-plane or out-of-plane while remaining bounded but not exactly repeating. In nonlinear dynamics, they correspond to quasi-periodic orbits near the chief.
