# Mechanical Energy in Earth-Moon Space

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The mechanical energy of a spacecraft relative to the Earth-Moon barycenter, influenced by the combined gravitational forces of Earth and Moon. Unlike Keplerian energy relative to a single body, mechanical energy changes due to gravitational work from celestial bodies when moving within Earth-Moon space.

## Application Value

Mechanical Energy in Earth-Moon Space plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
