# Insertion Impulse

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Final impulse applied at DRO insertion, combining with departure impulse to form total velocity increment; in Pareto front solutions, it decreases from 98.5 m/s to 2.2 m/s as transfer time increases.

## Application Value

In the field of dynamics, Insertion Impulse is a key element for understanding and analyzing system behavior. This concept is widely used in spacecraft trajectory design, dynamics modeling, and mission planning, providing theoretical support for engineering practice.
