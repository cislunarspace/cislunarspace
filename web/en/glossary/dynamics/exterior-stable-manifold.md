# Exterior Stable Manifold

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The half of the stable manifold of a halo orbit that extends away from the Moon. A spacecraft on the exterior stable manifold moves outward from the halo orbit and can be used to design exterior transfer trajectories from LEO to L1 or L2 halo orbits.
