# Centrifugal Acceleration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The apparent acceleration experienced by an object in a rotating reference frame due to the frame's own rotation, equal to the double cross product of angular velocity and position vector (-omega×(omega×R)). In the paper, the lunar gravitational acceleration consists of the central gravitational acceleration and the centrifugal acceleration. In the soft landing model, centrifugal acceleration varies with the spacecraft's distance from the lunar rotation axis and is an essential non-inertial force term in the body-fixed frame dynamics.

## Application Value

在离心加速度的分析中，可用于轨道传播和机动设计，帮助工程师评估航天器在不同动力学环境下的运动特性 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了理论基础，尤其在平动点轨道设计和低能转移分析中具有重要应用价值 利用离心加速度进行轨迹优化，可以有效降低任务燃料消耗，提高任务经济效益 在任务设计中，离心加速度的分析有助于理解航天器在复杂引力场中的行为，指导轨道保持策略的制定
