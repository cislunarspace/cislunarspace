# Leakage

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Spurious frequency components produced in discrete Fourier transform due to time domain truncation that is not a multiple of the period; an inherent error in Fourier analysis.

## Application Value

Leakage plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
