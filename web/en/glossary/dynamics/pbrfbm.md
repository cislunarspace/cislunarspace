# Planar Bicircular Restricted Four-Body Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A planar four-body dynamic model where two primary bodies revolve on coplanar circular orbits about their barycenter, a third body perturbates the system periodically in a circular orbit around the barycenter, and the spacecraft moves under the gravity of all three bodies.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
