# Section-Forced Refinement

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A staged refinement procedure for transforming coupled-RTBP connecting trajectories into JPL ephemeris coordinates. The EM leg is refined freely to the Poincaré section, then the resulting position at the section is forced as the initial condition for refining the SE leg. This yields position-continuous trajectories in JPL coordinates, though a velocity jump remains at the coupling point.
