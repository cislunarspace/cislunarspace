# LVLH, Local-Vertical Local-Horizon Reference Frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A rotating reference frame centered at the target spacecraft center of mass, with one axis along the local vertical and another along the local horizontal. The i-axis points toward the Earth or along the velocity direction, the j-axis is perpendicular to the orbital angular momentum, and the k-axis completes the right-handed system. Suitable for describing relative motion and designing control laws near libration points.

## Application Value

在局部垂直局部水平坐标系的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 为航天器的精确控制提供理论依据，确保任务执行的可靠性。
