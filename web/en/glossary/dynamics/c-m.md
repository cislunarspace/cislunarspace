# Conley-McGehee Tube, C-M Tube

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A 2D hypersurface formed by stable and unstable invariant manifolds of periodic orbits as proposed by Conley and McGehee, separating transit from non-transit trajectories.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
