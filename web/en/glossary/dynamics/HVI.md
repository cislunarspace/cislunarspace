# Hyper-volume Indicator, HVI

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A metric in multi-objective optimization measuring the area covered by a Pareto front. Defined as the hypervolume of the objective space bounded by a reference point and enclosed by the Pareto front. Larger HVI indicates broader coverage and better diversity, serving as the core scalar evaluation metric for action selection in the MO-MCTS algorithm.

## Application Value

This concept is significant for space-based optical observation and space situational awareness, used for evaluating spacecraft visibility to targets.
