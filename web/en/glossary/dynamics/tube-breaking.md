# Tube Breaking

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The phenomenon where transportation tube walls lose continuity due to time-varying solar gravity perturbation in SBCM. Tube breaking exposes enclosed transiting trajectories, drastically reducing available trajectories while making transfer time finite instead of infinite.

## Application Value

在管道断裂的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 用于分析航天器在复杂引力场中的运动特性。
