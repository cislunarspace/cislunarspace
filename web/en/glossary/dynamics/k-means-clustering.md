# K-Means Clustering

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A classical clustering method that partitions data into K clusters based on Euclidean distance. Each cluster is represented by its centroid (mean vector), and the algorithm iteratively assigns data points to the nearest centroid and updates centroid positions. The paper applies it to objective-space clustering to analyze architectural characteristics in different regions of objective space.
