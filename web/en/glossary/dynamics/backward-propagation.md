# Backward Propagation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Integrating the equations of motion backward in time from a given NRHO state to determine the required prior maneuver conditions. The paper extensively uses backward propagation to design EPRM trajectories of various types: propagating backward from the NRHOI state to determine PLSB targeting conditions and the timing and magnitude of intermediate maneuvers. Compared to forward propagation from Earth, backward propagation is better suited for exploring feasible transfer trajectories starting from the target orbit.

## Application Value

反向轨道传播从终点向前推算初始条件，是边值问题求解的辅助方法。
