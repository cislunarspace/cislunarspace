# Adams-Cowell Integrator

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A linear multistep numerical integration method combining Adams predictor-corrector and Cowell formulas. The Cowell formula solves second-order ODEs directly without introducing velocity as an intermediate variable, suitable for long-arc precision satellite orbit integration.
