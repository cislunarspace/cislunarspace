# Jacobi Constant, JC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The constant energy integral in the rotating frame, representing the energy level of a trajectory and useful for estimating minimum transfer costs between orbits.

## Application Value

The conserved energy in CRTBP corresponding to Jacobi energy, a key parameter for classifying periodic orbits and evaluating transfer feasibility.
