# Three-Body Resonance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A resonance relationship among three celestial bodies where frequencies satisfy n_1ω_1+n_2ω_2+n_3ω_3≈0. Three-body resonances are common in asteroid belts and planetary satellite systems.
