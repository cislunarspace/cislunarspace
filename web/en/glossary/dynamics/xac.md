# x-Axis Crossing Control, XAC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A shooting-based station-keeping method for cislunar libration point orbits. XAC executes maneuvers near perilune at the xz-plane crossing, matching the x-direction velocity component between the predicted trajectory and the baseline at their respective xz-plane crossings. XAC exploits the orbit's xz-plane symmetry but can control at most three state components per maneuver, inherently causing phase deviation. Both the CAPSTONE mission and Gateway adopt or study XAC.

## Application Value

The x轴穿越控制 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
