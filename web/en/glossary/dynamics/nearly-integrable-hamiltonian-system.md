# Nearly-Integrable Hamiltonian System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A system resulting from a small perturbation of an integrable Hamiltonian system. Its dynamical behavior is described by KAM theorem and Nekhoroshev theorem; a central research subject in celestial mechanics.
