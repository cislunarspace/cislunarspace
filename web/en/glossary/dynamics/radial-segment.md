# Radial Segment

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A ray emanating from the small primary at angle θ relative to the line connecting the two primaries. The WSB definition is based on transition points between stable and unstable regimes along this segment.

## Application Value

This term has important application value in the design, analysis, and control of cislunar space missions, providing theoretical support for research and engineering practice in related technical fields.
