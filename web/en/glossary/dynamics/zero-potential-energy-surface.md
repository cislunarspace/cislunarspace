# Zero Potential Energy Surface

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The surface in the Sun-B1 rotating frame where solar gravitational potential energy equals zero, defined by y equals plus or minus sqrt(2) times x. This surface serves as the asymptote for contours of gravitational potential energy.

## Application Value

该概念是分析地月空间动力学行为的基础工具。在轨道设计和任务分析中具有重要应用价值。
