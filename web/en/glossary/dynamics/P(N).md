# Percentage of Lambert Solutions per Revolution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The proportion of N-revolution Lambert solutions among complete uncertain Lambert solutions, influenced by orbit-dynamical boundary constraints and decreasing as revolution count increases.

## Application Value

The distribution of multi-revolution Lambert solution percentage with increasing flight time, used to evaluate solution complexity for long-time-of-flight missions.
