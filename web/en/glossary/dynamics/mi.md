# Momentum Integral, MI

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Line integral of the position vector along a trajectory, used to assess periodicity and boundedness of a path, and as a metric to detect departure from a reference orbit.

## Application Value

Momentum Integral, MI is an important concept in cislunar orbit design, crucial for understanding spacecraft motion in the Earth-Moon space.
