# Theory of Functional Connections

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A functional interpolation framework that derives constrained functionals analytically satisfying boundary constraints, converting two-point boundary value problems into nonlinear least-squares problems. Unlike shooting methods, TFC requires no pre-computed initial guess, and its constrained functionals ensure analytical satisfaction of boundary conditions throughout the time of flight.

## Application Value

Theory of Functional Connections plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
