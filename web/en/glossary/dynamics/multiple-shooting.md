# Multiple-Shooting

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that discretizes a trajectory into multiple arcs and solves two-point boundary value problems by simultaneously satisfying continuity constraints between segments.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
