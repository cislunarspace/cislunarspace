# Moon Capture

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The final stage of an Earth-Moon transfer where the spacecraft is captured by lunar gravity and enters a lunar orbit, being the last step of distant periodic orbit transfers, achieved at zero cost via stable manifold trajectories.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
