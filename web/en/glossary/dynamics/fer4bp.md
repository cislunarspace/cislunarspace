# Full Ephemeris Restricted 4-Body Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-fidelity dynamics model that considers the Sun as a fourth body and uses precise ephemerides to compute the real motion of Earth and Moon, providing more accurate orbit predictions than CR3BP.

## Application Value

This provides a fundamental theoretical framework for cislunar dynamics modeling and analysis, serving as a mathematical tool for studying spacecraft motion.
