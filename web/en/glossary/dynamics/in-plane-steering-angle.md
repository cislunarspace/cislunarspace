# In-Plane Steering Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle of the thrust vector's projection onto the instantaneous orbital plane, measured relative to the local horizontal. It describes the in-plane forward or backward thrust component and serves as the primary means of in-plane trajectory control.

## Application Value

在面内转向角的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 为航天器的精确控制提供理论依据，确保任务执行的可靠性 为目标跟踪和状态估计提供关键技术支撑。
