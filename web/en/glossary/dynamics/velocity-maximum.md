# Velocity Maximum

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The point along a trajectory where the speed reaches a local maximum. Applying a deceleration maneuver at this point maximizes the change in Jacobi constant for a given velocity increment, thereby lowering the spacecraft's energy at minimum cost. This is the location where the second impulse is applied in the four-impulse low-energy transfer scheme.

## Application Value

Velocity Maximum plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
