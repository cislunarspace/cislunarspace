# Qualitative Analysis Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Methods of studying properties of differential equation solutions through phase diagrams and stability analysis without seeking exact solutions, an important research direction in celestial mechanics pioneered by Poincaré.
