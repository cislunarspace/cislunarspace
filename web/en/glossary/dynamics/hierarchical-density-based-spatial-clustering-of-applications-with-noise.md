# Hierarchical Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A hierarchical density-based clustering algorithm that does not require a priori knowledge of the number of clusters or neighborhood size, can discover clusters of various shapes and densities, and selects the most stable clusters across a hierarchy of mutual reachability distances.
