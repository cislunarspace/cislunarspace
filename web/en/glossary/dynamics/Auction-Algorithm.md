# Auction Algorithm

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-computational-cost scheduling method that allocates customer satellites to servicers through a bidding mechanism while accounting for phasing constraints.

## Application Value

This method can be applied to solve optimization problems in cislunar missions, such as orbit transfer, fuel consumption minimization, or task scheduling.
