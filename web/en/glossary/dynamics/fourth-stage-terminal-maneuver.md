# Fourth-Stage Terminal Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A short-duration thrust maneuver executed shortly before rendezvous to correct velocity differences remaining from the powered-phase guidance, matching the vehicle velocity to the target velocity exactly. Derived from the Tsiolkovsky rocket equation. Requires pre-compensating the aimpoint for position shift caused by the maneuver.
