# Thrust Acceleration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Acceleration magnitude produced by engine thrust, equal to thrust divided by instantaneous spacecraft mass. It is a key parameter in the equations of motion for low-thrust trajectory optimization.

## Application Value

Thrust Acceleration plays a critical role in trajectory optimization and multi-body dynamics analysis for solving complex trajectory design problems.
