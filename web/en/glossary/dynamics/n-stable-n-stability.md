# n-Stable / n-Stability

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory starting from a radial segment is n-stable if it makes n complete turns around the small primary without completing one full turn around the large primary, with all transverse intersection points with the radial segment having negative Kepler energy relative to the small primary. Otherwise the motion is n-unstable.

## Application Value

This term is valuable in the design and analysis of cislunar space missions, supporting orbit design, mission planning, and system optimization. Researchers can analyze its physical mechanisms and engineering applicability according to specific mission requirements, advancing the development of cislunar space exploration technology.
