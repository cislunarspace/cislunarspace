# Correlated Velocity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The velocity the spacecraft would need at its current position to reach terminal conditions under gravity alone; the reference quantity defining velocity-to-be-gained in the Q system.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
