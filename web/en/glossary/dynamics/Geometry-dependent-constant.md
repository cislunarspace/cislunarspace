# Geometry-dependent constant

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

m = (-1)^N √(r₀ rf) cos(θ/2); its sign change in multi-revolution cases enables capturing multiple revolutions without an extra term.

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
