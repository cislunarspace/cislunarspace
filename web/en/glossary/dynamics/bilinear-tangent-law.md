# Bilinear Tangent Law

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The propellant-optimal thrust attitude law for landing in a uniform gravity field. The tangent of the thrust angle is a rational function of time. Degenerates to the linear tangent law or constant thrust attitude law for specific boundary conditions.

## Application Value

In spacecraft control system design, this method can be used to design attitude control laws or guidance laws, achieving precise control of spacecraft attitude and orbit. In practical missions, controllers based on this method improve attitude stability and trajectory tracking accuracy.
