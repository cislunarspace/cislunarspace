# CRTBP Mass Parameter

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The fundamental parameter of the circular restricted three-body problem, defined as the ratio of the Moon's mass to the total Earth-Moon mass, i.e., μ = M₂/(M₁+M₂). The Earth-Moon system's mass parameter is approximately 0.01215. Under normalization, this parameter uniquely determines the system's dynamical properties, including Lagrange point locations, zero-velocity curve shapes, and orbit family structures.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
