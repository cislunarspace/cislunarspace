# Eigenvalue Analysis

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method for determining orbital stability by computing the six eigenvalues of the monodromy matrix. Eigenvalues with modulus less than 1 correspond to stable modes, greater than 1 to unstable modes, and equal to 1 to motions within the center subspace. Orbits where all eigenvalue moduli equal 1 are termed marginally stable.

## Application Value

在轨道力学分析和任务设计中，This概念为轨道特性评估和方案比选provides理论依据，有助于优化轨道设计参数，提高任务经济性。
