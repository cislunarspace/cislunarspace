# Stable Subspace

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The subspace corresponding to eigenvalues with magnitude less than unity, where flow along this subspace approaches the equilibrium point.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
