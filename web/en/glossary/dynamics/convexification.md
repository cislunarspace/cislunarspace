# Convexification

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The mathematical technique of converting a nonconvex optimization problem into a convex one. In powered descent guidance, it enables efficient solution via interior-point methods by relaxing nonconvex constraints or making convex approximations of nonconvex terms.
