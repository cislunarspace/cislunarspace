# Gravity Turn Guidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A simple lunar soft-landing guidance method in which the lander's thrust direction always opposes the velocity vector, relying on lunar gravity to naturally alter the flight direction. This method is easy to implement but cannot optimize for time or fuel, and is typically used in scenarios with modest guidance accuracy requirements.
