# Three-Impulse Orbit Insertion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The standard orbit insertion method from LEO to DRO. The first impulse departs LEO, the second at perilune redirects to DRO transfer, and the third at DRO insertion completes the transfer. Total impulse equals the sum of perilune and insertion impulses.

## Application Value

Three-impulse insertion from LEO to DRO comprises departure, perilune redirection, and insertion maneuvers, with total impulse equaling the sum of perilune and insertion impulses.
