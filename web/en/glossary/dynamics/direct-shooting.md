# Direct-Shooting

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A direct optimization method in trajectory optimization that discretizes the continuous control problem and iteratively searches for control parameters satisfying boundary constraints. The paper uses direct-shooting to optimize the total velocity increment of impulsive transfer trajectories, with tangential constraints connecting the LEO parking orbit and target DRO, solving for optimal two-impulse and three-impulse transfer parameters.
