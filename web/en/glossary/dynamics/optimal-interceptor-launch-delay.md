# Optimal Interceptor Launch Delay

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The optimal interceptor launch timing that minimizes the fourth-stage ΔV: when the interceptor launch point is downrange from the ballistic target trajectory, delaying launch yields a lower-altitude loft trajectory with a shallower approach angle, reducing the velocity-matching ΔV requirement; crossrange offsets require early launch.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
