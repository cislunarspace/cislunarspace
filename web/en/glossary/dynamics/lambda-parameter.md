# Lambda Parameter, λ

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

λ = |r|V²/μ, a dimensionless parameter representing twice kinetic energy divided by potential energy; it determines trajectory type: λ < 2 gives elliptical, λ > 2 gives hyperbolic, and λ = 2 gives parabolic trajectories.

## Application Value

This orbital mechanics concept is fundamental to trajectory design and mission analysis in cislunar space.
