# Arnold Tori

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Phase space structure composed of invariant tori in nearly integrable Hamiltonian systems; the KAM theorem guarantees the persistence of some tori under non-resonant conditions.

## Application Value

共振轨道可用于设计长期稳定的任务轨道，减少轨道维持代价。
