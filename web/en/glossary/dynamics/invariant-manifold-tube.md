# Invariant Manifold Tube

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A tube-shaped surface in phase space formed by the complete set of stable or unstable manifolds associated with a periodic orbit (e.g., a halo orbit). Transit orbits reside inside the tube; nontransit orbits remain outside. The tube surface acts as a separatrix dividing the flow into distinct dynamical regions and serves as the topological basis for low-energy transfer design.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
