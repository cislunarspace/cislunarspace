# Orbit Insertion Impulse

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The velocity increment applied when a spacecraft enters the target orbit from a transfer trajectory, typically occurring at perilune or at the target orbit entry point.

## Application Value

Orbit Insertion Impulse plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
