# Perturbational Lambert Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A two-point boundary value problem solved under multi-body gravitational perturbation environments. The classical Lambert problem solves a transfer orbit from position r1 to r2 under the two-body assumption, while the perturbational Lambert problem must be solved under cislunar three-body or four-body dynamics models. Due to perturbational motion, the initial Lambert solution cannot precisely reach the target position, requiring iterative correction through the homotopy iteration method until convergence to a transfer velocity meeting accuracy requirements.

## Application Value

在轨道设计和转移轨道优化中，该方法用于确定最优转移时机和轨道形状，以最小化燃料消耗或飞行时间. 通过数值仿真和迭代优化，可获得满足任务约束的可行轨道方案.
