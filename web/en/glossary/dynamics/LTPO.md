# Low-Thrust Periodic Orbit, LTPO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A periodic orbit in the CR3BP-LT model, existing near low-thrust equilibrium points. Its linear stability is characterized by the superposition of a center subspace and a saddle subspace.

## Application Value

Periodic orbits near low-thrust equilibrium points in the CR3BP-LT model, with linear stability characterized by superposition of center and saddle subspaces, a novel orbital form for future low-thrust missions.
