# Thrust Direction Cosines

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The three projection components of the thrust unit vector onto the coordinate axes, denoted ar, at, and ah. Their squares always sum to unity. They are used in continuous-thrust trajectory optimization to specify the thrust vector direction, avoiding the singularities that can arise with pitch and yaw angle combinations.

## Application Value

This concept is relevant to analysis and design in cislunar space applications.
