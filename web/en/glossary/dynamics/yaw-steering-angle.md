# Yaw Steering Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle between the thrust vector and the orbital plane in the local orbital frame. The yaw angle controls the out-of-plane thrust component, working with the pitch angle to achieve full 3D thrust direction control for changing orbital inclination and other out-of-plane parameters.
