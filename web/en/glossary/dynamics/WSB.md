# Weak Stability Boundary, WSB

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In the planar circular restricted three-body problem, the locus of points on a radial segment emanating from the small primary where trajectories transition from n-stable to n-unstable. For some energy range, these points lie on the stable manifolds of Lyapunov orbits with zero radial velocity and negative Kepler energy relative to the small primary. The WSB is a fractal set used to construct low-energy Earth-Moon transfer trajectories.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
