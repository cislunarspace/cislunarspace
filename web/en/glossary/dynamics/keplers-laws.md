# Kepler's Laws

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Three empirical laws of planetary motion: elliptical orbits, the law of areas, and the harmonic law.

## Application Value

This concept is applied in cislunar space: 在轨道设计和转移轨道优化中，该方法用于确定最优转移时机和轨道形状，以最小化燃料消耗或飞行时间. This concept is applied in cislunar space: 通过数值仿真和迭代优化，可获得满足任务约束的可行轨道方案.
