# Restricted Four-Body Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dynamical model describing the motion of a spacecraft of negligible mass under the gravitational influence of Earth, Moon, and Sun. Compared with the restricted three-body problem, it adds solar gravitational terms for more accurate modeling of solar perturbation during Earth-Moon transfers. The equations of motion are formulated in the Earth-Moon rotating coordinate system with normalized units.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
