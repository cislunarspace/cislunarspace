# Homotopy Continuation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A progressive root-finding algorithm that starts from a known solution of a simplified problem and gradually converges to the solution of a more complex problem by tracing an implicitly defined curve. The paper combines it with Theory of Functional Connections to perform trajectory continuation between simplified three-body models and high-fidelity four-body models.
