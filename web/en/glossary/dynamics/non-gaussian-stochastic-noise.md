# Non-Gaussian Stochastic Noise

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Non-Gaussian Stochastic Noise; random noise that does not follow a Gaussian distribution; stochastic clock errors exhibit time-correlation and cannot be simply described by Gaussian white noise models, requiring characterization via power spectral density and Allan variance.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
