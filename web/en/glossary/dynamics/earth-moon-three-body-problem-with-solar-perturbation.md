# Earth-Moon Three-Body Problem with Solar Perturbation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A planar dynamical model that extends the Earth-Moon CR3BP by adding the Sun's gravitational perturbation. Used to analyze solar perturbation effects on Earth-Moon libration point orbits such as DRO. The system no longer has a conserved Jacobi constant.
