# Stable Interaction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The stable interaction between rigid-body modes and all flexible modes when actuators and sensors are collocated on flexible structures, with all modes phase-stabilized.
