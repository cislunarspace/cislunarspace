# Two-Level Targeter, TLT

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A differential-correction-based guidance computation tool introduced by Howell and Pernicka in 1987. It places impulsive maneuvers at the endpoints of each guidance arc and solves for feasible trajectories through two-level iteration (inner constraint satisfaction, outer target matching). A constrained variant was subsequently developed for Orion spacecraft autonomous Earth-return guidance.

## Application Value

The 两级靶点器 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
