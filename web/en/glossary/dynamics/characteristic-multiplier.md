# Characteristic Multiplier

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Eigenvalues of the monodromy matrix C in Floquet theory. Characteristic multipliers govern the evolution of perturbations near a periodic orbit: multipliers with modulus greater than 1 correspond to exponentially divergent unstable modes, modulus equal to 1 to neutral modes (center manifold), and modulus less than 1 to decaying stable modes. For Halo orbits, the six multipliers are one real value greater than 1, its reciprocal, two unit real values, and one complex conjugate pair.

## Application Value

在特征乘子的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
