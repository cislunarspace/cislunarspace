# Conjugate Unscented Transformation, CUT

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A non-product quadrature method that selects symmetric points on specially defined axes in multidimensional space to construct higher-order quadrature rules, enabling computation of multidimensional expectation integrals with minimal full model propagations.
