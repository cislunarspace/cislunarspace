# Angular Acceleration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The time derivative of angular rate. In the analytical derivation of the shape-based approach, angular acceleration is expressed in terms of angular rate and its partial derivatives, and is a necessary component for determining thrust acceleration.

## Application Value

This术语在地月空间任务的设计、分析和控制中具有important应用价值，为相关技术领域的研究和工程实践provides理论支撑。
