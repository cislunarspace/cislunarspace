# Bayesian Compressive Sensing

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A sparse coefficient regression method for adaptive polynomial chaos expansion that uses a Bayesian inference framework to automatically select sparse terms.

## Application Value

Bayesian Compressive Sensing plays an important role in orbital mechanics and mission planning, applicable for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics and optimizing transfer trajectories.
