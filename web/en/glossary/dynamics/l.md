# Libration Point / Lagrange Point

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Equilibrium points in the rotating frame where the third body remains fixed relative to the primaries. The restricted three-body problem has five libration points: three collinear points on the line of the primaries and two triangular points forming equilateral triangles with the primaries.

## Application Value

Libration points are core infrastructure for cislunar missions. The L1 point, located between Earth and the Moon, serves as a natural gateway to lunar orbits; the L2 point, on the Moon's far side, is ideal for relay communications; the L4 and L5 points can function as long-term on-orbit storage platforms. Halo orbits and Lissajous orbits around these points are ideal locations for space stations. NASA's Artemis program utilizes the NRHO at L2 for crewed lunar landings.
