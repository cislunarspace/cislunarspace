# Small Divisor Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Terms of the form 1/(k₁ω₁ + k₂ω₂ + ...) appearing in analytic expressions for quasi-periodic orbits, causing convergence difficulties when frequencies are nearly linearly dependent.
