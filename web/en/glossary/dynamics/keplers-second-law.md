## Definition

The line connecting a planet to the Sun sweeps out equal areas in equal times.

## Application Value

Kepler's Second Law states that planets sweep out equal areas in equal times, which is a direct manifestation of angular momentum conservation. In orbital mechanics, this law forms the basis for deriving Kepler's equation and calculating flight time.
