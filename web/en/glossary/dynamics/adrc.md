# Active Disturbance Rejection Control

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A robust control method that does not rely on an accurate mathematical model. It uses an extended state observer to estimate the total disturbance (including model uncertainties and external disturbances) in real time, then compensates with a nonlinear feedback control law. Compared to traditional PID control, ADRC has stronger adaptability to parametric uncertainties and unknown disturbances.

## Application Value

Active Disturbance Rejection Control plays a critical role in trajectory optimization and multi-body dynamics analysis for solving complex trajectory design problems.
