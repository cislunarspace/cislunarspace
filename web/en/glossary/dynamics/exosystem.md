# Exosystem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In nonlinear regulation theory, an autonomous dynamical system describing the source of external signals (reference trajectories and disturbances). In Earth-Moon L2 Halo orbit station-keeping, the exosystem consists of two sinusoidal oscillators at the in-plane and out-of-plane natural frequencies, dynamically generating the quasi-Halo orbit reference trajectory and periodic eccentricity perturbations.
