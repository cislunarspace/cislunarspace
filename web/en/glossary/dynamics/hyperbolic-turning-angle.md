> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle between the incoming and outgoing hyperbolic excess velocity vectors during a gravity assist flyby. Determined by the periapsis distance and v-infinity magnitude, it is a core parameter in gravity assist design.
