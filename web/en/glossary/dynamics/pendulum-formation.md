# Pendulum Formation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A formation geometry that generates cross-track baselines through differences in orbital plane inclination. The along-track baseline component remains constant, enabling safe achievement of larger cross-track baselines for cross-track interferometry.
