# Debris Cloud Evolution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The spatiotemporal evolution of fragment swarms generated by spacecraft break-up in the cislunar multi-body gravitational field. The paper evaluates the temporal characteristics of fragment collisions with the Moon and Earth and entry into protected regions through 100-year long-term propagation.

## Application Value

In spacecraft system design and mission analysis, this concept describes the motion characteristics or mission modes of a spacecraft, providing a theoretical basis for mission concept demonstration and system design.
