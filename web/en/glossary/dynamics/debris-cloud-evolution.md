# Debris Cloud Evolution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The spatiotemporal evolution of fragment swarms generated by spacecraft break-up in the cislunar multi-body gravitational field. The paper evaluates the temporal characteristics of fragment collisions with the Moon and Earth and entry into protected regions through 100-year long-term propagation.

## Application Value

在航天器系统设计和任务分析中，该概念用于描述航天器的运动特性或任务模式，为任务方案论证和系统设计提供理论基础.
