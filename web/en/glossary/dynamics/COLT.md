# Collocation with Optimization for Low-Thrust

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A direct collocation-based trajectory design and optimization tool for low-thrust transfers, integrating the IPOPT optimizer, mesh refinement strategies, and various constraints to solve continuous optimal control problems.

## Application Value

A low-thrust trajectory design and optimization tool integrating the IPOPT optimizer with mesh refinement strategies, capable of handling complex constraints suitable for engineering mission design.
