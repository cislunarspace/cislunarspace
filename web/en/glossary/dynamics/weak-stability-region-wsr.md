# Weak Stability Region, WSR

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A corridor along the Earth-Moon line including the three collinear Lagrange points L1, L2, and L3, where a spacecraft can overcome Earth-Moon gravity without being captured, and requires only tiny thrust to orbit around unstable points.
