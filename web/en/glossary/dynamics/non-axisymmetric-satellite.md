# Non-axisymmetric Satellite

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Satellite with three distinct principal moments of inertia.

## Application Value

A Non-axisymmetric Satellite has three distinct principal moments of inertia. Attitude dynamics analysis must account for inertia coupling effects. Attitude control designers select appropriate control strategies based on this to avoid attitude instability.
