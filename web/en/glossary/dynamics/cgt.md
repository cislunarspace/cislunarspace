# Cauchy-Green Tensor, CGT

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The product of the state transition matrix and its transpose; used to extract principal stretching directions and magnitudes, serving as a core tool in manifold analysis.

## Application Value

Cauchy-Green Tensor, CGT plays an important role in orbital mechanics and mission planning, applicable for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics and optimizing transfer trajectories.
