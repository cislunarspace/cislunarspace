# Unstable Floquet Mode

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In Floquet theory, a mode whose eigenvalue is greater than one, corresponding to the exponentially diverging direction of an orbit and associated with the unstable manifold.
