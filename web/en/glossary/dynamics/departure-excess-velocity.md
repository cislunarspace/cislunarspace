# Departure Excess Velocity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The square of the hyperbolic excess velocity of a spacecraft relative to the departure body as it leaves the gravitational sphere of influence: C3 = v_infinity². It is a key constraint in launch mission design, determining launcher capability requirements. The paper shows that without C3 constraints, the Earth-Mars optimum degenerates to a ballistic arc (J = 0), indicating that exponential sinusoids are primarily suited for thrust-driven low-thrust mission scenarios.

## Application Value

在出发超速的分析中，可用于轨道传播和机动设计，帮助工程师评估航天器在不同动力学环境下的运动特性 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了理论基础，尤其在平动点轨道设计和低能转移分析中具有重要应用价值 利用出发超速进行轨迹优化，可以有效降低任务燃料消耗，提高任务经济效益 在任务设计中，出发超速的分析有助于理解航天器在复杂引力场中的行为，指导轨道保持策略的制定
