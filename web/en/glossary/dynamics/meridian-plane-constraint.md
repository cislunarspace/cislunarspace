# Meridian Plane Constraint

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A terminal constraint on the longitude angle at lunar orbit insertion, requiring the polar orbit's plane to maintain a specific angle relative to the Earth-moon line. Satisfying this constraint facilitates communication with a lunar base or Earth. The optimal free-meridian orbit is nearly perpendicular to the Earth-moon line; constraining alignment requires approximately 6.5% more fuel.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
