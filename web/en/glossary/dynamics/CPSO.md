# Compound Particle Swarm Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An improved particle swarm optimization algorithm that emphasizes the role of local optimal particles in the early stage to enhance global search capability, and emphasizes the role of global optimal particles in the later stage to improve convergence and precision. This algorithm is used to solve the optimization problems of three-impulse and four-impulse low-energy transfer orbits.

## Application Value

This method plays an important role in cislunar mission trajectory design and optimization, improving computational efficiency and solution quality.
