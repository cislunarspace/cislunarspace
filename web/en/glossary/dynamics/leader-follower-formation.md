# Leader-Follower Formation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A formation flying architecture where a leader spacecraft follows a reference orbit while one or more follower spacecraft maintain desired positions relative to the leader through controllers. The leader handles orbit maintenance; followers handle formation deployment and keeping. This architecture simplifies control design but makes the leader a single point of failure.

## Application Value

Leader-Follower Formation has practical applications in cislunar space mission design and analysis.
