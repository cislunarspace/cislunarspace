# Truncation Factor

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Parameter M that truncates infinite state space to finite space for MDP solving; truncated MDP converges to original MDP when M is sufficiently large.

## Application Value

Truncation Factor plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
