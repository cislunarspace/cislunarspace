# Switching Surface

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A codimension-two submanifold in the cotangent bundle where the switching function vanishes. The extremal flow may switch control direction (from positive to negative thrust) or enter singular arcs at these points. The stratification of the switching surface (Σ_-, Σ_0, Σ_+) determines the local structure of optimal controls.

## Application Value

在航天器姿态和轨道控制中，该方法用于实现高精度跟踪和稳定保持. 通过设计合适的控制律，可以有效抑制外部扰动的影响，保证航天器在复杂动力学环境中的可靠运行.
