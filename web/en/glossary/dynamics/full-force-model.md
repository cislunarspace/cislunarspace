# Full Force Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A spacecraft equation of motion accounting for all major perturbation sources, including central body gravity, nonspherical gravitational perturbation, third-body perturbations from the Sun and major planets, and solar radiation pressure. Compared with the simplified CR3BP, the full force model more realistically captures orbital evolution but at significantly higher computational cost.
