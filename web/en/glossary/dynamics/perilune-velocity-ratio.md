# Perilune Velocity Ratio

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of velocity magnitude after to before the perilune impulse, quantifying the velocity change during powered flyby. Values greater than 1 indicate acceleration, less than 1 indicate deceleration. Combined with insertion velocity ratio, it forms a design variable for transfer trajectories.
