# Bounding Unstable Periodic Orbit, BUPO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An unstable periodic orbit lying at the fringe of the stable distant retrograde region. Classified as family g3, it appears as a period-3 orbit on the surface of section. Its stable and unstable manifolds form the frontier of the stable region, governing direct transport into and out of the sticky regions bordering KAM tori.
