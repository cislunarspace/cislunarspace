# J2 Perturbation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Perturbation caused by the second-degree zonal harmonic (J₂) of Earth's non-spherical gravitational potential, one of the dominant perturbations for near-Earth satellites, causing precession of the orbital plane and variation of perigee argument.

## Application Value

在轨道力学分析和任务设计中，This概念为轨道特性评估和方案比选provides理论依据，有助于优化轨道设计参数，提高任务经济性。
