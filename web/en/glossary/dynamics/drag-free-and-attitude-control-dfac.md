# Drag-Free and Attitude Control

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Attitude control technique that maintains a perturbation-free orbit by compensating non-gravitational perturbations such as atmospheric drag; GOCE satellite is a typical application.

## Application Value

The design of the control strategy directly affects spacecraft mission performance and lifespan. A well-designed control method can ensure mission completion while minimizing propellant consumption.
