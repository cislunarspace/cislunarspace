# Battin-Giorgi Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A mathematical formulation for computing third-body gravitational perturbation acceleration. By introducing an auxiliary variable q, it avoids direct subtraction of nearly equal large numbers, offering superior numerical stability compared to the classical disturbing-acceleration approach. Applicable to high-accuracy computation of Earth and Sun perturbations in cislunar space.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
