# Oberth Effect

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The principle that a propulsive maneuver performed at high velocity produces a greater change in orbital energy than the same maneuver at low velocity. Since thrust power equals force times velocity, higher speed yields more energy change per unit of propellant. This effect underlies the fuel savings of powered lunar flybys.

## Application Value

Oberth Effect plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
