# KAM Theorem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The Kolmogorov-Arnold-Moser theorem, stating that non-resonant invariant tori persist under sufficiently small perturbations in nearly integrable Hamiltonian systems.

## Application Value

KAM Theorem plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
