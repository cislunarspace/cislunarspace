# Riccati Difference Equation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A recursive equation in LQR problems for computing feedback control gains, backward-propagated from terminal boundary conditions to obtain the optimal gain matrix sequence.

## Application Value

Riccati 差分equation is LQR problem中solve反馈control增益 of 递推equation, 从终端boundarycondition反向递推obtainoptimal增益序列. is 线性二次optimalcontrol of coreequation.
