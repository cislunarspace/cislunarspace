> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Angle representing the out-of-plane component of thrust direction relative to the orbital plane.

## Application Value

平面外推力角是推力方向与轨道面的夹角，设计师用它控制轨道面外的轨道变化。
