# Mean Earth/Polar Axis

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar body-fixed coordinate system defined by the Moon's mean rotational pole and the mean direction of Earth, with X-axis pointing in the mean Earth direction.

## Application Value

The Mean Earth/Polar Axis system defines a lunar body-fixed coordinate frame using the Moon's mean rotational pole and the mean direction of Earth. It is a commonly used reference frame for describing spacecraft orbits near the Moon.
