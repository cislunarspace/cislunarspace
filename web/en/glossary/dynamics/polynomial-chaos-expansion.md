# Polynomial Chaos Expansion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An expansion that transforms nonlinear uncertainty mapping into a sum of orthogonal polynomials multiplied by coefficients, serving as a surrogate model approach in uncertainty quantification.


## Application Value

This dynamic method or concept has significant application value in cislunar space mission analysis, helping to understand and optimize spacecraft motion characteristics.
