# Standard Map

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A two-dimensional conservative map introduced by Chirikov, with equations y'=y+εsin x, x'=x+y'. A classic model for studying the transition from integrability to non-integrability and the onset of chaos.

## Application Value

The standard map is a classic model for studying the transition from integrability to chaos in Hamiltonian systems, applicable to orbital dynamics and resonance phenomena in cislunar space.
