# EGM2008 Earth Gravitational Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Spherical harmonic coefficient model describing Earth's gravitational potential at 50×50 order/degree, used to compute non-spherical Earth gravity effects on spacecraft trajectories.

## Application Value

EGM2008 is 50×50阶Earth引力场model, used for calculateEarth非球形引力对spacecraftorbit of affect. In 近地orbitpredict中need考虑这一摄动因素.
