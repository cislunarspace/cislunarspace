# In-Plane Frequency

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angular frequency of oscillation in the xy-plane of the synodic frame for orbits near a libration point, denoted ω. In the Lindstedt-Poincaré expansion, ω is corrected as a power series in the amplitude parameters. Halo orbits arise when the in-plane frequency equals the out-of-plane frequency (ω = ν), producing the same period in both directions and a closed orbit.

## Application Value

面内频率是轨道面内振动的频率参数，用于分析轨道动力学特性和稳定性。
