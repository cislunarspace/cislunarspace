# Particle Swarm Optimization, PSO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A swarm-intelligence based stochastic optimization algorithm that simulates bird flock foraging behavior, where particles fly through the solution space and iteratively update their positions and velocities to minimize an objective function. In the hybrid optimization framework, PSO serves as the outer global searcher to optimize the placement and spacing of intermediate patch points for the inner indirect optimization problem.
