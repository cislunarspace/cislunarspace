# Sphere of Influence (SOI)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A spherical region centered on a celestial body within which its gravitational influence dominates the motion of a spacecraft. In the Earth-Moon system, the Moon's SOI has a radius of approximately 66,000 km and serves as the critical boundary separating geocentric and selenocentric orbital dynamics. Inside the SOI, lunar gravity dominates; outside it, Earth's gravity prevails. The classical patched-conic method uses the SOI boundary as the switching surface between dynamical models.

## Application Value

The Sphere of Influence defines the region where a celestial body's gravitation is dominant, serving as an important reference for determining the boundaries of gravitational models.
