# Nonspherical Lunar Gravity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The deviation of the Moon's gravity field from a perfect sphere, modeled by spherical harmonic expansion. For orbits with low perilune altitude (e.g., NRHOs), NSLG perturbations significantly increase station-keeping cost. For distant orbits (e.g., DROs, halos), the effect is negligible.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
