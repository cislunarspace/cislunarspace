# Robust Extended Kalman Filter

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Extended Kalman filter variant resilient to measurement malfunctions, adaptively adjusting measurement noise covariance via scale factors

## Application Value

Robust Extended Kalman Filter plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
