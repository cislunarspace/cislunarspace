# Probabilistic Roadmap, PRM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A sampling-based motion planning algorithm that builds a roadmap by randomly sampling the configuration space and attempting connections; effective for high-dimensional and complex environments.

## Application Value

This concept is applied in cislunar space: 概率路线图通过在配置空间随机采样构建路线图，适用于高维和复杂环境中的路径规划，是深空探测器自主导航规.
