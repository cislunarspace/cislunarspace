# Earth Access Region, EA Region

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An arc along a halo orbit whose associated stable or unstable manifolds, upon globalization, pass close to the Earth. The EA region is a critical design segment for Earth-to-halo transfers: outbound transfers use the EA segment of the stable manifold for low-cost approach, and return transfers use the EA segment of the unstable manifold for natural descent. The location and extent of the EA region depend on the halo amplitude; larger halos produce manifolds that pass closer to Earth.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
