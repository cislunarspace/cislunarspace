# Eleventh Local Integral

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An integral of the variational equations derived from scale transformation and symplecticity relations, valid only for first-order approximation of variational equations; a local rather than global integral.
