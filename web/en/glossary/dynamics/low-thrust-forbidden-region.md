# Low-Thrust Forbidden Region

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Regions that bound low-thrust motion at a specific low-thrust Hamiltonian value, analogous to natural CR3BP forbidden regions, supplying minimum energy requirements for transit.

## Application Value

在设计地月转移方案时，利用该轨道特性可降低任务总速度增量需求。对于大质量航天器的长时间转移，该推进方式可大幅降低推进剂消耗。
