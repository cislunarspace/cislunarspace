# numerical continuation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A numerical analysis method that obtains solutions over a wider parameter domain by progressively adjusting parameters from known solutions. The paper uses it to verify launch window width (extending 0.5 hours before and after the optimal departure time) and to analyze the impact of extended lunar surface stays (4 to 7 days) on feasible windows, assessing window robustness and mission backup capability.

## Application Value

Numerical Continuation obtains solutions over a wider parameter domain by progressively adjusting parameters from known solutions. It is used to verify launch window robustness and analyze the impact of extended lunar surface stays on feasible windows.
