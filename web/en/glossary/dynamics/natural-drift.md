# Natural Drift

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The relative position and pointing variation that develops between formation spacecraft in the absence of active control, caused by differences in the dynamical environment. The magnitude of natural drift depends on formation size, integration time, reference trajectory type, and the initial orientation of the deputy.


## Application Value

This control method can be applied to spacecraft attitude stabilization, orbit keeping, and maneuver trajectory optimization in cislunar space missions, improving mission execution flexibility and reliability.
