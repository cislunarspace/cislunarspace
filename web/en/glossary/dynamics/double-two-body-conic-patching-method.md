# Double Two-Body Conic Patching Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A preliminary trajectory design method that splits an Earth-Moon transfer into two phases: an Earth-centered two-body segment near Earth and a Moon-centered two-body segment near the Moon, with the two conic sections joined at an appropriate boundary. Computationally efficient for conceptual design but neglects three-body coupling effects, yielding lower accuracy than full numerical integration.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
