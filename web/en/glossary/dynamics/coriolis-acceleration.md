# Coriolis Acceleration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The apparent acceleration experienced by an object moving in a rotating reference frame, equal to twice the cross product of angular velocity and relative velocity (-2omega×VL). The paper shows that in the soft landing dynamics model with lunar rotation, Coriolis acceleration couples the in-plane and out-of-plane motions of the spacecraft. Neglecting this term leads to significant landing point errors and degraded guidance accuracy. Coriolis acceleration, together with centrifugal acceleration, constitutes the main non-inertial forces in a rotating reference frame.

## Application Value

用于分析航天器在复杂引力场中的运动特性 用于评估导航系统的精度上限，指导滤波器设计。
