# Lindstedt-Poincaré Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A perturbation technique that eliminates secular terms from series expansions via timescale rescaling to obtain approximate analytical solutions of nonlinear periodic vibrations.
