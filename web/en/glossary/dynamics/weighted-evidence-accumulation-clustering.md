# Weighted Evidence Accumulation Clustering

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A consensus clustering method that generates a robust final clustering result by combining multiple clustering results through a weighted ensemble co-association matrix.

## Application Value

In orbit design and optimization, this concept is used to analyze spacecraft motion characteristics in the cislunar multi-body gravitational field, providing a theoretical basis for low-energy transfer trajectory design.
