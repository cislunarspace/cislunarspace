> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

## Definition

V-bar Approach; rendezvous strategy approaching target along V-bar direction, commonly used in final station-keeping phase.

## Application Value

该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
