# Collision Cross-sectional Radius

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The radius of the collision sphere defined as the sum of the radii of the primary and secondary objects.

## Application Value

Collision Cross-sectional Radius plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
