# Implicit Costate Transformation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Closed-form relation that maps the costate at the end of one arc to the costate at the beginning of the next arc in multi-arc optimal control problems, bypassing independent corner condition solutions.

## Application Value

Implicit Costate Transformation has practical applications in cislunar space mission design and analysis.
