# Conley-McGehee Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A geometric method developed by Conley and McGehee for analyzing orbital structure near equilibrium points in the three-body problem.

## Application Value

Conley-McGehee方法是研究动力系统渐近行为的方法，用于分析不变集和吸引域的结构。
