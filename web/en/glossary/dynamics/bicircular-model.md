# Bicircular Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A four-body approximation model that considers the circular orbital motion of the Sun, Earth, and Moon simultaneously.

## Application Value

Bicircular Model plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
