# Lunar Capture Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit that enters the Moon realm and never leaves. Conley's theorem states that if a crossing asymptotic orbit exists, capture orbits exist arbitrarily close to it; hence the set of capture orbits is dense in the boundary of asymptotic transit orbits. Within the isomorphic mapping framework of transit disks, capture orbits reside on the borders of the striped bands and theoretically require infinite precision to locate exactly.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
