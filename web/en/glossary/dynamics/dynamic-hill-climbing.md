# Dynamic Hill-Climbing
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An optimization method that refines locally optimal solutions by dynamically adjusting the search step size, also known as steepest descent. The paper uses it to refine candidate solutions from the genetic algorithm until the velocity increment change is less than 10^-5 or the time change is less than 4 seconds.

## Application Value

The Dynamic Hill-Climbing plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
