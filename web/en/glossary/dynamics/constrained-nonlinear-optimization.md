# Constrained Nonlinear Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Nonlinear optimization methods that find optimal solutions under given constraints. MATLAB's fmincon is used in this paper to optimize direct transfer trajectories, minimizing the DRO insertion delta-v as the objective.

## Application Value

在轨道控制律设计中，该方法通过优化推力方向和大小实现燃料消耗最小化，是深空任务的核心技术。
