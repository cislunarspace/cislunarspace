# Insertion Maneuver, IM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The impulsive maneuver applied when a spacecraft reaches the connection point along the unstable manifold, causing it to transition onto the stable manifold and be captured into the L1 Halo orbit.

## Application Value

Insertion Maneuver, IM is an important concept in cislunar orbit design, crucial for understanding spacecraft motion in the Earth-Moon space.
