# Artificial Potential Function, APF

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method representing the robot environment by potential functions distributed over state space, where attractive potentials encode goals and repulsive potentials encode obstacles; gradient ascent/descent traces feasible paths that circumnavigate obstacles.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
