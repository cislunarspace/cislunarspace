# Low Drift Region

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A spatial region near a reference trajectory where the relative acceleration between spacecraft is minimal. The low drift regions are determined by the eigenstructure of the second-order moment matrix U^T U derived from the variational equations, and take the form of quadric surfaces (ellipsoids).
