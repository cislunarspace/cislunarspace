# Curvature-Based Sampling

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory discretization scheme that samples states at equal intervals of total absolute curvature along a trajectory, placing more samples in regions of higher curvature to efficiently capture the global shape.
