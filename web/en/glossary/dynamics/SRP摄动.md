# Solar Radiation Pressure Perturbation, SRP Perturbation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Perturbation caused by momentum change from solar photons impinging on spacecraft surfaces, related to surface material reflectivity, exposed area, and attitude.

## Application Value

Perturbation from solar photon momentum exchange is proportional to area-to-mass ratio, significantly affecting long-term orbital evolution of high area-to-mass objects such as formation flying satellites.
