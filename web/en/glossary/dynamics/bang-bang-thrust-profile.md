# Bang-Bang Thrust Profile

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A thrust sequence that switches between maximum and minimum thrust values; in optimal control for large planetary body landing, switches at most twice (max-min-max or min-max).

## Application Value

The control curve describes the range of attainable low-thrust Hamiltonian values, providing a basis for optimal trajectory design.
