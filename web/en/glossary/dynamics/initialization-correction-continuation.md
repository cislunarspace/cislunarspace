> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The standard method for generating families of quasi-periodic invariant tori in the CRTBP. First, a grid of states is created near the center manifold of the reference periodic orbit, then corrected by imposing constraints including stroboscopic map fixing, zero drift along the torus, and period matching, and finally continued through perturbations along the tangential direction of the family.
