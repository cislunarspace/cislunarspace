# Monte Carlo Trajectory Shooting (MCTS)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that randomly samples parameter sets to propagate corresponding trajectory segments and construct databases.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space dynamics modeling and analysis, and can be applied to trajectory design and mission planning.
