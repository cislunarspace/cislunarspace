# DRO-Lyapunov-DRO Transition Phasing, DLD

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An indirect phasing strategy in which the spacecraft transfers tangentially from a DRO to a tangent Lyapunov transition orbit, orbits for several revolutions to accumulate phase difference due to the period difference, then returns tangentially to the DRO. Total fuel consumption equals the sum of two tangential impulses.

The DRO经Lyapunov轨道过渡调相 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
