# Eigenspace

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A vector subspace spanned by eigenvectors of the monodromy matrix. The stable eigenspace E^s is spanned by eigenvectors whose eigenvalues have modulus less than one; the unstable eigenspace E^u is spanned by eigenvectors whose eigenvalues have modulus greater than one. At the fixed point, the stable and unstable manifolds are tangent to their respective eigenspaces, which provide the local linear approximation of the manifolds.
