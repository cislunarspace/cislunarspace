# 1-1 Resonance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The resonance when the short-period frequency equals the solar synodic frequency, identified as the source of unstable tori in the bicircular problem.

## Application Value

1-1共振是周期比为1:1的轨道共振现象，在地月系统中表现为航天器轨道周期与月球轨道周期相同。
