# Iterative Nonlinear Equation Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The third stage of NRHO phasing optimization: after position correction, the phasing problem is modeled as an input-output system (inputs: impulsive velocity increment and transfer time; outputs: position error and total velocity increment). A system of nonlinear equations is constructed and solved iteratively by progressively reducing the velocity increment target, further decreasing fuel consumption.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
