# Departure Window

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A possible time interval during which a target enters its transfer orbit. More departure windows reflect greater uncertainty about the target's departure time, requiring the constellation to cover more time sequences and thus increasing the satellite count.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
