# lunar escape velocity increment
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The velocity increment required for a spacecraft to escape lunar orbit into a Moon-Earth transfer trajectory. The paper reduces this increment through a three-impulse escape method: entering an elliptical orbit at perilune, phasing at apolune, and escaping at the next perilune, with each impulse's magnitude determined by orbital parameters and phase angle.

## Application Value

The lunar escape velocity increment plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
