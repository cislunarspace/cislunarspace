# Kirkwood Gaps

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Gaps in the asteroid belt at locations where orbital periods have simple integer ratios with Jupiter's orbital period, cleared by resonance interactions. The most prominent are at 4:1, 3:1, 5:2, and 2:1.

- Murray & Dermott 1999, Sect. 1.6.7, 9.8
