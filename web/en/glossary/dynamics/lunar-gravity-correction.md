# Lunar Gravity Correction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The discrepancy between two-body initial estimates and three-body model actual values, caused by lunar gravity. The correction is significant when transfer time is less than 3 days and diminishes to near-constant for transfer times exceeding 3 days.
