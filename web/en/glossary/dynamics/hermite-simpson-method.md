# Hermite-Simpson Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A direct collocation method that uses third-order Hermite interpolation polynomials matching state and derivative values at interval endpoints, with dynamics satisfied at midpoints.
