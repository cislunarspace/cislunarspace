# Patching Region

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In the patched three-body approach, a bounded region in state space used to connect the Sun-Earth and Earth-Moon trajectory legs. Unlike a single patch point, the patching region allows searching for optimal matching points within it, typically located near the lunar sphere of influence boundary.
