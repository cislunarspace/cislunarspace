# Phasing and Orbit Circularization
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A two-step maneuver sequence at the end of the circumlunar parking phase. The phasing maneuver adjusts the lander's orbital phase by modifying the semi-major axis to position it near the rendezvous point; the orbit circularization maneuver then reduces eccentricity to meet docking constraints.

## Application Value

The Phasing and Orbit Circularization plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
