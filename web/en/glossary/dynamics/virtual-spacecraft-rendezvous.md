# Virtual Spacecraft Rendezvous

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A reformulation of the trajectory correction problem as a rendezvous problem. After a launch error, the actual spacecraft departs from the nominal trajectory; a virtual spacecraft is imagined to remain on the nominal trajectory. Correction maneuvers aim to match the actual spacecraft's position and velocity with the virtual spacecraft at a specified time, after which the actual spacecraft follows the nominal trajectory.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
