# Bifurcation Theory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Theory studying qualitative changes in solution topology as system parameters vary; in periodic orbits, a tangent bifurcation occurs when eigenvalues of the monodromy matrix collide on the real axis, accompanied by generation of three-dimensional orbits.
