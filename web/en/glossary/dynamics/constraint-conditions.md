# Constraint Conditions

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Restrictions that must be satisfied in an optimization problem, including equality constraints (zero position error at the Poincaré section) and inequality constraints (bounded deviation).

## Application Value

Constraint conditions define the feasible region for trajectory optimization, including boundary conditions and path constraints.
