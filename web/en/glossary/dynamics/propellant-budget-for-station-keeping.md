# Propellant Budget for Station-keeping

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Estimate of propellant required to maintain a periodic orbit, positively correlated with the stability index; orbits with stability index close to 1 require minimal propellant.

## Application Value

Propellant budget for station-keeping is positively correlated with the stability index. Orbits with stability index close to 1 require minimal propellant. Mission designers prioritize orbits with low propellant consumption during orbit selection to save mission costs.
