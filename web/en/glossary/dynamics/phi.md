# Insertion Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle between the negative x-axis and the radial line connecting the Moon to the manifold insertion point in the xy-plane, describing the azimuth of the insertion point in the Moon-centered frame.

## Application Value

The Insertion Angle is a key geometric parameter for describing cislunar manifold insertion characteristics. When designing L2 halo orbit transfer missions, the insertion angle determines the orientation of manifold entry into the lunar shadow region, directly affecting lighting conditions and communication outage duration. By selecting an appropriate insertion angle, mission designers can optimize the transfer trajectory's geometric relationship with the lunar surface, reduce propellant consumption, and ensure continuous communication links during critical periods. This parameter is closely related to visibility analysis of the Moon's far side from Earth.
