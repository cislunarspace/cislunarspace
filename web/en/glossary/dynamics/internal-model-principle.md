# Internal Model Principle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A fundamental principle in output regulation theory: the controller must internally contain a model of the exosystem (i.e., the generator of reference and disturbance signals) in order to achieve asymptotic tracking of the reference signal and asymptotic rejection of disturbances. This provides the theoretical basis for regulation controller design and guides the choice of exosystem structure.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
