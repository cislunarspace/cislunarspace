# Differential Algebra, DA

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A computational framework that approximates ODE flow solutions via polynomial maps, enabling automatic high-order expansions for fast trajectory propagation and maneuver computation.

## Application Value

Obtaining high-order derivatives through algebraic operations rather than perturbations, the theoretical foundation for global dynamical analysis and sensitivity analysis.
