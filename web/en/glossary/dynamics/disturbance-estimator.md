# Disturbance Estimator

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An algorithm that estimates the statistical properties (mean and covariance matrix) of disturbances online in robust control. Typically uses recursive formulas with a forgetting factor to weight historical data, progressively updating the estimates. The results feed into chance-constrained methods, converting unknown disturbances into quantifiable probabilistic constraint parameters.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
