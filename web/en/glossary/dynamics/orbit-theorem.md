# Orbit Theorem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A theorem in control theory describing the geometric structure of the attainable set. It states that the orbit of the pseudogroup generated by flows of a family of vector fields through an initial point is an immersed submanifold of the manifold, whose tangent space is generated by the Lie algebra.

## Application Value

在轨道力学分析和任务设计中，This概念为轨道特性评估和方案比选provides理论依据，有助于优化轨道设计参数，提高任务经济性。
