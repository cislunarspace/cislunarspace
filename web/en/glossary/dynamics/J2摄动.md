# Earth's Oblateness Perturbation, J2 Perturbation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Perturbation due to Earth's non-spherical gravitational field, primarily associated with the J2 coefficient, affecting long-term evolution of orbital RAAN and argument of periapsis.

## Application Value

Earth oblateness perturbation causes long-term precession of the orbital plane, affecting RAAN and argument of periapsis evolution, and cannot be neglected in medium-high altitude orbit missions.
