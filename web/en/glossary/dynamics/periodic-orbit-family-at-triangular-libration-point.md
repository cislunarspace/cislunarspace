# Periodic Orbit Family at Triangular Libration Point

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Families of periodic orbits around the triangular libration points L4 or L5, consisting of short-period and long-period families. The short-period family has a limiting period of ~6.58 dimensionless units, and the long-period family has a limiting period of ~21.07 dimensionless units.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
