# Mean Motion Resonance, MMR

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dynamical locking state formed when the ratio of orbital mean motion frequencies of two bodies approaches a ratio of integers. In the Earth-Moon system, MMR occurs when k·n ≈ k_m·n_m, where n and n_m are the mean motion frequencies of the spacecraft and Moon, and k, k_m are positive integers. MMR produces periodic close encounters that maintain long-term orbital configuration stability, serving as the foundation for constructing periodic orbits in cislunar space.

## Application Value

This orbit type has important applications in cislunar mission design, useful for constructing stable observation platforms.
