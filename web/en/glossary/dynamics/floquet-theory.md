# Floquet Theory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A mathematical theory for analyzing the stability of linear periodic time-varying systems. After linearization about a periodic orbit, the state transition matrix evaluated over one full period yields the monodromy matrix (Floquet matrix), whose eigenvalues (characteristic multipliers) determine the orbit's linear stability. In station-keeping, Floquet theory identifies unstable directions and designs continuous control laws; for example, the pole-placement technique constructs optimal continuous control based on Floquet decomposition.

## Application Value

Floquet Theory has practical applications in cislunar space mission design and analysis.
