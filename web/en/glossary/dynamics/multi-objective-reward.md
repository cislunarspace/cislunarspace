# Multi-Objective Reward

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A reward function design that simultaneously considers competing objectives such as transfer time and fuel consumption. A2PPO uses a linear weighted combination of time and fuel, achieving time-fuel trade-off through adjusting weight coefficients in a single policy training run.
