# LP to T_min to CEV Continuation Chain

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Solution strategy proceeding from unperturbed limited power trajectory, through minimum-thrust trajectory for angular distance correction, to constant exhaust velocity finite-thrust trajectory via continuation method.

## Application Value

This term has important applications in the dynamics field and is a fundamental concept in cislunar space research and mission planning.
