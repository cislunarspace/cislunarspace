# Collision Avoidance Maneuver, CAM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Active maneuvers performed to prevent the follower from approaching or colliding with the leader. The paper embeds the collision avoidance constraint into the MPC quadratic programming framework, approximating the KOZ sphere as a tangent plane through linearization and expressing it as an inequality constraint.

## Application Value

Active maneuvers preventing follower approach or collision with leader, embedded in MPC quadratic programming framework with collision avoidance constraints to ensure formation flying safety.
