# Half-Manifold

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

One branch of a stable or unstable manifold after splitting it at the fixed point. For example, a one-dimensional stable manifold W^s is divided at the fixed point X^H into two half-manifolds W^{s+} and W^{s-}, each being a single complete trajectory. Half-manifolds are the basic building blocks for computing the global structure of a manifold: displace from the fixed point along the eigenvector by a small distance d, then integrate numerically forward (unstable) or backward (in time) to generate the full branch.

## Application Value

在半流形的分析中，可用于轨道传播和机动设计，帮助工程师评估航天器在不同动力学环境下的运动特性 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了理论基础，尤其在平动点轨道设计和低能转移分析中具有重要应用价值 利用半流形进行轨迹优化，可以有效降低任务燃料消耗，提高任务经济效益 在任务设计中，半流形的分析有助于理解航天器在复杂引力场中的行为，指导轨道保持策略的制定
