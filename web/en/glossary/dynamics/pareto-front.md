# Pareto Front

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In multi-objective optimization, the boundary formed by all non-dominated solutions, representing trade-offs between transfer time and fuel consumption that cannot be simultaneously improved.
