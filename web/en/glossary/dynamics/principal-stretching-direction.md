# principal stretching direction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The eigenvector corresponding to the largest singular value of the Cauchy-Green tensor. Impulses applied along this direction produce the maximum change in final state, serving as a practical substitute for unstable manifolds in low-energy transfer design.

## Application Value

The principal stretching direction is the eigenvector direction associated with the largest stretching eigenvalue. Orbit designers maneuver along it to change orbital energy fastest, making it a reference direction for transfer trajectory design.
