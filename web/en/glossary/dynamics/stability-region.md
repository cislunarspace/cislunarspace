# Stability Region

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The region of initial conditions around a triangular libration point within which the spacecraft remains bounded over its operational lifetime. For the Earth-Moon system under CR3BP, initial deviations up to tens of thousands of km are permissible, but solar perturbations render this region impractical without active control.

## Application Value

In orbit design and control system analysis, the Lyapunov method provides an effective means to assess system stability without solving differential equations, particularly suitable for analyzing trans-lunar transfer orbits and orbit stability near small celestial bodies.
