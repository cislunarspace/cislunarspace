# Offset

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter measuring the divergence of a perturbed DRO from an ideal DRO over a given mission lifetime. Computed as the sum of squared differences between the intercepts of the perturbed and ideal DROs with the coordinate axes, used to determine the launch window of a long term DRO.
