> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An incremental sampling-based motion planning algorithm that builds a tree structure through random sampling; rapidly explores state space with asymptotic optimality guarantees under mild conditions.

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
