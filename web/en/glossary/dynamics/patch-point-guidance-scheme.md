> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An overall scheme that selects multiple correction epochs (patch points) along a transfer trajectory and plans corrections at each. Design must balance propagation duration (longer means greater nonlinear error), computational cost, and fuel consumption, with priority given to post-injection, apogee, and perilune nodes.
