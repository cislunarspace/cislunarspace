# Rician Distribution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A probability distribution that arises when integrating an isotropic Gaussian probability density function over an offset circle. Widely used in signal detection and also in spacecraft collision probability computation.
