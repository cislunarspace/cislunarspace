# Mascon (Mass Concentration)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Regions of anomalously high density beneath the lunar surface that cause locally enhanced gravitational pull. Lunar mascons are primarily located beneath large impact basins (such as Imbrium, Crisium, etc.), formed by dense basaltic material filling the basins. Mascons produce significant gravitational perturbations on orbiting spacecraft, causing orbit prediction errors to accumulate rapidly over time. This is the key reason why Chandrayaan-1's orbit could not be accurately predicted long after contact was lost.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
