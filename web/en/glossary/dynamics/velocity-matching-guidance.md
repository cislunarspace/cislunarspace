# Velocity-Matching Guidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A guidance strategy that ensures both the position and velocity vectors of a vehicle match those of the target at rendezvous. Position matching is achieved by Lambert guidance; velocity matching is achieved by a terminal maneuver. After matching, the vehicle follows the same ballistic trajectory as the target.

## Application Value

为航天器的精确控制提供理论依据，确保任务执行的可靠性 为目标跟踪和状态估计提供关键技术支撑 在任务规划阶段用于评估不同推进方案的优劣。
