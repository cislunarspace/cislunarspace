# High-Altitude Lunar Flyby

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of lunar flyby with perilune altitude much greater than the Moon's radius. Individual changes are small, but repeated flybys cumulatively reduce transfer fuel consumption. This study found that long-time-of-flight Pareto solutions commonly exploit such flybys to gradually adjust orbital energy to the level required for lunar capture.
