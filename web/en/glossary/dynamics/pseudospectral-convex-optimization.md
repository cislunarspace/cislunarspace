# Pseudospectral Convex Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Trajectory optimization technique that discretizes optimal control problems using pseudospectral methods and solves them via convex optimization, achieving higher convergence rate and solution accuracy than Euler or Runge-Kutta methods.

## Application Value

In the orbit design phase, the method can solve for low-energy transfer trajectories while reducing propellant consumption. The optimization method is computationally efficient and suited to constrained trajectory optimization problems.
