# Patched Three-Body with Tilted Planes
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A modification of the patched three-body approximation that accounts for the ~5.1° inclination between the lunar orbital plane and the ecliptic. The patching point is placed on the line of nodes, allowing two planar CRTBPs to be joined in three-dimensional space. Used to generate initial guesses for fast Earth-Moon transfers.

## Application Value

The Patched Three-Body with Tilted Planes plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
