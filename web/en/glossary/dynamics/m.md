# Mean Anomaly

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An angular parameter describing a body's position along its orbit. Defined as the angle swept from periapsis by a fictitious body moving at a uniform mean angular rate. Seven years after losing contact with Chandrayaan-1, JPL's ground-based radar detection revealed its mean anomaly had drifted approximately 160 degrees relative to the 2009 estimate, confirming the satellite was still in lunar orbit but with a significantly shifted position. Mean anomaly drift is an important indicator of orbit evolution for lost spacecraft.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
