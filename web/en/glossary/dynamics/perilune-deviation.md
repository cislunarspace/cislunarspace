# Perilune Deviation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The difference in perilune epoch between the steered trajectory and the baseline trajectory, used to evaluate the phase-tracking performance of the station-keeping controller. PC-SCoP's phase constraint keeps perilune deviation bounded throughout the simulation.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
