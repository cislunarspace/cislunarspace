# Chief-Deputy Configuration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A relative motion description framework for formation flying. The chief spacecraft travels along the reference trajectory while the deputy is positioned on a sphere surrounding the chief. The variational equations describe the deputy's motion relative to the chief, enabling analysis of formation dynamics.

## Application Value

In orbital design, analysis, and control, dynamics models are used to predict spacecraft trajectories, with equations of motion solved numerically or analytically. This concept underpins critical mission capabilities including orbital maneuver design, orbit improvement, and formation flying.
