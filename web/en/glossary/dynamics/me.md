# Mean Earth/Polar Axis

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar body-fixed coordinate system defined by the Moon's mean rotational pole and the mean direction of Earth, with X-axis pointing in the mean Earth direction.

## Application Value

Mean Earth/Polar Axis plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
