# Local Observability

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A theoretical concept assessing the short-term distinguishability of states near an initial condition. When the observability Gramian is full-rank at a given initial state, the state is locally observable, meaning measurements over finite time suffice to distinguish it from nearby states. A rank-deficient Gramian indicates weak or no local observability. For cislunar domain awareness, local observability determines whether passive measurements can effectively estimate a target's orbit.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
