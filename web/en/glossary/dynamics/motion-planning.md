# Motion Planning

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Algorithms that generate sequences of decisions to safely navigate a robot from initial states to goal states; classified into exact (combinatorial) and approximate (sampling-based) algorithms.
