# Halo Orbit Insertion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The process of applying the second impulse to introduce a spacecraft from a transfer trajectory into a target halo orbit. The insertion location and velocity components are design unknowns, determined through differential evolution optimization.

## Application Value

In orbital design, analysis, and control, dynamics models are used to predict spacecraft trajectories, with equations of motion solved numerically or analytically. This concept underpins critical mission capabilities including orbital maneuver design, orbit improvement, and formation flying.
