# Greene's Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A numerical method for computing the breakdown threshold of KAM invariant tori. By finding invariant curves with specified rotation numbers and measuring their smoothness degradation, the critical parameter value can be determined.

## Application Value

Greene's Method plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
