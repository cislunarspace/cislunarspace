# Total Absolute Curvature

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The integral of curvature with respect to arclength, capturing the cumulative angle swept out by the tangent to the curve within the osculating plane, monotonically increasing by 2π with each revolution.


## Application Value

This concept is of significant importance in cislunar space research and technical applications, helping to deepen understanding of related physical mechanisms and engineering implementations.
