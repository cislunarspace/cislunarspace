# Minimum Norm Targeting

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A differential correction strategy that seeks the solution minimizing the norm of velocity corrections while satisfying multiple constraints. Compared to standard targeting, this approach produces the smallest corrections, keeping transfer trajectories in the higher-fidelity ephemeris model close to the original CR3BP design. The paper uses this method to transition CR3BP solutions into the ephemeris model while preserving geometric characteristics.

## Application Value

在设计地月转移方案时，利用该轨道特性可降低任务总速度增量需求。该方法可用于精确修正轨道偏差，提高轨道预报精度。该模型是分析地月空间动力学、设计低能量转移轨道的基础工具。
