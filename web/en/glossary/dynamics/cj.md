# Jacobi Constant

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The sole conserved quantity (integral of motion) in the CR3BP, defined as JC = 2U - ẋ² - ẏ² - ż². It combines the pseudo-potential and velocity components, playing a role analogous to energy conservation. Orbits sharing the same JC value reside at the same energy level, enabling transfers between them that require only geometry changes rather than energy changes.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
