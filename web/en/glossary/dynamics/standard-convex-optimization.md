# Standard Convex Optimization (SCvx)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Convex optimization method using lower-order discretization such as trapezoidal method, with lower accuracy compared to pseudospectral convex optimization.

## Application Value

在轨道设计阶段，该方法可用于求解低能量转移轨道，减少推进剂消耗。该优化方法计算效率高，适合处理带约束的轨迹优化问题。
