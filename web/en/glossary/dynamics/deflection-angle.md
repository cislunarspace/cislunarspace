# Deflection Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Angle of deflection of insertion impulse relative to DRO velocity direction, describing directional characteristics of the DRO insertion maneuver.

## Application Value

Deflection Angle plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
