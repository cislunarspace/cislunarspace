# Transfer Time Traversal
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A search strategy in NRHO phasing optimization: multiple equally-spaced time points are sampled within the maximum transfer time range, and the optimal impulse is computed at each point to obtain the velocity increment distribution over transfer time. This identifies the optimal transfer mode and approximate optimal solution interval, avoiding the high computational cost of global search.

## Application Value

The Transfer Time Traversal plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
