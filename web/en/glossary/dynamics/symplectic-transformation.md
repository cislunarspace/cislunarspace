# Symplectic Transformation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Canonical transformation that preserves the symplectic structure of the phase space in Hamiltonian systems. The paper uses symplectic transformations to extract three modes of motion (hyperbolic, center×center) from the linearized system.

This术语在地月空间任务的设计、分析和控制中具有important应用价值，为相关技术领域的研究和工程实践provides理论支撑。
