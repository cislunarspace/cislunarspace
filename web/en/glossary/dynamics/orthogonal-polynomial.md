# Orthogonal Polynomial

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A family of polynomials mutually orthogonal over a specified interval, such as Legendre and Chebyshev polynomials, used to construct basis function expansions.

## Application Value

Orthogonal Polynomial plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
