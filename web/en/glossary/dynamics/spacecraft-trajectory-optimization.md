# Spacecraft Trajectory Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Techniques for determining spacecraft trajectories that satisfy specified initial and terminal conditions and accomplish a mission while minimizing some quantity of importance. The most common objective is to minimize propellant required, equivalently maximizing the fraction of spacecraft not devoted to propellant.
