# Augmented Lagrangian Iterative LQR, AL-iLQR

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Algorithm combining augmented Lagrangian method with iterative LQR to solve discrete constrained optimization problems, applicable to both impulsive and continuous-thrust missions.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
