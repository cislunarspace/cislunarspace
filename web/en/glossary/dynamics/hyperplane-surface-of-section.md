# Hyperplane Surface of Section

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Hyperplane section defined by rotation angle θ, used to reduce 4D trajectory and manifold information to 2D Poincaré section for analysis.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
