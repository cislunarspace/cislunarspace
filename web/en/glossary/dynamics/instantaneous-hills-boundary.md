# Instantaneous Hill's Boundary

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The boundary of the zero-velocity surface defined by the Hamiltonian function, varying periodically with time under solar gravitational perturbation, used to define equivalent libration points.

## Application Value

This analysis method is used to evaluate the long-term stability of spacecraft orbits. In cislunar space missions, where perturbation factors are complex, stability analysis is of great guiding significance for mission orbit design and mission duration prediction.
