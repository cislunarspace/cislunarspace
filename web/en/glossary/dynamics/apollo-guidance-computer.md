# Apollo Guidance Computer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Digital computer on Apollo spacecraft for guidance, navigation and control, featuring 16-bit word length and limited memory; the core of humanity's first fully digital onboard autonomous navigation system.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
