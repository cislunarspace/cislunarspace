# Grid Scan on Poincaré Section

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A systematic method that discretizes feasible initial conditions on a Poincaré section into a dense grid, integrates each point forward, and classifies the resulting trajectories by behavior (capture, escape, collision). Compared with the traditional single-point search, this approach reveals the full landscape of orbital behaviors and automatically filters solutions meeting mission design requirements.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
