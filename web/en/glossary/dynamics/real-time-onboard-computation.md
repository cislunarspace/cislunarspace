# Real-Time Onboard Computation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The capability to generate optimal trajectories in real time on a spacecraft flight computer; the TFC method shows promise due to millisecond-level inner-loop convergence.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
