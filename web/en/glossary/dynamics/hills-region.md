# Hill's Region

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Projection of the energy manifold onto configuration space, describing the accessible region for the third body at a given energy level.

## Application Value

该概念在轨道能量分析中用于量化航天器的能量状态，是评估转移方案经济性的重要指标。
