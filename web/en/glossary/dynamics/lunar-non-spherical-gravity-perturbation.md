# Lunar Non-Spherical Gravity Perturbation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Additional gravitational acceleration due to the Moon's non-uniform mass distribution, including mascons and other anomalous regions. The lunar gravity field is more irregular than Earth's and cannot be neglected in high-precision trajectory modeling when the spacecraft approaches the Moon, requiring accurate lunar gravity field models.
