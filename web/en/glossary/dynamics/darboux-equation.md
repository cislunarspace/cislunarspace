# Darboux Equation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The differential equation describing the evolution of proper orthogonal tensors: Q̇ + ~ω·Q = 0. Its solution R~-ω represents the rotation tensor with instantaneous angular velocity -ω.


## Application Value

This concept is of significant importance in cislunar space research and technical applications, helping to deepen understanding of related physical mechanisms and engineering implementations.
