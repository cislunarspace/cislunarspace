# Phasing Efficiency

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of the absolute time phase difference to the total fuel consumption, measuring the phase adjustment achieved per unit of fuel consumed. A higher ratio indicates better phasing performance.
