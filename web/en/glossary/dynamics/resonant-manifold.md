# Resonant Manifold

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Invariant manifold associated with a resonant orbit. The unstable manifold of a resonant orbit provides low-energy transfer paths from near-Earth orbits to various libration points.
