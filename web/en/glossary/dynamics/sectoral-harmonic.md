# Sectoral Harmonic

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Harmonic terms in Earth's gravitational potential depending on both longitude and latitude, such as J22.

## Application Value

田谐项是月球引力位中与经度相关的非对称分量，影响月球卫星轨道的长期演化，精密轨道计算必须考虑田谐项摄动。
