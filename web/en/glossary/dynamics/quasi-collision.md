# Quasi-Collision

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Motion where two bodies come extremely close but do not collide, having significant probability in three-body system evolution.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
