# Indirect Gravitational Perturbation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The equivalent force arising from the non-inertial nature of a reference frame caused by a third body's gravitational pull on the central body. In the Earth-Moon rotating coordinate system, the Sun's gravitational pull on Earth alters the angular velocity of the rotating frame, introducing changes in Coriolis and centrifugal forces that constitute the indirect solar gravitational perturbation term.

## Application Value

In analyses involving indirect gravity, it can be applied to orbit propagation and maneuver design, helping engineers evaluate spacecraft motion characteristics across different dynamical environments. This concept provides the theoretical basis for cislunar mission design, with significant application value especially in libration point orbit design and low-energy transfer analysis. Using indirect gravity for trajectory optimization can effectively reduce mission fuel consumption and improve mission cost-effectiveness. In mission design, analyzing indirect gravity helps understand spacecraft behavior in complex gravitational fields and guides the formulation of station-keeping strategies.
