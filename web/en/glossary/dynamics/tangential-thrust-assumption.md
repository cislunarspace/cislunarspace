# Tangential Thrust Assumption

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The simplification that the thrust direction is always aligned with the local velocity direction. Under this assumption, the thrust angle equals the flight-path angle, and thrust changes only the orbital energy without altering the orbital plane shape, simplifying the inverse solution of the equations of motion. This is a common fundamental assumption in shape-based methods.

## Application Value

在轨道设计和转移轨道优化中，该方法用于确定最优转移时机和轨道形状，以最小化燃料消耗或飞行时间. 通过数值仿真和迭代优化，可获得满足任务约束的可行轨道方案.
