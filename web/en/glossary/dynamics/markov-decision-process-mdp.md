# Markov Decision Process, MDP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A discrete-time stochastic process where the probability of each state depends only on the previous state. Defined by the tuple (S, A, T, R, γ) with state space, action space, transition probabilities, reward function, and discount factor. The goal is to find a policy maximizing cumulative future rewards. While fully observable, large state/action spaces make finding the optimal policy intractable, requiring sampling-based heuristics.
