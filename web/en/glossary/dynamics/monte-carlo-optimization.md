# Monte Carlo Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A stochastic optimization method that estimates the feasible solution domain through extensive random sampling of the parameter vector. The paper uses simplified Monte Carlo optimization (with parameter vector [θ₀, ΔV₀]) to search the Pareto-optimal set for transit orbit transfer to target DROs, obtaining tradeoffs between transfer time and velocity increment.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
