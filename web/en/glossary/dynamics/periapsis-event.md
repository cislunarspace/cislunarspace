# Periapsis Event

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A break-up event occurring at the spacecraft's periapsis position. The paper finds that periapsis break-up consequences differ significantly from other locations: higher lunar impact fraction (~37% within 30 days for NRHO case), but fewer fragments remain in the Earth-Moon system, resulting in relatively smaller threats to Earth's protected regions and the Gateway.
