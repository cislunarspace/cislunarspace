# Bridge Impulse

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In Earth-Moon manifold transfers, the intermediate impulse needed to transfer a spacecraft from a trans-halo trajectory onto the stable manifold. Since Earth-Moon stable manifolds do not pass close to Earth, this bridge impulse is needed to connect the transfer trajectory and manifold.

## Application Value

Bridge Impulse plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
