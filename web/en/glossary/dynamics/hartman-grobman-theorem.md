# Hartman-Grobman Theorem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A theorem stating topological equivalence between nonlinear systems and their linearizations near hyperbolic equilibrium points. This theorem guarantees that stability analysis of hyperbolic equilibria can be carried out in the linearized system.
