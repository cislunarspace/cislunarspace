> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Plane with effective phases (Φ,Ψ) as coordinates where Lissajous trajectories appear as straight lines; convenient for mission design.

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
