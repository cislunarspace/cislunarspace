# Nonminimum-phase Filter

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A filter with poles in the left-half plane that can produce large-phase lag up to 360 degrees, including all-pass, high-pass, low-pass, notch, and bandpass types, used for stabilizing unstably interacting flexible modes.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
