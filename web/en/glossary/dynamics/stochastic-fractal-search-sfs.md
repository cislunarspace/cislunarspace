# Stochastic Fractal Search, SFS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A metaheuristic global optimization algorithm inspired by fractal growth. It iterates through a diffusion phase (generating candidates via Gaussian walks and retaining the best) and an updating phase (exchanging information among individuals to accelerate convergence). In lunar transfer design, SFS efficiently explores the parameter space without integrating high-fidelity equations of motion at every candidate.
