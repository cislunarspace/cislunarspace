# Time of Flight, ToF

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The transfer time from initial to final position, one of the three boundary conditions in Lambert's problem.

## Application Value

One of three boundary conditions in Lambert's problem, it determines transfer energy requirements and maneuver timing, directly affecting fuel consumption in mission planning.
