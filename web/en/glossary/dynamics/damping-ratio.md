# Damping Ratio, ζ

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dimensionless parameter characterizing the degree of damping, equal to the ratio of actual damping coefficient to critical damping coefficient.

## Application Value

This concept plays a foundational supporting role in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical missions, appropriate analytical methods must be selected based on specific application scenarios and constraints.
