# Transportation Tube Wall

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A 2D tubular wall structure formed by invariant manifolds of Halo or Lyapunov orbits in phase space. It encloses transiting trajectories inside the tube while non-transiting ones remain outside. Under SBCM, tube walls deform or break, drastically reducing derived transiting trajectories.

## Application Value

In the orbit design phase, this orbit type can be used to build candidate orbit pools, providing references for constellation deployment and mission orbit selection. During on-orbit operation, this orbit characteristic supports stationkeeping strategy design and reduces propellant consumption. In transfer planning, its stable manifold structure guides the search for low-energy transfer corridors.
