# Projection Functional

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The component in the Theory of Functional Connections that projects constraint conditions into the solution space. Through cooperation with switching functions, it analytically 'embeds' boundary constraints into function expressions, ensuring boundary conditions are precisely satisfied at constraint points.
