# Jacobi Decomposition

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An analytical method that decomposes the three-body problem into an inner orbit (two-body reduced mass motion) and an outer orbit (third body motion relative to the center of mass), which can be approximated as two perturbed Keplerian motions when mass or distance ratios are favorable.
