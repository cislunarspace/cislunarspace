# Double Lunar Gravity Assist

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A gravity assist strategy involving two close lunar flybys within a short interval. The ARTEMIS P1 spacecraft performed a second lunar gravity assist only 13 days after the first, using the combined effect of both flybys to precisely adjust energy and orbital orientation for reaching the Sun-Earth weak stability boundary region. This offers greater design freedom than a single flyby.
