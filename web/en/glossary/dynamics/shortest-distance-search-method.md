# Shortest Distance Search Method
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A search strategy for selecting the optimal landing point among field-of-vision subareas. Subareas are ranked by the distance from their center points to the current target landing point, from nearest to farthest. The search stops at the first subarea satisfying safety constraints, taking its feature point as the new alternative landing site. Compared with the spiral search method, it yields more concentrated landing points and is independent of spiral direction.

## Application Value

The Shortest Distance Search Method plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
