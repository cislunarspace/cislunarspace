# Modified Equinoctial Elements, MEE

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A set of non-singular orbital elements [p, f, g, h, k, l] where p = a(1-e²), f = e cos(Ω+ω), g = e sin(Ω+ω), h = tan(i/2) cos Ω, k = tan(i/2) sin Ω, l = Ω+ω+ν. Free from singularities except at i=180°, and valid for circular, elliptic and hyperbolic trajectories, making them ideal for low-thrust trajectory optimization.

## Application Value

The 改进型平近点元 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
