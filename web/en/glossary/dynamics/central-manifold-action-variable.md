# Central Manifold Action Variable

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Parameters I2 and I3 in the characteristic parameter set, representing the amplitude of spacecraft motion on the central manifold. I2 denotes the amplitude in the XY plane of the synodic coordinate system, while I3 denotes the amplitude in the Z direction.
