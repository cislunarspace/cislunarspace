# Horizontal Instability

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The instability of planar periodic orbits in the in-plane direction. When stable and unstable eigenvectors have in-plane components, the orbit has horizontal instability, associated with transport phenomena between different in-plane regions.
