# Triangular Libration Points

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Two libration points forming equilateral triangles with the two primaries, denoted L₄ and L₅. They are linearly stable when the mass ratio of the two bodies satisfies certain conditions.

## Application Value

Triangular Libration Points plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
