# Modal Analysis

## Definition

Modal Analysis is an analytical method in structural dynamics used to determine the natural frequencies, mode shapes, and damping characteristics of structures. By solving the eigenvalue problem of a structure, modal parameters at each order are extracted, providing fundamental data for spacecraft structural design, vibration control, and load analysis. During spacecraft launch, on-orbit operations, and docking phases, modal analysis is critical for avoiding resonance and ensuring structural integrity.
