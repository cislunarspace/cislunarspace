# Collision Probability

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The probability estimate of collision between two space objects at closest approach, computed by integrating the joint probability density of position uncertainties (assumed Gaussian) projected onto the encounter plane. It is the core metric for assessing collision risk and planning avoidance maneuvers.
