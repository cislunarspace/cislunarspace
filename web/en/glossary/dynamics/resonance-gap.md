# Resonance Gap

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Cleared gaps in the asteroid belt at locations where orbital resonances with a planet occur, such as the 3:1 and 2:1 resonances with Jupiter forming Kirkwood gaps.
