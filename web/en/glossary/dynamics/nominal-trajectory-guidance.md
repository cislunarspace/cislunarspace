# Nominal Trajectory Guidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A landing guidance method that pre-computes an optimal trajectory, stores it on the on-board computer, and tracks it in real time during descent. While theoretically fuel-optimal, its practical application is limited by the short duration of soft landing and constrained on-board computational resources.
