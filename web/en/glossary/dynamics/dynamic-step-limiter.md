# Dynamic Step Limiter

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Technique that dynamically scales quasi-Newton steps via P_{i+1}=P_i+ΔP_i/(1+||ΔP_i||), limiting oscillation from large steps while preserving search direction and expanding the radius of convergence.

## Application Value

In orbit design and optimization, this concept is used to analyze spacecraft motion characteristics in the cislunar multi-body gravitational field, providing a theoretical basis for low-energy transfer trajectory design.
