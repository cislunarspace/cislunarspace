# Gauss Variational Equations

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Equations describing the time rates of change of orbital elements, used to compute the angular velocity of the LVLH frame and its time derivative.

## Application Value

Gauss Variational Equations plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
