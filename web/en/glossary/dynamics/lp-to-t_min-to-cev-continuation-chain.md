# LP to T_min to CEV Continuation Chain

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Solution strategy proceeding from unperturbed limited power trajectory, through minimum-thrust trajectory for angular distance correction, to constant exhaust velocity finite-thrust trajectory via continuation method.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in the design of cislunar space missions, involving multiple aspects such as orbital design, navigation control, and dynamics analysis, providing theoretical support for cislunar space exploration missions.
