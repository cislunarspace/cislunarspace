# Reaching Law

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A differential equation in sliding mode control that governs how system states approach the sliding surface. The reaching law design determines the dynamic quality during the approaching phase. Common forms include constant-rate, exponential, and power reaching laws. This paper adopts a fractional-power reaching law whose convergence time also has an upper bound, combined with the fixed-time sliding surface to achieve overall fixed-time convergence.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
