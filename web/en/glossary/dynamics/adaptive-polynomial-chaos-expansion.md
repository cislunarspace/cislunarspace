# Adaptive Polynomial Chaos Expansion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An adaptive polynomial chaos expansion surrogate model that uses adaptive coefficient regression methods such as least angle regression to overcome the curse of dimensionality, enabling efficient solution of uncertain multiple-revolution Lambert problems.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
