# pseudostate trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A spacecraft trajectory computed using pseudostate theory, composed of analytically computable conic sections (hyperbolas and ellipses) and straight-line segments. It avoids complex numerical integration under three-body dynamics. Pseudostate trajectories connect with precise dynamics at the pseudostate sphere boundary, greatly improving computational efficiency while maintaining acceptable accuracy.
