# Hyperelliptic Curve
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The algebraic curve appearing in the averaged Hamiltonian integral. For circular target orbits (p_θ=0), the integral reduces to a genus-2 hyperelliptic curve, affecting both the structure of the optimal control and numerical difficulty.

## Application Value

The Hyperelliptic Curve plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
