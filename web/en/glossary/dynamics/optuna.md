# Optuna

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An open-source hyperparameter optimization framework that uses Tree-structured Parzen Estimator sampling and Median Pruning for efficiency. A2PPO employs Optuna for two-stage hyperparameter tuning: an initial broad sweep of 100 trials followed by a refined search of another 100 trials focused on promising regions.
