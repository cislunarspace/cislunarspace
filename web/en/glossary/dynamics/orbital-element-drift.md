# Orbital Element Drift

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The slow, continuous change of orbital elements (RAAN, inclination, eccentricity, etc.) over time due to perturbing forces such as non-spherical gravity fields. In circumlunar orbits, lunar higher-order gravitational harmonics and Earth's third-body perturbation are the primary drivers.
