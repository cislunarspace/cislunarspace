# Collision Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Solid angle of all random thrust directions that could lead to collision, expressed in degrees. Used to assess collision risk after actuator failure; a component of the passive safety cost function in hold point optimization.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
