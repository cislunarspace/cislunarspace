# Ejection Velocity, ΔV
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The velocity increment acquired by fragments upon separation from the parent spacecraft. In the NASA SBM, ejection velocity direction is uniformly distributed on a sphere, with magnitude determined by fragment area-to-mass ratio. The heaviest fragment's ejection velocity must be corrected to satisfy momentum conservation.

## Application Value

The Ejection Velocity, ΔV plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
