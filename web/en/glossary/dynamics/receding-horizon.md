## Definition

A prediction horizon management strategy for MPC that uses a fixed-length prediction window shifting synchronously at each sampling instant. This scheme allows flexible trade-off between accuracy and computational speed by properly setting the horizon length and number of steps, but does not directly control the transfer time of flight.
