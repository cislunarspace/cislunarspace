# Kessler Effect

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A cascading effect caused by space debris collisions. Proposed by Kessler and Cour-Palais in 1978: when the density of space debris in orbit reaches a critical value, collision frequency between fragments increases sharply, collisions produce more debris, and more debris triggers more collisions, forming an irreversible chain reaction. This effect poses the same threat in cislunar space and is a key motivation for studying debris cloud evolution patterns.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
