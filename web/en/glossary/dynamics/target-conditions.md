# Target Conditions

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Terminal constraints in transfer trajectory design, including flight path angle and altitude, used to define the tangential departure condition from a low Earth orbit.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
