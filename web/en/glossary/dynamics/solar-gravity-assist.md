# Solar Gravity Assist

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Positive role of solar gravitational perturbation in the transfer process, including doing positive work along velocity direction in early transfer to raise apogee altitude, and solar 'drag' effect promoting perigee elevation during return phase.
