# Manifold Patching

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory design technique that connects stable or unstable manifolds of different orbits in state space to form continuous transfer paths. Typically, manifolds of periodic orbits are used to link LEO with target orbits, exploiting three-body dynamics to reduce transfer energy.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
