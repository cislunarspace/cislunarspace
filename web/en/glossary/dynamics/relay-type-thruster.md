# Relay-Type Thruster

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A propulsion device that can only deliver a fixed-magnitude thrust (on/off states) without continuous thrust modulation. Continuous-thrust effects can be approximated in engineering via pulse-width modulation (PWM), converting continuous control signals into on/off duty cycles. Relay-type thrusters are structurally simple and highly reliable, widely used on micro/nano-satellites and on-orbit servicing spacecraft.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
