# Station-Keeping

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The general term for control strategies that keep a spacecraft's actual trajectory close to the desired nominal orbit. In cislunar space, perturbations from solar gravity, solar radiation pressure, and nonspherical gravity cause long-term drift, making station-keeping essential for long-duration missions.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
