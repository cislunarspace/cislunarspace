# Asymmetry

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A quantitative indicator of gravitational asymmetry, defined as the ratio of the third-body perturbation acceleration magnitude to the sum of all acceleration magnitudes, ranging from 0 to 1.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
