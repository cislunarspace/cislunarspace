# g-family

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

One of the five families of symmetric periodic orbits discovered by Hénon in Hill's problem, originating from orbits around the smaller primary, evolving into distant periodic orbits in the Earth-Moon system. These orbits become unstable when the integral constant G < 4.99986.

## Application Value

在周期轨道设计中，该指标用于评估轨道的有界性和长期稳定性，指导轨道维持策略的制定。
