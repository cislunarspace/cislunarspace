## Definition

Planets move in elliptical orbits with the Sun at one focus.

## Application Value

Kepler's First Law establishes that planetary orbits are ellipses with the Sun at one focus, serving as a foundational law of orbital mechanics. In spacecraft orbit design, all orbits are ellipses (including circles as special cases of ellipses).
