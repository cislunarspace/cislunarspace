# Complex Dual Numbers Automatic Differentiation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Automatic differentiation using complex dual numbers for computing both first and second derivatives simultaneously, avoiding complex analytical derivation of perturbing accelerations with respect to state vector.

## Application Value

Complex Dual Numbers Automatic Differentiation plays an important role in orbital mechanics and mission planning, applicable for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics and optimizing transfer trajectories.
