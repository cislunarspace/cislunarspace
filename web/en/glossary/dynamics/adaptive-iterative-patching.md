# adaptive iterative patching

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A numerical method in pseudostate theory for stitching together conic trajectory segments. It employs adaptive parameter adjustment to improve computational efficiency and robustness, well-suited for Earth-Moon transfer optimization involving multiple analytically computable conic arcs. It offers faster convergence and stronger noise resistance compared to fixed-parameter patching.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
