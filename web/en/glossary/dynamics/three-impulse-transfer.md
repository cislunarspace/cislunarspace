# Three-Impulse Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer scheme applying three impulses sequentially at departure, perilune, and orbit insertion for LEO-to-DRO powered lunar flyby transfers. The departure impulse is provided by the launch vehicle; perilune and insertion impulses are carried by the spacecraft.
