# Tangential Injection

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An injection strategy in interior transfers where the impulsive maneuver is perpendicular to the position vector (i.e., along the velocity direction), entering the asymptotic orbit tangentially. Offers shorter transfer time but relatively higher fuel consumption.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
