# Euler Angles

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Three angles describing a rigid body's orientation relative to a reference coordinate system.

## Application Value

Euler Angles are three angles describing a rigid body's orientation relative to a reference coordinate system. They are fundamental to spacecraft attitude representation and control.
