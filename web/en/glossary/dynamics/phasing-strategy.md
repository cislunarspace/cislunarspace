# Phasing Strategy

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Strategy to adjust phase from end of Earth-Moon transfer to start of proximity operations with target

## Application Value

定相策略解决从地月transferorbit末端到targetorbit近距操作起点之间 of 相位调整problem. In NRHO交will对接mission中, 合理 of 定相策略可显著降low燃料消耗.
