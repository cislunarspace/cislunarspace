# Variable-Size Design Space, VSDS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An optimization problem where the number of design variables itself is part of what is being optimized. Both the number of spacecraft and the allocation of orbit families enter as design variables, causing the feasible solution space dimensionality to change dynamically with the candidate architecture.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
