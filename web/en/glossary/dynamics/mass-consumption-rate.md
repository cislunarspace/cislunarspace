# Mass Consumption Rate

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A constant parameter α in the fuel-optimal control model governing the rate of propellant mass depletion, appearing in the mass equation dm/dt = -α||T||. It is determined by the engine specific impulse and directly relates thrust magnitude to fuel consumption rate.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
