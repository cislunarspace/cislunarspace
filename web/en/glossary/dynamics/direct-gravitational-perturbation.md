# Direct Gravitational Perturbation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The direct gravitational acceleration of a third body (e.g., the Sun) on a spacecraft. In the Earth-Moon libration point navigation dynamics model, the difference between the Sun's gravitational acceleration on the spacecraft and its gravitational acceleration on the Earth-Moon barycenter constitutes the direct solar gravitational perturbation term.

## Application Value

直接引力是第三体对航天器的直接引力加速度，是三体系统中主要的摄动源之一，轨道设计师计算轨道时必须考虑直接引力摄动。
