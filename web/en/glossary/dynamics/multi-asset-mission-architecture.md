# Multi-asset Mission Architecture

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Mission architecture where the mothership handles wide-area perception and planning while mobile platforms handle local perception; a hierarchical collaborative approach that reduces computational and power burdens on mobile assets.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
