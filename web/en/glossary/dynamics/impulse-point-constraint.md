> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Constraints on the characteristics of impulse application points, such as perilune constraints requiring the impulse point to be within 500km of the lunar surface with position vector perpendicular to velocity vector.
