# Triangular Libration Point

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Equilibrium points in the restricted three-body problem forming equilateral triangles with the two primary bodies, located on opposite sides of the line connecting them, also known as L4 and L5 points.

## Application Value

In analyses involving the triangular libration points, it can be applied to orbit propagation and maneuver design, helping engineers evaluate spacecraft motion characteristics across different dynamical environments. This concept provides a theoretical foundation for cislunar mission design, with significant applications in libration point orbit design and low-energy transfer analysis. Using the triangular libration points for trajectory optimization can effectively reduce mission fuel consumption and improve mission economics. In mission design, analyzing the triangular libration points helps understand spacecraft behavior in complex gravitational fields and informs stationkeeping strategy development.
