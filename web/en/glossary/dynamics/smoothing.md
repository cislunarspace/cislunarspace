## Definition

Continuity processing applied at the piecewise junction of the comprehensive cost evaluation indicator function. The paper applies smooth connection between adjacent segments at the 1.7 km/s velocity increment segmentation point to prevent evaluation weight jumps, ensuring continuity and stability of assessment results.
