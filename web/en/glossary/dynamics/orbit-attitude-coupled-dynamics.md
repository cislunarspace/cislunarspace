# Orbit-Attitude Coupled Dynamics

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The nonlinear dynamical phenomenon where a spacecraft's orbital and attitude motions interact and co-evolve through gravity-gradient torques and coupling terms.

## Application Value

Orbit-Attitude Coupled Dynamics plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
