# Fourier series expansion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method to expand periodic functions into series of sine and cosine terms for approximation. Used in this paper to expand periodic components of Floquet modal matrix to obtain analytical solutions of bounded relative motion.

## Application Value

Fourier series expansion plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
