# Relative Orbital Motion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The problem of describing the motion of a deputy spacecraft relative to a chief spacecraft, both orbiting under the gravitational attraction of the same center. It is the core dynamic problem in spacecraft formation flying, rendezvous operations, and distributed spacecraft missions.

## Application Value

该概念是分析地月空间动力学行为的基础工具。在轨道设计和任务分析中具有重要应用价值。
