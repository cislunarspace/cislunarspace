# Direct Single Shooting

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that treats the entire transfer trajectory as a single optimization segment, with all unknown initial conditions and parameters as design variables, solving the TPBVP via direct maximization of the objective function.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
