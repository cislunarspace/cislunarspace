# Pseudo-potential Function

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In CR3BP, a potential function describing the motion of the third body, containing centrifugal potential and gravitational potentials of both primaries.

## Application Value

Simulating potential functions guiding spacecraft motion through artificial potential fields, common methods for path planning and collision avoidance.
