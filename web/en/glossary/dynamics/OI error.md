# Orbit Injection Error

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Initial position and velocity deviations at orbit injection, modeled with zero-mean Gaussian distributions.

## Application Value

The orbit insertion maneuver transitioning a spacecraft to its target orbit. Orbit injection error directly affects mission fuel budget and target orbit accessibility.
