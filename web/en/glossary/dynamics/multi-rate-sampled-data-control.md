# Multi-Rate Sampled-Data Control

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A digital control strategy that uses different sampling rates for measurement and actuation on the same system. For instance, measurements are sampled at instants tk while control switches at finer sub-intervals. This design avoids the unstable zero-dynamics induced by single-rate sampling and recovers the input-output structure consistent with the continuous-time relative degree, serving as a key technique for sampled-data nonlinear control.
