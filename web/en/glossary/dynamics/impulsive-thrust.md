# Impulsive Thrust

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Short-duration high thrust from chemical rockets, where burn duration is negligible compared to total flight time and is modeled as instantaneous velocity changes.

## Application Value

Impulsive Thrust plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
