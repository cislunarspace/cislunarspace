# Fixed-Time-of-Arrival Guidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A guidance law that drives the spacecraft to arrive at a target position at a specified time.

## Application Value

Guidance strategies requiring spacecraft arrival at target points at specified times, core requirements for rendezvous and docking and landing missions.
