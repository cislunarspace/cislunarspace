# Indirect Multi-Stage Formulation, IMF

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that divides a complete trajectory into a series of connected stages via equality constraints, optimizing control independently for each stage.

## Application Value

Indirect Multi-Stage Formulation, IMF plays an important role in orbital mechanics and mission planning, applicable for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics and optimizing transfer trajectories.
