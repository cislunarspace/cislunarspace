# Kepler's Third Law

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The square of a planet's orbital period is proportional to the cube of its semi-major axis.

## Application Value

This orbit type can serve as a transfer station in the cislunar space transportation network or as a target orbit for missions. This law is fundamental to orbital mechanics and is widely applied in orbit design and mission analysis.
