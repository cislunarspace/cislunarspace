# Collision Avoidance Maneuver, CAM
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Active maneuvers performed to prevent the follower from approaching or colliding with the leader. The paper embeds the collision avoidance constraint into the MPC quadratic programming framework, approximating the KOZ sphere as a tangent plane through linearization and expressing it as an inequality constraint.

## Application Value

The Collision Avoidance Maneuver, CAM plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
