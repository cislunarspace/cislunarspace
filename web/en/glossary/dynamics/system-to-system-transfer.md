# System-to-System Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer that exploits the intersection of invariant manifold tubes from two overlapping three-body systems (e.g., Earth–Moon and Sun–Earth) to deliver a spacecraft from a libration point orbit in one system to another. The four-body Sun–Earth–Moon problem is decomposed into two coupled three-body problems, and manifold tube overlaps enable transfers at very low or zero ΔV cost.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
