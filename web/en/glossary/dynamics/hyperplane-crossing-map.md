# Hyperplane Crossing Map

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Map recording the position and velocity states at hyperplane crossings for trajectories propagated from maneuvers along maximum stretching directions, used to generate initial guesses for transfer trajectory design.
