# Thrust Vector Error

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The deviation of the spacecraft's actual thrust direction and magnitude from nominal values. Thrust direction error (pointing error) and thrust magnitude error (efficiency error) are major sources of transfer trajectory errors. The paper assumes Case 1 with 1.0-degree direction error (3σ) and 1.0% magnitude error (3σ), and Case 2 with 1.5-degree and 1.5% respectively, to evaluate EPRM robustness against thrust errors. Thrust vector errors are propagated through the entire transfer trajectory via Monte Carlo analysis.

## Application Value

Thrust Vector Error plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
