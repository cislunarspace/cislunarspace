# Phasing Flyby

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A strategy of multiple gravity flybys to adjust orbital phasing. At high Jacobi constants (low energies), phasing flybys of Earth or moon are the primary method for connecting Earth-moon transfers, gradually reshaping the trajectory through successive close approaches.

## Application Value

In transfer trajectory design, the method is used to analyze transfer corridors and energy-optimal trajectories, underpinning low-energy transfer schemes.
