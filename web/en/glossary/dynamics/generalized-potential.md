# Generalized Potential

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The equivalent potential function V(r, ṙ, t) in the relative motion equations, incorporating contributions from Coriolis force, centrifugal force, and gravitational perturbations, used to derive the energy integral.

## Application Value

用于分析航天器在复杂引力场中的运动特性 描述系统状态随时间的变化规律，是轨道预报的基础。
