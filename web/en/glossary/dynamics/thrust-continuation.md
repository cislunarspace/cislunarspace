# Thrust Continuation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method to handle optimization difficulty at very low thrust acceleration levels. It starts from a high initial thrust level and gradually reduces the thrust ceiling, using the optimal trajectory from the previous step as the initial guess for the next step.
