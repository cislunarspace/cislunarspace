# Jet Vane

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Deflectable surface immersed in rocket engine exhaust stream for thrust direction control

## Application Value

Jet vanes provide thrust direction control by deflecting the engine exhaust, enabling pitch and yaw control during rocket ascent.
