# Trajectory Correction Maneuver (TCM)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Small maneuvers performed at key nodes along a transfer trajectory to correct accumulated deviations from initial insertion errors and PLSB execution errors. The EPRM Type 3 scheme arranges six TCMs: one day after TLI to correct PLSB targeting accuracy, again one day before PLSB, 12 hours after PLSB to correct large errors from PLSB, and subsequently at each apolune of the elliptical orbit to correct preceding maneuver errors. The advantage of EPRM is that the lunar orbital segment spans several weeks, providing ample time windows for multiple TCMs, reducing the position error at NRHO arrival to approximately 30 km.

## Application Value

Trajectory correction maneuvers correct accumulated errors at key nodes. The EPRM Type 3 scheme arranges six TCMs to reduce the position error at NRHO arrival to approximately 30 km, ensuring the success rate of docking missions.
