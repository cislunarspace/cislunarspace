# Thrust Continuation Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An iterative strategy that starts from a high thrust level and progressively reduces the thrust ceiling, using the previous optimal trajectory as the initial guess, combining reduction and thrust continuation phases to reduce computational divergence risk at very low thrust.
