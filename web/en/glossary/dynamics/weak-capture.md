# Weak Capture

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The phenomenon where a spacecraft, upon entering the vicinity of a celestial body (such as the Moon), remains there for a minimum period or completes at least one revolution without propulsive expenditure. Weak capture is the central concept of the Weak Stability Boundary theory. Unlike strong capture, it does not require permanent confinement within a closed region, only a temporary ballistic residence.

## Application Value

在航天器系统设计和任务分析中，该概念用于描述航天器的运动特性或任务模式，为任务方案论证和系统设计提供理论基础.
