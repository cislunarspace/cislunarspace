# Variable Specific Impulse Engine, VSI Engine

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An electric propulsion engine whose specific impulse varies with thrust level, allowing optimization of propellant consumption by adjusting exhaust velocity.

## Application Value

Variable Specific Impulse Engine, VSI Engine plays an important role in orbital mechanics and mission planning, applicable for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics and optimizing transfer trajectories.
