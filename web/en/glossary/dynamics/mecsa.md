# Method of Equivalent Cross Section Area

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that converts the projected areas of components of a complex spacecraft structure into equivalent circular areas for computing collision probability.

## Application Value

Method of Equivalent Cross Section Area plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
