# Low-thrust Station-keeping

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Using low-thrust propulsion (e.g., electric propulsion) to maintain a spacecraft's position on a target orbit.

## Application Value

Low-thrust Station-keeping plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
