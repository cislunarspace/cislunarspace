# Perilune Limiter

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A logic device installed to avoid high-cost maneuvers near the NRHO perilune. Due to the high velocity and low lunar distance at perilune, impulse maneuver costs are extremely high. This limiter ensures all transfers complete before crossing perilune within the default 48-hour time of flight window.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
