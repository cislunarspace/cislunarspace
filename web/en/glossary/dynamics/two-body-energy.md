# Two-Body Energy (E₂)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Two-body energy of spacecraft relative to the Moon, used to describe orbital characteristics in the DRO capture phase; greater than zero in non-ballistic capture trajectories.

## Application Value

该轨道类型可作为地月空间运输网络的中转站或任务的目标轨道。该机制使得航天器无需主动减速即可被目标天体引力捕获，显著节省推进剂。
