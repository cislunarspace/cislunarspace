# Chaos

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The phenomenon of sensitive dependence on initial conditions and unpredictable long-term behavior in deterministic systems. In the three-body problem, it arises from homoclinic/heteroclinic tangling of stable and unstable manifolds.

## Application Value

Chaos plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
