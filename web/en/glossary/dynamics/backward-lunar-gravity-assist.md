# Backward Lunar Gravity Assist

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An assist mechanism during flight in the first and third quadrants where solar gravitational perturbation decreases the spacecraft's Jacobi energy and angular momentum while lowering the perigee altitude.

## Application Value

该概念在轨道能量分析中用于量化航天器的能量状态，是评估转移方案经济性的重要指标。
