# Ultra-Short-Arc Orbit Determination

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Orbit determination where the observation arc is much shorter than the orbital period. Cislunar objects have periods of about one month, while a single ground station's effective nightly observation spans only a few hours (less than 1% of the period). Angular constraints are adequate in right ascension and declination, but weak along the line of sight, where most of the determination error concentrates.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
