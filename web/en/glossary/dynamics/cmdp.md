> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Markov Decision Process with constraints, solved via Lagrange relaxation into an unconstrained MDP, used to minimize average per-device AoI under energy constraints.
