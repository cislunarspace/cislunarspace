# Amplitude Correction Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A velocity increment applied after the spacecraft reaches the target Lissajous orbit to adjust its actual oscillation amplitude to the designed value. Used as a fallback when the refined zero-cost transfer arrives at an orbit significantly different from the intended target.
