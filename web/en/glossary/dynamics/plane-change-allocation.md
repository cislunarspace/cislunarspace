# Plane Change Allocation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In three-dimensional low-thrust transfers, the process of distributing the total plane change among the Earth-escape, translunar coast, and moon-capture segments. The optimization freely allocates inclination changes to minimize fuel consumption.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
