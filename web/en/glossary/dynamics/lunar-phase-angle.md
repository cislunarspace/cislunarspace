# Lunar Phase Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle measured counter-clockwise from the Sun–Earth X-axis to the PS plane in the Sun–Earth rotating frame, typically ranging from 80° to 120°. Together with the Earth–Moon orientation angles (α and β), ψ determines the timing constraints of the transfer in the ephemeris model. The lowest-cost transfers for various halo orbit combinations cluster near specific values of ψ (e.g., 110°).

## Application Value

Lunar phase angle ψ determines the timing constraints of manifold tubes in real ephemeris models. The lowest transfer costs for various Halo orbit combinations occur near specific lunar phase angles (e.g., 110°). Mission planning requires selecting favorable lunar phase angle windows.
