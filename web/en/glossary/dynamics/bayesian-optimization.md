# Bayesian Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A global optimization strategy based on Bayes theorem that constructs a probabilistic surrogate model of the objective function and iteratively updates it with observed data, suitable for problems with high computational cost and intractable derivatives. The paper uses this method to solve the orbit identification optimization model.
