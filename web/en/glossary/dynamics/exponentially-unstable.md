# Exponentially Unstable

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The instability property where state deviations grow exponentially when the equilibrium has eigenvalues with nonzero real parts. The linearized system at a collinear libration point has a real eigenvalue pair ±d₁, yielding an exponentially growing (unstable) and decaying (stable) direction. Any initial deviation along the unstable component grows as e^{d₁t}, making continuous orbit control mandatory for spacecraft operating near collinear libration points.
