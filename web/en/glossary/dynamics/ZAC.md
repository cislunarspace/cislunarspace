# Zero Acceleration Contour, ZAC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The locus of low-thrust equilibrium points, representing locations where the net acceleration acting on the spacecraft sums to zero.

## Application Value

The locus of equilibrium points where net acceleration sums to zero, representing stationary positions under low thrust, foundational for analyzing low-thrust orbits.
