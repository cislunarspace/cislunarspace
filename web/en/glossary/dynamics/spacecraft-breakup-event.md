# Spacecraft Breakup Event

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An event in which a spacecraft disintegrates due to explosion, collision, or other causes. As of 2024, over 560 spacecraft breakup events have occurred worldwide, generating debris that accounts for more than half of all cataloged space objects. Breakup debris moves at high speeds in space, threatening other in-orbit spacecraft; debris-on-debris collisions further generate new fragments, potentially triggering cascading Kessler chain collision effects.

## Application Value

Spacecraft Breakup Event plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
