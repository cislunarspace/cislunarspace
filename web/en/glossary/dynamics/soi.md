# Sphere of Influence, SOI

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A spherical region centered on a celestial body within which its gravitational influence dominates the motion of a spacecraft. In the Earth-Moon system, the Moon's SOI has a radius of ~66,000 km and serves as the boundary separating geocentric and selenocentric orbital dynamics. The patched conic method uses the SOI boundary to switch between different gravitational models.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
