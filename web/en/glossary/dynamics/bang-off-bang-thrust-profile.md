# Bang-off-Bang Thrust Profile

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The characteristic thrust profile of fuel-optimal low-thrust transfers. The engine alternates between maximum thrust (bang, throttle = 1) and shutdown (off, throttle = 0), forming a sequence of alternating thrust and coast arcs. Unlike minimum-time transfers that maintain full thrust throughout, bang-off-bang saves fuel by coasting at appropriate times, with switching times determined by the sign of the switching function.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
