# Robust Lambert Solver, RLS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A robust Lambert solver based on Izzo's and Blanchard's algorithms, capable of solving single- and multi-revolution Lambert problems without requiring an initial guess, with fast convergence.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
