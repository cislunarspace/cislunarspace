# Drag-Free and Attitude Control, DFAC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Attitude control technique that maintains a perturbation-free orbit by compensating non-gravitational perturbations such as atmospheric drag; GOCE satellite is a typical application.

## Application Value

Drag-Free and Attitude Control, DFAC plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
