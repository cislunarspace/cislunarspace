# Ejection Velocity, ΔV

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The velocity increment acquired by fragments upon separation from the parent spacecraft. In the NASA SBM, ejection velocity direction is uniformly distributed on a sphere, with magnitude determined by fragment area-to-mass ratio. The heaviest fragment's ejection velocity must be corrected to satisfy momentum conservation.

## Application Value

Velocity increment acquired by fragments upon separation, with direction uniformly distributed on a sphere and magnitude determined by area-to-mass ratio, a core output of breakup models.
