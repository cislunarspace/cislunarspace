# Manifold Tube

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A three-dimensional tubular channel bounded by stable and unstable manifolds together. Transit orbits inside the manifold tube always remain between the two manifold boundaries in position space. The manifold tube is the geometric vehicle for applying manifold theory to transfer trajectory design; the paper uses it to describe the feasible region of the transfer trajectory family from HLOs to large DROs.
