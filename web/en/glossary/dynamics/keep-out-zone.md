# Keep-Out Zone

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Keep-Out Zone; safety distance zone that spacecraft must maintain during rendezvous to prevent collision.


## Application Value

对接是两个航天器在轨组装和物资补给的前提，需要精确的相对导航和控制。
