# Velocity Minimum

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The point along a trajectory where the speed reaches a local minimum. Applying a direction-adjustment maneuver at this point minimizes the energy cost of changing the velocity direction. This is the location where the first impulse is applied in the four-impulse low-energy transfer scheme.

## Application Value

In analyses involving velocity minima, it can be applied to orbit propagation and maneuver design, helping engineers evaluate spacecraft motion characteristics across different dynamical environments. This concept provides a theoretical foundation for cislunar mission design, with significant applications in libration point orbit design and low-energy transfer analysis. Using velocity minima for trajectory optimization can effectively reduce mission fuel consumption and improve mission economics. In mission design, analyzing velocity minima helps understand spacecraft behavior in complex gravitational fields and informs stationkeeping strategy development.
