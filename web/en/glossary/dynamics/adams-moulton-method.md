# Adams-Moulton Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An implicit multi-step method for numerically integrating ordinary differential equations. The paper uses the Adams-Moulton routine DIVPAG with a truncation error of 10⁻⁷. Compared to the explicit Adams-Cowell method, Adams-Moulton is more suitable for solving stiff problems.

## Application Value

Adams-Moulton Method is valuable for dynamics modeling and analysis in cislunar space research.
