# Manifold Tube Intersection

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The common region in phase space where manifold tubes from two different three-body systems overlap. It is the physical basis for system-to-system transfers: a spacecraft departs along an unstable manifold tube from one system and enters along a stable manifold tube of the other, with the intersection point defining the transfer location. In 3D, the search for tube intersections uses PS plane projections and spline interpolation.
