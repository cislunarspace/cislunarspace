# Perilune Poincaré Map

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A Poincaré section constructed at the perilune, used to filter transfer trajectories from DRO backward integration that satisfy WSB and perilune conditions, serving as a key tool for initial guess design of LGA+WSB transfers.

## Application Value

Perilune Poincaré Map plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
