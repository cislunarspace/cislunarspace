# Minimum Perturbation Magnitude Threshold

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The minimum ratio of perturbative acceleration to central-body gravitational acceleration that justifies including a perturbing body in the orbit determination model. For conservative forces, the threshold equals Σ/(2nδT), where Σ is the required accuracy, n is the mean motion, and δT is the prediction span.
