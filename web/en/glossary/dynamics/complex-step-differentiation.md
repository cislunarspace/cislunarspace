# Complex Step Differentiation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method for approximating first-order derivatives using imaginary part of a complex perturbation with step size h, with much higher accuracy than finite differences.

## Application Value

用于评估导航系统的精度上限，指导滤波器设计 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
