# Tensor Invariant

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A constant tensor relationship in relative motion, relating the deputy's motion in the LVLH frame to the chief's motion, serving as a propagator for the deputy spacecraft state.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
