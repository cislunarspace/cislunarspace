# Loading Tide

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Additional tidal deformation caused by changes in ocean or surface loading, distinct from equilibrium tide as it accounts for the body's internal structure response to tides.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
