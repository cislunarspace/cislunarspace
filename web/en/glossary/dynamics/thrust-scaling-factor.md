# Thrust Scaling Factor

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dimensionless parameter in the range [0,1] used to modulate the actual thrust magnitude of an electric propulsion system. Together with the thrust angle, it forms the control degrees of freedom for thrust direction and magnitude, and serves as an optimization variable. The paper discretizes the thrust scaling factor and thrust angle as control vector components, optimizing thrust laws via nonlinear programming.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
