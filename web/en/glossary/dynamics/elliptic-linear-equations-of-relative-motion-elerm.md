# Elliptic Linear Equations of Relative Motion (ELERM)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Linearized version of ENERM, obtained by first-order expansion of the non-linear gravitational terms. Has small errors near the aposelene and can be used for preliminary guidance design. Forms the error comparison baseline together with CNERM and CLERM.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space dynamics modeling and analysis, and can be applied to trajectory design and mission planning.
