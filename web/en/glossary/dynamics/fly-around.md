# Fly-Around

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A maneuver in which a deputy spacecraft executes periodic relative motion around a chief spacecraft while progressively closing the distance. Distinguished from formation flying (which maintains a fixed relative geometry) by requiring the trajectory to be both cyclic and monotonically convergent; used in space debris removal, target identification and proximity operations.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
