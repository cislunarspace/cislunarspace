# Sampling Zero-Dynamics

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Zero-dynamics that arise from discretization (sampling) of a continuous-time system and do not exist in the original continuous system. When sampling reduces the system's relative degree, the additional lower-order channels produce extra internal dynamics. These dynamics are typically unstable, preventing inversion-based methods on single-rate sampled models from guaranteeing internal stability, and form the core motivation for multi-rate sampled-data control design.
