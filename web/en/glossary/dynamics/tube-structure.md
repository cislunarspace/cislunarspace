# Tube Structure

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The tubular topological structure formed by invariant manifolds of small-amplitude libration point periodic orbits in phase space. Spacecraft can travel through transit orbits inside the tube to transfer between different spatial regions.

## Application Value

The tube structure describes tubular channels formed by invariant manifolds in a dynamical system; orbit designers exploit this structure to plan low-energy transfer paths.
