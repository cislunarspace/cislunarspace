# Pseudostate Theory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An analytical method for approximating Earth-Moon three-body trajectories, proposed by Wilson in 1969. It decomposes a trans-lunar trajectory into three segments: an Earth-centered two-body leg, a linear back-propagation leg, and a Moon-centered two-body leg, switching between regimes at the pseudostate transformation sphere boundary. The Earth two-body segment propagates from the initial state to the PTS boundary; the linear back-propagation segment maps the spacecraft's Moon-relative state back to the PTS boundary; the Moon two-body segment propagates from the PTS boundary to the arrival state. By splitting the three-body problem into analytically solvable two-body segments, it achieves high computational efficiency and provides both approximate trajectories and state transition matrices.
