# Spherical Harmonic Gravity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A model representing the Earth's gravitational potential using spherical harmonic expansion, accounting for the Earth's non-spherical gravitational effects. Expansion coefficients include zonal coefficients (Jl) and sectoral/tesseral coefficients (C lm, Slm), used for high-precision orbit calculations.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
