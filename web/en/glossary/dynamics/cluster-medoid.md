# Cluster Medoid

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The member of a cluster that is most similar to all other members, i.e., minimizes the sum of distances to all other members, used as the representative trajectory of that cluster.
