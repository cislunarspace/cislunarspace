# Three-Body Sphere of Influence

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A spherical region of approximately 159,200 km around the Moon where Earth-Moon three-body dynamics dominate.

## Application Value

Three-Body Sphere of Influence plays an important role in orbital dynamics and attitude control, serving as a critical tool for analyzing spacecraft motion characteristics. Research on this concept enables optimization of orbit transfer schemes and improvement of control system performance.
