# Energy Coordinate System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Coordinate system with x-axis aligned with the Earth-Moon rotating frame and y-axis at x=0.5-μ; spacecraft mechanical energy decreases in quadrants I and III, and increases in quadrants II and IV.

## Application Value

The Energy Coordinate System has x-axis aligned with the Earth-Moon rotating frame and y-axis at x=0.5-μ. Spacecraft mechanical energy decreases in quadrants I and III, and increases in quadrants II and IV.
