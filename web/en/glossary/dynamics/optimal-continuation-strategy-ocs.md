# Optimal Continuation Strategy, OCS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A stationkeeping maneuver algorithm for libration point orbits that does not require a reference trajectory. Using differential correction and nonlinear optimization, OCS applies position or velocity constraints at successive x-z plane crossings for 1-2 revolutions downstream. ARTEMIS mission demonstrated annual stationkeeping costs of approximately 5-7 m/s.
