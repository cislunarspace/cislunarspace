# Richardson's Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An analytical approximation method for constructing periodic orbits near libration points, proposed by Richardson in 1980. The equations of motion are expanded to third order at the libration point, and the Lindstedt-Poincaré perturbation method is used to eliminate secular terms, yielding a third-order analytical solution for Halo orbits (including in-plane and out-of-plane components). This method is the first step in numerical computation of Halo orbits, providing initial guesses for subsequent differential correction.

## Application Value

This term has important application value in cislunar space missions. In orbit design, it can be used to optimize transfer trajectories and reduce mission fuel consumption. In attitude control and dynamics analysis, it helps understand spacecraft motion characteristics in complex gravitational fields, providing theoretical support for mission planning. In navigation and orbit determination, methods based on this term can improve orbit prediction accuracy and support the development of autonomous navigation algorithms.
