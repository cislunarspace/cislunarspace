# Nonspherical Gravitational Perturbation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Perturbation forces arising from the deviation of a body's gravitational field from a perfect sphere, described by spherical harmonic coefficients. For distant cislunar orbits, nonspherical perturbations from the Earth and Moon are negligible. They must be considered in low-orbit missions such as low lunar orbit.
