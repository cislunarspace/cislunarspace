# Mutual Reachability Distance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A distance metric defined in HDBSCAN that captures both the dissimilarity between members of a dataset and the density of their local neighborhoods, further separating members located in sparse regions to increase clustering robustness.
