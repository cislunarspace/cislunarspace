# Time-Optimal Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit optimization problem with minimum transfer time as the objective function. In the low-thrust scenario, the engine throttle parameter is always 1 (full thrust), and the thrust direction is determined by Pontryagin's minimum principle. Compared to fuel-optimal transfer, time-optimal transfer consumes more propellant but achieves shorter flight duration.

## Application Value

Time-Optimal Transfer plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
