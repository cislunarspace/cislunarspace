# Runge-Kutta-Fehlberg 7(8) Numerical Integration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An adaptive step-size Runge-Kutta method comparing 7th and 8th order formulas to automatically adjust step size, ensuring accuracy while improving computational efficiency. Used for high-fidelity orbit propagation with relative tolerance 1×10^-12 and absolute tolerance 1×10^-9.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
