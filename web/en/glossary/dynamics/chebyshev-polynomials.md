# Chebyshev Polynomials

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A class of orthogonal polynomials with excellent numerical approximation properties on the interval [-1,1]. The paper uses them as basis functions in the Theory of Functional Connections to approximate spacecraft position coordinates during transfer, leveraging their derivative relations for efficient trajectory differential computation.
