# Second-Order Moment Matrix

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A symmetric matrix formed by the product of the transpose and the original gravitational potential Hessian matrix, i.e., U^T U. Its eigenvalues and eigenvectors determine the shape, size, and principal directions of the low drift ellipsoids, serving as the core mathematical tool for analyzing formation drift characteristics.

## Application Value

该几何表示可用于描述姿态机动中转动惯量的各向异性。
