# Anti-Tangential Thrust

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Thrust directed opposite to the orbital velocity vector. In lunar capture phases, anti-tangential thrust decelerates the spacecraft, progressively lowering its orbit from high to low lunar orbit. It is the optimal steering direction that maximizes the rate of change of semi-major axis for descending trajectories.

## Application Value

The 反切向推力 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
