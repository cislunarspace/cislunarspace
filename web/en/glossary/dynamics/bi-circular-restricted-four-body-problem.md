# Bi-Circular Restricted Four-Body Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dynamical model that extends CR3BP by incorporating solar gravitational effects, where the Earth and Moon orbit their common barycenter in circular motion and the Sun orbits in the same plane. Compared to CR3BP, BCR4BP more accurately describes long-term orbital evolution in cislunar space, suitable for debris evolution modeling and observation region analysis.
