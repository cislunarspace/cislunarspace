# double two-body patching

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A simplified method that treats the Earth-Moon transfer trajectory as solutions to two separate two-body problems connected at a patch point. This method is computationally simple but has lower accuracy, failing to fully account for three-body effects. Using a high-fidelity three-body model as reference, the paper analyzes the error characteristics of the double two-body patching approach.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
