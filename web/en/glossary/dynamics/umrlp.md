# Uncertain Multiple-Revolution Lambert Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A multiple-revolution Lambert problem with uncertainties in initial position vector, terminal position vector, and flight time, where the solution distribution depends on both the probability distributions of numerical parameters and orbit-dynamical boundary constraints tied to the revolution count.

## Application Value

Uncertain Multiple-Revolution Lambert Problem is an important concept in cislunar orbit design, crucial for understanding spacecraft motion in the Earth-Moon space.
