# Bang-Coast-Bang Thrust Profile

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A typical thrust pattern in low-thrust orbital maneuvers, consisting of an initial thrust arc, a coast arc near periselene, and a final thrust arc. The A2PPO-learned halo-to-NRHO transfer strategy in Scenario S2 exhibits this structure.
