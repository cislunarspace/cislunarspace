# Earth-to-Orbit Round-Trip System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A component of the cislunar aerospace transportation system responsible for round-trip transport of crew and cargo between the ground and low Earth orbit. As the first link in the transportation chain, it delivers payloads and propellant to LEO for docking with the orbital transfer stage. Required launch capacity is on the order of 100 tons, with thousands of launches per year.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
