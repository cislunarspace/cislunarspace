# Traversal Rendezvous

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A flight mode where a service spacecraft successively rendezvous with multiple target spacecraft. Requires planning the optimal visiting sequence and transfer times to minimize total fuel consumption or mission duration.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
