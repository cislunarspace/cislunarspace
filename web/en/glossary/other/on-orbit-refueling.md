# On-Orbit Refueling

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The technology of replenishing propellant to launch vehicles or spacecraft in space. It is a core enabler for airline-flight-mode cislunar transportation, eliminating the need for a vehicle to carry all required propellant at launch and dramatically reducing initial liftoff mass requirements. The one-stop solution performs refueling at LEO; the relay-style solution performs two refueling operations at the LEO depot and EML1 depot respectively.
