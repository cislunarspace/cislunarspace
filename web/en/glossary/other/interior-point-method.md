# Interior-Point Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A class of algorithms for constrained optimization. Starting from the interior of the feasible region, it transforms the constrained problem into a sequence of unconstrained or equality-constrained subproblems via barrier functions or complementary slackness conditions. In trajectory optimization, it is used for local correction of position errors to satisfy terminal constraints.

## Application Value

Combined with numerical optimization algorithms, high-performance trajectory planning can be achieved Used for evaluating the upper accuracy limit of navigation systems and guiding filter design.
