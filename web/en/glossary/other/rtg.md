# Radioisotope Thermoelectric Generator

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A nuclear power device that converts heat from radioactive isotope decay (such as 238Pu) directly into electricity through the Seebeck effect in thermocouples. The Chang'e-4 lander uses an RTG based on 238Pu, providing no less than 2.5 W of electrical power during the 14-day lunar night, while simultaneously providing thermal energy for cabin temperature control, solving the power supply challenge when solar energy is unavailable.

## Application Value

Radioisotope thermoelectric generators provide reliable deep space power independent of solar energy, key technology for lunar farside exploration.
