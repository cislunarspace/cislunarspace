# Human-Machine Cooperative Hazard Avoidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A mode during landing approach and hovering phases where astronauts participate in hazard avoidance decisions through a manual control simulation subsystem. The system automatically identifies hazards first, the astronaut confirms or corrects the avoidance plan, and manually controls the vehicle to avoid obstacles when necessary, combining automated efficiency with human judgment flexibility.
