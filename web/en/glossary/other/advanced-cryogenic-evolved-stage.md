# Advanced Cryogenic Evolved Stage

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-performance space transfer vehicle using LOX/LH2 propellants, representing an advanced international direction for upper stage technology. It features long-duration on-orbit capability and multiple on-orbit refueling capacity, enhancing orbital transfer performance and expanding upper stage applications for cislunar transportation to meet large-scale exploration and exploitation demands.

## Application Value

In orbital design and transfer trajectory optimization, this method is used to determine optimal transfer timing and orbital geometry, minimizing propellant consumption or flight time. Feasible orbital solutions satisfying mission constraints can be obtained through numerical simulation and iterative optimization.
