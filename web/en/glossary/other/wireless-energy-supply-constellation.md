# Wireless Energy Supply Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)
## Definition

A satellite constellation deployed in orbits close to the Moon, equipped with solar arrays, batteries, and lasers to store and transmit solar energy to surface receivers for wireless power supply on the lunar surface.
