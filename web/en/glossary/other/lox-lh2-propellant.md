# LOX/LH2 Propellant

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-performance bipropellant combination using liquid oxygen as oxidizer and liquid hydrogen as fuel. With a vacuum specific impulse of approximately 453 s, it is the highest-performing chemical propellant combination, suitable for space transfer missions demanding high mass efficiency. In the relay-style solution it is used for the orbital transfer stage and lunar lander/ascender, though long-term on-orbit storage of liquid hydrogen presents significant technical challenges.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
