# Space Science Research Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Constellations whose configuration is primarily determined by space science objectives, such as constellations for lunar ionospheric occultation measurements, consisting of at least two satellites in lunar Keplerian orbits that create occultation events through radio signals traversing the ionosphere.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in Earth-Moon space trajectory design, describing spacecraft motion characteristics in the Earth-Moon gravitational field.
