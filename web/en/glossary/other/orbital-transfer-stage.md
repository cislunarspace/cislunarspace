## Definition
A dedicated spacecraft in the relay-style architecture responsible for orbital transfer between LEO and Earth-Moon Lagrange points. It permanently stations at the LEO depot, receives on-orbit propellant refueling, then transports payloads and propellant for the lunar lander/ascender to the EML1 depot before returning to LEO. It can use LOX/LH2 propulsion or high-power electric propulsion, with a mass scale of approximately 65 tons.
