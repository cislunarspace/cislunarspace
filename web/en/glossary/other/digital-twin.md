# Digital Twin

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-fidelity digital replica of a physical system used for simulation, prediction, and decision support. The paper proposes building an open-source digital twin platform for cislunar SSA, integrating Monte Carlo simulation and threat assessment to form a closed-loop 'sense-decide-respond' system.

## Application Value

In aerospace transportation systems, this concept plays an important role in understanding spacecraft motion characteristics and mission design in cislunar space.
