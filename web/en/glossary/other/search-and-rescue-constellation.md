# Search and Rescue Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A constellation designed to provide search and rescue services when spacecraft in cislunar space experience accidents, with satellites rapidly reaching target regions through orbital maneuvers and the nearest satellite initiating rescue operations.

## Application Value

在轨道机动规划中，该概念用于分析机动时机、速度增量大小和方向，优化变轨策略。结合误差动力学分析，可设计鲁棒的机动控制器，提高任务安全性。
