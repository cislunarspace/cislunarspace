# Asteroid Defense Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A constellation of kinetic impact interceptors deployed at Lagrange points, designed for rapid interception and disruption of threatening asteroids within limited warning times, with potential dual-purpose early warning capability.

## Application Value

A constellation of kinetic impact interceptors deployed at Lagrange points enables rapid interception of threatening asteroids within limited warning times, with dual-purpose early warning capability. This represents an important safeguard for future cislunar space security.
