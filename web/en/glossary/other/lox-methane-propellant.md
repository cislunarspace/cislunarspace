# LOX-Methane Propellant

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A bipropellant combination using liquid oxygen as oxidizer and liquefied natural gas (methane) as fuel. It balances high specific impulse (vacuum Isp approximately 380 s) with storable characteristics suitable for long-duration missions with on-orbit refueling. It is the sole propellant in the one-stop solution and used for the Earth-to-orbit rocket in the relay-style solution. SpaceX's Raptor engine uses LOX-methane propellant.
