# Global Express Transportation System

## Definition

A component of the cislunar aerospace transportation system that enables transport of personnel and cargo between two surface points via suborbital flight within hours. Driven by future commercial and military applications, companies such as SpaceX, ULA, and agencies like DLR are each developing their own global express transportation systems.

## Application Value

A component of the cislunar aerospace transportation system that enables transport of personnel and cargo between two surface points via suborbital flight within hours has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operation of cislunar space missions and is an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
