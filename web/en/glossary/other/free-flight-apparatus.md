# Free-Flight Apparatus

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A test apparatus that allows a test vehicle to fly freely in the air without cable constraints. Compared to tower-cable suspension, it more realistically simulates six-degree-of-freedom flight dynamics but is harder to control and has shorter test durations. Used for verifying precision landing and autonomous hazard avoidance technologies.
