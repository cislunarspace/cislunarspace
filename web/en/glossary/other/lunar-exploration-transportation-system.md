# Lunar Exploration Transportation System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A component of the cislunar aerospace transportation system responsible for round-trip transport of crew and cargo between lunar orbit and the lunar surface, as well as rapid transportation between a lunar base and distant exploration sites. In the relay-style architecture, this system comprises a lunar lander/ascender and a long-range surface mobility vehicle, permanently stationed at the EML1 depot, receiving on-orbit refueling before transporting payloads to the surface and returning.

## Application Value

In aerospace transportation systems, this concept plays an important role in understanding spacecraft motion characteristics and mission design in cislunar space.
