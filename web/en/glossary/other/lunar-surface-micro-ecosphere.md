# Lunar Surface Micro-Ecosphere

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A 3 kg sealed cylindrical public engagement payload carried on the Chang'e-4 lander, containing plant seeds (tentatively potato) and insect eggs (tentatively silkworm) forming a simple ecosystem. A light guide tube at the top introduces sunlight for photosynthesis, polyester film insulation and semiconductor thermoelectric coolers provide temperature control, and lithium-sulfur batteries combined with lander power supply enable continuous day-night power. It validates photosynthesis principles under natural lunar sunlight and plant/animal growth under lunar low-gravity conditions.

## Application Value


Lunar Surface Micro-Ecosphere在地月空间科学与工程中有广泛应用，涉及轨道设计、任务规划、导航控制等多个技术领域。
In-depth study of Lunar Surface Micro-Ecosphere helps understand the complex dynamical environment of cislunar space, providing theoretical support and engineering references for future mission design.
In practical missions, it is necessary to combine numerical simulation and analytical methods to verify Lunar Surface Micro-Ecosphere, ensuring the design meets mission constraints and performance indicators.
