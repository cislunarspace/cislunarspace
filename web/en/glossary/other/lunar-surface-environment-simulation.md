# Lunar Surface Environment Simulation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A ground laboratory facility that replicates lunar surface illumination conditions, reflectance properties, and terrain features. It provides near-realistic lunar surface environment inputs for landing navigation sensors to verify their performance under lunar conditions.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
