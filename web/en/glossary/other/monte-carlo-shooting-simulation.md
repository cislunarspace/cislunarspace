## Definition
A batch statistical simulation method that considers random distributions of model parameters and initial states. Through numerous randomly sampled single-run simulations, it statistically evaluates probability distributions of propellant consumption, navigation accuracy, landing point dispersion, and other end-to-end metrics to assess system robustness.
