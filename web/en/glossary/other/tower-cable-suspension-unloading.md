# Tower-Cable Suspension Unloading
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A test method that applies upward pulling forces on a test vehicle through a tower and cable system to offset partial gravity, simulating the 1/6 lunar gravity environment. Widely used for lander hovering hazard avoidance tests and full-system comprehensive verification, it is the primary ground verification method for celestial body landing.

## Application Value

The Tower-Cable Suspension Unloading plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
