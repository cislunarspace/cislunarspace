# Solar Constant

> Author: [Tianjiang Says](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The solar constant ($S_0$) is the total solar radiant energy received per unit time per unit area perpendicular to the direction of the Sun's rays, at a distance of one astronomical unit (AU) from the Sun, outside Earth's atmosphere.

## Numerical Value

$$S_0 \approx 1367 \text{ W/m}^2$$

## Application in Solar Radiation Pressure Calculation

The solar constant is a fundamental parameter for calculating Solar Radiation Pressure (SRP). The SRP acceleration formula is:

$$a_{SRP} = k C_R \frac{S_0}{c} \frac{A}{m} \left(\frac{R_0}{R}\right)^2 \hat{n}$$

where $S_0/c$ is the solar radiation pressure, approximately 4.56 μN/m².
