# Vertical Take-off Horizontal Landing

## Definition

A reusable launch vehicle that takes off vertically and lands horizontally. During launch it follows a conventional vertical ascent; during return it uses aerodynamic wing lift to glide and land horizontally, similar to the Space Shuttle's reentry approach. It is one of the candidate configurations for the Earth-to-orbit round-trip system in the relay-style cislunar transportation architecture.

## Application Value

A reusable launch vehicle that takes off vertically and lands horizontally has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operation of cislunar space missions and is an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
