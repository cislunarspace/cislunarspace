# Shackleton Crater

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An impact crater near the lunar south pole (center coordinates approximately 89.9 deg S, 0.0 deg E), about 21 km in diameter. Its floor is in permanent shadow and may preserve water ice and other volatiles. Due to its proximity to illuminated regions, relatively flat terrain, and scientific value, it is widely used as a reference target point for lunar south pole coverage and base site analysis.
