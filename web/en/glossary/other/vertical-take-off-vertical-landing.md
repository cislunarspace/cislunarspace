# Vertical Take-off Vertical Landing

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A reusable launch vehicle that takes off and lands vertically. This is the dominant configuration for current international reusable launch vehicles, adopted by SpaceX's Falcon 9 and Blue Origin's New Glenn. In the relay-style cislunar transportation architecture, VTVL is one of the candidate configurations for the Earth-to-orbit round-trip system.

## Application Value

This term has important reference value in cislunar space research and engineering applications.
