# Integrated Communication, Navigation and Remote Sensing

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An infrastructure design philosophy that integrates communication, navigation, and remote sensing functions into the same satellite or satellite network. The paper proposes deploying such integrated satellites at strategic locations including lunar orbits, Earth-Moon Lagrange points, and DRO to build a cislunar information hub.

## Application Value

In orbital design and transfer trajectory optimization, this method is used to determine optimal transfer timing and orbital geometry, minimizing propellant consumption or flight time. Feasible orbital solutions satisfying mission constraints can be obtained through numerical simulation and iterative optimization.
