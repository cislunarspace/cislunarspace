# Reusable Launch Vehicle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A launch system that achieves dramatic cost reduction through recovery and reuse of launch vehicle components. Life-cycle cost comprises six categories: development, manufacturing, maintenance, recovery, operations, and miscellaneous. As reuse count increases, per-launch cost decreases continuously; at 100 reuses, total cost drops to roughly 6% of an expendable vehicle, a reduction of one to two orders of magnitude. SpaceX's Falcon 9 has demonstrated multiple booster reuses, while Starship/Super Heavy targets full vehicle reuse.
