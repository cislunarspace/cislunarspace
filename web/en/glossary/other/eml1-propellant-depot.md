# EML1 Propellant Depot

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An in-space propellant supply facility stationed at the Earth-Moon Lagrange L1 point, serving as a key relay node in the relay-style cislunar transportation architecture. The lunar lander/ascender permanently stations here, receiving on-orbit refueling before executing round-trip missions between EML1 and the lunar surface. By leveraging EML1's low energy threshold for Earth-Moon transfers, it effectively reduces the delta-V requirements for lunar surface transportation.
