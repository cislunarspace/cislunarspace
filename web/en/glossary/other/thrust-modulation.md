# Thrust Modulation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Technology to adjust thrust by switching engines on/off

## Application Value


Thrust Modulation在地月空间科学与工程中有广泛应用，涉及轨道设计、任务规划、导航控制等多个技术领域。
In-depth study of Thrust Modulation helps understand the complex dynamical environment of cislunar space, providing theoretical support and engineering references for future mission design.
In practical missions, it is necessary to combine numerical simulation and analytical methods to verify Thrust Modulation, ensuring the design meets mission constraints and performance indicators.
