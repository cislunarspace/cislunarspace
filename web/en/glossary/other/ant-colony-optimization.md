# Ant Colony Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A swarm-intelligence metaheuristic optimization algorithm proposed by Dorigo et al. A population of search agents explores the solution space simultaneously, guided by pheromone intensity deposited by predecessors; after local searches are complete, global pheromone updates steer the population toward the optimum. Applicable to multi-parameter, nonlinear, constrained optimization problems.
