# Swingby

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A mission analysis and trajectory design software developed at NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center. This paper uses Swingby release 2002-01 for numerical integration verification of triangular libration point formations, employing a Runge-Kutta-Nystrom integrator with a time step of 0.5 days (0.02 days for perturbed models). The software does not automatically compute relative orbits; relative trajectories are obtained by manually subtracting the positions of two satellites.

## Application Value

This concept is relevant to cislunar space operations and mission design.
