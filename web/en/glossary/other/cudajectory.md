# CUDAjectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An open-source software tool developed at ESA's European Space Operations Centre (ESOC), utilizing massive parallelization capabilities of Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) for double-precision trajectory propagation, used in the paper for 100-year long-term debris propagation in the high-fidelity Sun-Earth-Moon model.
