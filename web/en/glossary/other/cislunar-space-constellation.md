# Cislunar Space Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A satellite network system deployed across multiple orbits in cislunar space to provide communication and navigation services for lunar exploration activities. The paper's final configuration is 22 satellites ('2 southern NRHO + 2 northern NRHO + 8 ELFO + 10 DRO'), achieving 100% quadruple continuous coverage of the full lunar surface.

## Application Value

This term has important reference value in cislunar space research and engineering applications.
