> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

## Definition

A packet generation model allowing new status update packets to be generated at any time, with the scheduling policy determining when they are actually transmitted.

## Application Value

该模型允许灵活调度数据传输，优化通信资源利用率。
