# Free-space Propagation Loss

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Free-space Propagation Loss; the power attenuation of radio waves propagating in free space due to wavefront spreading, inversely proportional to the square of the propagation distance, fundamental to long-distance inter-satellite communication link design.

## Application Value

This term has important reference value in cislunar space research and engineering applications.
