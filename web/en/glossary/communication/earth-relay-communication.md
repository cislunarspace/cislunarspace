# Earth-Relay Communication

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Signal relay through intermediate satellites to enable data transmission between Earth and lunar regions (far side, polar regions) that lack direct Earth line-of-sight. The lunar far side is blocked by the Moon itself, and the south polar region has a very low Earth elevation angle, both requiring relay satellite support.
