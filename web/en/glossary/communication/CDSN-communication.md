# China Deep Space Network

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

China's ground-based deep space tracking and communication network, comprising stations at Kashi (25 m dish), Kiamusze (35 m dish), and Argentina (35 m dish), providing range and Doppler tracking data with approximately 8 hours of daily coverage. CDSN is the primary ground infrastructure for China's lunar exploration program and deep space missions.

## Application Value

Providing approximately 8 hours of daily tracking coverage, CDSN serves as the primary ground infrastructure for China's lunar exploration and deep space missions.
