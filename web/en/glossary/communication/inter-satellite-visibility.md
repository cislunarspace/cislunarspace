# Inter-Satellite Visibility

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The geometric condition enabling inter-satellite link communication between two satellites. This paper shows that Halo orbiters provide coverage to LMO reaching or exceeding 60%, with lunar occlusion periods being short and uniformly distributed.

## Application Value

在轨道设计阶段，可利用该轨道类型构建候选轨道池，为星座部署和任务轨道选择提供参考。在轨运行时，该轨道特性可用于轨道维持策略设计，降低推进剂消耗。在轨道转移规划中，其稳定流形结构可指导低能量转移走廊的搜索。
