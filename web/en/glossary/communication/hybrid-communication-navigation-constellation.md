# Hybrid Communication/Navigation Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar CN constellation composed of satellites on different orbit types. A typical design uses Halo orbit satellites for polar coverage and DRO satellites for equatorial coverage, complementing each other to achieve full lunar surface coverage.

## Application Value

This concept is significant for space-based optical observation and space situational awareness, used for evaluating spacecraft visibility to targets.
