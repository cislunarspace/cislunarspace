# Heterogeneous Relay Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Complementary-coverage relay constellation composed of ordinary circular orbit and Halo orbit satellites, compensating for the long coverage gaps of a single Halo satellite over the lunar far side.

## Application Value

Heterogeneous Relay Constellation has practical applications in cislunar space mission design and analysis.
