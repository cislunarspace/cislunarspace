# Inter-Satellite Link

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A means of establishing direct communication and measurement links between satellites, enabling inter-satellite ranging, time synchronization, and data exchange, effectively reducing spacecraft dependence on ground tracking and control resources.

## Application Value

This technique is crucial for cislunar navigation and orbit determination, providing high-precision position and velocity information for spacecraft.
