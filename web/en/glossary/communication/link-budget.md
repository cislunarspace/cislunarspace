# Link Budget

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A quantitative analysis of signal power gains and losses from transmitter to receiver across the entire communication path. In Earth-Moon halo relay communication, the link budget is primarily governed by the Earth-relay-Moon path length: longer paths incur greater free-space loss, demanding higher transmit power and antenna gain.

## Application Value

Link budget analysis quantifies signal power gains and losses across communication paths, governing feasibility of relay communication in Earth-Moon halo orbit constellations.
