# Relay Satellite

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dedicated satellite stationed on a Halo orbit around the Earth-Moon L2 point to provide Earth-relay communication services for lunar farside spacecraft. The Chang'e-4 relay satellite is built on the CAST100 platform, equipped with a 4.2 m deployable umbrella parabolic antenna, carrying about 400 kg of hydrazine propellant with over 500 m/s delta-V capability. Its mission orbit is a southern-family Halo orbit with Z-amplitude of approximately 13,000 km and average period of about 14 days, maintaining simultaneous line-of-sight to both Earth and the lunar farside for full-time relay service.
