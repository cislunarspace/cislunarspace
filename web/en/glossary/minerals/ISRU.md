# In-Situ Resource Utilization, ISRU

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The use of local resources at a destination celestial body for supply, life support, or production of propellant.

## Application Value

Technology for manufacturing propellant and production materials using local resources on the Moon and other celestial bodies, a key technology for reducing deep space mission launch mass.
