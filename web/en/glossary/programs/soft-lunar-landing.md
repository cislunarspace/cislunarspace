# Soft Lunar Landing

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Delivering a spacecraft to the lunar surface at near-zero velocity. When departing from an NRHO, the approaching velocity is near the lunar escape speed (~2.19 km/s), requiring substantial deceleration.

## Application Value

Soft lunar landing delivers spacecraft to the lunar surface at near-zero velocity; departing from NRHO requires deceleration from near lunar escape speed (~2.19 km/s).
