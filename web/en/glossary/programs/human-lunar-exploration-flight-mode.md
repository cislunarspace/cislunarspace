# Human Lunar Exploration Flight Mode

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A systematic scheme that divides the manned lunar vehicle into multiple segments, performing rendezvous and docking assembly at a designated orbital location to reduce the mass of any single vehicle. The flight mode determines vehicle mass, launch vehicle capability, mission reliability, and programmatic risk.
