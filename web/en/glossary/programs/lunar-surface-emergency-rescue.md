# Lunar Surface Emergency Rescue

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An emergency supply delivery and crew evacuation mission performed by a rescue spacecraft stationed in lunar orbit when astronauts face danger on the lunar surface during crewed missions. Unlike Earth-based rescue, the vast Earth-Moon distance precludes waiting for a new launch from Earth, requiring an on-orbit standby system with real-time response capability. The mission involves multiple phases including orbit transfer, phasing, and powered descent, demanding globally time-optimal guidance.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
