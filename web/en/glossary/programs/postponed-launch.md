# postponed launch

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A delay of the actual launch time relative to the planned time. The paper assesses tolerance for postponed launches by analyzing the extensibility of launch windows (0.5 hours before and after), quantifying window backup capability through the distribution of feasible windows within consecutive 2-day and 2-month periods.

## Application Value

Assessing postponed launch tolerance through launch window extensibility analysis (0.5 hours before and after) quantifies mission flexibility and robustness.
