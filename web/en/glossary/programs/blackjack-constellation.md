# Blackjack Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A US DARPA-led military LEO small-satellite constellation program, serving as the tracking layer of the Next Generation Space Architecture. It aims to build a highly resilient military LEO constellation for tactical distribution of information and intelligence and hybrid military-commercial networking.

## Application Value

该系统代表了当前地月空间开发的主流方向，对未来月球基地建设和任务规划具有重要参考价值。
