# Orbit Selection Evaluation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A quantitative comparison of candidate orbits across dimensions including delta-V, flight time, tracking and communication, lunar surface accessibility, landing windows, and deep-space mission support, to determine the optimal orbit for a specific mission.
