# Genesis Mission

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A NASA Discovery-class solar wind sample return mission designed by JPL, launched in 2001 to a Halo orbit near the Sun-Earth L1 Lagrange point. The spacecraft collected solar wind particles for two years before returning to Earth via a heteroclinic connection, landing at the Utah Test and Training Range. The three-year mission required only one deterministic maneuver (~13 m/s at Halo orbit insertion). It was the first mission whose transfer trajectory was fully designed using dynamical systems theory.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
