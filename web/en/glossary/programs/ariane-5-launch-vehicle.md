# Ariane-5 Launch Vehicle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The launch vehicle manufactured by Arianespace, used in the paper to launch the SMART-1 probe into Earth transfer orbit. SMART-1 entered GTO orbit after separation from Ariane-5 and began low-thrust transfer.

## Application Value

Ariane-5 has been used to launch lunar probes such as SMART-1 into Earth transfer orbit, demonstrating the capability of European launch vehicles for lunar exploration missions.
