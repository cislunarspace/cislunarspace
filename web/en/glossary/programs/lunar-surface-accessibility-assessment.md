# Lunar Surface Accessibility Assessment

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Assessment of how much delta-V and time are required to reach different lunar surface regions from a given orbit. Accessibility depends on transfer trajectory geometry and fuel budget, directly affecting mission coverage. Different orbits show significantly different accessibility to polar, equatorial, and far-side regions.
