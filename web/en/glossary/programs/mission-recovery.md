# Mission Recovery

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Technologies that redesign mission trajectories using natural dynamics (especially weak stability boundaries) after spacecraft failure or underperformance to achieve partial or full mission objectives.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
