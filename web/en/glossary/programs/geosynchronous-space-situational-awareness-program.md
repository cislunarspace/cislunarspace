# Geosynchronous Space Situational Awareness Program

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A US Air Force space-based surveillance system consisting of satellites orbiting near geosynchronous orbit, used for close-range observation and cataloguing of spacecraft and debris in the GEO region.

## Application Value

该系统代表了当前地月空间开发的主流方向，对未来月球基地建设和任务规划具有重要参考价值。
