# Asteroid Redirect Mission, ARM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A NASA deep space mission concept that planned to capture a boulder from an asteroid and relocate it to a cislunar DRO for subsequent research and sample return. The mission demonstrated the potential of DROs as transit stations for deep space operations.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
