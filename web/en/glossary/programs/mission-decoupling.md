# Mission Decoupling

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The decomposition of a crewed lunar mission into two independent parts (Earth-station transport and lunar landing) through a space station waypoint. Crew first transit to the station, wait for optimal windows, then execute landing, eliminating mission cycle constraints and improving flexibility.
