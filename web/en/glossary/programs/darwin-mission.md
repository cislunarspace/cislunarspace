# Darwin Mission

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An infrared space interferometry mission concept proposed by ESA. It employs an array of 3 to 4 telescopes with 3-meter apertures, synthesizing an equivalent large aperture through coherent processing of signals from each telescope, aimed at searching for and characterizing Earth-like exoplanets. Telescope spacing must be controlled to micrometer precision and wavefront error to nanometer precision, making the technical challenge enormous. The mission has been indefinitely postponed. The Darwin concept drove research into spoke-wheel formation control.

## Application Value

为航天器的精确控制提供理论依据，确保任务执行的可靠性 用于评估导航系统的精度上限，指导滤波器设计。
