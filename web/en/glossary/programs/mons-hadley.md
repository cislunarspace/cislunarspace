# Mons Hadley

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar mountain located at approximately 14.6°N, 3.7°E on the lunar near side, near the Apollo 15 landing site. The paper lists it as one of the low-latitude candidate landing zones for manned lunar exploration, at selenographic latitude 14.61° and longitude 35.71°, with high scientific exploration value.
