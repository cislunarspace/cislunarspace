# THEMIS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

NASA Earth science mission; its P1 and P2 probes later evolved into ARTEMIS mission.

## Application Value

THEMIS是NASA地球科学探测卫星任务，其P1和P2探测器后续演化为ARTEMIS任务，开创了利用科学卫星研究平动点轨道的先例。
