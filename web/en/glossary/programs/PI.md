# Propellant Infrastructure, PI

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A fuel storage facility in a long elliptical Earth orbit providing on-orbit refueling for reusable orbital transfer vehicles. With apogee ~230,000 km and perigee ~300 km, it stores propellant delivered by high-thrust rockets to high apogee, allowing OTVs to shuttle to lunar orbits without the large delta-V penalty of climbing from LEO, significantly reducing fuel requirements and system size.

## Application Value

This propulsion technology is important for deep space missions, serving as a key means for efficient orbital transfers.
