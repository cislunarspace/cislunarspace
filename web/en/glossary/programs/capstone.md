> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A NASA 12U CubeSat technology demonstration mission launched in 2022. CAPSTONE was the first spacecraft to operate in a near-rectilinear halo orbit in cislunar space, primarily validating navigation technology for the Lunar Gateway's target orbit. The mission adopted XAC as its station-keeping strategy.
