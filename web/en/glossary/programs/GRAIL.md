# Gravity Recovery and Interior Laboratory, GRAIL

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

NASA lunar mission using two satellites in formation flight to measure the Moon's gravitational field.

## Application Value

NASA's dual-satellite formation mission precisely measuring lunar gravitational field, providing high-resolution gravity models for orbit design, a milestone in lunar science and engineering.
