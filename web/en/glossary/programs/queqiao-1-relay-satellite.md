# Queqiao-1 Relay Satellite

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A Chinese relay satellite launched in 2018, deployed in a halo orbit around the Earth-Moon L2 point. It was the first relay satellite to use an L2 halo orbit, providing Earth-relay communication for the Chang'e-4 mission — the first-ever soft landing on the lunar far side.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
