# Oracle Program

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A cislunar space situational awareness demonstration program initiated by the US Air Force Research Laboratory, originally named the Cislunar Highway Patrol System. It aims to track spacecraft and debris in the increasingly crowded cislunar space, with current plans to deploy remote sensing imaging satellites in the cislunar region to detect potential debris and non-cooperative spacecraft threats.
