## Definition

This concept holds application value in spacecraft system design and mission analysis; specific application scenarios require further investigation.

## Application Value

Series design enables parts standardization and manufacturing efficiency while accommodating diverse mission requirements, significantly reducing development cycles and costs for on-orbit servicing systems.
