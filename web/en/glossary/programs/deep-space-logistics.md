# Deep Space Logistics

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The capability to transport materials and personnel supporting infrastructure construction and mission execution in cislunar space and beyond; foundational to the future space economy.

## Application Value

深空后勤是当代航天技术发展的重要方向，对于提升我国航天能力具有重要意义 在实际任务中，深空后勤的应用能够支撑多种月球及深空探测任务 深空后勤技术的研究与发展，为末来更远的深空探测任务奠定基础 推进深空后勤相关的技术攻关，有助于提升我国在航天领域的竞争力
