# engineering constraint indicator

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Mandatory engineering constraint parameters in manned lunar exploration missions, including illumination condition constraints and return-landing constraints. These indicators have inviolable rigidity and are incorporated into the comprehensive cost evaluation criterion model alongside performance indicators, with their relative importance in the assessment framework reflected through weighting.

## Application Value

工程约束指标评估任务方案的技术成熟度和可行性，帮助任务决策者在设计早期筛选出不可行的方案。
