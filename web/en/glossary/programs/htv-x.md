# HTV-X

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Next-generation unmanned cargo spacecraft developed by JAXA, an upgraded version of the H-II Transfer Vehicle, planned for resupply missions to the Lunar Gateway and launched by the H3 rocket.

## Application Value

HTV-X is a next-generation cargo spacecraft developed by JAXA, planned to resupply the Lunar Gateway space station. Orbit designers need to accurately calculate transfer trajectories from Earth to NRHO to ensure safe docking of the cargo spacecraft.
