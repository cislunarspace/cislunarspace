# Cislunar Bus Stop

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A space infrastructure concept using periodic orbits in the Earth–Moon system, where spacecraft periodically travel between Earth and Moon, providing relay services for cislunar communication, satellite navigation, and space rescue. Proposed by Xu and Liang (2013).

## Application Value

在周期轨道设计中，该指标用于评估轨道的有界性和长期稳定性，指导轨道维持策略的制定。
