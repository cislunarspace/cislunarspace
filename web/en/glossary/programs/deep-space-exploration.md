# Deep Space Exploration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Exploration activities beyond Earth's gravitational influence into solar system and cosmic space, primarily including lunar exploration, Sun-Earth exploration and interplanetary exploration.

## Application Value

深空探测是当代航天技术发展的重要方向，对于提升我国航天能力具有重要意义 在实际任务中，深空探测的应用能够支撑多种月球及深空探测任务 深空探测技术的研究与发展，为末来更远的深空探测任务奠定基础 推进深空探测相关的技术攻关，有助于提升我国在航天领域的竞争力
