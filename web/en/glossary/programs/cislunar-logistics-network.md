# Cislunar Logistics Network

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transportation infrastructure in cislunar space composed of periodic orbits and transfer vehicles; analogous to railway or subway networks to enable continuous Earth-Moon transportation.

## Application Value

A cislunar logistics network enables continuous Earth-Moon transportation through periodic orbits and transfer vehicles, analogous to railway or subway networks for sustainable space infrastructure.
