# Constellation Construction Roadmap

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A phased construction plan for the near-lunar space constellation. The paper proposes three stages: Stage 1 deploys 2 ELFO satellites for full-time Earth-relay communication to the south polar region; Stage 2 expands to 9 satellites (6ELFO+2NRHO+1Halo) for full-time south polar navigation and whole-Moon communication; Stage 3 reaches 21 satellites (12ELFO+4NRHO+2Halo+3DRO) for 100% quadruple coverage of the entire Moon.
