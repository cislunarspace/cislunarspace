# Phased Deployment

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A strategy for cislunar constellation construction where the constellation is progressively deployed and expanded in multiple phases, typically initially providing single services for key regions and later expanding to a global system, requiring different services for different regions during intermediate phases.

## Application Value

该系统代表了当前地月空间开发的主流方向，对未来月球基地建设和任务规划具有重要参考价值。
