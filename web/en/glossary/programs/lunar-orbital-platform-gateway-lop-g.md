# Lunar Orbital Platform-Gateway, LOP-G

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A NASA-led lunar orbital space station program, later renamed Gateway, as a key component of the Artemis program. The station operates on a 9:2 resonant southern NRHO near the Earth-Moon L2 point. It is designed to support crewed lunar landings, deep-space exploration preparation, and space science research, and serves as the landmark engineering application of NRHO orbits.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
