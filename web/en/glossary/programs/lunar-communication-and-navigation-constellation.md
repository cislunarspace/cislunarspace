# Lunar Communication and Navigation Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A general term for satellite constellations that provide communication relay and navigation/positioning services for lunar exploration missions. Major space agencies have related programs: NASA's LunaNet, ESA's Moonlight/LCNS, JAXA's LNSS, and China's Queqiao series.
