# Propulsive Braking Return

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A return technique in which a vehicle uses its own engine firings to decelerate upon returning to LEO. Unlike atmospheric braking, it avoids aerodynamic heating and ablation but requires additional propellant expenditure. The paper shows that the propulsive braking reusable scheme has a slightly lower life-cycle cost than the atmospheric braking scheme.
