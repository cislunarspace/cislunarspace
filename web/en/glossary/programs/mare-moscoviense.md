# Mare Moscoviense

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A large lunar mare on the far side of the Moon near the northern pole. The paper lists it as one of the mid-high-latitude candidate landing zones. Compared with other mid-high-latitude zones, Mare Moscoviense shows significantly better velocity increment, mission window, and return landing indicators, resulting in a relatively favorable comprehensive assessment ranking among mid-latitude zones.

## Application Value

在航天器系统设计中，需要综合考虑任务需求、技术成熟度和工程约束。该概念对于系统方案选择、技术路线制定和任务规划具有指导作用。
