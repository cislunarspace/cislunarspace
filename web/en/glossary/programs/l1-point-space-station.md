# L1 Point Space Station

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A crewed space facility near the Earth-Moon L1 libration point. L1 lies between Earth and Moon, maintaining a fixed position relative to the Moon in the rotating frame, enabling lunar departure windows at all times. Continuous tracking is achievable via deep space stations and relay satellites.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
