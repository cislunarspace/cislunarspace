# Artemis Program

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

NASA's crewed lunar exploration program initiated in 2017, aiming to establish sustainable human presence on and around the Moon. Its core facility is the Lunar Orbital Platform-Gateway (LOP-G), which operates in a near rectilinear halo orbit. The Artemis program is a direct driver of cislunar station-keeping research; its demand for long-duration NRHO and DRO occupancy has generated extensive studies of continuous-thrust station-keeping strategies.

## Application Value

该系统代表了当前地月空间开发的主流方向，对未来月球基地建设和任务规划具有重要参考价值。
