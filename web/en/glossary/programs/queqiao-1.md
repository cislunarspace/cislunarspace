# Queqiao-1

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The world's first communication relay satellite operating in Earth-Moon L2 halo orbit, launched by China in May 2018. It provided critical Earth-Moon communication relay services for the Chang'E-4 far-side soft landing mission, pioneering human cislunar communication relay capabilities and serving as the foundation for the subsequent Queqiao-2.
