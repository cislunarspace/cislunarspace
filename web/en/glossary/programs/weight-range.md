## Definition

The value range of weight coefficients for each evaluation indicator in comprehensive cost evaluation. Through extensive numerical simulations, the paper determines the specific weight ranges for dynamic weights, providing upper and lower bounds for velocity increment, transfer duration, mission windows, lunar surface stay duration, regional scientific value, and return landing constraint indicators, offering reference for engineering applications.
