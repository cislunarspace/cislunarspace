> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A larger spacecraft acting as a dynamic home base for smaller servicer spacecraft, supporting multiple servicing missions.
