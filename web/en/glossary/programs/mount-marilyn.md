# Mount Marilyn

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar mountain near the lunar equator on the near side, listed in the paper as one of the low-latitude candidate landing zones for manned lunar exploration. The Mount Marilyn zone exhibits the highest number of annual feasible launch windows among all candidate zones, and its equatorial location provides unique orbital accessibility advantages.

## Application Value
