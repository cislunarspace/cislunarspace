# Lunar IceCube

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A NASA planned mission to deliver a 6U CubeSat to low lunar orbit to investigate water transport across the lunar surface, equipped with a BIT-3 Busek ion thruster.

## Application Value

NASA's 6U CubeSat pathfinder mission demonstrating low-lunar-orbit exploration, equipped with a BIT-3 ion thruster for investigating lunar water molecule transport.
