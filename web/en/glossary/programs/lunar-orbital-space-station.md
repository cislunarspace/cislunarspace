# Lunar Orbital Space Station

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A crewed space station deployed on a periodic orbit in near-lunar space, serving as a transit hub for lunar landing missions. Typical deployment orbits include NRHO, L2 Halo, and DRO. Functions include logistics transfer, crew exchange, lunar tracking and communication, and deep-space mission staging. NASA's Gateway is a representative example.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
