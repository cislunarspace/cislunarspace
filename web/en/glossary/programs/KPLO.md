## Definition

Lunar orbiter developed by Korea Aerospace Research Institute that utilizes NASA's Deep Space Network facilities for communications.

## Application Value

Korea Aerospace Research Institute's lunar orbiter using NASA's Deep Space Network for communications, carrying multiple scientific payloads, a pioneering Korean lunar exploration mission.
