## Application Value

Gas-liquid separation is a key technology in propulsion systems, preventing gas from entering the pump inlet and causing cavitation, ensuring reliable propellant delivery. It is a critical design focus for liquid propulsion systems in spacecraft and on-orbit service platforms.
