# Deployment Benefit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A benefit value measuring satellite deployment cost as the energy differential between the target orbit and L1/L2 halo orbits.

## Application Value

Deployment benefit quantifies satellite deployment cost as energy differential between target orbit and L1/L2 halo orbits, guiding orbit selection decisions.
