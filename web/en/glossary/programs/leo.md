# Low Earth Orbit Service Station

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An on-orbit refueling and vehicle turnover facility in low Earth orbit, serving as the origin and destination of airline-flight-mode cislunar round-trip transport. In this paper, it orbits at 350 km altitude with 35° inclination, subject to Earth's central gravity and J2 perturbation, with a 3-day docking duration. Transport vehicles refuel here before departing for the DRO service station, and return here for recovery and reuse.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
