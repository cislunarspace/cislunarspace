# Distant Retrograde Orbit Service Station

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An on-orbit refueling facility built on a lunar distant retrograde orbit (DRO), receiving transport vehicles from LEO service stations for propellant transfer. In this paper, it orbits with a period equal to half the lunar orbital period, with a 2-day docking duration. In the airline-flight-mode cislunar transport architecture, the DRO service station is a key node in the Earth-Moon supply chain, supporting lunar base construction and deep-space missions.

在航天器系统设计中，需要综合考虑任务需求、技术成熟度和工程约束。该概念对于系统方案选择、技术路线制定和任务规划具有指导作用。
