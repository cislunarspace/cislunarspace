# Reusable Earth-Moon Transfer Stage

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A dedicated vehicle departing from a LEO space station to perform trans-lunar and trans-Earth maneuvers between LEO and lunar orbit. Unlike expendable stages, it returns to the station for on-orbit refueling and maintenance to enable multiple uses. Can be configured as one-, two-, or three-stage designs. The paper estimates that for missions exceeding 2 flights, the reusable approach yields lower life-cycle cost.
