# Three-Body Orbit Rendezvous and Docking

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Rendezvous and docking performed on three-body periodic orbits. Unlike LEO rendezvous, the strongly nonlinear dynamics, sensitivity of lunar gravity assist transfers to precision, and high demands on autonomous navigation make this significantly more challenging. As of the paper's publication, no engineering demonstration existed.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
