# ARTEMIS Stationkeeping Strategy

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The stationkeeping strategy adopted by the ARTEMIS mission. It combines the orbit balancing method with a weekly maneuver frequency at or near x-axis crossings. The delta-V budget is about 25 m/s (deterministic and statistical) covering the entire libration orbit phase from insertion to end-of-mission. Orbit continuation conditions span at least 1.5 revolutions to capture lunar eccentricity and solar gravity perturbations. Results show realistic stationkeeping costs of about 15 m/s/year including navigation errors, much lower than earlier estimates of 60 m/s/year.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
