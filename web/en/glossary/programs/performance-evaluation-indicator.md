# performance evaluation indicator

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Performance-related parameters used to assess the comprehensive cost of manned lunar exploration missions, including velocity increment, transfer duration, mission windows, lunar surface stay duration, and regional scientific value. These indicators reflect mission performance and efficiency, and together with engineering constraint indicators form the inputs of the comprehensive cost evaluation criterion.
