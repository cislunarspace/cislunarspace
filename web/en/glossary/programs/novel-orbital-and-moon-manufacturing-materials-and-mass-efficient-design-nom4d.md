# Novel Orbital and Moon Manufacturing, Materials, and Mass Efficient Design, NOM4D

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A DARPA-funded program aimed at enabling space-based systems too large to launch from Earth by developing advanced in-orbit fabrication techniques. Current targets include 100-m RF reflectors and kilometer-class optical apertures, serving as a key enabling technology for breakthrough cislunar SSA capabilities.

## Application Value

NOM4D develops in-orbit fabrication techniques to enable space systems too large for Earth launch, targeting 100-m RF reflectors and kilometer-class optical apertures.
