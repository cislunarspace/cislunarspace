# Starshade Observation Scheduling

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A key optimization problem in exoplanet direct imaging missions where a space telescope works in conjunction with a starshade. The starshade must transfer between different positions around the telescope to block starlight. The fuel cost of each transfer is a subproblem in the outer scheduling optimization; the STT precomputation method enables rapid cost estimation, making the bilevel optimization tractable.

## Application Value

该系统代表了当前地月空间开发的主流方向，对未来月球基地建设和任务规划具有重要参考价值。
