# Lunar Base

A lunar base is a long-term habitation facility established on the lunar surface or in lunar orbit, designed for scientific research, in-situ resource utilization, and serving as a staging point for deep-space exploration. International efforts led by NASA's Artemis program are driving the development of bases near the lunar south pole, laying the groundwork for future Mars missions.
