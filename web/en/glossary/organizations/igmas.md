# international GNSS Monitoring & Assessment Service

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An international GNSS monitoring and assessment system initiated by China, aimed at establishing a global real-time tracking network to monitor and assess GNSS operational status, generating precise ephemeris, clock differences, Earth rotation parameters and other products to support multi-GNSS research and applications.

## Application Value

international GNSS Monitoring & Assessment Service plays an important role in global satellite navigation and space cooperation, providing technical support and services for international space activities.
