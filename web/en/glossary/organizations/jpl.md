# Jet Propulsion Laboratory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A NASA center at the California Institute of Technology, dedicated to deep space mission design and navigation.

## Application Value

Jet Propulsion Laboratory plays an important role in global satellite navigation and space cooperation, providing technical support and services for international space activities.
