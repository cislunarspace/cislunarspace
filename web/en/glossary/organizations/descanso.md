# Deep Space Communications and Navigation Systems Center of Excellence

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A NASA center established at JPL in 1998, dedicated to deep-space communications and navigation technology development.

## Application Value

Deep Space Communications and Navigation Systems Center of Excellence plays an important role in global satellite navigation and space cooperation, providing technical support and services for international space activities.
