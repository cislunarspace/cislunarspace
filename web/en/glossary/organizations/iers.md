# International Earth Rotation and Reference Systems Service

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The international organization responsible for maintaining reference system definitions and providing Earth orientation parameters.

## Application Value

International Earth Rotation and Reference Systems Service plays an important role in global satellite navigation and space cooperation, providing technical support and services for international space activities.
