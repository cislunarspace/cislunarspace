# International Astronomical Union

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The international professional organization responsible for defining celestial reference frames and astronomical constants.

## Application Value

International Astronomical Union plays an important role in global satellite navigation and space cooperation, providing technical support and services for international space activities.
