# Multi-GNSS Experiment

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The global multi-GNSS monitoring experiment initiated by IGS in 2012, aimed at promoting monitoring, assessment and product services for multi-system (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BDS, QZSS, etc.) navigation satellites.

## Application Value

In practical applications, this concept plays an important role in understanding spacecraft motion characteristics and mission design in cislunar space.
