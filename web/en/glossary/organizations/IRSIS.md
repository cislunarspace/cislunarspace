# International Rendezvous System Interoperability Standards

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Standards created by ISS member agencies to facilitate collaborative spacecraft operations in cislunar and deep space environments.

## Application Value

International interoperability standards for rendezvous and docking systems, specifying interfaces and protocols for spacecraft from different countries.
