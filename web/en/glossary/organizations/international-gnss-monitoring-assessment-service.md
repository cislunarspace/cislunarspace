# iGMAS (international GNSS Monitoring & Assessment Service)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An international GNSS monitoring and assessment system initiated by China, establishing a global real-time tracking network to monitor GNSS operational status, generate products such as precise ephemerides, clock offsets, and Earth rotation parameters, and support multi-GNSS research and applications.

## Application Value

The standards and specifications developed by this organization provide guidance for cislunar space activities and form the foundation for industry technical exchange.
