# European Cooperation for Space Standardization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The organization developing European space engineering standards and guidelines, whose document ECSS-E-ST-10-04C specifies space environment modeling standards.

## Application Value

European Cooperation for Space Standardization plays an important role in global satellite navigation and space cooperation, providing technical support and services for international space activities.
