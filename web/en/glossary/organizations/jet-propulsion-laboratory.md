# Jet Propulsion Laboratory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A NASA center at the California Institute of Technology, dedicated to deep space mission design and navigation.


## Application Value

该导航或观测技术在地月空间任务中可用于目标跟踪、相对导航和轨道确定，提升任务自主性和安全性。
