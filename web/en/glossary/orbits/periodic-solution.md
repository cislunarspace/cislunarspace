# Periodic Solution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The bounded solution in the fundamental solution set of linearized relative motion corresponding to the neutral real eigenvalue λ=1 of the monodromy matrix. In nonlinear dynamics, the periodic solution corresponds to co-orbital formation flight on the same DRO with a small phase offset, where the deputy maintains a fixed relative position to the chief without collision risk or exceeding the cooperative distance. It forms the dynamical basis for DRO close natural formation flight.
