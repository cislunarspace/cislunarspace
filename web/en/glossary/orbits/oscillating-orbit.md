# Oscillating Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit that crosses the equilibrium region L infinitely many times. Alpha-oscillating orbits cross L infinitely often as t approaches negative infinity; omega-oscillating orbits cross L infinitely often as t approaches positive infinity. Theorem 1 proves that orbits oscillating in both directions form a G_delta set of relative measure 1 on each disk of transit orbits, meaning almost all transit orbits are eventually oscillating.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
