# DRO Benchmark Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In DRO constellation design, the trajectory of one DRO period is treated as a reference orbit, on which other satellites are deployed at equal phase differences. The number, initial phase angle, z-direction motion amplitude, and period of benchmark orbits collectively determine the overall constellation configuration.
