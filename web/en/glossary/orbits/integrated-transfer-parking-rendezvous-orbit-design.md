# Integrated Transfer-Parking-Rendezvous Orbit Design

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A design method that jointly optimizes the Earth-Moon transfer, circumlunar parking, and rendezvous docking orbits as a unified system for lunar landers launched ahead of crewed spacecraft. It backward-infers parking orbit initial states from rendezvous orbital plane constraints, then constrains transfer orbit perilune states using parking orbit perturbation drift patterns.


## Application Value

This control method can be applied to spacecraft attitude stabilization, orbit keeping, and maneuver trajectory optimization in cislunar space missions, improving mission execution flexibility and reliability.
