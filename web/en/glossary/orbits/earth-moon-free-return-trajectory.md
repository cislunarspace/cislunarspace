# Earth-Moon free return trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer trajectory in the Earth-Moon system with free-return characteristics, where the crewed spacecraft uses lunar gravity assist to return to Earth without requiring additional maneuvers. The paper optimizes such trajectories under a high-fidelity cislunar three-body dynamics model, constituting the core subject of transfer trajectory design for manned lunar exploration.

## Application Value

This concept is used in orbital analysis and has significant impact on mission design.
