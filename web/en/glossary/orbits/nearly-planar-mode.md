# Nearly Planar Mode

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A phase during quasi-periodic orbital motion when the z-amplitude is near zero. In this mode, the orbit lies approximately in the plane connecting the two primary bodies, enabling low-cost transitions from large out-of-plane motion to low-inclination orbits. The ARTEMIS mission exploited the nearly planar mode of large quasi-Halo orbits for libration point orbit transfers and low-inclination lunar orbit insertion.
