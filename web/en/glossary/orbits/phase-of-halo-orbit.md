# Phase of Halo Orbit (τ)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A phase parameter on a halo orbit, dividing the halo orbit into 360 equally-spaced points, used to select different departure/arrival points to increase transfer opportunities. Introducing the halo orbit phase as a second variable can greatly increase transfer opportunities.

## Application Value

The halo orbit family provides long-term stable staging points for cislunar space missions, serving as a transit station to the lunar south pole region and deep space.
