# Powered Lunar Flyby, PLF

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit transfer strategy that reduces transfer cost by performing a propulsive maneuver near lunar closest approach during a flyby. The burn exploits the Oberth effect at high velocity to efficiently change orbital energy. Compared to direct transfers, PLFs can save approximately 300 m/s in total delta-v.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
