# Highly Elliptical Orbit, HEO (HEO)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An elliptical orbit with high eccentricity, low perigee, and high apogee. In the cislunar context, when the orbital period forms an integer ratio with the lunar sidereal period (resonance ratio such as 3:1), the spacecraft periodically passes near L3, L4, and L5 Lagrange points, offering significant scientific and operational value.

## Application Value

该轨道类型可作为地月空间运输网络的中转站或任务的目标轨道。共振轨道可用于设计长期稳定的任务轨道，减少轨道维持代价。该几何表示可用于描述姿态机动中转动惯量的各向异性。
