# central elliptical arc

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The flight arc on the central ellipse inside the pseudostate sphere within the approach phase of an Earth-Moon free-return trajectory. Its velocity is obtained by solving Lambert's problem, serving as a key transition between the main elliptical arc and the pseudostate return arc of the approach phase.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
