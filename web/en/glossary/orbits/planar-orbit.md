# Planar Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A family of periodic orbits near the L4 and L5 libration points, lying entirely in the X-Y plane of the Earth-moon rotating coordinate frame. The paper finds that planar orbits are used less frequently, mainly because observation of planar transfer trajectories is heavily constrained by solar pointing constraints.
