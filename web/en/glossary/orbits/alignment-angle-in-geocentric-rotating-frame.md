# Alignment Angle in Geocentric Rotating Frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle between the spacecraft position vector and the negative x-axis of the geocentric rotating frame at trans-lunar injection, positive counterclockwise. Together with the trans-lunar injection velocity increment, it forms the two control variables for two-impulse trans-lunar trajectory design.
