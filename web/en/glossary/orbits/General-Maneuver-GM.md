> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Arbitrary maneuvers not aligned with manifolds, covering the entire control space.

## Application Value

This maneuver type plays an important role in cislunar transfer and orbit adjustment missions, affecting energy consumption and flight time.
