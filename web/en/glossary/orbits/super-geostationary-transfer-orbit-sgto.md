# Super-Geostationary Transfer Orbit (sGTO)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An elliptic transfer orbit with an apogee far beyond geosynchronous altitude, typically several times the GEO radius. In cislunar missions, sGTO serves as a departure orbit for low-thrust transfers, where its high apogee approaches the boundary of Earth's gravitational dominance, facilitating low-energy Earth-Moon transfers via invariant manifolds.

## Application Value

This orbit type has important applications in cislunar space mission trajectory design, serving as a basis for mission planning and orbit selection.
