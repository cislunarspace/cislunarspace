# High-Elliptical Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An elliptical orbit with high eccentricity (typically above 0.7), whose apogee altitude is much higher than perigee. In GNSS navigation augmentation schemes, the high apogee of HEO satellites provides broader signal coverage, benefiting GDOP improvement and visible satellite counts for cislunar space users.
