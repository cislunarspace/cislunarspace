# Lunar Orbit Insertion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A deceleration maneuver performed when a spacecraft reaches the vicinity of the Moon, allowing it to be captured by lunar gravity from a transfer orbit into a lunar orbit. This is a critical trajectory maneuver in Earth-Moon transfer: insufficient delta-V causes the spacecraft to fly past the Moon without capture, while excessive delta-V wastes propellant. Both the Chang'e-4 relay satellite and lander-rover combination perform LOI at perilune to enter lunar orbits.

## Application Value

Lunar orbit insertion is the critical deceleration maneuver for entering lunar orbit, requiring precise control to ensure successful capture.
