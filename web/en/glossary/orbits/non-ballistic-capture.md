> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

DRO capture mode where the two-body energy is greater than zero; spacecraft remains outside the DRO for a period before entering in a non-ballistic manner.
