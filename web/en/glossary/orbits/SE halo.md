# SE Halo Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A three-dimensional periodic orbit near the Sun–Earth L1 or L2 libration point.

## Application Value

Sun-Earth Halo orbits around Sun-Earth L1 or L2 points, ideal platforms for solar observation and space weather monitoring.
