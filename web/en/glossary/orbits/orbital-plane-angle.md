# Orbital Plane Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter in PRM defined as the incidence angle at the perilune of each revolution of the long elliptical lunar orbit relative to the Y-axis of the Moon-Earth rotating frame. This angle increases linearly with revolutions, inverting approximately 180 degrees after about 13.5 days (half a lunar month). This property allows waiting for the orbital plane to coincide with the target NRHO before insertion.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
