# Halo-to-GEO Transfer, Halo2GEO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer trajectory starting from an L1 halo orbit, performing a manifold insertion followed by an impulse maneuver into a connecting orbit to geosynchronous Earth orbit (GEO). Used in the paper as a representative surveillance target in cislunar space, with a transfer duration of approximately 23 days.

## Application Value

The Halo至GEO转移 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
