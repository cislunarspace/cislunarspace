# L1 Transfer Corridor

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In the circular restricted three-body problem, when the Jacobi constant drops to the critical value C1 at L1, the two zero-velocity surfaces enclosing Earth and Moon touch at L1, opening a narrow passage through which spacecraft can transfer to the Moon. This is a necessary but not sufficient condition; actual transfer requires a connection arc linking the parking orbit to the L1 stable manifold. Due to the narrow opening and long transfer time (potentially hundreds of days), it is unsuitable for crewed missions.

## Application Value

在L1转移走廊的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 在任务规划阶段用于评估不同推进方案的优劣。
