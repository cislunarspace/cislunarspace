# Orbit Plane Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle between the lunar orbital velocity direction and the spacecraft's orbital velocity direction in the Earth-Moon rotating frame. The NRHO perilune velocity direction is defined as 0 degrees. In PRM, after powered lunar swing-by the spacecraft's orbit plane angle is 180 degrees, inverted from the target; it must wait through multiple orbital revolutions for the angle to gradually align to 0 degrees before insertion.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
