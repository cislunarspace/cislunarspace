# Bi-impulsive Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital transfer accomplished through two instantaneous velocity changes (impulses): the first injects the spacecraft into a transfer trajectory, and the second achieves orbit insertion at the target. Under chemical propulsion, the impulsive approximation is valid because thrust durations are negligible compared to total transfer time.

## Application Value

在双脉冲转移的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 在任务规划阶段用于评估不同推进方案的优劣。
