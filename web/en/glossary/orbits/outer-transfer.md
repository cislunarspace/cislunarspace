# Outer Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

One of the inner/outer transfers via LL2. It essentially achieves WSB transfer by patching the stable manifold of LL2 with the invariant manifolds of EL1 (or EL2) Halo orbits. This provides the minimum-energy WSB transfer.

## Application Value

Outer Transfer plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
