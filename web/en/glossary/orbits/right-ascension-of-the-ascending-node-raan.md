# Right Ascension of the Ascending Node, RAAN

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

One of the six classical orbital elements, defined as the angle from the vernal equinox to the orbit's ascending node (where the satellite crosses the equatorial plane northward). It describes the orientation of the orbital plane in space. In SST autonomous orbit determination, RAAN is the key parameter responsible for the overall constellation rotation ambiguity.
