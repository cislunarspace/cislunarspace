# Elliptical Lunar Frozen Orbit, ELFO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A highly stable lunar orbit whose shape varies minimally in the Moon's non-uniform gravity field, requiring little station-keeping for stable positioning. A candidate orbit type for lunar navigation satellite systems, offering reduced maintenance demands and stable positioning references compared to other lunar orbits.

## Application Value

This technique is crucial for cislunar navigation and orbit determination, providing high-precision position and velocity information for spacecraft.
