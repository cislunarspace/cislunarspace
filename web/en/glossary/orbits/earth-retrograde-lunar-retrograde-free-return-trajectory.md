# Earth-Retrograde Lunar-Retrograde Free-Return Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of free-return trajectory where the spacecraft departs Earth in a retrograde direction relative to the geocenter and arrives at the Moon in a retrograde direction relative to the lunar center. Transfer time and velocity increment are similar to the Earth-prograde lunar-retrograde type.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
