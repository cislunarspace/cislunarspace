# Frozen Lunar Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Orbits with constant average eccentricity and argument of periapsis, discovered by analyzing the lunar gravitational potential to find regions where perturbation effects are cancelled or minimized.
