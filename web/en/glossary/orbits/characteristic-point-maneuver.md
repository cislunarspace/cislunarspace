# Characteristic Point Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital maneuver strategy for circumlunar rendezvous that executes burns at key positions along the orbit. A typical scheme involves four burns: raising perilune altitude at perilune, correcting orbital plane deviation at the node, and performing in-plane corrections at apolune and another node. The velocity increment is mainly determined by the transfer orbit altitude.

## Application Value

Applying orbital maneuver impulses at key positions during circumlunar rendezvous (perilune, nodes, apolune) is a fundamental approach in crewed lunar mission orbit design. Reasonable allocation of velocity increments across four maneuvers enables efficient lunar orbit insertion and rendezvous.
