# Transfer Opportunity

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Earth-Moon transfer windows measured by solar phasic angle β intervals; only when the β intervals of the Earth-escaping and Moon-captured segments intersect can a complete transfer trajectory successively orbiting Earth and Moon be achieved.

## Application Value

在设计地月转移方案时，利用该轨道特性可降低任务总速度增量需求。
