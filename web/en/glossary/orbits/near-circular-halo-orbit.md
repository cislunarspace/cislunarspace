# Near-Circular Halo Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An alternative name for near-rectilinear halo orbit. With eccentricity near 1, the motion near apolune is approximately circular, hence the name. Emphasizes the circular characteristics near apolune.
