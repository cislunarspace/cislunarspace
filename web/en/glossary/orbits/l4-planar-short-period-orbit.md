# L4 Planar Short-Period Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A short-period periodic orbit around the Earth-Moon L4 triangular libration point confined within the lunar orbital plane. Its short period and small deviation from the lunar plane make it useful for constructing near-coplanar navigation configurations when paired with a DRO.
