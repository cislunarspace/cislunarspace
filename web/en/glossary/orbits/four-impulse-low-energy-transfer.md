# Four-Impulse Low-Energy Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-energy transfer scheme designed based on the relationship between Jacobi constant changes and velocity increments. Four impulses are applied at: the velocity minimum on the unstable manifold (direction adjustment), the velocity maximum (deceleration to lower energy), and the endpoints of a Lambert arc (connection to the target orbit). Compared to the three-impulse method, this approach fixes maneuver locations at velocity extrema, compressing the search space and achieving lower energy cost (total Delta-v approximately 1.47 km/s).
