# Coplanar Orbital Plane Constraint

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A constraint requiring the departure and return orbital planes of a vehicle to be approximately coplanar with the space station's orbital plane for rendezvous and docking. This constraint limits lunar surface stay time (to avoid out-of-plane return) and increases control complexity. The paper identifies this as one of the principal drawbacks of the space-station-based mission mode.

## Application Value

This constraint limits lunar surface stay time to avoid out-of-plane returns and increases engineering control complexity. It is identified as one of the principal drawbacks of the space-station-based mission mode in cislunar exploration.
