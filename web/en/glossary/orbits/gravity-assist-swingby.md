# Gravity Assist / Swingby

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Maneuvers that use celestial body gravity to alter spacecraft orbit and velocity. In low-energy transfers, lunar gravity assist is a key element in achieving weak stability boundary transfers.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
