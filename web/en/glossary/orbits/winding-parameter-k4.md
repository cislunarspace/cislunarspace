# Winding Parameter k4

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter in the additional term k3 θ cos(k4 θ + φ) of the modified exponential sinusoid that controls the oscillation frequency of the cosine term. Similar to k2, k4 determines the winding density of the added deformation term and can be tuned during optimization to improve trajectory shape.
