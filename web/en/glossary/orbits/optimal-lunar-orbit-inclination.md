# Optimal Lunar Orbit Inclination

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The lunar orbit inclination optimized for crewed landing missions, determined by landing site latitude, lunar surface stay duration, and ascent rendezvous requirements. The optimization minimizes the peak out-of-plane angle between the ascending lander and the orbiting spacecraft for any ascent time during the surface stay. Solved iteratively from a nonlinear system involving landing latitude, stay duration, and lunar rotation rate.

## Application Value

This concept is used in orbital analysis and has significant impact on mission design.
