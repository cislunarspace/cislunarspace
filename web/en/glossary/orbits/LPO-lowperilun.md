# Low-Perilune Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar elliptical orbit with a low perilune altitude, serving as a transport platform between the lunar surface and the working orbit. Due to the irregular lunar gravity field, orbits stabilizable by a single impulse from NRHOs typically have perilune altitudes above 100 km.

## Application Value

Lunar elliptical orbits with low perilune altitude serving as transport staging platforms between the lunar surface and working orbits; due to irregular lunar gravity, single-impulse stabilizable LPOs typically have perilune altitudes above 100 km.
