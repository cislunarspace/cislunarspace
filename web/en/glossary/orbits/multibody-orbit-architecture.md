# Multibody Orbit Architecture

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A satellite constellation orbit scheme designed under a three- or four-body dynamical model. Unlike two-body models, multibody models naturally incorporate gravitational perturbations beyond the primaries (e.g., solar gravity), yielding orbits more robust in high-fidelity models. This paper uses the approach to design two-spacecraft constellations for lunar south pole coverage.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
