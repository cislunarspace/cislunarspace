# Low-Energy Earth-Moon Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-propellant Earth-Moon transfer exploiting invariant manifolds of two coupled three-body systems (Sun-Barycenter and Earth-Moon). Characterized by long transfer times (months) but saves approximately 1 km/s delta-V compared to Hohmann transfer. The transfer cost has a definite functional relationship with the Jacobi constant.
