# Emergency Ascent Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An ascent trajectory to insert the lander into circumlunar orbit when a fault threatening crew safety occurs during descent or lunar surface stay. The design difficulty is that it can be triggered at any fault moment and must jointly consider insertion parameters with subsequent rendezvous, making it a variable-target variable-constraint nonlinear optimization problem.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
