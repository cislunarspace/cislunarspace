# Homoclinic Connection

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory that departs from the unstable manifold of a periodic orbit, traverses the three-body system, and arrives back at the stable manifold of the same orbit.

## Application Value

该轨道类型可作为地月空间运输网络的中转站或任务的目标轨道。利用流形结构可设计低能量转移轨道，减少对推进剂的依赖。该模型是分析地月空间动力学、设计低能量转移轨道的基础工具。
