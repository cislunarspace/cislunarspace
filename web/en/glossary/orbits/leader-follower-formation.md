# Leader-follower Formation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A formation in which one satellite follows another along exactly the same orbital path, trailing behind by a time offset. Achieved by setting satellite 2's position at time t+1 equal to satellite 1's position at time t; effectively the leader satellite is actually following the deputy. This formation requires relatively high in-plane initial position accuracy, and out-of-plane motion is dominated by out-of-plane oscillation.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
