# Collision Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit that terminates at or originates from the surface of a celestial body. In the restricted three-body problem, computation of collision orbits forms the foundation for analyzing lunar landing trajectories by systematically enumerating all ballistic paths to or from the surface.
