# Orthogonal Plane-Crossing Condition

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A periodicity condition in halo orbit design, requiring the orbit to cross the x-z plane orthogonally twice (at t=0 and t=T/2). Orthogonality is ensured by specific velocity component values.
