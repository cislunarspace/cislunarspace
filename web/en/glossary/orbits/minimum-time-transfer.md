# Minimum Time Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory optimization criterion that minimizes mission duration as the objective. Compared to minimum fuel transfer, minimum time transfer has shorter coast time to achieve faster arrival at the target orbit, at the cost of slightly increased propellant consumption. In the paper, minimum time transfer has total flight time of approximately 198 days with final mass of 275 kg, saving about 3.8 days but consuming 0.34 kg more propellant than minimum fuel transfer.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
