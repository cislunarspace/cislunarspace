# Resonant Orbit, RES

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Orbits whose period bears an integer ratio to the Moon's orbital period around Earth.

## Application Value

Resonant Orbit, RES is an important concept in cislunar orbit design, crucial for understanding spacecraft motion in the Earth-Moon space.
