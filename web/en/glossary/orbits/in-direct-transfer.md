# In-Direct Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar transfer method where the spacecraft inserts into the target NRHO directly after powered lunar swing-by at perilune, completing the process within about one day. Short total transfer period (about 7 days), suitable for crewed missions. However, NRHOI requires changing both speed and direction, involving large non-velocity-vector increments and high fuel consumption. Time constraints allow only one trajectory correction maneuver, resulting in lower insertion accuracy.

## Application Value

In low-thrust trajectory optimization, minimum-time trajectories serve as a prerequisite, first determining the minimum flight time, then using it as the terminal time constraint for the fuel-optimal problem. This hierarchical optimization strategy significantly reduces the computational complexity of multi-objective trajectory design.
