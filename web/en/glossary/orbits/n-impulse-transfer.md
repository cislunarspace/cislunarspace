# n-Impulse Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital transfer employing n impulsive thrusts between two terminal orbits. The Hohmann transfer is the two-impulse special case. Edelbaum et al. demonstrated that up to four impulses may be required for absolute minimum fuel expenditure under certain conditions. The optimality conditions are derived by requiring stationarity of characteristic velocity with respect to intermediate orbital parameters.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
