# Orbit Insertion Cost

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The delta-V required for a satellite to transition from launch to its designated orbit, measured as the ratio of remaining mass to initial mass after the maneuver. Defined as C^In = exp(-ΔV / (Isp·g0)), where higher values indicate lower cost. Costs vary significantly by orbit type: DRO is cheapest (0.941), CLO is most expensive (0.809).

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
