# Libration Point Orbit, LPO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Families of periodic orbits emanating from the vicinity of libration points, including Lyapunov, halo, vertical, and axial families.

## Application Value

Orbital mechanics is the foundation of cislunar space mission design. Accurate orbital calculation and analysis capabilities directly determine whether a mission can be successfully implemented.
