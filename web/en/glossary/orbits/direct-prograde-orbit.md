# Direct Prograde Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A family of Moon-centered periodic orbits exhibiting counterclockwise motion about the Moon when viewed in the rotating frame. Unlike DROs, DPOs can serve as transfer mechanisms between L1 and L2 Lyapunov orbits and as lunar parking orbits. The family includes distant and low prograde subfamilies.

## Application Value

Based on its definition, a family of moon-centered periodic orbits exhibiting counterclockwise motion about the moon when viewed in the rotating frame. unlike dros, dpos can serve as transfer mechanisms between l1 and l2 lyap.
