# Lunar Parking Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A near-circular orbit around the Moon used for waiting at the transfer endpoint or executing subsequent missions. This paper selects a 100 km altitude orbit as the lunar parking orbit; the large-amplitude Lyapunov orbit tangentially contacts it on the x-axis, and the spacecraft enters the parking orbit via a tangential deceleration impulse at the tangent point.

## Application Value

This concept is used in orbital analysis and has significant impact on mission design.
