# Ascending Node Longitude Constraint

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The longitude of the ascending node of a lunar orbit in the lunar-fixed frame, determined by the orbital inclination and landing site coordinates. Different formulas apply for ascending-segment and descending-segment landings. This constraint, along with altitude and inclination, ensures the lunar orbit covers the target landing site.
