# Highly Elliptical Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit with high eccentricity. In this paper: apoapsis ~38,760 km, periapsis ~1,590 km, inclination 63.58 deg. Deploying optical sensors on HEO positions them away from the ecliptic plane, potentially helping avoid solar and lunar exclusion angle geometry.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
