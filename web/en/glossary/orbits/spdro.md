# Stable Periodic Distant Retrograde Orbit, SPDRO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A stable periodic orbit residing at the core of the distant retrograde region in the circular restricted three-body problem. It is symmetric about the x-axis of the rotating frame and is uniquely specified by its maximum amplitude along the x-axis (SPDRO size). Quasi-periodic distant retrograde orbits envelope the SPDRO at most energies.

## Application Value

The 稳定周期远距离逆行轨道 concept provides technical support and analytical methods for cislunar space research and lunar exploration missions.
