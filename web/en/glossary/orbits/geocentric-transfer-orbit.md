# Geocentric Transfer Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The Earth parking orbit entered after launch by Ariane-5, with GTO parameters: semiparameter 24661 km, eccentricity 0.716, inclination 7°, argument of perigee 178°. This is the departure orbit for the SMART-1 mission, where the spacecraft begins low-thrust spiral acceleration.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
