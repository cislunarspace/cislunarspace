# Perilune Rendezvous Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An Earth-to-Moon transfer method intermediate between direct transfer and WSB transfer. After powered lunar swing-by, the spacecraft enters an elongated elliptical lunar orbit, waits for the orbital plane to align with the target NRHO, then applies a small velocity increment at perilune for insertion. Transfer period is about 18 days with total delta-V roughly 40-50 m/s less than direct transfer. Since PRM insertion requires only velocity-aligned thrust, it is highly efficient; the two-week orbital waiting period absorbs most trajectory errors, yielding higher insertion accuracy than direct transfer.

## Application Value

This term在cislunar space missions中has important application value. In orbit design, it can be used foroptimizing transfer trajectories, reducing mission fuel consumption. In attitude control and dynamics analysis, it helps understandthe motion characteristics of spacecraft in complex gravitational fields, providing theoretical support for mission planning. In navigation and orbit determination, methods based on this termcan improve orbit prediction accuracy, supporting the development of autonomous navigation algorithms.
