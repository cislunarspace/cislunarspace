# Orbit Family

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A continuous set of periodic orbits of the same type parameterized by a quantity such as period or energy. In the Earth-Moon DRO family, larger orbits have longer periods and higher energies. The orbit family concept enables mission designers to select optimal target orbits by comparing transfer costs across the family.

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
