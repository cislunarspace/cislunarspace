# Lunar Flyby Gravity Assist

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital correction technique where a spacecraft near perilune uses the Moon's gravity to change both the magnitude and direction of its velocity vector. In Earth-to-halo transfer design, the lunar flyby can alter the transfer trajectory plane, enabling insertion onto the target orbit's stable manifold without additional maneuvers.

## Application Value

These coefficients form an important tool in lunar theory, essential for high-precision orbit calculations in lunar missions.
