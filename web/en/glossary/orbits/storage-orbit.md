# Storage Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A periodic orbit selected for long-term parking of space-based infrastructure such as propellant depots or habitats. Selection criteria include low annual station-keeping delta-V, accessibility from Earth or libration point orbits, and period/geometry that do not severely constrain launch windows. DROs are typical candidates due to their low maintenance cost and favorable geometry.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
