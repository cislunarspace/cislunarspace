# Terminal Coast

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Coasting phase on the initial or final orbit in impulsive transfer. Adjusting initial/final impulse timing can reduce propellant consumption.

## Application Value

Terminal coast on initial or final orbits in impulsive transfer enables timing optimization; adjusting impulse timing can reduce total propellant consumption.
