# DRO Phase Factor

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of the spacecraft's moment on the DRO to the orbital period. Zero phase is defined as the instant when the spacecraft is at the right vertical crossing point on the DRO.
