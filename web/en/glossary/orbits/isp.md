# Interplanetary Superhighway, ISP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A network of low-energy trajectories utilizing invariant manifolds around Lagrange points throughout the solar system, connecting planets with minimal propulsion requirements.

## Application Value

Interplanetary Superhighway, ISP is a key element in cislunar space orbit design, with significant value for planning Earth-Moon transfer trajectories and designing formation flying scenarios. It is widely applied in lunar exploration missions and small body explorations.
