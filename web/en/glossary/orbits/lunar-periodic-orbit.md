# Lunar Periodic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A closed orbit encircling the Moon in the planar CR3BP. Like Lyapunov orbits about L1, these orbits can be analyzed together with invariant manifolds in the same phase space under the cylindrical isomorphic mapping. By computing the intersection between the manifold and a lunar periodic orbit in (x, y, γ) space, transfer trajectories requiring only modest velocity increments can be identified.
