# Secularly Precessing Elliptical Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit model retaining only secular perturbation effects after removing short-period variations. The semi-major axis and eccentricity are approximately constant while the longitude of ascending node and argument of pericenter precess slowly. Used in the paper to model the real lunar orbit dynamics.
