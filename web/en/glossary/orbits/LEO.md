# Low Earth Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Earth orbit at approximately 200 to 2000 km altitude, used in this paper as one of the application scenarios for the uncertain Lambert problem.

## Application Value

Low Earth orbit is the most active region for human space activities, where most satellites and space stations operate, and the starting point for cislunar transfers.
