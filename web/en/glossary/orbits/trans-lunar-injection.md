# Trans-Lunar Injection

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The first impulsive maneuver in a direct lunar halo transfer, performed from a LEO parking orbit to inject the spacecraft onto an Earth-Moon transfer trajectory. Executed near the Earth where the spacecraft travels fastest, this maneuver provides the majority of the velocity change (approximately 3100-3200 m/s) and is the most energy-efficient portion of the transfer.

## Application Value

地月转移入射是月球探测任务的起点，其性能直接影响任务有效载荷能力和燃料消耗。在任务设计时，TLI的时机和大小需与轨道设计紧密耦合，以充分利用地球自转速度和轨道力学特性。阿波罗任务中，TLI在近地点实施，典型速度增量约为3.0 km/s。现代任务可结合月球借力转移等策略，降低对运载火箭能力的依赖。
