# Closed Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit where the spacecraft returns to its initial position and velocity after one revolution. Unlike a strictly periodic orbit, in the presence of time-dependent perturbations the external environment (e.g., the Sun's position) may not return to its initial state. The paper constructs such orbits using the symmetry of the dynamics equations.
