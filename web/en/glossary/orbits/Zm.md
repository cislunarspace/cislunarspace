# z-direction Motion Amplitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The maximum value of the DRO's position component in the z-direction (perpendicular to the Earth-Moon orbital plane). A larger amplitude yields higher visibility elevation angles for lunar polar regions and stronger constellation coverage of high-latitude areas.

## Application Value

This concept is significant for space-based optical observation and space situational awareness, used for evaluating spacecraft visibility to targets.
