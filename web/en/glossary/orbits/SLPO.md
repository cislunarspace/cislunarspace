# Special Long-Period Orbit, SLPO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A three-body dynamics orbit based on invariant manifold stitching, formed by connecting two L1 Halo orbits with their low-energy transfer trajectories, with a period of approximately 79 days, covering the entire cislunar space.

## Application Value

Special long-period orbit types for specific scientific mission requirements, an extension of libration point orbit research.
