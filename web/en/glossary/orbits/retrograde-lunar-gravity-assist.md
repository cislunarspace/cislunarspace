# Retrograde Lunar Gravity Assist

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Type of lunar gravity assist with lower perilune altitude and longer translunar time (3.6-16.3 days), with spacecraft orbiting the Moon in a retrograde direction.

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
