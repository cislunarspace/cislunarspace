# P2HO Orbit Family

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Orbit families that evolve from period-doubling bifurcations in the NRHO region of the L₂ halo family. Members have orbital periods approximately double that of the parent NRHO. Two sub-families exist: P2HO₁ (butterfly family), featuring figure-eight shapes spanning both sides of the Moon, and P2HO₂, featuring east-west motion patterns. Both sub-families contain synodic resonant members useful for transfer design.
