# Circular Lunar Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A circular orbit around the Moon with zero eccentricity. The paper analyzes 12-hour period CLOs: a 0° inclination CLO provides the highest single coverage for the equator (~0.356) and zero for the poles; a 90° inclination CLO reverses this pattern. Orbit insertion requires ~645 m/s delta-V with negligible maintenance cost.

## Application Value

Based on its definition, a circular orbit around the moon with zero eccentricity. the paper analyzes 12-hour period clos: a 0° inclination clo provides the highest single coverage for the equator (~0.356) and zero for the pol.
