# Perilune Rendezvous Method (PRM)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The foundational method of EPRM. After TLI and PLSB, the spacecraft enters an elliptical lunar orbit and waits five orbital periods for the orbital plane angle to flip from 180 degrees back to the NRHO direction of 0 degrees. It then inserts into NRHO at perilune with a velocity increment of approximately 50 m/s. Near perilune, NRHO is influenced by the Earth-Moon gravitational balance, where small velocity increments can significantly alter the orbit; PRM exploits this characteristic for efficient orbit insertion. Total delta-V is approximately 322 m/s with a transfer time of approximately 20 days.

## Application Value

Rendezvous and docking is a key technology for manned spaceflight and space station operations, and reliable docking mechanisms form the foundation of successful missions.
