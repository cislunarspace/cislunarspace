# EM Halo Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A three-dimensional periodic orbit near the Earth–Moon L1 or L2 libration point, characterized by a large amplitude in the z-direction.

## Application Value

Earth-Moon Halo orbits are a type of cislunar libration point orbit, candidate orbits for lunar missions and popular solutions for space station deployment.
