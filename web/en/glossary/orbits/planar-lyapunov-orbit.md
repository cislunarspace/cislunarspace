# Planar Lyapunov Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Planar periodic orbits around the collinear libration points (L1, L2, L3), confined to the xy-plane with zero z-component. They originate from the periodic solution branches of the linearized dynamics near collinear points. Small-amplitude orbits approximate ellipses; larger amplitudes exhibit nonlinear shaping. When Lyapunov orbits about L1 and L2 share the same Jacobi constant, their stable and unstable manifolds may form heteroclinic connections enabling zero- or near-zero-fuel transfers.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
