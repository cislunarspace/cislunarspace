# L3 Halo Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Halo orbits about the third collinear Lagrangian point (L3) of the Earth-Moon system. L3 lies on the opposite side of Earth from the Moon. These orbits are unstable and require active station-keeping, but they provide line-of-sight relay between Earth and Moon. Orbits with perigee below Earth's surface are infeasible; usable orbits reach apogee heights near 740,000 km with periods near 27 days.

## Application Value

L3 halo orbits provide line-of-sight relay between Earth and Moon with approximately 740,000 km apogee and 27-day period, though they require active station-keeping.
