# Nontransit Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit in the equilibrium region satisfying alpha1*alpha2 > 0, meaning it is pushed away from the periodic orbit toward the same side in both time directions. In Conley's diagram, nontransit orbits occupy six of the eight components of region R. These orbits either approach and depart from the left primary or the right primary, but cannot cross the equilibrium region.

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
