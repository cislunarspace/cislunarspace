# Aposelene

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The point on a lunar orbit farthest from the Moon, analogous to apogee for Earth satellites. On NRHO, the paper found minimum relative motion errors near aposelene, making it the optimal region for executing rendezvous.

## Application Value

在远月点的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 用于评估导航系统的精度上限，指导滤波器设计。
