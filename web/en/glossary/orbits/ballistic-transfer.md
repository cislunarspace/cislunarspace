# Ballistic Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer method where the spacecraft applies no thrust during the transfer phase, relying entirely on gravitational forces and initial conditions. Applicable to low-energy transfer scenarios such as WSB transfers.
