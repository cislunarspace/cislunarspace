# Low Moon Orbit, LMO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A satellite in low lunar orbit, specified in this paper as a 100km circular polar orbit. In SST orbit determination, it forms a hybrid constellation with Halo orbiters, utilizing the force model differences to break systematic correlation.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
