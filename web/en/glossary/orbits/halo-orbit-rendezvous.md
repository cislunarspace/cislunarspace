# Halo Orbit Rendezvous

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The technology for two spacecraft to meet at a libration point Halo or near-Halo orbit. Using effective phase methods and phase impulse maneuvers can reduce rendezvous costs.

## Application Value

This concept is used in orbital analysis and has significant impact on mission design.
