# Deployment Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory arc propagated forward from the deployment position, consisting of an initial coast period, thrust segment, and second coast period.

## Application Value

A deployment trajectory arc consisting of initial coast, thrust, and second coast phases, bridging satellite launch to normal operation.
