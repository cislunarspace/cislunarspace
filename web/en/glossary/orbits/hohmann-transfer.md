# Hohmann Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Fuel-optimal two-impulse transfer orbit between two circular orbits. Proposed by Hohmann in 1925, the transfer orbit is coplanar and tangent to both circular orbits. A classic proof of two-impulse optimality.

## Application Value

Hohmann Transfer is the fuel-optimal two-impulse transfer between two circular orbits. Since its proposal in 1925, it has become a classic method for orbital transfer, widely used by spacecraft orbit designers for transfers between low Earth orbit and target orbits.
