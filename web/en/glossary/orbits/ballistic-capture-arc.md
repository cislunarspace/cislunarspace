# Ballistic Capture Arc

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A thrust-free arc that naturally guides a spacecraft from outside a moon toward and around it using the L2 stable manifold. The spacecraft drifts along the stable manifold, gradually reducing its relative velocity until it forms multiple closed orbits around the moon. In the paper, capture arcs last from several days to nearly one month depending on the manifold perturbation parameter.
