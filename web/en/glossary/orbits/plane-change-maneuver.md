# Plane Change Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A maneuver that changes the orientation of a spacecraft's orbital plane, typically costly in velocity increment. In this paper, the first maneuver at the ecliptic crossing nullifies the z-velocity component to align the trajectory with the ecliptic plane, enabling subsequent in-plane transfer design.
