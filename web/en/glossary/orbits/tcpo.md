# Touring Cislunar Periodic Orbit, TCPO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A class of periodic orbits traversing wide spatial expanses of the Earth-Moon system, capable of periodically visiting the Earth, Moon, and multiple Lagrange points; suitable for logistics missions requiring coverage of large cislunar volumes.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar orbit design and transfer analysis, useful for studying orbital stability, designing transfer schemes, and evaluating mission performance.
