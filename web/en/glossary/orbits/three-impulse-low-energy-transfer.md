# Three-Impulse Low-Energy Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-energy transfer scheme based on disturbed manifold and Lambert arc patching. Three impulses are applied: one to enter the disturbed manifold, and two at the endpoints of the Lambert arc. It supports both energy-optimal and energy-time-optimal objectives. The energy-optimal solution requires approximately 1.55 km/s total Delta-v over about 40 days; the energy-time-optimal solution requires approximately 1.68 km/s over about 17 days.
