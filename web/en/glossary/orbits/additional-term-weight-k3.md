# Additional Term Weight k3

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The weight coefficient of the additional term in the modified exponential sinusoid. The added term k3 θ cos(k4 θ + φ) grows linearly with angular travel, deforming the spiral along the line joining the two primaries. k3 = 0 reduces to the classical exponential sinusoid; larger k3 produces greater deformation.

## Application Value

附加项权重k3是地月空间轨道设计中的重要参考轨型，可用于目标轨道选择和转移方案设计 在实际任务中，基于附加项权重k3的轨道设计能够满足特定的任务约束，如通信仰角、覆盖范围和燃料最优等要求 利用附加项权重k3的特性，可以优化转移轨道设计，实现从地球到月球的高效转移 附加项权重k3为月球探测任务提供了多样化的轨道选择方案，有助于任务的灵活规划
