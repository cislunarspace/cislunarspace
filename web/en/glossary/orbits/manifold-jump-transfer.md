# Manifold-Jump Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer strategy in which a small maneuver shifts a spacecraft from the vicinity of one invariant manifold to another, combining multiple manifold arcs into a trajectory that meets mission constraints. Since natural manifolds have fixed target energies and topologies, practical missions often cannot follow a single manifold to the goal and must jump between neighboring manifolds. ARTEMIS trajectory corrections exploited this mechanism.
