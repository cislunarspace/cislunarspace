# Out-of-plane (Z) Amplitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The amplitude of the satellite orbit component perpendicular to the lunar orbital plane (the normal direction). Larger out-of-plane amplitude leads to more pronounced motion in the N direction, improving autonomous orbit determination accuracy in that direction. Adjusting the out-of-plane amplitude effectively improves short-arc orbit determination precision.


## Application Value

This orbital design method can be applied to transfer trajectory design, orbit keeping, and mission planning in cislunar space missions, improving spacecraft performance and mission flexibility.
