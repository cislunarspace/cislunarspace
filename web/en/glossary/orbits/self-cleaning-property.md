# Self-cleaning Property

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The natural ability of an orbit to clear debris. NRHO and large Halo orbits can naturally clear most debris from the Earth-Moon system within 1-10 years (through lunar impact or escape), while DRO's linear stability means debris clearance takes significantly longer, posing more lasting threats to cislunar sustainability.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
