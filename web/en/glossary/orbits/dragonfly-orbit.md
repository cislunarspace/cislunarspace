# Dragonfly Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A special family of periodic orbits in the Earth-moon three-body system, named for their dragonfly-like shape. These orbits have large periods and amplitude ranges near the L2 point, and in the paper's clustering analysis are often combined with other orbital families to form efficient SDA architectures.

## Application Value

在轨道设计和转移轨道优化中，该方法用于确定最优转移时机和轨道形状，以最小化燃料消耗或飞行时间. 通过数值仿真和迭代优化，可获得满足任务约束的可行轨道方案.
