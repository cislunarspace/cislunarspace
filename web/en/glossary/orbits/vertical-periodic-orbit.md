# Vertical Periodic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A special case of Lissajous orbit with motion only in the z-direction; its projection on the x-y plane is a point.
