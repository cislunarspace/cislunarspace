# Captured Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit entering a bound state after gravitational capture by a primary, with negative Kepler energy. The WSB method can achieve nearly fuel-free ballistic capture.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
