# Osculating Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ideal Keplerian orbit defined at each instant by the spacecraft's current position and velocity vectors, osculating (kissing) the actual perturbed orbit at that point.
