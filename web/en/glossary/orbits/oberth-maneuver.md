# Oberth Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Propulsion burn performed at high speed near periapsis to maximize the effective velocity change from propellant.

## Application Value

Oberth Maneuver plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
