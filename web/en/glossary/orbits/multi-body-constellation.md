# Multi-body Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A satellite constellation with spacecraft distributed across different orbital locations such as L1 and L2 halo orbits to achieve global or polar coverage.

## Application Value

A constellation with satellites distributed across different orbital locations such as L1 and L2 halo orbits, achieving global or polar coverage, a candidate architecture for cislunar space infrastructure.
