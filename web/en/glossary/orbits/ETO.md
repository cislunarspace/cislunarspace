# Ellipse Transition Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Elliptical orbit segments near the Moon in the three-impulse maneuver scheme. A pulse at the perilune of the free-return trajectory enters the first transition orbit (ETO1); after orbital plane adjustment, the spacecraft enters the second transition orbit (ETO2), and finally enters the target lunar orbit at the new perilune. The semi-major axis and eccentricity of the transition orbit are determined by the orbital period ratio parameter and the perilune altitude.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
