# LGA+WSB Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-energy transfer combining Lunar Gravity Assist (LGA) with Weak Stability Boundary (WSB). The spacecraft first flybys the Moon via a lunar transfer orbit, raising the apogee to the WSB region, then uses solar gravity to enter the target orbit. Compared to direct WSB transfer, it reduces the launch delta-v by 60 to 70 m/s.

## Application Value

A low-energy transfer combining Lunar Gravity Assist with Weak Stability Boundary that reduces launch delta-v by 60-70 m/s compared to direct WSB transfer.
