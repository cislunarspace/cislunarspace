# Phase Angle of Perigee

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In the Earth-Moon rotating frame, the phase angle of the perigee position relative to the Earth's velocity direction. Along with the phase angle of perilune, this angle determines the spatial configuration of a transfer trajectory and is a key parameter in Poincaré-mapping-based classification.
