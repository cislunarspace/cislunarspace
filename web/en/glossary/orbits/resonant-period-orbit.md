# Resonant Period Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit whose period forms an integer multiple of a reference orbit's period. In resonant constellation design, all orbits with periods that are integer multiples of the baseline period are screened from the orbit library to form a resonant orbit set.

## Application Value

该轨道类型可作为地月空间运输网络的中转站或任务的目标轨道。共振轨道可用于设计长期稳定的任务轨道，减少轨道维持代价。
