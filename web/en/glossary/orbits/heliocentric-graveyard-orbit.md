# Heliocentric Graveyard Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An end-of-life disposal method for libration point missions: maneuvering the spacecraft into a heliocentric orbit far from Earth, ensuring it will not return to Earth's vicinity for hundreds of years, thereby eliminating threats to Earth-orbiting satellites and ground populations. ESA's Herschel spacecraft completed disposal this way in May 2013, with designed results showing no return for at least 300 years.
