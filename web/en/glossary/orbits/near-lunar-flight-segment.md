# Near-Lunar Flight Segment

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The segment of the Earth-Moon transfer orbit where the spacecraft approaches and arrives in the vicinity of the Moon, specifically the flight phase within the Moon's sphere of influence. This segment carries the densest set of constraints in transfer orbit design: near-lunar-point altitude, solar incidence angle, lunar surface photography visibility, and continuous ground station observation of the near-lunar point must all be satisfied simultaneously. The paper analyzes TT&C and illumination constraints in this segment to retroactively constrain the entire transfer orbit design.

## Application Value

These coefficients form an important tool in lunar theory, essential for high-precision orbit calculations in lunar missions.
