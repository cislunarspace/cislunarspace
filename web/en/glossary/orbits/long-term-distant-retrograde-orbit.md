# Long Term Distant Retrograde Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar DRO that remains bounded under Sun's gravitational perturbation over a predefined mission lifetime. The orbit is quasi-periodic rather than strictly periodic, and whether it stays bounded depends on the initial solar phase angle (launch window).
