# Molniya Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A highly eccentric, critically inclined (about 63.4 deg) Earth orbit with perigee of a few hundred km and apogee near 40,000 km. With argument of periapsis at 270 deg, the satellite dwells over high northern latitudes during its slow apogee phase. First used by the Soviet Union in the 1960s for communications and military reconnaissance. The 63.4 deg inclination nullifies apsidal precession.

## Application Value

在轨道设计和转移轨道优化中，该方法用于确定最优转移时机和轨道形状，以最小化燃料消耗或飞行时间. 通过数值仿真和迭代优化，可获得满足任务约束的可行轨道方案.
