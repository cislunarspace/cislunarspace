# Axial Resonant Orbit, ARO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of three-dimensional resonant orbit symmetric about the x-axis, analogous to axial orbits in libration point orbits, generated from planar resonant orbits through bifurcation theory.

## Application Value

Axial Resonant Orbit, ARO is an important concept in cislunar orbit design, crucial for understanding spacecraft motion in the Earth-Moon space.
