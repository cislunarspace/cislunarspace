# Cislunar Resonant Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Cislunar orbits whose period forms a simple integer ratio with the lunar orbital period, such as 2:1 and 3:1 resonant orbits. These orbits offer repeating ground tracks and wide coverage, and can complement libration point periodic orbits, making them preferred orbit families for cislunar SSA sensor deployment.
