# Vertical Orbit at Triangular Libration Point

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Non-planar periodic orbits near the L4 or L5 triangular libration points, primarily extending in the direction perpendicular to the ecliptic plane. These orbits feature prominently in the optimal resonant constellations identified in this study.

## Application Value

The 三角平动点垂直轨道 concept provides technical support and analytical methods for cislunar space research and lunar exploration missions.
