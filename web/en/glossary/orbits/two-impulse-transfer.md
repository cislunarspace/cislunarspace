# Two-Impulse Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer trajectory design requiring only two impulses. The first impulse injects the spacecraft from a parking orbit into the transfer trajectory, and the second impulse inserts it into the target orbit. Unlike three-segment manifold transfers requiring a bridge impulse.
