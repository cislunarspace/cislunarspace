# Exterior Capture Low-Energy Transfer Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-energy transfer orbit in which the spacecraft enters the Earth-Moon system from outside and is captured by the Moon, corresponding to Belbruno's weak stability boundary theory. It exploits the patching of the L2 stable flow tube with the Sun-Earth unstable flow tube at Poincaré sections, requiring solar gravity assist. Its drawback is long transfer time due to the spacecraft being pulled far from the Earth-Moon system by solar gravity.

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
