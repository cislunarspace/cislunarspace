# Near-Lunar Space Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A constellation of multiple satellites deployed in cislunar orbits (lunar orbits and libration point orbits) that provides Earth-relay communication and navigation/positioning services for lunar surface probes and crew. The paper proposes a phased construction scheme culminating in a 21-satellite configuration (12 ELFO + 4 NRHO + 2 Halo + 3 DRO) achieving 100% quadruple coverage of the entire lunar surface.
