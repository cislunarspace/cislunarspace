# Triangular Libration Point Formation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Spacecraft formation configurations designed near the triangular (L4/L5) libration points of the Sun-Earth or Earth-Moon system. Unlike collinear points, triangular points support stable quasi-periodic orbit families suitable for long-duration formation missions; despite no spacecraft having yet reached Earth-Moon triangular points due to high transfer cost, they offer potential for deep-space observation, radiation monitoring, and communications relay.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
