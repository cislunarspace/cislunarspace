# Near-planar DRO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A Distant Retrograde Orbit with small-amplitude motion in the Z-axis direction. Near-planar DROs are found to be more stable than fully three-dimensional DROs.

## Application Value

Near-planar DRO plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
