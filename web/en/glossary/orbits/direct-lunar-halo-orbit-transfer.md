# Direct Lunar Halo Orbit Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A two-burn transfer trajectory from Earth parking orbit directly to a lunar halo orbit. The first burn (trans-lunar injection) is performed from a LEO parking orbit; the second burn (manifold insertion) is a tangential maneuver that injects the spacecraft onto the stable manifold of the target halo orbit, causing it to asymptotically approach the halo orbit.
