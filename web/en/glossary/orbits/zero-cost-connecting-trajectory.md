# Zero-Cost Connecting Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A natural transfer trajectory connecting libration point orbits of two different restricted three-body problems in a realistic ephemeris model, requiring no impulsive maneuvers. Obtained by iteratively eliminating the velocity mismatch at the coupling point via modified multiple shooting.

## Application Value

在零代价连接轨道的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 为航天器的精确控制提供理论依据，确保任务执行的可靠性。
