# Coplanar Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Two or more orbits lying in the same plane. Under coplanar conditions, the transfer problem reduces to two dimensions and impulses need only modify in-plane velocity components, greatly simplifying analysis. The Hohmann transfer and the optimal inter-elliptical transfers discussed by Lawden both assume coplanar orbits.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
