# Horizontal Lyapunov Orbit, HLO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A family of unstable periodic orbits centered at L1 or L2 in the PCRTBP, which can be continued toward the second primary.

## Application Value

Horizontal Lyapunov orbits near L1 or L2 points, important members of the libration point orbit family.
