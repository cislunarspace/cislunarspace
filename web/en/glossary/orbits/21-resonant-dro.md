# 2:1 Resonant DRO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A Distant Retrograde Orbit whose period is half the lunar synodic period, approximately 14.77 days. A new mission opportunity arises twice every synodic month, advantageous for accommodating launch delays. The orbit has a perilune distance of approximately 70,000 km and apolune of 90,000 km, with Earth-Moon-Sun geometry repeating every half month, supporting lunar surface missions requiring specific lighting conditions.

## Application Value

This orbit type is widely used in Earth-Moon transfer trajectory design to optimize transfer trajectories and reduce delta-V consumption. When designing cislunar space missions, engineers can use this concept to evaluate performance differences between various orbits and select the optimal scheme satisfying mission constraints. It can also be used to analyze the feasibility of orbital maneuver plans, supporting mission planning and decision-making.
