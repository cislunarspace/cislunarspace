# In-Direct Transfer, IDT

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A fast but ΔV-expensive transfer method used by crewed vehicles (e.g., Orion) to reach NRHO within 7 days. After TLI and PLSB, the spacecraft spends only about one day in an elliptical lunar orbit before performing NRHOI. Because the velocity direction at NRHOI significantly deviates from the target direction, the velocity increment carries a large non-velocity-direction component, resulting in 390 m/s total ΔV with poor fuel efficiency. The one-day gap between PLSB and NRHOI leaves a very short window for trajectory correction.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
