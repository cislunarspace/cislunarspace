# EM Halo Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A three-dimensional periodic orbit near the Earth–Moon L1 or L2 libration point, characterized by a large amplitude in the z-direction.

## Application Value

EM Halo Orbit is an important concept in cislunar orbit design, crucial for understanding spacecraft motion in the Earth-Moon space.
