# Flyby Perigee

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The closest approach point of a spacecraft to a body during a gravity assist flyby. In this paper, the second impulsive maneuver targets a flyby perigee altitude between 800 and 5000 km, which directly affects the subsequent orbital energy and state at the x-axis crossing.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in orbital design and control, and plays a vital role in mission success.
