# Trans-Lunar Injection

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The maneuver of applying a velocity increment from Earth parking orbit to inject the spacecraft into a lunar transfer orbit. TLI is typically performed at the perigee of the parking orbit and is the first critical maneuver in a lunar transfer mission.

## Application Value

Trans-Lunar Injection is an important orbit type in cislunar trajectory design, widely applied in mission planning.
