# Forward-departure LGA Trajectory
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

LGA trajectory type where the spacecraft's position relative to the Moon at the assist moment is in the first or fourth quadrant, with retrograde velocity direction at perilune.

## Application Value

The Forward-departure LGA Trajectory plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
