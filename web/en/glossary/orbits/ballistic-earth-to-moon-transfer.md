# Ballistic Earth-to-Moon Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An Earth-to-Moon transfer that relies solely on initial conditions and the natural gravitational environment without intermediate maneuvers. The ARTEMIS mission exploited the Sun-Earth-Moon multi-body dynamical environment, using lunar gravity assists to ballistically transfer from the Sun-Earth system to Earth-Moon libration point orbits. This transfer approach determined the orbit insertion conditions and initial orbital configuration.

## Application Value

Based on its definition, an earth-to-moon transfer that relies solely on initial conditions and the natural gravitational environment without intermediate maneuvers. the artemis mission exploited the sun-earth-moon multi-body.
