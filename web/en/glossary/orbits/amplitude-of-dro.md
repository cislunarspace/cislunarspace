# Amplitude of DRO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The distance from the Moon when the DRO crosses the x-axis along the negative direction of the y-axis, defining the size of the DRO.
