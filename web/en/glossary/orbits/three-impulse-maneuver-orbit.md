# Three-Impulse Maneuver Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer scheme that begins orbit maneuvers at the perilune of a free-return trajectory, using three impulses to reach a high-inclination lunar orbit. The first impulse enters an ellipse transition orbit at perilune; the second changes the orbital plane and initiates an in-plane transfer; the third circularizes the orbit at the new perilune to enter the target lunar orbit. The spacecraft can return to Earth unpowered throughout the process, providing high safety. Compared with hybrid orbit schemes, the three-impulse maneuver is more advantageous in velocity increment consumption.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
