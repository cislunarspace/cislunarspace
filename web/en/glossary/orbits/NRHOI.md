# Near-Rectilinear Halo Orbit Insertion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The maneuver of applying a velocity increment at the NRHO perilune to insert the spacecraft into the target orbit. In IDT, NRHOI changes both velocity direction and magnitude, involving large non-velocity-vector components and high fuel consumption; in PRM, NRHOI is velocity-aligned and more efficient.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
