# Fast Transfer Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer trajectory achieving rapid DRO insertion using the invariant manifold of LL1 Lyapunov orbit. The spacecraft accelerates twice into Hohmann transfer, then into large elliptical orbit (Lyapunov stable manifold), finally a third acceleration into DRO. Shorter transfer time but higher fuel consumption.
