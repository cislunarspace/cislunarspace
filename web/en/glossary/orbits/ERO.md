# Earth Return Orbit, ERO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

When HLOs are continued to a close neighborhood of the Earth, they are categorized as EROs since they periodically approach and leave the vicinity of the Earth.

## Application Value

Transfer orbits returning from the Moon or other planets to Earth, the key phase completing cislunar round-trip missions.
