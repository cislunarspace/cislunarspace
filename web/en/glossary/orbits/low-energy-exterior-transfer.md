# Low-Energy Exterior Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-propellant Earth-Moon transfer trajectory that utilizes solar perturbation and weak stability boundary characteristics, passing through the exterior region of the Earth-Moon system (near the L2 point). Compared to high-energy Hohmann transfers, it consumes less fuel but requires longer flight time (typically over 160 days).

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
