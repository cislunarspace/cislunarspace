# Lissajous Orbit Insertion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A maneuver performed when a spacecraft reaches the vicinity of a libration point, transitioning it from the transfer trajectory into a Lissajous orbit around that point. Unlike lunar orbit insertion, this targets quasi-periodic orbits near a collinear libration point. ARTEMIS P1 and P2 used this maneuver to enter Earth-Moon L2 and L1 Lissajous orbits, respectively.
