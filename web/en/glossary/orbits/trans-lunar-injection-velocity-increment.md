# Trans-Lunar Injection Velocity Increment

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The tangential impulsive velocity increment applied at a parking orbit to inject the spacecraft into a trans-lunar trajectory. Estimable analytically via Hohmann transfer formula under two-body assumptions, with lunar gravity corrections needed for actual values.
