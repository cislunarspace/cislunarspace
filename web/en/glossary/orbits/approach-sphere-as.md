# Approach Sphere, AS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A safety zone centered on the target spacecraft. In rendezvous operations, the chaser vehicle must not enter this region before receiving approach authorization; its free-drift trajectory must also clear this sphere for a specified duration even under loss of control. This paper uses a 1 km radius approach sphere around the Gateway.

## Application Value

The approach sphere defines a safety zone around target spacecraft; the chaser must not enter before receiving approach authorization, and its free-drift trajectory must clear this sphere even under loss of control.
