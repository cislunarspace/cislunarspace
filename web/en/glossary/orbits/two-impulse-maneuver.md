# Two-Impulse Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital maneuver that applies two instantaneous velocity changes (impulses) along the chaser trajectory. The first impulse changes the trajectory toward the target, and the second impulse brings the chaser to rest at the target.

脉冲机动是轨道修正和转移的基本操作方式。
