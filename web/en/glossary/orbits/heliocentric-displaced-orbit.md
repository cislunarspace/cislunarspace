# Heliocentric Displaced Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of non-Keplerian orbit where solar radiation pressure balances solar gravity, enabling a spacecraft to maintain a stable position out of the ecliptic plane or offset from the Sun-Earth line without propellant consumption. Solar sails are the primary propulsion method for heliocentric displaced orbits, applicable to near-Earth asteroid observation and solar polar observation missions.
