# EL1 Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Periodic orbit near the Sun-Earth L1 point. In the four-body framework, EL1 orbit's invariant manifold intersects that of LL2, providing a dynamical channel for WSB transfer construction.
