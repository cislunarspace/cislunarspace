# lunar gravity assist transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer technique that uses the Moon's gravity to change spacecraft velocity direction. During a lunar flyby, the hyperbolic excess velocity magnitude is preserved while its direction changes, yielding a net velocity increment without fuel expenditure. Suited for time-critical crewed missions.

## Application Value

月球借力转移利用月球引力辅助改变轨道能量，是降低燃料消耗的有效方法。
