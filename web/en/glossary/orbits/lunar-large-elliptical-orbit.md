# lunar large elliptical orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-eccentricity lunar orbit used in the paper's three-impulse braking and escape maneuvers, with a period set at 0.5 days. This orbit serves as an intermediate transition orbit in the three-impulse sequence, enabling phase adjustment at perilune and plane change at apolune to reduce total velocity increment expenditure.
