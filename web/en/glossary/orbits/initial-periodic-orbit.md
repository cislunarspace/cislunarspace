# Initial Periodic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The periodic orbit around Earth from which a spacecraft begins a low-thrust transfer. In this study, the IPO is a nearly circular, approximately one-day-period orbit in the PCR3BP with radius similar to GEO, parameterized by a dimensionless time variable τ that uniquely defines the ignition position.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
