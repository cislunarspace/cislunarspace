# Interplanetary Departure

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The phase when a spacecraft departs a planet's gravitational field.

## Application Value

The interplanetary departure phase determines the initial conditions for spacecraft escaping a planet's gravitational field, critical for mission trajectory design and propellant budgeting.
