# Weak Stability Region Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Low-energy Earth-Moon transfer trajectory design utilizing the characteristics of the weak stability region, where spacecraft can travel long distances with minimal velocity increments.

## Application Value

Weak Stability Region Transfer is a key element in cislunar space orbit design, with significant value for planning Earth-Moon transfer trajectories and designing formation flying scenarios. It is widely applied in lunar exploration missions and small body explorations.
