# Halo Orbit Insertion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The insertion strategy in which the spacecraft performs its final maneuver directly onto the target Halo orbit at a specified position and time. Compared with stable manifold insertion, HOI targets a curve rather than a surface, yielding a smaller parameter space and better optimization convergence. In the Genesis mission, this required a single impulsive maneuver at the Halo orbit point.

## Application Value

This term has significant practical applications in cislunar mission planning and execution.
