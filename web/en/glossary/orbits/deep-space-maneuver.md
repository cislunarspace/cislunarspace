# Deep Space Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A large delta-V maneuver executed in the far-Earth segment of a lunar transfer trajectory, distinct from trajectory correction maneuvers that address small errors. In the ARTEMIS mission, DSMs are performed after lunar gravity assists to accurately target Earth periapsis or libration point insertion states, representing the most fuel-expensive maneuvers in the transfer design.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
