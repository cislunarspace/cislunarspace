# Low-Energy Transfer Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Earth-Moon transfer trajectories consuming less energy than conventional Hohmann transfer, designed using dynamical properties of libration points and invariant manifolds in the three-body problem. Instantaneous eccentricity remains below 1 throughout transfer. Core types include minimum-energy transfer via LL1, WSB transfer via LL2, and (M,N)-loopy transfer via Halo orbits.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
