# Retrograde Low Earth Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low Earth orbit whose direction of motion is opposite to Earth's rotation. In the CR3BP rotating frame, the angular momentum direction of a retrograde orbit is opposite to the Moon's orbital motion. In this paper, the starting condition for two-impulse Earth-Moon transfers is a retrograde LEO at 167 km altitude.
