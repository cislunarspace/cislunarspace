# LL1 Point Transit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The dynamical process where a spacecraft crosses from Earth's gravity well to Moon's via LL1. Under SBCM, the time to reach or depart LL1 changes from infinite to finite, and motion direction changes from bidirectional to unidirectional. LL1 transit achieves minimum-energy low-energy transfer.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
