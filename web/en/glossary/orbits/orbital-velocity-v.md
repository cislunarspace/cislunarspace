# Orbital Velocity (vᵢ)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The rate at which a spacecraft travels along a periodic orbit; one of the important parameters in orbit design.

## Application Value

Orbital mechanics is the foundation of cislunar space mission design, and precise orbit calculation and analysis capabilities directly determine whether a mission can be successfully executed.
