# Shadow-Arc Embedding Configuration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The relative positional relationship between eclipse arcs (Earth and Moon shadows) and thrust/coast arcs in cislunar transfer trajectories. This paper classifies shadow-arc embedding into three types: eclipse on thrust arcs (T1-E-T1, E-T1, etc., 70.99%), eclipse on coast arcs (C-E-C, 24.89%), and eclipse between thrust and coast arcs (E-T1, T1-E-C, etc., 4.12%).
