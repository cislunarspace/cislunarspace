# Spiraling Motion

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Motion pattern under continuous low thrust where the spacecraft gradually changes orbital semimajor axis and eccentricity, appearing as a spiral inward or outward.
