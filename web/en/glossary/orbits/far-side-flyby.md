# Far-Side Flyby

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar flyby where the spacecraft passes on the anti-Earth side of the Moon. Far-side flybys save delta-v compared to near-side flybys for most insertion angles, with savings up to 0.5 km/s. In PLF strategies, far-side flybys combined with retrograde burns near perilune further reduce transfer cost.

## Application Value

在设计地月转移方案时，利用该轨道特性可降低任务总速度增量需求。
