# Period-tripling DRO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A periodic orbit bifurcated from DRO with three times the period of DRO. Its nearby manifolds determine the boundary of the sticky region, which low-energy captured spacecraft must overcome.
