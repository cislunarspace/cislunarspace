# Radial Amplitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The maximum displacement of a halo orbit along the x-axis (the line connecting the two primary bodies) in the rotating frame. Unlike the out-of-plane amplitude A_z and the in-plane amplitude A_y, A_x represents the extent of the orbit along the primary-primary direction. In the Richardson third-order analytical approximation, A_x is uniquely determined by A_z, and the constraint relationship among the three amplitudes is a fundamental condition for halo orbit design.

## Application Value

Representing the extent of a halo orbit along the primary-primary line, A_x is uniquely determined by the out-of-plane amplitude in Richardson's third-order approximation, constituting a fundamental constraint in halo orbit design.
