# Position-Keeping

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital control strategy to maintain a spacecraft near a target position. The paper adapts GEO satellite positioning techniques, combining east-west control (cross-track thrust for semi-major axis correction) and north-south control (normal thrust for inclination correction) to keep the spacecraft near the triangular libration point.

## Application Value

Position-Keeping plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
