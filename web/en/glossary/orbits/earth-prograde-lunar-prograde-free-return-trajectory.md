# Earth-Prograde Lunar-Prograde Free-Return Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of free-return trajectory where the spacecraft departs Earth prograde relative to the geocenter and arrives at the Moon prograde relative to the lunar center. Compared to retrograde types, this trajectory has longer flight time and higher insertion impulses due to opposing DRO motion direction.

## Application Value

该轨道类型可作为地月空间运输网络的中转站或任务的目标轨道。脉冲机动是轨道修正和转移的基本操作方式。
