# Synodic Period Resonant Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Periodic orbits whose periods are integer multiples or fractions of the Earth-Moon synodic period (approximately 29.5 days). The paper uses Lyapunov, Halo, axial, and vertical orbits near L1/L2, and planar/long orbits near L4/L5 with resonance ratios including 1:1, 1:2, 1:3, 1:4, 2:1, 3:1, 3:2, 4:3, 5:2, 5:3, 5:4, and 6:5 as candidate observer orbits. Synodic resonant orbits have the advantage that their relative geometry repeats with the synodic period, facilitating observation mission planning.
