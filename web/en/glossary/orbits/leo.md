# Low Earth orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An Earth orbit with altitude generally below 2000 km. In DRO formation missions, LEO satellites receive GNSS signals for autonomous orbit determination and establish inter-satellite links with DRO satellites to provide inertial frame references.

## Application Value

The 近地轨道 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
