# Family Curve of Transfers

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Complete family of transfer solutions obtained via pseudo-arclength continuation, describing how transfer trajectories vary with energy (Jacobi constant), exhibiting local minima, local maxima, and inflection points.

## Application Value

Family Curve of Transfers plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
