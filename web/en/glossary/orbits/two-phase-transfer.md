# Two-Phase Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A design strategy that divides the Earth-moon low-thrust transfer mission into two phases. The first phase raises altitude from LEO to GEO, where third-body effects are weak and the classical shape function suffices. The second phase transfers from GEO to L1, where both primaries exert comparable gravity, requiring the modified shape function. The two phases satisfy position and flight-path angle continuity at the junction.

## Application Value

The two-phase transfer is an important reference trajectory type in cislunar orbit design, applicable to target orbit selection and transfer scheme design. In actual missions, orbit designs based on the two-phase transfer can satisfy specific mission constraints such as communication elevation angle, coverage, and fuel optimality. Exploiting the characteristics of the two-phase transfer enables optimized transfer trajectory design for efficient Earth-to-Moon transfers. The two-phase transfer provides diverse orbit options for lunar exploration missions and supports flexible mission planning.
