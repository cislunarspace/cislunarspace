# Conditionally Periodic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit whose motion is governed by two or more independent frequencies; the orbit is strictly closed only when the frequency ratios are rational. Near collinear libration points, the linearized solution has generally incommensurable in-plane (ω₀) and out-of-plane (ν₀) frequencies, so the motion is conditionally periodic and fills a 2D torus without closing. Halo orbits are a special case where the frequency ratio is 1:1 and the orbit degenerates to a closed curve on a 1D torus.
