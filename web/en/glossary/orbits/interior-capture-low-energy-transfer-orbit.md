# Interior Capture Low-Energy Transfer Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-energy transfer orbit that remains entirely within the Earth-Moon system using invariant manifolds of L1 periodic orbits. Unlike exterior capture orbits that rely on solar gravity assist, interior capture orbits keep shorter transfer times by staying within the Earth-Moon system. Implementation approaches include transit through small-amplitude manifold tubes, manifold-to-parking-orbit patching, and direct connection via large-amplitude Lyapunov orbit stable manifolds.

## Application Value

该方法在轨道转移设计中被用于分析转移通道和能量最优轨迹，为低能量转移方案提供理论支撑。
