# North-South Control

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A latitudinal station-keeping strategy that applies small thrust in the orbit-normal direction to correct orbital inclination, controlling the spacecraft's out-of-plane drift. The paper uses normal thrust proportional to the inclination deviation from the lunar orbit.
