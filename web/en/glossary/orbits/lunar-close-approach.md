# lunar close approach

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The orbital geometry configuration corresponding to the closest approach distance between a spacecraft trajectory and the lunar surface. By controlling the lunar close approach position (i.e., the selenographic latitude and longitude of perilune), the paper achieves Earth-targeted return, making it the core control variable in Earth-Moon free-return trajectory design.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
