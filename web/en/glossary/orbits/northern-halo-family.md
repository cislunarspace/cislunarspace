# Northern Halo Family

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A family of Halo periodic orbits around a collinear libration point (L1 or L2) whose out-of-plane apoapsis lies above the x-y plane. Symmetric to the southern Halo family with respect to the x-y plane. Provides better coverage of the lunar north polar region.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
