# Ballistic Lunar Surface Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A collective term for unpowered ballistic trajectories from Earth to the lunar surface. This study surveys the complete spectrum from direct transfers (~3 days) to low-energy transfers (>100 days) in both CRTBP and ephemeris models, covering most of the lunar surface.
