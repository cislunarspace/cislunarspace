# Perilune Altitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The distance from the spacecraft to the Moon's surface at perilune, equal to perilune distance minus the Moon's mean radius. Lower altitude enhances gravitational acceleration, reducing transfer impulse. For retrograde orbits, each 100 km altitude increase raises total impulse by approximately 1.8 to 2.7 m/s.
