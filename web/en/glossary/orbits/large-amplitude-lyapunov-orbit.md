# Large Amplitude Lyapunov Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A planar periodic orbit around an L1 or L2 collinear libration point in the Earth-Moon CR3BP with large enough amplitude to lose its tubular manifold structure yet extend close to Earth and Moon. When amplitude is sufficient, the orbit tangentially contacts the lunar parking orbit on the x-axis, and its Earth-directed stable manifold reaches the vicinity of low-Earth orbits. This large amplitude is the geometric prerequisite for directly connecting low-Earth and low-lunar orbits via a single manifold.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
