# Non-Keplerian Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit that does not follow two-body Keplerian laws, prevalent in cislunar space. Unlike the circular or elliptical planar orbits of near-Earth space, cislunar trajectories are generally non-conic sections without fixed orbital planes. In CR3BP, only the Jacobi constant exists and classical orbital elements cannot describe them.
