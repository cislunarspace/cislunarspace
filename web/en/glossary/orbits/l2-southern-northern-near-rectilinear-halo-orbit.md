# L2 Southern/Northern Near-Rectilinear Halo Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Near-rectilinear halo orbits near the Earth-Moon L2 point, split into southern (L2SH) and northern (L2NH) symmetric families. These are the preferred orbit types in the optimal resonant constellation configurations for near-Earth and combined regions.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
