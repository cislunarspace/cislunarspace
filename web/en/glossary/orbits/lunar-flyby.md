# Lunar Flyby

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A maneuver where a spacecraft flies near the Moon and uses lunar gravity to change its orbit, reducing the velocity increment required for transfer trajectories.

## Application Value

Lunar flyby maneuvers reduce transfer trajectory velocity requirements by leveraging lunar gravity, enabling more efficient Earth-Moon transfers.
