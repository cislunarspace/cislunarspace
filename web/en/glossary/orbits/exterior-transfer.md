## Definition

Transfer to a lunar DRO by patching together Sun-Earth and Earth-Moon three-body solutions, entering via the asymptotic orbit of the Earth-Moon L2 Lyapunov orbit. The trajectory passes outside the Earth-Moon region, requiring less fuel but longer flight time.
