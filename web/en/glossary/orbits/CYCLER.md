# Earth-Moon Cycler Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A periodic trajectory that repeatedly transits between Earth and Moon without being captured near either body. It is a resonant periodic orbit in the Earth-Moon restricted three-body problem, with its period in a fixed ratio to the synodic period. A typical cycler has a ~14-day period, encountering the Moon every ~28 days.

## Application Value

This orbit type has important applications in cislunar mission design, useful for constructing stable observation platforms.
