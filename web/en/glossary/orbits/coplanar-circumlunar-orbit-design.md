# Coplanar Circumlunar Orbit Design

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit design method that exploits the Moon's low rotation rate to match circumlunar orbit inclination with landing site latitude and surface stay duration, ensuring the lander's ground track passes through the landing site during both descent and ascent for coplanar flight and reduced propellant consumption. Longer surface stays and lower landing latitudes increase the orbital plane deviation during emergency ascent.
