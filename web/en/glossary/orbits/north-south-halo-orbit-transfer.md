# North-South Halo Orbit Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Transfer between northern and southern halo orbit families near the Earth-Moon L2 libration point. Northern and southern halo pairs are symmetric with respect to the X-Y plane, having opposite out-of-plane amplitudes and identical orbital periods, making them direct transfer targets for each other. Essential for lunar relay communication and navigation constellation reconfiguration.

## Application Value

The 南北Halo轨道转移 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
