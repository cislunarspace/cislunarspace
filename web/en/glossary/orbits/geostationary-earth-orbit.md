# Geostationary Earth Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A circular orbit at 35,793 km altitude with zero inclination and zero eccentricity. A satellite in GEO remains stationary relative to Earth's surface, enabling continuous ground communication. It resides in the outer radiation belt with a harsher radiation environment than LEO.
