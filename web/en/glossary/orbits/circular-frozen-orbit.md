# Circular Frozen Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar circular orbit with zero eccentricity whose inclination is optimized to maximize polar coverage. Compared with elliptical frozen orbits, circular frozen orbits offer finer phase control (phase error within 0.1 degrees), making them suitable for navigation support of precision landing missions in permanently shadowed regions near the lunar south pole. However, circular orbits provide weaker coverage of equatorial regions.

## Application Value

Circular Frozen Orbit plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
