# Bidirectional Orbital Classification

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method of classifying planar trans-lunar trajectories into four combinations based on the direction of motion (prograde or retrograde) in both the geocentric and selenocentric segments. The four types differ in velocity increment and alignment angle.
