# Geostationary Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Circular geostationary orbit with period equal to Earth's rotation period, zero eccentricity and zero inclination; satellite appears stationary relative to Earth

## Application Value

Geostationary Orbit is a key element in cislunar space orbit design, with significant value for planning Earth-Moon transfer trajectories and designing formation flying scenarios. It is widely applied in lunar exploration missions and small body explorations.
