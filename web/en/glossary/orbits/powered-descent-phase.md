# Powered Descent Phase

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The controlled flight phase from the near-lunar point to the lunar surface during a lunar soft landing. The braking engine ignites at the near-lunar point, initiating powered descent; at a certain altitude above the surface, horizontal velocity is reduced to zero, the attitude is adjusted, and the spacecraft descends vertically to the surface. No atmospheric braking is available in this phase; deceleration relies entirely on engine thrust, making it the core subject of soft landing optimal trajectory design. The paper studies the fuel-optimal braking scheme for this phase from the near-lunar point to the surface.

## Application Value

Powered Descent Phase plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
