# Elliptical Frozen Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar elliptical orbit whose eccentricity and argument of perilune remain constant over long periods. By selecting specific eccentricity (e.g., 0.185) and argument of perilune (90 or 270 degrees), the J2 perturbation from the lunar gravity field naturally freezes the orbital elements without active control. When the apolune is locked over the lunar south polar region, it provides sustained high-precision navigation coverage for polar areas, with station-keeping fuel consumption roughly 40% lower than non-frozen elliptical orbits.

## Application Value

This class of orbits has significant application value in lunar mission site selection and space station deployment, providing spacecraft with a stable dwelling environment and favorable mission geometry.
