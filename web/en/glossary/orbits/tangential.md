# Tangential

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The geometric relationship where two orbits share a single tangent point. For distant retrograde orbits (DRO), two Lyapunov orbits are each tangential to the DRO at one point, enabling design of DRO entry and exit transfer trajectories.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
