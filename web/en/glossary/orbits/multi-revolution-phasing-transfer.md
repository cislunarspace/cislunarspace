# Multi-Revolution Phasing Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An emergency transfer strategy when initial orbit insertion energy is insufficient. The spacecraft enters an Earth-bound phasing orbit, completes several revolutions to gradually adjust its phase relative to the Moon, then performs a transfer maneuver when alignment is achieved. Compared with direct transfer, total flight time is longer but the required velocity increment is substantially reduced, significantly expanding the acceptable range of insertion errors. Chang'e 1 used this approach, progressing through 16h, 24h, and 48h phasing orbits before trans-lunar injection.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
