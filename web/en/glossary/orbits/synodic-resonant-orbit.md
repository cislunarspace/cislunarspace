# Synodic Resonant Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Periodic orbits whose period relates to the lunar synodic period by a simple integer ratio, such as 9:2, 3:4, or 4:3 resonance. When resonance ratio and epoch are carefully selected, these orbits naturally avoid Earth and Moon shadows in higher-fidelity models, a property critical for spacecraft power and thermal design on long-duration missions. They also serve as intermediate pathways connecting different types of periodic orbits.
