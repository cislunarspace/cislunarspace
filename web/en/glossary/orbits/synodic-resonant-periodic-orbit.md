# Synodic Resonant Periodic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Periodic orbits whose periods are integer multiples of the synodic period of the Earth-Moon-Sun system. Such orbits provide favorable geometric coverage and lighting conditions for SDA missions.

## Application Value

在周期轨道设计中，该指标用于评估轨道的有界性和长期稳定性，指导轨道维持策略的制定。
