# QSO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A family of periodic orbits in the restricted three-body problem, linearly stable and in close proximity to the secondary body, adopted for proximity operations around small celestial bodies.

## Application Value

The 准卫星轨道 concept provides technical support and analytical methods for cislunar space research and lunar exploration missions.
