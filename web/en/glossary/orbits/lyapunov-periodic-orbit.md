# Lyapunov Periodic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A two-dimensional planar periodic orbit near a libration point, existing only in the XY plane of the Earth-Moon rotating frame with no Z-component. Since it approaches the Earth-Moon line and suffers lunar occultation, it is unsuitable as a space station parking orbit.

## Application Value

Lyapunov Periodic Orbit plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
