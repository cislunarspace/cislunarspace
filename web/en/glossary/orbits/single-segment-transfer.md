# Single-Segment Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory design where the entire transfer is completed within a single continuous segment, without the piecewise patching required by manifold methods. Simplifies design complexity compared to traditional multi-segment transfers.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
