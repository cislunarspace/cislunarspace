# Two-Body Initial Value Estimation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method of analytically estimating control variable initial values by approximating the trans-lunar trajectory as a Hohmann transfer ellipse (Earth-centered, with periapsis at parking orbit radius and apoapsis at Earth-Moon distance plus perilune distance). Accurate for transfer times greater than 3 days.
