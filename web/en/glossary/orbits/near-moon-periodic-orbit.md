# Near-Moon Periodic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A collective term for periodic orbits near the Moon in the three-body problem, including Halo orbit families, Lyapunov orbit families, distant retrograde orbits, butterfly orbits, and low prograde orbits. The geometric characteristics of these orbits (such as perilune/apolune distances and lunar apparent diameter variation range) directly affect orbit determination performance for both optical and GNSS navigation.

## Application Value

Near-Moon Periodic Orbit plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
