# Lunar Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit around the Moon. In this paper, it specifically refers to a lunar orbit at approximately 100 km altitude, serving as the terminal target orbit for two-impulse Earth-Moon transfers. The spacecraft enters this orbit via the second impulse deceleration.

## Application Value

近月轨道是距离月球较近的轨道，是月面着陆任务的目标轨道，设计师需要精确控制进入近月轨道的参数。
