# High z-Amplitude Mode

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A phase during quasi-periodic orbital motion when the z-amplitude reaches its peak, characterized by strong out-of-plane motion. On a Poincaré map, large quasi-Halo orbit crossings correspond to both high and low z-amplitude regions. The ARTEMIS mission exploited the high z-amplitude mode during L2 quasi-Halo orbit insertion to accommodate the out-of-plane arrival conditions from ballistic transfers.
