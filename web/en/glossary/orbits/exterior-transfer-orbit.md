# Exterior Transfer Orbit
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of transfer orbit between NRHOs and DROs where the trajectory travels far from the Moon before returning. Exterior transfers have longer transfer times but typically much lower delta-v costs, as the close lunar flyby redistributes velocity components for efficient orbital insertion.

## Application Value

The Exterior Transfer Orbit plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
