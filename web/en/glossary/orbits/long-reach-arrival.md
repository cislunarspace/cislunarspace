# Long-Reach Arrival

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An arrival mode in which the Earth-Moon transfer flight time exceeds half the period of the geocentric transfer orbit, i.e., the true anomaly at lunar arrival satisfies f > pi. Flight time exceeds 5 days and increases with transfer velocity increment, making it unsuitable for crewed lunar missions in both time and energy.

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
