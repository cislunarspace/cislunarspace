# Orbit-Raising Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A strategy of repeated burns near periapsis to progressively raise apoapsis altitude. In the ARTEMIS mission, two spacecraft starting from highly elliptical Earth orbits executed a series of carefully timed ORMs to gradually raise apoapsis to lunar distance, setting up the conditions for subsequent lunar gravity assists.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space orbit design and optimization, providing theoretical support for orbital transfer and station-keeping operations.
