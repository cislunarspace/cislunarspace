# Outer WSB Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Earth-Moon transfer trajectory with the same geometric shape as Belbruno's WSB theory, constructed by patching stable manifolds near the Sun-Earth L1/L2 points with unstable manifolds near the translunar L2 point.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
