# Rescue Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-energy transfer path from a launch point on the Moon's surface to a libration point orbit (Halo or Lissajous) via the associated stable invariant manifold. Departure speed is near the lunar escape velocity (~2.4 km/s); direct transfers take about 10 days. The feasible departure regions on the Moon depend on manifold phase and expand with additional lunar loops: allowing at least 3 loops enables departure from any surface point.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
