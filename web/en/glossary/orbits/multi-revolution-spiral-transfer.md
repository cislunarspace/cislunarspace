# Multi-Revolution Spiral Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer mode in which a spacecraft with continuous low thrust gradually raises or lowers its nearly circular orbit over many revolutions. Because thrust acceleration is far smaller than gravitational acceleration, the per-revolution velocity increment is tiny, requiring hundreds to thousands of revolutions. The large computational cost is the primary challenge in its optimization.
