# Formation Flight

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A space mission mode where multiple spacecraft fly in coordination with a specific relative spatial configuration. In DRO formations, maintaining formation configuration is more challenging than in two-body dynamics due to strong nonlinearity. Only the periodic solution component guarantees long-term safety and boundedness; introducing other fundamental solution components may cause configuration breakdown or collision risk.

## Application Value

Formation Flight plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
