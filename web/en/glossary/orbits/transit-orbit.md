# Transit Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit that travels inside the tubular structure formed by invariant manifolds of small-amplitude Lyapunov orbits, enabling the spacecraft to pass from the Earth region to the Moon region (or vice versa) without propellant expenditure. Transit orbits cannot directly reach low-Earth or low-lunar orbits and require additional patching segments and impulses.

## Application Value

Transit orbits traverse the interior of manifold tubes from the Earth region to the Moon region, achieving zero-cost space transfer. They are fundamental concepts in designing low-energy transfer orbits using invariant manifolds.
