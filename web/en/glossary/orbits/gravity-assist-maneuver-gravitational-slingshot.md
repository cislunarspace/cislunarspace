# Gravity-assist Maneuver / Gravitational Slingshot

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A planetary flyby maneuver where a spacecraft passes near a planet, using the planet's motion relative to the Sun to change the spacecraft's energy with respect to the Sun; energy can increase or decrease depending on whether the pass is ahead or behind the planet.
