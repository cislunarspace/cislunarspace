# Minimum Parking Orbit, MPO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A circular orbit with the largest perigee radius reachable by the stable manifolds of a Lyapunov orbit around L₁, with radius approximately 0.20 nondimensional units (~80,000 km). It serves as the starting point for the Earth segment of low-energy cislunar transfers, and the intersection between stable manifolds and this orbit determines the minimum time of flight.

## Application Value

The starting point for the Earth segment of low-energy cislunar transfers. The intersection between its stable manifold and the orbit determines minimum flight time, serving as a critical reference for assessing transfer feasibility.
