# Low-Thrust Trajectory Optimization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Spacecraft trajectory optimization problems using low-thrust systems such as electric propulsion. Thrust acceleration is about 10^-5g and must be applied continuously or nearly continuously, fundamentally different from impulsive thrust problems.

## Application Value

在轨道力学分析和任务设计中，This概念为轨道特性评估和方案比选provides理论依据，有助于优化轨道设计参数，提高任务经济性。
