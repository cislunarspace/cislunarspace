# Lunar Ballistic Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-energy Earth-Moon transfer trajectory exploiting the intersection of invariant manifolds near the Sun-Earth and Earth-Moon L2 points. Design proceeds by computing Poincare sections of both restricted three-body systems, selecting manifold intersections for geometric patching, then numerically refining in the full-force model. Minimum energy is ~3787 m/s with ~100 day transfer time. First proposed by Belbruno (1987); Koon et al. later explained the dynamical mechanism via invariant manifolds.
