# Out-of-plane Amplitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The maximum out-of-plane displacement of a halo orbit in the z-direction, characterizing its three-dimensional nature. In the L2 halo family, Az first increases then decreases as the orbit approaches the primary.

## Application Value

The 面外振幅 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
