# Back-Propagation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit design method that calculates the initial orbital state by forward propagation from the target orbital state. This paper back-propagates from the known NRHO state and rendezvous epoch to compute the Earth-to-NRHO transfer trajectory. Since the only rocket launch constraints are perigee altitude and orbital inclination of the Earth parking orbit, back-propagation simplifies the design and facilitates convergence.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
