# 9:2 Resonant NRHO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A near-rectilinear halo orbit with a 9:2 resonance with the lunar period, with an orbital period of approximately 6.5 days, perilune of approximately 3200 km, and apolune of approximately 70000 km. The paper selects this orbit as the operational orbit for the Gateway space station, whose high stability makes it the best candidate for deploying a human outpost in cislunar space.
