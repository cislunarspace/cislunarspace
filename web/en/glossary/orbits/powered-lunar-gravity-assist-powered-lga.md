# Powered Lunar Gravity Assist, Powered LGA

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A gravity assist technique in which the spacecraft applies an impulsive thrust at the lunar flyby point. Unlike unpowered flybys, powered LGA enables active velocity modification near perilune, allowing more flexible trajectory patching at the flyby point.
