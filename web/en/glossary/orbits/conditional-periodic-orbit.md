# Conditional Periodic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In the restricted three-body problem, a periodic orbit that exists only for specific initial conditions (e.g., symmetry about the XZ plane, constraints on puncture point state variables). Halo orbits are typical conditional periodic orbits, whose existence reflects the strong asymmetry of the CRTBP gravitational field near libration points.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
