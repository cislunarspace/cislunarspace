# Terminal Orbit Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A maneuver scheme in which the spacecraft follows a free return trajectory to the perilune vicinity, then enters a large elliptical lunar orbit to change orbital plane orientation. Typically requires three impulses: the first and third modify semi-major axis, and the second changes orbital plane at the apolune. Maintains free-return safety throughout the transit. Energy cost increases steeply with large plane changes, and flight time is extended by the transfer ellipse.
