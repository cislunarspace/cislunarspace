# 9:2 Monthly Resonance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital resonance characteristic of NRHO, where the spacecraft completes 9 revolutions in two months. This fixed resonance relationship allows the Lunar Gateway to rendezvous with spacecraft at the same relative position each orbit, facilitating resupply mission scheduling.

## Application Value

The 9:2 monthly resonance enables the Lunar Gateway to rendezvous with spacecraft at the same relative position each orbit, facilitating resupply mission scheduling. It is an important dynamical characteristic of NRHO orbits.
