# Minimum transfer time

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The transfer time corresponding to zero radial velocity component for a given transfer geometry; used for initial guessing and bound determination in multi-revolution cases.
