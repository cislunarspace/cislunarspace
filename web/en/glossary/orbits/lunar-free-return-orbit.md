# Lunar Free-Return Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer trajectory from Earth that passes near the Moon and returns to Earth's vicinity through natural dynamics without requiring mid-course braking impulses. This orbit type is typical for crewed lunar missions, providing a safe-return guarantee for astronauts, though placing a satellite into an LFO incurs higher launch costs.

## Application Value

This concept is used in orbital analysis and has significant impact on mission design.
