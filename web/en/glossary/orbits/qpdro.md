# Quasi-Periodic Distant Retrograde Orbit, QPDRO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A quasi-periodic orbit that envelopes the stable periodic distant retrograde orbit. On a surface of section, QPDROs appear as closed curves surrounding the SPDRO. Together with the SPDRO, they compose the stable portion of the distant retrograde region, which vanishes only at two values of the Jacobi constant.

## Application Value

The 准周期远距离逆行轨道 concept provides technical support and analytical methods for cislunar space research and lunar exploration missions.
