# Low-Thrust Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital transfer executed with low-thrust engines such as electric propulsion. Thrust is much less than the spacecraft's weight, requiring continuous operation over hundreds of revolutions with spiral trajectories. Fuel efficiency far exceeds impulsive maneuvers, but design is complex due to multi-revolution iteration and multi-body dynamics.

## Application Value

This propulsion technology is important for deep space missions, serving as a key means for efficient orbital transfers.
