# Lunar South Pole Coverage Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit that provides continuous coverage of the lunar south pole region; certain L1 or L2 halo orbit family members maintain line-of-sight with the lunar south pole over most of the orbital period.
