# Mixed-Orbit Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A constellation design strategy that leverages combinations of multiple orbital types to fulfill diverse mission requirements, fully utilizing the advantages of different orbits and representing an important future development direction for cislunar constellations.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar orbit design and transfer analysis, useful for studying orbital stability, designing transfer schemes, and evaluating mission performance.
