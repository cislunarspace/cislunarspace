# Heterospace-system Libration Point Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer method connecting libration point orbits of different three-body systems (such as Sun-Earth and Earth-Moon systems), achieving cross-system transfer by joining invariant manifolds of the two systems.
