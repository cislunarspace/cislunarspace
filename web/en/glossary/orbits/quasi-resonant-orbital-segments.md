# Quasi-Resonant Orbital Segments

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Orbital segments in the DRO capture phase exhibiting 2:1, 3:1, 4:1, and 5:1 resonant characteristics; the 4:1 quasi-resonant segment exists only at higher Jacobi energy, corresponding to ballistic capture mechanism.
