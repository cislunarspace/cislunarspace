# P4HO Orbit Family

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Orbit families evolving from period-quadrupling bifurcations in the NRHO region of the L₂ halo family. Members have orbital periods approximately quadruple that of the parent NRHO, split into P4HO₁ and P4HO₂ sub-families. These orbits possess motion components both within and perpendicular to the orbital plane, making them suitable for transfers requiring plane changes. Smaller members approximate NRHO-like motion but include four lunar passes per period.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
