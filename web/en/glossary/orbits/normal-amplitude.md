# Normal Amplitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A key parameter characterizing the size of a Halo orbit. A Halo orbit is symmetric about the xz-plane and intersects it at two points; the normal amplitude is the distance from the farther intersection point to the x-axis. A larger amplitude means the Halo orbit extends farther from the collinear libration point, resulting in a larger angle between the perilune velocity vector of the corresponding stable manifold and the lunar orbital plane, which degrades the lunar gravity assist effect and increases fuel consumption for indirect transfer.

## Application Value

在轨道设计和转移轨道优化中，该方法用于确定最优转移时机和轨道形状，以最小化燃料消耗或飞行时间. 通过数值仿真和迭代优化，可获得满足任务约束的可行轨道方案.
