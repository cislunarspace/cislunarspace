# Exterior Phase

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The phase of a low-energy transfer trajectory where the spacecraft exits the gravitational dominance region of the Earth-Moon system. During the exterior phase, the trajectory extends far from the Earth and subsequently returns to the Earth-Moon system through a close lunar flyby. In the Earth-centered inertial frame, transfers with an exterior phase appear as highly eccentric elliptic orbits and are characteristic of low-energy transfers to LEO. The exterior phase typically does not appear in transfers to MEO or GSO.

## Application Value

The exterior phase is characteristic of low-energy transfers to LEO, appearing as highly eccentric elliptic orbits in the Earth-centered inertial frame. It typically does not appear in transfers to MEO or GSO.
