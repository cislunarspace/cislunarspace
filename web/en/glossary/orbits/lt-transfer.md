# Low-Thrust Transfer Orbit

A low-thrust transfer orbit is a flight trajectory that uses continuous small-thrust propulsion systems (such as electric propulsion) to achieve orbital transfer. Unlike traditional impulsive orbital transfers, low-thrust transfer gradually changes orbital energy and angular momentum through sustained small thrust over long durations.
