# Semi-Major Axis Deviation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The difference between actual and nominal semi-major axis at orbit insertion, a key indicator of insertion accuracy. Early shutdown of the rocket engine directly causes increased semi-major axis deviation. For Chang'e-5 with a nominal insertion semi-major axis of ~207,000 km, deviations exceeding ~70,000 km surpass midcourse correction capability and require multi-revolution phasing strategies. The paper traverses deviation ranges from 10,000 to 120,000 km, revealing a non-monotonic relationship between total velocity increment and deviation.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
