# Hierarchical Search Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory design method that progressively refines the solution through three hierarchical layers: first treating the Moon as a point mass to ensure encounter, then using B-plane targeting under two-body assumptions, and finally applying mission-specific constraints to obtain the accurate trajectory.

## Application Value

This method progressively refines trajectory solutions through three hierarchical layers, enabling efficient convergence from initial Moon-encounter design to accurate mission-specific trajectories.
