# LL1 Lyapunov Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A periodic orbit near the L1 point in the Earth-Moon system. LL1 Lyapunov orbit and its stable/unstable manifolds form the key component of Earth-Moon transportation tubes, serving as the basis for low-energy transfer design. Its invariant manifolds guide spacecraft from Earth elliptical orbits into the Lyapunov orbit.

## Application Value

This concept is used in orbital analysis and has significant impact on mission design.
