# Controlled Fly-Around Formation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A fly-around formation configuration on DRO achieved through active control, where the deputy performs controlled periodic fly-around motion around the chief. Unlike natural co-fly formation (where the deputy and chief are on the same DRO with different phases), the controlled fly-around formation can be circular or other configurations but requires continuous control force to maintain.
