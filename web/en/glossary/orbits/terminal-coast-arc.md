# Terminal Coast Arc

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An unpowered arc at the end of a transfer trajectory where the engine is off and the spacecraft coasts along an invariant manifold. In manifold-aided low-thrust Earth-Moon transfers, the spacecraft uses a powered phase to reach the manifold insertion point, then coasts naturally along the manifold to the target NRHO vicinity. Terminal coast arcs significantly reduce propellant consumption at the cost of increased total flight time (typically tens of days).

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
