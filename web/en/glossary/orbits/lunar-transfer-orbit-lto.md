# Lunar Transfer Orbit (LTO)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The elliptical orbit entered after Trans-Lunar Injection (TLI) from an Earth orbit, with perigee near Earth and apogee extending into the lunar orbital region. LTO is the intermediate phase of Earth-Moon transfer; the spacecraft flies along the LTO for approximately 5 days before arriving near the Moon to execute PLSB and enter lunar orbit.

## Application Value

In orbital design and transfer trajectory optimization, this method is used to determine optimal transfer timing and orbital shape, minimizing fuel consumption or flight time. Through numerical simulation and iterative optimization, feasible orbital solutions satisfying mission constraints can be obtained.
