# Transition Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer trajectory that bridges the gap between distinct regimes of motion. Transition orbits typically evolve along the manifolds of a periodic orbit but are precariously positioned between two types of motion. They appear near insertion points where transfer parameters change rapidly, marking the transition of transfer families from one mode to another.

## Application Value

在轨道设计和转移轨道优化中，该方法用于确定最优转移时机和轨道形状，以最小化燃料消耗或飞行时间. 通过数值仿真和迭代优化，可获得满足任务约束的可行轨道方案.
