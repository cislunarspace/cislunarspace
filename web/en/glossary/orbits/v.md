# Lunar DRO Insertion Delta-V

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Velocity increment required to insert into lunar DRO at the end of a transfer trajectory, representing the main fuel consumption for LEO-to-DRO transfers.

## Application Value

In the field of orbits, Lunar DRO Insertion Delta-V is a key element for understanding and analyzing system behavior. This concept is widely used in spacecraft trajectory design, dynamics modeling, and mission planning, providing theoretical support for engineering practice.
