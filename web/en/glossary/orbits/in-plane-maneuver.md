# In-Plane Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital maneuver whose velocity increment lies within the orbital plane without changing the orbital plane orientation. In this paper, after out-of-plane dynamics are eliminated, the third impulse at flyby perigee adjusts orbital energy to reach the desired x-axis crossing, constituting an in-plane maneuver.
