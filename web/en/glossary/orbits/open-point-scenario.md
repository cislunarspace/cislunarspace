# Open-Point Scenario

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer configuration method that opens the manifold insertion maneuver location from the traditional perigee point to any point along the stable manifold, adding a degree of freedom to the transfer design and enabling searches over a larger parameter space for low-energy transfer orbits. The maximum propagation time is one month for exterior manifolds and two months for interior manifolds.

## Application Value

The open-point scenario is an important reference trajectory type in cislunar orbit design, applicable to target orbit selection and transfer scheme design. In actual missions, orbit designs based on the open-point scenario can satisfy specific mission constraints such as communication elevation angle, coverage, and fuel optimality. Exploiting the characteristics of the open-point scenario enables optimized transfer trajectory design for efficient Earth-to-Moon transfers. The open-point scenario provides diverse orbit options for lunar exploration missions and supports flexible mission planning.
