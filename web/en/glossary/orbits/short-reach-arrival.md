# Short-Reach Arrival

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An arrival mode in which the Earth-Moon transfer flight time is less than half the period of the geocentric transfer orbit, i.e., the true anomaly at lunar arrival satisfies f <= pi. Short-reach arrival is superior in both energy and time to long-reach arrival and is the preferred mode for crewed lunar Hohmann transfers.
