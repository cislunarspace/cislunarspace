# Commensurate Period

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

When the ratio of two periodic orbits' periods is a rational number (e.g., 2:1, 1:1), the periods are commensurate. Commensurate period is a key criterion for dual-spacecraft constellation design: orbit combinations with commensurate periods cause the relative phase relationship to repeat periodically, enabling systematic optimization of coverage. This paper finds the 2:1 commensurate combination of 7-day and 14-day orbits particularly suitable for lunar south pole coverage.

## Application Value

通约周期是地月空间轨道设计中的重要参考轨型，可用于目标轨道选择和转移方案设计 在实际任务中，基于通约周期的轨道设计能够满足特定的任务约束，如通信仰角、覆盖范围和燃料最优等要求 利用通约周期的特性，可以优化转移轨道设计，实现从地球到月球的高效转移 通约周期为月球探测任务提供了多样化的轨道选择方案，有助于任务的灵活规划
