# Low Earth orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An Earth orbit with altitude generally below 2000 km. In DRO formation missions, LEO satellites receive GNSS signals for autonomous orbit determination and establish inter-satellite links with DRO satellites to provide inertial frame references.

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
