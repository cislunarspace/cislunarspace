# Prograde in Perigee and Retrograde in Perilune

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A classification of trans-lunar trajectories by orbital direction: the spacecraft moves prograde (same as Earth's rotation) in the geocentric segment and retrograde (opposite to Moon's revolution) near the Moon. Free-return trajectories for crewed lunar missions typically use this type.

## Application Value

在设计地月转移方案时，利用该轨道特性可降低任务总速度增量需求。
