# Limit Transiting Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In CR3BP, transiting trajectories derived from transportation tube walls whose transfer time approaches infinity in the limit. Under SBCM, solar perturbation makes the transfer time finite and significantly reduces their number.
