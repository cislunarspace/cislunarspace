# High Earth Orbit, HEO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-altitude orbit around Earth, specifically referring here to an orbit with semi-major axis of approximately 10 Earth radii. In SEP Earth-Moon transfers, HEO serves as the terminal point of Earth-orbit ascending and the starting point of the trans-lunar segment, where propagation switches from AOAT to numerical integration.

## Application Value

HEO serves as the terminal point of Earth-orbit ascending and the starting point of the trans-lunar segment in SEP Earth-Moon transfers, where propagation switches from analytical orbit averaging techniques to numerical integration.
