# Limited Power Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Trajectory optimization problem with limited power, assuming continuously adjustable thrust and constant spacecraft mass, used to provide initial guess for subsequent problems.

## Application Value

Limited Power Trajectory is an important concept in cislunar orbit design, crucial for understanding spacecraft motion in the Earth-Moon space.
