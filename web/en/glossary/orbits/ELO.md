# Elliptical Lunar Orbit, ELO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Equatorial orbits with low perilune distance providing favorable access to the lunar surface at reduced cost from Earth.

## Application Value

Equatorial orbits with low perilune distance, favorable for reduced-cost access to lunar surface from Earth, candidate orbits for lunar landing missions.
