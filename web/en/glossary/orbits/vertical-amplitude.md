# Vertical Amplitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The oscillation amplitude of a Lissajous or Halo orbit in the z-direction (perpendicular to the primaries' orbital plane), denoted β. In the linearized solution β is independent of the in-plane amplitude α and set by initial conditions; for Halo orbits, however, α and β must satisfy a specific resonance condition (ω(α,β)=ν(α,β)) to produce a closed periodic orbit.

## Application Value

垂直振幅是地月空间轨道设计中的重要参考轨型，可用于目标轨道选择和转移方案设计 在实际任务中，基于垂直振幅的轨道设计能够满足特定的任务约束，如通信仰角、覆盖范围和燃料最优等要求 利用垂直振幅的特性，可以优化转移轨道设计，实现从地球到月球的高效转移 垂直振幅为月球探测任务提供了多样化的轨道选择方案，有助于任务的灵活规划
