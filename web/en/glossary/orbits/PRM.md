# Perilune Rendezvous Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An Earth-to-Moon transfer method intermediate between direct transfer and WSB transfer. After powered lunar swing-by, the spacecraft enters an elongated elliptical lunar orbit, waits for the orbital plane to align with the target NRHO, then applies a small velocity increment at perilune for insertion. Transfer period is about 18 days with total delta-V roughly 40-50 m/s less than direct transfer. Since PRM insertion requires only velocity-aligned thrust, it is highly efficient; the two-week orbital waiting period absorbs most trajectory errors, yielding higher insertion accuracy than direct transfer.

## Application Value

This propulsion technology is important for deep space missions, serving as a key means for efficient orbital transfers.
