# Translunar Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer trajectory from a near-Earth orbit to the Moon. A direct translunar orbit takes about 5 days. Autonomous navigation accuracy during this phase directly affects the accuracy of subsequent lunar orbit insertion and landing. The paper uses a direct translunar orbit as a case to evaluate the constellation's navigation service capability.
