# In-Plane Amplitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The oscillation amplitude of a Lissajous or Halo orbit in the xy-plane (the rotation plane of the synodic frame), denoted α. In the linearized solution near a collinear libration point, α determines the oscillation magnitudes in the ξ and η directions, and together with the vertical amplitude β defines the spatial extent and shape of the orbit.
