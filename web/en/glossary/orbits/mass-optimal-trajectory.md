# Mass-Optimal Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An optimal trajectory with maximum final mass (minimum fuel consumption) as the performance index. In low-thrust transfers, mass-optimal solutions form the lower bound of the Pareto front: longer flight times yield fuel consumption closer to the theoretical minimum.
