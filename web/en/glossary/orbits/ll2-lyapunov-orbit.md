# LL2 Lyapunov Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A periodic orbit near the L2 point in the Earth-Moon system. The stable manifold of LL2 Lyapunov orbit patched with the unstable manifold of EL1 (or EL2) Halo orbit achieves WSB transfer. Its stable manifold also serves as the entry for low-energy transfer to DRO.

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
