# Cislunar Periodic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A special class of resonance orbits in the Earth-Moon three-body system, lying in the X-Y plane of the barycentric rotating frame and symmetric about the X-Z plane. These orbits periodically revisit multiple libration points or celestial bodies in the Earth-Moon system, making them suitable for space observation missions.

## Application Value

Cislunar periodic orbits are periodic orbits closed within cislunar space, such as DROs and NRHOs, and provide the basis for siting future cislunar space stations.
