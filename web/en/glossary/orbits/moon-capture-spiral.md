# Moon-Capture Spiral

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The flight phase after the spacecraft enters the moon's gravitational dominance, where continuous low-thrust gradually shrinks the spiral orbit until capture into the target low lunar orbit. In the moon-centered rotating frame it appears as an inward-converging spiral. It forms the symmetric counterpart to the Earth-escape spiral.

## Application Value

Moon-Capture Spiral plays an important role in orbit maneuver design, analysis, and transfer planning for cislunar missions.
