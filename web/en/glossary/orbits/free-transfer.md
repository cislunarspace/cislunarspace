# Free Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit transfer achieved entirely through natural dynamics of the system without any impulsive maneuver. A free transfer exists if and only if the initial and target orbits share the same Jacobi constant, such that the unstable manifold of the departure orbit intersects the stable manifold of the arrival orbit in phase space, forming a heteroclinic connection.
