# Orbit-to-Orbit Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Transfer of a spacecraft from one complete orbit to another. Unlike point-to-point transfers, orbit-to-orbit transfers do not fix departure and arrival locations but optimize the transfer between entire orbits. The moving point strategy slides endpoints along target orbits to search for globally optimal solutions.

## Application Value

The 轨道间转移 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
