# High Lunar Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A high-altitude circular orbit around the Moon. In this study, HLO serves as an intermediate orbit that truncates the moon-capture spiral before reaching LLO, simplifying the numerical optimization by removing hundreds of near-circular revolutions.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
