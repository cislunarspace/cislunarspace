# Long Elliptical Lunar Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The intermediate orbit in PRM. After a powered lunar swing-by deceleration at perilune, the spacecraft enters a highly eccentric lunar orbit with perilune altitude of approximately 1800-2000 km. The spacecraft orbits for several revolutions (about 13 days), waiting for the orbital plane to align with the target NRHO before executing insertion.

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
