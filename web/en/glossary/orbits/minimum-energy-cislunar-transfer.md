# Minimum Energy Cislunar Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Earth-to-Moon transfer with minimum energy consumption, achieved via LL1 point or halo orbit, with spacecraft eccentricity always less than 1 during transfer.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
