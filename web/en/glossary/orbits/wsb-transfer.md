# Weak Stability Boundary Transfer, WSB Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that exploits gravitational balance regions between celestial bodies to construct low-energy transfer trajectories. The spacecraft first reaches the Earth's WSB (at approximately four Earth-Moon distances) via a lunar gravity assist, then uses the dynamical sensitivity of this region to transition with minimal velocity increment to a ballistic capture trajectory connecting to the lunar WSB. This approach saves approximately 18% of the insertion ΔV compared to a Hohmann transfer. First demonstrated by Japan's Hiten spacecraft in 1991.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
