# Sub-Synchronous Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit at altitude below geostationary orbit (~36,000 km). Sub-synchronous orbit constellations can survey GEO belt targets, but their observation geometry and coverage characteristics differ from surveillance satellites near GEO altitude. The cited Zhang Keke et al. study compared ascending/descending and single/double satellite configurations in sub-synchronous orbits.
