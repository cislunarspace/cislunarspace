# DRO Insertion Angle, τ

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An angular parameter describing the spacecraft's insertion position on a Distant Retrograde Orbit. In the rotating frame, τ advances clockwise at a constant rate, analogous to the mean anomaly of Keplerian orbits. The choice of τ determines transfer delta-v cost and flight time, making it a key optimization variable in DRO mission design.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
