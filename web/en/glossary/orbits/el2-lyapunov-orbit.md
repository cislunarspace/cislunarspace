# EL2 Lyapunov Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A periodic orbit near the L2 point in the Sun-Earth system. Similar to EL1 Lyapunov orbit, its invariant manifold can patch with that of LL2 Lyapunov orbit to form the ballistic capture channel for WSB transfer.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
