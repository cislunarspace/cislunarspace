# Lunar Synodic Resonance, LSR

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An integer ratio relationship between the spacecraft's orbital period and the Moon's synodic (revolution) period. Gateway's NRHO employs a 9:2 lunar synodic resonance, enabling long-term avoidance of Earth eclipses, which is critical for solar-powered spacecraft.
