# Halo Orbit Classification

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A classification scheme for halo orbits based on their spatial orientation in the rotating frame. Class I (northern) halos extend in the positive z-direction; Class II (southern) halos extend in the negative z-direction. The two classes exhibit markedly different manifold behaviors: transfers between Class I and Class II halos generally require larger total delta-V than intra-class transfers, and a minimum amplitude threshold exists below which such inter-class transfers do not exist.
