# Weak Stability Boundary Transfer (WSB Transfer)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that exploits gravitational balance regions between celestial bodies to construct low-energy transfer trajectories. The spacecraft first reaches Earth's WSB (approximately four Earth-Moon distances) via a lunar gravity assist, then uses the dynamical sensitivity of this region to transition with minimal velocity increment to a ballistic capture trajectory connecting to the lunar WSB. This approach saves approximately 18% of the insertion delta-V compared to a Hohmann transfer. First demonstrated by Japan's Hiten spacecraft in 1991.

## Application Value

Weak Stability Boundary Transfer exploits solar gravitational perturbations to achieve low-energy transfers. The transfer arc extends beyond the lunar orbit, resulting in longer flight times but significantly reduced propellant consumption.
