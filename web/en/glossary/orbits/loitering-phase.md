# Loitering Phase

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The intermediate phase where the chaser spacecraft maintains a controlled drift around the target space station while preparing for subsequent rendezvous and docking operations.

## Application Value

The loitering phase maintains controlled drift around the target space station while preparing for subsequent rendezvous and docking, providing flexibility in operational timelines.
