# Stable Halo Orbit Family

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The subset of a halo orbit family for which both stability indices nu1 and nu2 are real-valued with absolute value at most 1. These orbits can be maintained long-term without active control at very low station-keeping cost. In the Earth-Moon system, stable L1 halo orbits have periods near 9.4 d and out-of-plane amplitudes near 74,000 km, while L2 orbits are near 10 d and 77,600 km, making them ideal for lunar navigation constellations.

## Application Value

This concept is used in orbital analysis and has significant impact on mission design.
