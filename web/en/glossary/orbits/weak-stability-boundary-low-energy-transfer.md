# Weak Stability Boundary Low-energy Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A low-energy Earth-Moon transfer method that exploits the weak stability boundary, where the spacecraft transitions between orbits using solar perturbation with minimal velocity impulses.

## Application Value

In orbit design and control system analysis, the Lyapunov method provides an effective means to assess system stability without solving differential equations, particularly suitable for analyzing trans-lunar transfer orbits and orbit stability near small celestial bodies.
