# Libration Point Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Periodic or quasi-periodic orbits around libration points in the restricted three-body problem, including Halo orbits and Lyapunov orbits. Collinear LPOs near L1 and L2 are unstable under the full force model and require frequent station-keeping. Triangular LPOs near L4 and L5 have slower instability due to solar perturbation and need less maintenance.

## Application Value

Halo and Lyapunov orbits serve as key target orbits for cislunar missions, usable for navigation constellation deployment or as transfer staging points.
