# Non-coplanar Hohmann Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A Hohmann transfer where the transfer orbit plane is not coplanar with the initial orbit plane, requiring two velocity maneuvers.

## Application Value

A Hohmann bi-elliptic transfer with non-coplanar transfer and initial orbits, suitable for orbital maneuvers changing orbital inclination.
