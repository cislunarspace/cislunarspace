# Near Rectilinear Halo Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A family of halo orbits near Earth-Moon L1/L2 libration points, characterized by large vertical amplitude and nearly rectilinear shape, with orbital periods of approximately 8.5 days; a favorable candidate orbit for constructing a deep space station.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
