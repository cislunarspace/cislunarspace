# Transfer Cost Map

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A two-dimensional map with the departure orbit phase and arrival orbit phase as independent variables, showing the ΔV cost of each transfer via color or contour lines. Zero-ΔV regions correspond to free transfers, and the ΔV cost increases rapidly as the phase moves away from these points.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
