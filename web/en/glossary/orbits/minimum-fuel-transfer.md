# Minimum Fuel Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A trajectory optimization criterion that minimizes propellant consumption as the objective. Under low-thrust conditions, minimum fuel transfer typically involves continuous acceleration within two long thrust arcs, resulting in relatively short mission duration but optimal fuel consumption. In the paper, minimum fuel transfer has total flight time of approximately 201 days with final mass of 275 kg.

## Application Value

Minimum Fuel Transfer has practical applications in cislunar space mission design and analysis.
