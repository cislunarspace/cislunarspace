# Halo-to-GEO Transfer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer trajectory starting from an L1 halo orbit, performing a manifold insertion followed by an impulse maneuver into a connecting orbit to geosynchronous Earth orbit (GEO). Used in the paper as a representative surveillance target in cislunar space, with a transfer duration of approximately 23 days.

## Application Value

Halo至GEO转移是地月空间轨道设计中的重要参考轨型，可用于目标轨道选择和转移方案设计 在实际任务中，基于Halo至GEO转移的轨道设计能够满足特定的任务约束，如通信仰角、覆盖范围和燃料最优等要求 利用Halo至GEO转移的特性，可以优化转移轨道设计，实现从地球到月球的高效转移 Halo至GEO转移为月球探测任务提供了多样化的轨道选择方案，有助于任务的灵活规划
