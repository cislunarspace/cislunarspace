# Symmetric Free-Return Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A free-return trajectory with geometric symmetry in the three-body dynamical coordinate system. Symmetry simplifies trajectory design by constructing initial conditions at perilune and forward-integrating. The trajectory guarantees Earth return without maneuvers in case of mission abort or propulsion failure.

## Application Value

在对称自由返回轨道的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 为航天器的精确控制提供理论依据，确保任务执行的可靠性 用于分析航天器在复杂引力场中的运动特性。
