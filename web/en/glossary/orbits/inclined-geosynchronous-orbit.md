# Inclined Geosynchronous Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Inclined geosynchronous orbit with period matching Earth's rotation but non-zero inclination, producing a figure-8 ground track. IGSO satellites together with GEO satellites form the Earth reference layer of BDS.
