# Projected Circular Formation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A formation in which satellites do not maintain constant relative distance, but their relative motion appears circular when projected onto a plane viewed from a specified direction. It generalizes the circular formation by rotating the plane of relative motion, and is applicable to distributed telescope observations of celestial targets.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
