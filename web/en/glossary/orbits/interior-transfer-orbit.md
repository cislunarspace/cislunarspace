# Interior Transfer Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of transfer orbit between NRHOs and DROs where the trajectory remains in the vicinity of the Moon throughout the transfer. Interior transfers typically have shorter transfer times but higher delta-v costs compared to exterior transfers.

## Application Value

在内部转移轨道的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
