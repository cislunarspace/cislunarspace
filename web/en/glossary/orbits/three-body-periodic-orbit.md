# Three-Body Periodic Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An umbrella term for periodic orbits in the Earth-Moon three-body system, covering two families: libration point orbits around the collinear points (NRHO, Halo, etc.), and Moon-centered periodic orbit families with the Moon as their center (DRO, etc.). Unlike Keplerian orbits, their shape and stability are governed by the gravitational fields of two primary bodies, serving as parking, relay, and deep-space transfer nodes for lunar missions.
