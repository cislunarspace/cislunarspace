# Earth-Return Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A family of unstable periodic orbits (the a family) discovered by Hénon in Hill's modification of the three-body problem. A spacecraft following an ERO periodically approaches and departs from Earth; when continued toward L1, the orbit degenerates into a simple quasi-elliptic HLO. The half-cycle path of an ERO serves as part of the LEO-to-DRO transfer trajectory and is the foundation of Ocampo's classical transfer method.

## Application Value

在轨道设计与任务规划中，需要选择合适的轨道类型并分析其动力学特性。该概念对于转移轨道设计、轨道插入和轨道保持具有重要指导意义。
