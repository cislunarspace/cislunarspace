# Horizontal Lyapunov Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A planar periodic orbit near a collinear libration point, dominated by in-plane motion with zero out-of-plane component. In the CRTBP, Lyapunov orbits are classified as vertical or horizontal; HLOs have larger in-plane than out-of-plane amplitude and serve as anchors for constructing the hyperbolic network and stable/unstable manifolds around L1 in the Sun-Earth system, acting as intermediate waypoints for Earth-Moon transfer trajectory design.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
