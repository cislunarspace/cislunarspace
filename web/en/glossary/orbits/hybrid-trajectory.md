# Hybrid Trajectory

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A transfer scheme combining a free return trajectory with a midcourse maneuver. The spacecraft follows a free return trajectory until malfunction is ruled out, then performs a single midcourse maneuver to enter the target orbit. The midcourse delta-v is typically less than 100 m/s, extending landing coverage from low latitudes to the entire lunar surface. The tradeoff is loss of the free-return safety property after the maneuver.

## Application Value

This concept is used in orbital analysis and has significant impact on mission design.
