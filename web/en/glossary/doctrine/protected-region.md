# Protected Region

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Space regions defined according to IADC guidelines that require debris mitigation measures. Low Earth Orbit (LEO) is defined as the spherical region from Earth's surface to 2000 km altitude; Geostationary Orbit (GEO) is defined as the spherical shell segment at 35786 km altitude ±200 km and latitude ±15°.
