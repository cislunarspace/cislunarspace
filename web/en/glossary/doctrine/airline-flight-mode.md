# Airline-flight-mode

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An aerospace transportation operational concept modeled on commercial airline operations, requiring launch vehicles to achieve high-frequency, low-cost, highly reliable, and reusable capabilities to support large-scale cislunar exploration and exploitation through regular, scheduled launch services. Airline-flight-mode operations mark the transition from individual science missions to large-scale cislunar utilization, with key performance targets including thousands of launches per year, 100+ reuses per vehicle, and one to two orders of magnitude reduction in per-payload launch cost compared to expendable rockets.

## Application Value

Airline-flight-mode has practical applications in cislunar space mission design and analysis.
