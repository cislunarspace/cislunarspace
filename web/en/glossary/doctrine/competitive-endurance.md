# Competitive Endurance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Site: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Competitive Endurance is the theory of success published by the United States Space Force, describing how the Space Force will gain and maintain space superiority. The logic of Competitive Endurance aggregates assumptions, guiding principles, and conclusions to form a comprehensive framework for the Space Force's approach to operations in a competitive environment. It is not a specific operational plan but rather a strategic mindset that guides the Space Force in sustaining advantage during long-term competition.

## Key Elements

Competitive Endurance is built upon three core tenets:

### 1. Avoid Operational Surprise

The first core tenet requires the Space Force to maintain sufficient situational awareness and intelligence capabilities to ensure it is not caught off guard by adversary actions or unexpected capability breakthroughs in the space domain. This includes:

- **Persistent Surveillance**: Continuous monitoring and tracking of the space domain to maintain awareness of adversary space activities.
- **Intelligence Warning**: Identifying adversary counterspace capabilities under development through intelligence analysis to enable proactive responses.
- **Resilient Architecture**: Building resilient space architectures that can maintain critical capabilities even after a sudden attack.

### 2. Deny First-Mover Advantage

The second core tenet aims to eliminate the possibility of adversaries gaining a decisive advantage by striking first in space. This means:

- **Distribution**: Using distributed and redundant architectures so that adversaries cannot paralyze all space capabilities with a single strike.
- **Rapid Recovery**: Developing means to rapidly rebuild and replenish space capabilities, reducing the strategic value of a first strike.
- **Deterrence Effect**: Making adversaries recognize that a first strike cannot achieve the expected strategic gains, thereby deterring them from initiating attacks.

### 3. Responsible Counterspace Campaigning

The third tenet requires the Space Force to conduct counterspace operations when necessary, but in a responsible manner that avoids uncontrollable escalation risks and long-term damage to the space environment. This includes:

- **Precision Control**: Ensuring counterspace operations have sufficient precision and controllability to avoid collateral damage.
- **Escalation Management**: Fully considering escalation risks when conducting counterspace operations, maintaining consistency with the overall national strategy.
- **Space Environment Responsibility**: Minimizing the long-term impact of counterspace operations on the space environment, such as debris generation.

## Context in Space Force Vector 2025

*Space Force Vector 2025* establishes Competitive Endurance as the Space Force's top-level strategic guidance. The document states:

> "The Space Force has published a theory of success, which we call Competitive Endurance. The logic of Competitive Endurance aggregates assumptions, guiding principles, and conclusions to describe how the Space Force will gain and maintain space superiority."

The Competitive Endurance theory permeates all Space Force activities:

- **Force Design** determines future capabilities and architectures based on the logic of Competitive Endurance.
- **Force Generation** ensures deployable forces meet the requirements of Competitive Endurance.
- **Force Employment** implements the three core tenets of Competitive Endurance in actual operations.
- **DOTMLPF-P** framework elements must all serve the strategic objectives of Competitive Endurance.

The introduction of this theory marks the Space Force's strategic transition from a "capability acquisition" mindset to a "continuous competition" mindset, reflecting the increasingly competitive nature of the space domain.
