# Space Traffic Coordination, STC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The operational layer of STM, referring to the concrete processes through which different spacecraft operators share information and coordinate communications to ensure safe coexistence. STM is the institutional framework; STC is the act of operating within it.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
