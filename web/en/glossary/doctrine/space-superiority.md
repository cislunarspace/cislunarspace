# Space Superiority

> Author: [天疆说](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Site: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Space Superiority is the degree of dominance in the space domain that ensures the Joint Force and the nation can freely access and exploit space while denying adversaries the ability to do the same. The United States Space Force was established with the core mission of gaining and maintaining space superiority on behalf of the Joint Force and the nation. This concept is established as the fundamental mission of the Space Force in *Space Force Vector 2025*.

Space superiority does not require the complete elimination of adversary activities in space. Rather, it ensures sufficient control at critical times and in critical areas to enable friendly space capabilities to function continuously while limiting adversaries' ability to use space in support of their military operations.

## Key Elements

### Domain Access and Exploitation

The primary function of space superiority is to guarantee domain access and domain exploitation:

- **Domain Access**: Ensuring friendly spacecraft can freely launch, operate in orbit, and execute missions without interference or obstruction from adversaries.
- **Domain Exploitation**: Ensuring space capabilities — such as communications, navigation, reconnaissance, and early warning — can continuously support the Joint Force, making space an indispensable component of modern warfare.

### Game-Changing Capabilities

*Space Force Vector 2025* emphasizes that space superiority enables the Space Force to continue delivering "game-changing capabilities." These include:

- Global precision navigation and timing (GPS)
- Global communications coverage
- Missile warning and tracking
- Intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR)
- Environmental monitoring and weather support

### Competitive Environment

In the contemporary security environment, space superiority faces unprecedented challenges. Multiple great powers have developed counterspace capabilities, including anti-satellite weapons, electronic warfare, directed energy weapons, and cyber attack means. Therefore, gaining space superiority is no longer a static objective but an ongoing competitive process.

## Context in Space Force Vector 2025

*Space Force Vector 2025* positions space superiority as the primary mission of the United States Space Force and describes how to gain and maintain it through the Competitive Endurance theory of success. The document states:

> "The Space Force was established to gain and maintain superiority in the space domain on behalf of the Joint Force and our Nation."

Space superiority in the document is not an isolated concept but is closely linked to four service-level activities: Force Design, Force Development, Force Generation, and Force Employment. Together, these activities form a complete system for achieving space superiority:

- **Force Design** determines the space force needed in the future
- **Force Development** optimizes the existing space force
- **Force Generation** builds and sustains deployable force elements
- **Force Employment** translates space power into actual military effects

Furthermore, the realization of space superiority depends on the entire DOTMLPF-P framework — Doctrine, Organization, Training, Materiel, Leader development, Personnel, Facilities, and Policy — all of which must be "purpose-built to contest and control the space domain."
