# Planetary Protection, PP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The principle of preventing biological contamination of celestial bodies by space activities and preventing extraterrestrial organisms from being brought back to Earth. In the STM context, planetary protection directly relates to debris control: landing debris and decommissioned spacecraft both contaminate celestial surfaces and orbital environments.

## Application Value

Planetary protection prevents Earth microorganisms from contaminating other celestial bodies.
