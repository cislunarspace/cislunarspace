> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A national-level space traffic coordination system in the United States, serving as a foundation for developing international open-architecture databases. It aims to integrate government data to support international safety services.
