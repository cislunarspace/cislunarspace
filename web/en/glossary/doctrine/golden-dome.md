# Golden Dome

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Golden Dome is a capability area referenced in *Space Force Vector 2025* for advancing space-based technology associated with space control and integrated space defense systems. The concept represents the Space Force's strategic direction for advancing space control technology and integrated space defense capabilities, aimed at enhancing space situational awareness, space control, and defense through space-based technologies.

Golden Dome is positioned in the document as a significant capability area for advancing space control technology, closely aligned with the Space Force's space superiority mission.

## Core Elements

### Space-Based Technology Advancement

The core of Golden Dome is advancing space-based technologies related to space control:

- **Space Situational Awareness Technology**: Advanced research and development of space-based sensors and surveillance systems
- **Space Control Technology**: Critical enabling technologies required for achieving space domain control
- **Integrated Defense Systems**: Integrating multiple space defense capabilities into a unified system architecture
- **Technology Integration**: Coordinating disparate space control technologies into a coherent capability system

### Space Control Linkage

Golden Dome is closely linked to the space control mission:

- **Active Control**: Ensuring freedom of action for friendly forces in the space domain
- **Passive Defense**: Protecting friendly space assets from adversary threats
- **Domain Control Capability**: Achieving control and influence in specific space regions
- **Technology Advantage Sustainment**: Maintaining technological superiority over potential adversaries through continuous technology development

### Strategic Positioning

Golden Dome's position in the Space Force's technology development strategy:

- **Capability Pillar**: Serves as a technology backbone for the Space Force's core capability areas
- **Technology Foresight**: Technology preparation oriented toward future threat environments
- **Resource Consolidation**: Consolidating space control technology research and development resources scattered across programs
- **Cross-Domain Synergy**: Supporting synergy between space-based capabilities and other domain operations

## Context in Space Force Vector 2025

Golden Dome is explicitly referenced in *Space Force Vector 2025* as a capability area requiring advancement:

> "Advance space-based technology associated with space control and Golden Dome."

While the document's direct exposition of Golden Dome is relatively brief, its placement indicates that it is a significant component of the Space Force's technology development strategy. Golden Dome appears in the context of space control technology, implying its close relationship with space domain control, defense, and situational awareness capabilities.

The introduction of this concept reflects the Space Force's forward-looking thinking about the future space warfare environment—as space militarization deepens, integrated space-based defense systems will become a critical capability for ensuring space superiority.

## Relevance to Cislunar Space

Golden Dome has potentially far-reaching implications for cislunar space defense:

- **Extended Defense Scope**: Space-based integrated defense systems need to cover the vast cislunar space region
- **Lunar Orbit Defense**: Golden Dome technology may be applied to the defense of lunar orbits and Lagrange points
- **Cislunar Space Situational Awareness**: Space-based sensor networks for comprehensive surveillance of cislunar space
- **Technology Validation Platform**: Cislunar space may serve as a frontier validation environment for Golden Dome technologies
