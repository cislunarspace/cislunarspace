# Counterspace Operations

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Counterspace Operations are military activities conducted in the space domain to deny adversary spacepower and prevent space-enabled attacks against the Joint Force. These operations encompass the ability to disrupt, degrade, or destroy enemy space capabilities. Counterspace Operations constitute the core content of the third operational tenet of the United States Space Force: "Responsible Counterspace Campaigning."

Counterspace Operations include both kinetic and non-kinetic means, with the critical constraint that operations must not generate hazardous debris that could imperil friendly space operations.

## Core Elements

### Operational Purpose

The fundamental purpose of Counterspace Operations is to protect the Joint Force from space-enabled threats:

- **Denying Adversary Spacepower**: Preventing opponents from leveraging space assets for intelligence, communications, navigation, and targeting advantages
- **Preventing Space-Enabled Attacks**: Eliminating the ability of adversaries to use space systems to support precision strikes, battlefield situational awareness, and command and control
- **Protecting the Joint Force**: Ensuring that Joint Force operations on land, at sea, and in the air are not threatened by adversary space capabilities

### Operational Means

Counterspace Operations encompass multiple categories of action:

- **Kinetic Means**: Physically destroying or damaging adversary spacecraft through direct strike (e.g., anti-satellite missiles)
- **Non-Kinetic Means**: Degrading or interrupting adversary space system functions through electronic warfare, laser dazzling, high-power microwave, and other directed energy techniques
- **Cyber Means**: Infiltrating, disrupting, or destroying adversary space system command and control chains through cyber attacks
- **Space Situational Control**: Achieving control over specific space regions through maneuver, proximity operations, and surveillance activities

### Responsible Action Principles

The unique constraint of Counterspace Operations lies in achieving military objectives while avoiding harmful consequences:

- **Debris Avoidance**: Operations must not generate hazardous debris that could imperil friendly or allied space operations
- **Controlled Degradation**: Preference for reversible means (e.g., electronic jamming) over irreversible means (e.g., physical destruction)
- **Long-Term Sustainability**: Operational decisions must consider long-term impacts on the space environment
- **International Norm Compliance**: Counterspace operations must be conducted within the framework of international law and space treaties

## Context in Space Force Vector 2025

Counterspace Operations are central to the third tenet of *Space Force Vector 2025*, "Responsible Counterspace Campaigning." The document articulates both the necessity and constraints of these operations:

> "Space forces must also be able to deny adversary spacepower to prevent space-enabled attack against the Joint Force. To that end, space forces must conduct military activities in space that can disrupt, degrade, or destroy enemy space capability."

The document simultaneously emphasizes the requirement for responsible conduct:

> "Critically, this must be achieved without generating hazardous debris that might imperil our own space operations."

This tenet reflects a core tension in U.S. Space Force counterspace doctrine—possessing effective counterspace capabilities while maintaining long-term sustainability of the space environment. This balance demands the development of more precise, controllable, and reversible counterspace technologies.

## Relevance to Cislunar Space

As military space activities extend into cislunar space, the scope of Counterspace Operations is also expanding:

- **High-Orbit Counterspace Challenges**: Detection and engagement of targets beyond geosynchronous orbit is significantly more difficult
- **Lunar Orbit Military Significance**: Control of lunar orbits and Lagrange points holds strategic value
- **Long-Range Operational Constraints**: The cislunar distance imposes real-time command and control communication delay challenges
- **Debris Environment Impact**: Counterspace operations in high orbits can produce debris persisting for centuries
