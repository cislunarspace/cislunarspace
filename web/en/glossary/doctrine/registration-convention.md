# Registration Convention

## Definition

The 1975 Convention on Registration of Objects Launched into Outer Space, requiring States to register space objects with the UN Secretary-General and provide orbital parameters and related information.

## Application Value

The 1975 Convention on Registration of Objects Launched into Outer Space, requiring States to register space objects with the UN Secretary-General and provide orbital parameters and related information has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operation of cislunar space missions and is an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
