# Outer Space Treaty, OST

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The foundational international treaty for space law, entered into force in 1967, establishing that outer space is free for all States to explore and use and is not subject to national appropriation by claim of sovereignty, by means of use or occupation, or by any other means.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
