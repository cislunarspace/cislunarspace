# Space Traffic Coordination, STC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The operational layer of STM, referring to the concrete processes through which different spacecraft operators share information and coordinate communications to ensure safe coexistence. STM is the institutional framework; STC is the act of operating within it.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
