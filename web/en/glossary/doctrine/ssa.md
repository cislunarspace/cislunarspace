# Space Situational Awareness, SSA

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The capability to monitor, track, and predict orbits of objects in space. SSA provides the foundational data for STM, including target identification, orbit determination, and collision probability computation. SSA focuses on the technical surveillance and prediction layer, whereas SDA additionally encompasses intent and threat assessment.

## Application Value

Space situational awareness is the foundation for understanding the space environment.
