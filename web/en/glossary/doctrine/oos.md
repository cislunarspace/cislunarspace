# On-Orbit Servicing

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Services provided to operational spacecraft in orbit, including repair, refueling, upgrade, or life extension. Requires designing transfer trajectories between libration point orbits.

## Application Value

On-orbit servicing extends spacecraft lifetime and reduces mission costs, an important approach for future space system maintenance.
