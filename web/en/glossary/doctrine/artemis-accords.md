# Artemis Accords

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An international cooperation framework for lunar exploration released by the US in 2020, establishing behavioral guidelines for nations and enterprises participating in the Artemis program. Covering principles of peaceful purposes, transparency, interoperability, and emergency assistance, the accords have been signed by over 40 nations and serve as an important foundation for cislunar governance.
