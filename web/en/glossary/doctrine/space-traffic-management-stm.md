# Space Traffic Management, STM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A comprehensive framework of rules, procedures, and technical standards for coordinating spacecraft operations to prevent collisions and ensure safety. While SDA focuses on perceiving and understanding the space environment, STM focuses on decision-making and coordinated actions based on that awareness. Covers the full lifecycle from launch coordination to end-of-life disposal.

## Application Value

STM provides a comprehensive framework for coordinating spacecraft operations to prevent collisions, covering launch coordination through end-of-life disposal.
