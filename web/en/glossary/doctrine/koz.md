> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A safety zone that a spacecraft must not enter during rendezvous operations.

## Application Value

The 禁入区 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
