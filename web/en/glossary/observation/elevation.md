# Elevation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle measured upward from the local horizontal plane to the target direction as seen from a ground station. Below a threshold elevation (typically 15 degrees), tropospheric and ionospheric errors increase sharply, and measurements are typically rejected.

## Application Value

仰角技术在地月空间态势感知中发挥重要作用，有助于提升对空间目标的监测能力 在实际应用中，仰角能够支持光学跟踪和目标识别任务 仰角为地月空间安全运营提供了重要的技术保障手段 利用仰角，可以有效提升对空间目标的探测和跟踪性能
