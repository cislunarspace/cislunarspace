# Lunar Surface SSA

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A cislunar SSA system built by deploying sensors on the lunar surface. Compared to circumlunar spacecraft, lunar surface platforms offer advantages such as unlimited sensor size, phased construction capability, and long mission life. Current research focuses on optimal placement of lunar optical sensors and hybrid architectures combining surface and orbital observers.

## Application Value

在月面态势感知的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
