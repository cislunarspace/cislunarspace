# Lunar Polar Region

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Lunar surface regions where |latitude| ≥ 60°, divided into the north polar region (φ ∈ [60°, 90°]) and south polar region (φ ∈ [-90°, -60°]). Due to special illumination and communication conditions, these are priority exploration areas that impose stringent requirements on constellation coverage design.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
