# Along-Track Baseline

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The component of the inter-spacecraft separation projected along the formation flight direction in interferometric measurements, denoted BH. In a rotation coordinate system with the chief spacecraft as origin and the observation target direction as reference, BH equals the absolute value of the relative position along the y'-axis. The along-track baseline determines the spatial resolution of the interferometer in the flight direction and is typically subject to a lower-bound constraint to ensure interferometric sensitivity.
