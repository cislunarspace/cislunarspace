# Celestial Sky Coverage

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The fraction of the celestial sphere that a radio telescope or radar antenna can observe, expressed as a percentage. The Green Bank Telescope's 100m fully steerable antenna achieves 85% sky coverage; in contrast, the Arecibo planetary radar, being a fixed spherical reflector, can only observe within 20 degrees of zenith, resulting in very limited sky coverage. High sky coverage means more potential targets can be observed and is a key metric for evaluating ground-based detection system capability.
