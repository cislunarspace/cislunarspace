# Wide Angle Camera, WAC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A camera with wide field of view, used for close-range (5 m-1 km) relative navigation. Range error is approximately 2%R, and lateral displacement error is approximately 0.2%R.

## Application Value

宽角相机In 近distance(5m-1km)relativenavigation中has 优势, 测距误差约2%R. In cislunar space交will对接 of 最终逼近段, 宽角相机可提供实When of relativenavigation信息.
