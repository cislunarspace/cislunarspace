# Near-Earth Object, NEO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A small solar system body whose orbit intersects or closely approaches Earth's orbit, including near-Earth asteroids and near-Earth comets. Survey techniques for NEOs, such as shift-and-add and sidereal tracking, share much in common with cislunar object detection methods, and many approaches have been adapted from NEO search programs.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
