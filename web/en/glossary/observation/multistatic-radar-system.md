# Multistatic Radar System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A radar system with multiple physically separated transmitters and receivers that generates at least two simultaneous radar datasets. Fully-active systems consist of identical spacecraft each capable of transmitting and receiving, while semi-active systems have one active illuminator with multiple passive receivers.
