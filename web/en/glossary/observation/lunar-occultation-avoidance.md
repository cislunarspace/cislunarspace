# Lunar Occultation Avoidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A constraint in libration point orbit design that prevents the Moon from blocking the line of communication between the spacecraft and Earth. When selecting halo or Lissajous orbits near the Earth-Moon L2 point, the full arc must be free of lunar occultation of the Earth communication link, directly affecting the choice of orbit amplitude and configuration.

## Application Value

This constraint ensures full-arc visibility of Earth communication links in L2 orbit design, directly affecting orbit amplitude and configuration selection.
