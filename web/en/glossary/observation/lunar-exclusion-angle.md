# Lunar Exclusion Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The minimum angular separation between the Moon and a target below which a sensor ceases observation due to lunar stray light overwhelming the target signal. This paper demonstrates LEA is the dominant variable for tracking uptime: reducing LEA from 5 deg to 1 deg increases trackable time from near-zero to ~50% of the synodic period for L2 Halo targets.

## Application Value

LEA is the dominant variable for optical tracking uptime: reducing LEA from 5 to 1 degree increases trackable time from near-zero to approximately 50% of the synodic period.
