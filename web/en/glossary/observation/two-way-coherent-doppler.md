# Two-Way Coherent Doppler

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A velocity measurement technique in which a ground station transmits an uplink signal to a spacecraft, which coherently transponds it as a downlink signal back to the ground station; the radial velocity is precisely measured by comparing the phase difference between uplink and downlink. Since uplink and downlink frequencies are locked to the same oscillator, measurement precision far exceeds one-way Doppler. NASA DSN's standard two-way coherent Doppler achieves velocity accuracy better than 0.001 m/s, roughly two orders of magnitude better than ground-based radar Doppler precision.

## Application Value

Two-Way Coherent Doppler achieves velocity accuracy better than 0.001 m/s, roughly two orders of magnitude better than ground-based radar Doppler precision. This high-precision measurement technique is essential for cislunar navigation and tracking.
