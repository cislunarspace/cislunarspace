# Cutoff Magnitude, m_co

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The threshold magnitude for visibility determination. A target is deemed visible when its apparent magnitude is brighter (smaller) than this cutoff value. Lower values require more sensitive observation equipment.

## Application Value

This concept is significant for space-based optical observation and space situational awareness, used for evaluating spacecraft visibility to targets.
