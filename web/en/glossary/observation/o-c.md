# Observation Residual, O-C (Observation minus Computation)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The difference between the measured and theoretically computed observables. In cataloguing orbit determination, it is used to assess convergence of orbit improvement, and is theoretically required to follow a white noise distribution.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
