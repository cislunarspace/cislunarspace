# Equivalent Phase Center

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The equivalent detection point defined as the midpoint between transmit and receive positions in bistatic radar. For SAR, the equivalent phase center position is related to target location and SAR processing parameters.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
