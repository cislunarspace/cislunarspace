# Pixel Skewing

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Distortion of pixel shape in bistatic SAR when iso-range and iso-Doppler lines are not perpendicular. When the bistatic angle approaches 0° or 180°, these two lines become nearly parallel, causing severe degradation of imaging performance.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
