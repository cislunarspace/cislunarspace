# Observation Geometry

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The spatial configuration relationship between tracking stations and a target spacecraft, determined jointly by station distribution and target orbit. Better observation geometry yields higher orbit determination accuracy. Three VLBI baselines provide stronger geometric constraints than a single deep space station baseline, resulting in higher combined orbit determination accuracy.

## Application Value

观测几何描述传感器与目标之间的几何关系，对导航精度有重要影响。
