> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The position of a celestial body when its diurnal parallel is tangent to the vertical circle, occurring when the body's declination exceeds the observer's latitude.
