# Space-Based Space Surveillance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An on-orbit deployed space target surveillance satellite system, designed to compensate for the inability of ground-based detection networks to provide seamless spatial and temporal coverage. Typical examples include the US SBSS constellation in LEO and the EU's space-based surveillance capabilities.

## Application Value

Space-based space surveillance systems achieve continuous monitoring of space objects, compensating for coverage blind spots of ground-based systems.
