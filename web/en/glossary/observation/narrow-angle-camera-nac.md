# Narrow Angle Camera, NAC

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A camera with narrow field of view, used for medium-range (1-10 km) relative navigation. Estimates range by measuring the target's angular size in images, and combined with attitude control can achieve high-precision relative state estimation.
