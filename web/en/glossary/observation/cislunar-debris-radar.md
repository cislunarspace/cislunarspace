# Cislunar Debris Radar
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A ground-based radar system designed for detecting and tracking debris in cislunar space. The JPL team built this system in 2023, achieving meter-level tracking of circumlunar targets by combining cislunar space surveillance radar with the Green Bank radio telescope.

## Application Value

The Cislunar Debris Radar plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
