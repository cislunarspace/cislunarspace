# Effective Reflecting Area

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The equivalent area of a space object that produces diffuse-reflected luminous flux under specific illumination and observation geometry. For a sphere, it equals the cross-sectional area multiplied by the radiation angle function, and is one of three factors determining apparent magnitude.

## Application Value

在航天器导航和定位中，该方法用于从传感器数据中提取有效信息，实现自主定轨和轨道预报. 支持地月空间探测任务的实时导航需求.
