# Solar Phase Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle formed by the Sun-target-observer line. The solar phase angle determines the illumination direction and intensity of the target, directly affecting apparent magnitude calculations and observation geometry, and is a key time-varying parameter in cislunar electro-optical monitoring.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
