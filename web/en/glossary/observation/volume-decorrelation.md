# Volume Decorrelation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Phase decorrelation caused by scatterers at different heights within volume scatterers (e.g., forests, ice/snow) in interferometric SAR. Volume decorrelation limits applications such as PolInSAR vegetation height retrieval.

## Application Value

This术语在地月空间任务的设计、分析和控制中具有important应用价值，为相关技术领域的研究和工程实践provides理论支撑。
