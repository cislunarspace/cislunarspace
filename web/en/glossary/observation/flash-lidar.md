# Flash LIDAR

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An active imaging sensor that rapidly acquires 3D elevation maps during motion. A single flash captures distance data across the entire field of view, with maximum operating altitude over 1400 m, elevation accuracy better than 0.1 m, and field of view angle of 6 to 30 degrees. Suited for scenarios requiring real-time terrain mapping during descent, such as planetary landing hazard avoidance.
