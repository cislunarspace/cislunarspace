# Root Mean Square Error, RMSE

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A statistical measure for orbit determination or prediction accuracy, computed as the square root of the mean squared position differences between the determined orbit and the true orbit at all observation epochs. The paper uses RMSE to evaluate orbit determination and prediction accuracy under different observation mechanisms.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
