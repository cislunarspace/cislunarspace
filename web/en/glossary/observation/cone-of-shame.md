# Cone of Shame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The lunar exclusion zone identified by the Air Force Research Laboratory, representing the region blocked by the Moon's body when viewed from Earth. Targets within this cone cannot be observed by ground-based sensors.
