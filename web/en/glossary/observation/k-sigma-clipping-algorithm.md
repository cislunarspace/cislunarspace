# k-Sigma Clipping Algorithm

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An iterative statistical background modeling method. It computes the mean and standard deviation of pixel values, rejects pixels exceeding k standard deviations (considered as celestial signals or outliers), then recomputes statistics. Iteration continues until convergence, yielding a clean background model free from celestial contamination.
