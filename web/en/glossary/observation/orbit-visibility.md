# Orbit Visibility

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The percentage of time within a given period during which an observation satellite on a given orbit can detect a target area. Three conditions must be met simultaneously: the target is within the field of view, the calculated apparent magnitude does not exceed the limiting value, and the target is not within the celestial body exclusion angle. The paper uses apparent magnitude ≤ 18.5 as the criterion.

## Application Value

在轨道可见性的设计与分析中，可用于优化转移方案，减少燃料消耗 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
