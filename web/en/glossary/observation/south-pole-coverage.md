# South Pole Coverage

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A design objective of ensuring the lunar south pole region is continuously within line-of-sight of at least one satellite in a constellation. The lunar south pole is the preferred site for future lunar bases due to potential water ice resources and continuous illumination. The core challenge is that the tilt and nutation of the lunar spin axis cause the south pole position to vary in inertial space, requiring sufficient margin in orbit design.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
