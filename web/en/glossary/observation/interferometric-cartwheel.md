# Interferometric Cartwheel

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A passive formation concept consisting of one active illuminator satellite and several receive-only microsatellites. The receiver satellites operate on near-circular orbits with slight eccentricity differences, producing periodically varying interferometric baselines.
