# Adaptive Bounding Box
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A target bounding box whose size is automatically adjusted based on the target's morphological features (major axis length). Size is determined by major axis times expansion factor k plus offset b. Different k and b parameters are used for tracked targets versus other targets, balancing tracking precision (compact boxes reduce mismatches) and fast target capture (expanded boxes cover large displacements).

## Application Value

The Adaptive Bounding Box plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
