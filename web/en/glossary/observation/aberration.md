# Aberration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The apparent shift in the observed position of a celestial body due to the motion of the observer.

## Application Value

光行差 is observe者motion导致 of 天体视位置偏移, is highaccuracy天文observe和navigation中must修正 of 效应. In cislunar spacemeasure建模中, 光行差修正对提high定位accuracy有important role.
