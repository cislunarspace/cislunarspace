# Instantaneous Coverage Rate

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The percentage of the GEO belt covered by the observation system at any single instant. Simulations show a single satellite covers approximately 72-75% with both cameras operational, 36-38% with one camera, and 0% during complete blackout. The three-satellite system maintains coverage through complementarity, ensuring at least two satellites are operational at any time.
