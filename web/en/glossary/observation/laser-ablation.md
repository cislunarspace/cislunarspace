# Laser Ablation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique that uses high-power laser irradiation on debris surfaces to cause vaporization and generate thrust, thereby altering the debris orbit. Proposed as a candidate technology for active removal of high-risk cislunar debris, to be paired with space-based quantum radar for all-weather tracking and removal.

## Application Value

Laser Ablation is significant for spacecraft tracking, identification, and perception in cislunar space.
