# Sensor Tasking

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The optimization process of assigning observation tasks to sensors under resource constraints. The goal is to distribute observation time and pointing across multiple space objects to maximize overall surveillance effectiveness. In cislunar space, sensor tasking is more challenging than in near-Earth space due to long orbital periods and complex illumination geometry.
