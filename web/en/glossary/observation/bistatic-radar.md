# Bistatic Radar

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A radar system in which the transmitter and receiver are stationed at geographically separate locations. In cislunar debris detection, the transmit and receive sites can be hundreds or even thousands of kilometers apart, with large-aperture antennas dedicated to transmitting and receiving respectively. In 2016, JPL first demonstrated this by using Goldstone (transmitter) and Green Bank (receiver) to successfully detect the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter. The advantage of bistatic configuration is that the transmit site can use the highest-power antenna while the receive site can use the most sensitive antenna, each optimized for its role.

## Application Value

Bistatic Radar is significant for spacecraft tracking, identification, and perception in cislunar space.
