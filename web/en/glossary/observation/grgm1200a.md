# GRGM1200A Lunar Gravity Field Model

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lunar gravity field spherical harmonic model published by NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, expanded up to degree and order 1200. This paper truncates it to degree and order 8 for computing lunar gravitational acceleration.

## Application Value

GRGM1200A Lunar Gravity Field Model is used to evaluate the observation performance of cislunar spacecraft, guiding mission planning and constellation deployment.
