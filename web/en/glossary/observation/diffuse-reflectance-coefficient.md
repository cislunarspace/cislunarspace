# Diffuse Reflectance Coefficient

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter describing the diffuse reflection capability of a surface, ranging from 0 to 1. The diffuse reflectance coefficient of satellite surface materials is typically between 0.3 and 0.7, and is a key parameter for calculating the apparent magnitude of space objects.

## Application Value

该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
