# Intent Recognition

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The capability in cislunar space situational awareness to assess the functional characteristics, operational purposes, and intentions of space targets. The paper identifies it as a core capability at the 'precise assessment' level, requiring inference of behavioral intent from target motion states, supporting the leap from simple cataloguing to comprehensive domain awareness.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
