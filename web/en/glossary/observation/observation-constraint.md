# Observation Constraint

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Constraints on formation geometric parameters imposed by interferometric observation missions. As formulated in this paper: the along-track baseline must exceed a given lower bound (to ensure flight-direction resolution), and the cross-track baseline must lie within upper and lower bounds (to ensure cross-direction resolution while avoiding baseline ambiguity). The optimization objective for formation configuration design is to maximize the duration of effective observation while satisfying these constraints.

## Application Value

在航天器导航和定位中，该方法用于从传感器数据中提取有效信息，实现自主定轨和轨道预报. 支持地月空间探测任务的实时导航需求.
