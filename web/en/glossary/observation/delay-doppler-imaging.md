# Delay-Doppler Imaging

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A radar imaging technique that produces two-dimensional images of a target using time delay and Doppler shift of the echo signal. Time delay maps to the range distribution of scattering centers, while Doppler shift maps to their radial velocity distribution; the two dimensions combined reconstruct a 2D radar cross-section image. In 2016, JPL achieved a range resolution of 37.5 m/pixel in delay-Doppler imaging of Chandrayaan-1, the first radar range measurement of a spacecraft at lunar distance.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
