# Least Squares

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method for estimating orbital elements by minimizing the sum of squared residuals between observed and model-predicted values. Linear least squares is used for linear problems; nonlinear least squares is used for iterative solutions in orbit determination.
