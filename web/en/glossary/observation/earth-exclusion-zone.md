# Earth Exclusion Zone (EEZ)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Zone where an object is not visible when the angle between the object-observer line and the object-Earth line is below a threshold (e.g., 10 degrees).

## Application Value

This term has important application value in the design, analysis, and control of cislunar space missions, providing theoretical support for research and engineering practice in related technical fields.
