# Subsatellite Point

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The point where the line connecting satellite and Earth's center intersects Earth's surface, i.e., the projection of satellite on ground

## Application Value

在编目系统中，该技术可提高对空间目标的探测、跟踪和编目能力。
