# Neutral Atom Analyzer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A scientific instrument mounted on the rover to observe energetic neutral atoms and positive ions in the 10 to 100 eV energy range at roving exploration points. Developed in collaboration with Sweden. Energetic neutral atoms (ENA) are neutral particles formed when solar wind ions interact with lunar surface materials through sputtering processes and acquire electrons, carrying information about the lunar surface plasma environment. This represents the first international ENA detection conducted on the lunar surface.

## Application Value

The neutral atom analyzer is an important remote sensing instrument for understanding lunar surface space environment. Energetic neutral atoms originate from charge exchange processes between solar wind ions and lunar surface materials; by measuring ENA energy and flux, scientists can invert lunar surface material chemical composition and surface properties. Chang'e-4's ENA detection on the lunar far side provided the first in-situ measurement data of lunar surface plasma environment, offering important basis for understanding lunar surface charging effects and space weather forecasting. Mounted on the rover, this instrument can achieve multi-point measurement during movement, mapping lunar surface plasma environment distribution and helping assess impacts of extreme lunar night conditions on detectors.
