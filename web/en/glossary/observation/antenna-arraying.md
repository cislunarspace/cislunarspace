# Antenna Arraying

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition
A technique that synthesizes signals from multiple antennas to increase effective collecting area or transmit power. Receiver arraying combines signals from multiple antennas to improve sensitivity; transmitter arraying synchronizes multiple antennas to emit the same signal to boost equivalent isotropic radiated power. China has achieved 4-antenna receiver arraying at the Kashi deep space station (X-band reception comparable to the 66m Jiamusi antenna) and demonstrated 3-antenna transmitter arraying (80% combining efficiency). Antenna arraying is a core technical approach for enhancing cislunar debris detection sensitivity.

## Application Value
This term has application value in the design and analysis of cislunar space missions, supporting trajectory design, mission planning, and system optimization. Researchers can analyze its physical mechanisms and engineering applicability based on specific mission requirements to advance cislunar space exploration technology.
