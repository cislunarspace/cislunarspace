# Motion Blur Streak

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A linear brightness pattern formed in astronomical images when a moving space target displaces during the exposure. Streak length depends on target velocity and exposure time, with brightness following an error function distribution along the motion direction. In cislunar observations, low-orbit fast-moving targets often appear as streaks.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
