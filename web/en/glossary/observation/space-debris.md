# Space Debris

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Non-functional man-made objects in orbit, generally including rocket bodies, defunct satellites, and mission-related debris. Due to their high orbital velocities, collisions with spacecraft can cause degradation or loss of exposed sensor surfaces, functional failures, or even catastrophic breakup. The majority of un-catalogued space debris poses a serious threat to spacecraft operations.
