# SDA Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Constellations designed to monitor space objects within a specific range in cislunar space, including monitoring of specific volume regions and objects in specific orbits, requiring optical or other sensing capabilities with more complex visibility criteria than communication and navigation constellations.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and engineering practice, helping to understand core principles of the field.
