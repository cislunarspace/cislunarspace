# Campaign Duration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The total duration of an SDA observation campaign. In this paper, 1825 days (5 years) is used as the minimum estimated nominal mission duration.


## Application Value

该导航或观测技术在地月空间任务中可用于目标跟踪、相对导航和轨道确定，提升任务自主性和安全性。
