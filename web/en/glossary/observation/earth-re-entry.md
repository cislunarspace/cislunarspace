# Earth Re-entry

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The event of debris entering Earth's atmosphere. Unlike LEO re-entries, debris originating from cislunar space have higher re-entry velocities (~10.5-11.5 km/s) and larger re-entry angles (median ~45°), meaning debris may begin breaking up at higher altitudes but not necessarily fully ablate in the atmosphere.
