# Lunar-Based Low-Frequency Radio Astronomical Observation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Deploying radio receivers on the lunar surface to conduct radio astronomical observations in the 0.1 to 40 MHz band, exploiting the lunar farside's natural shielding from terrestrial radio interference. On Earth, ionospheric interference makes observations below 10 MHz nearly impossible; even the lunar nearside is affected by Earth's ionospheric reflection and artificial radio noise. The lunar farside is the only location in the solar system that simultaneously shields from human-made radio interference and natural lightning/auroral emissions, potentially filling the observation gap in the 0.1 to 1 MHz band for studying solar wind shocks and coronal mass ejections.

## Application Value

This observation method exploits the unique radio-quiet environment of the lunar farside, enabling astronomical observations in frequency bands impossible to conduct from Earth or the lunar nearside.
