# Space-Based Tracking Station

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An observation platform deployed in orbit to track and measure space objects. Compared to ground-based stations, space-based stations are free from atmospheric interference and offer wider coverage, but their own orbit accuracy is a significant source of orbit determination error.
