# Short-Arc Orbit Determination Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A core challenge in cislunar initial orbit determination where extremely long orbital periods (some near 6 months) mean that even 1-2 hours of continuous observation covers less than a fraction of a percent of the complete orbit, making it difficult to converge on orbital information.

## Application Value

The 观测弧过短问题 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
