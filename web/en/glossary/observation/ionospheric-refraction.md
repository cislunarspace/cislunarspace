# Ionospheric refraction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Bending and delay of radio signals passing through the ionosphere due to refractive index changes caused by free electrons, producing range errors inversely proportional to the square of the frequency. Can be eliminated using dual-frequency measurements.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
