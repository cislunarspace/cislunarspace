# Observation Accuracy

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The measurement precision of inter-satellite ranging data. The paper simulates observation error as 1-meter Gaussian white noise; orbit determination accuracy is proportional to observation accuracy, and converges to the observation accuracy level when the sampling arc is sufficiently long.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
