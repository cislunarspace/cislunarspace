# Pixel Scale

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angular distance on the sky corresponding to one pixel of the imaging system, typically expressed in arcseconds per pixel (″/pixel). It is determined by the telescope focal length and detector pixel size, and directly governs the angular resolution and field of view. A typical 40 cm telescope has a pixel scale of about 1-2 arcseconds per pixel.

## Application Value

该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
