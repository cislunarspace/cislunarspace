# Diffuse Reflection Coefficient

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter quantifying the diffuse reflection component of a target's surface, affecting brightness in the apparent magnitude formula. A higher value means more light is reflected in all directions. The paper sets it to 0.2 with specular reflection coefficient set to 0, considering only ideal diffuse reflection.


## Application Value

This concept is of significant importance in cislunar space research and technical applications, helping to deepen understanding of related physical mechanisms and engineering implementations.
