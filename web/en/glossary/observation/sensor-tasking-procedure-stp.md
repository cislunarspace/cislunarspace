# Sensor Tasking Procedure, STP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A scheduling strategy that determines when observers take measurements and which targets they observe. Three procedures were tested: STP-A (all observers measure in unison), STP-B (sequential equally-spaced measurements), and STP-C (alternating group measurements).

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
