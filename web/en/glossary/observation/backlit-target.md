# Backlit Target

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

When the solar phase angle approaches 180 degrees, the target is positioned between the observer and the Sun, with light coming from behind. The target's surface is poorly illuminated, making it difficult for optical sensors to detect.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
