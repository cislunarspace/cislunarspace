# Cooperative Agent, CA

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A third-party user model introduced in this paper, representing a spacecraft that needs to use an SDA architecture to accomplish its own mission (e.g., transferring to L1/L2). Given full knowledge of the architecture layout, the CA generates optimal transfer trajectories by minimizing its own propellant consumption (delta-v) while maximizing its detectability by the architecture.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
