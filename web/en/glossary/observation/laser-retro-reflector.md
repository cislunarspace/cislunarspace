# Laser Retro-Reflector

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A passive optical device composed of three mutually orthogonal reflecting surfaces that reflects incident laser light back along its original direction. The laser retro-reflector carried on the Chang'e-4 relay satellite is a monolithic 17 cm hollow cube-corner reflector weighing 1.6 kg. Combined with ground-based laser ranging systems, it enables pure reflective laser ranging experiments over Earth-Moon distances with an expected one-way ranging error better than 15 mm.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
