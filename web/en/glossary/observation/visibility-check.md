# Visibility Check

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Determining whether an unobstructed line of sight exists between a lunar surface point and a CN satellite, based on whether the elevation angle h meets or exceeds the critical elevation angle h₀. For the atmosphereless Moon, h₀ can be set to 0°.
