# Celestial Body Exclusion Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The minimum angular separation required for an observation satellite to distinguish a space object from the background light of a celestial body. When the angle between the satellite-object line and the satellite-body line is smaller than this threshold, the object cannot be identified due to celestial body radiation. The exclusion angles for the Sun, Earth, and Moon are approximately 30°, 15°, and 6° respectively.

## Application Value

在编目系统中，该技术可提高对空间目标的探测、跟踪和编目能力。
