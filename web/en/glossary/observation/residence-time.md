# Residence Time

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The cumulative time a debris object spends inside a protected region during each crossing. The paper finds that the average residence time peaks for fragments in LEO and GEO regions are both less than 0.1 hours, because fragments cross these regions at very high velocities.
