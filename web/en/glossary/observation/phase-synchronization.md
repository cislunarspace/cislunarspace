# Phase Synchronization

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The process of aligning the phase of frequency sources between subsystems in a multistatic radar system, key to achieving coherent interferometry. Phase synchronization is far more demanding than time synchronization and typically requires dedicated synchronization subsystems.
