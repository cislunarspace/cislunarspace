# Cross-Track Baseline

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The component of the inter-spacecraft separation projected onto the plane perpendicular to the flight direction in interferometric measurements, denoted BV. Influenced by the payload look-down angle and computed via rotation matrix transformation, involving the projection length and azimuth of the relative position in the cross-track plane. The cross-track baseline determines the interferometer spatial resolution perpendicular to the flight direction, subject to both lower and upper bounds to avoid insufficient resolution or baseline ambiguity.

## Application Value

在观测系统设计中，需要考虑传感器性能、观测几何和数据处理方法。该概念支撑目标检测、跟踪和识别等任务。
