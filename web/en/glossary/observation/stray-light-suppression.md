# Stray Light Suppression

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Techniques to suppress or eliminate non-target light sources (direct sunlight, Earth-atmosphere radiation, sky background, etc.) entering the optical system FOV. Stray light suppression is especially critical for long-range cislunar observations where target signals are extremely faint (~21 apparent magnitude) and even weak stray light can overwhelm the signal. The paper's dual-limb observation mode and solar suppression/Earth-atmosphere off-axis angle designs are fundamentally stray light suppression measures.

## Application Value

杂散光抑制技术在地月空间态势感知中发挥重要作用，有助于提升对空间目标的监测能力 在实际应用中，杂散光抑制能够支持光学跟踪和目标识别任务 杂散光抑制为地月空间安全运营提供了重要的技术保障手段 利用杂散光抑制，可以有效提升对空间目标的探测和跟踪性能
