# Non-Cooperative Target Search and Tracking

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technical system for detecting, locating, and continuously tracking uncooperative or adversarial spacecraft. In cislunar space, it faces three challenges: sparse measurement data due to limited sensor coverage, strongly nonlinear state evolution from three-body gravity, and amplified uncertainty from unknown target maneuvers. A typical approach is a two-phase search-track strategy: a search phase using particle filters to construct reachable domains and search globally, switching to UKF for high-precision tracking once the target is detected.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
