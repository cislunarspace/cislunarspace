# Range measurement (radio)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Measuring the round-trip propagation time of a radio signal between a ground station and spacecraft, multiplied by the speed of light to obtain distance. The two-way average range is approximately equal to the geometric range at the midpoint of signal travel time.
