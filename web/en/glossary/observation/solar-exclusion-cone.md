# Solar Exclusion Cone

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A conical region with the observer at the apex and the Sun direction as the axis. When a target falls within this cone, optical instruments cannot effectively observe it due to intense solar stray light. The half-angle of the cone is the solar exclusion angle (typically 30 degrees). The solar exclusion cone creates intermittent gaps in the observable region of periodic orbits, making it a critical constraint for evaluating optical observation mission performance in cislunar space.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
