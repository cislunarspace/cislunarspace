# Observation Constellation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Constellations designed for specific observation targets, such as constellations for Earth polar region or lunar south pole observation, leveraging orbits close to observation targets (e.g., clover-shaped orbits, L2 vertical Lyapunov orbits) to achieve continuous coverage.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
