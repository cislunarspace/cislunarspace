# Along-Track Interferometry, ATI

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An interferometric technique that measures target velocity by comparing time-delayed radar echoes acquired by two phase centers separated along the flight direction. Applied to ocean current monitoring and moving target indication.

## Application Value

Along-Track Interferometry, ATI provides technical support for space situational awareness and target tracking, playing a key role in satellite navigation, space debris monitoring, and deep space communications.
