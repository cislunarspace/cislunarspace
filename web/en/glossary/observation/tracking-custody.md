# Tracking Custody

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Maintaining accurate knowledge of a target's orbit through periodic observations to update orbital elements. This paper's core goal is evaluating whether GEO/HEO sensors can track L2 dim targets frequently enough to support periodic custody updates.

## Application Value

Tracking Custody is significant for spacecraft tracking, identification, and perception in cislunar space.
