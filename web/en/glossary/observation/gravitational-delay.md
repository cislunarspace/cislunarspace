# Gravitational Delay

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The time delay in signal propagation through a gravitational field due to differences in gravitational potential, one of the model correction terms in inter-satellite link measurements.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
