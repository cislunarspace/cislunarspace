# Declination, Dec

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The latitude component in the celestial equatorial coordinate system, defined as the angle between the target position vector and the equatorial plane, positive northward. Together with right ascension, it constitutes the observable of space-based and ground-based optical observation.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
