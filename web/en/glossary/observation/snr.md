# Signal-to-Noise Ratio

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of target signal strength to background noise, the core metric for assessing detectability and trackability of dim targets. SNR of 0 means the target is invisible (no line of sight or within an exclusion zone). No consensus exists on minimum acceptable SNR (ranging from 1.5 to 6).

## Application Value

Signal-to-noise ratio is a key signal quality indicator.
