# Resident Space Object, RSO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Active satellites, orbital debris, and noncooperative satellites in cislunar space. SDA systems must detect, track, and characterize these objects.

## Application Value

Objects long-existing in cislunar space including operational satellites, dead rocket bodies, and breakup debris, targets for space situational awareness.
