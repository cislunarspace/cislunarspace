# Average Coverage Ratio

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of the lunar surface area where GDOP is better than 10 to the total lunar surface area, averaged over the entire constellation operation period. Unlike instantaneous coverage ratio, it reflects the overall coverage performance rather than a snapshot at a specific time.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
