> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

## Definition

Space telescope launched in 2021 by the US, operating in a Sun-Earth L₂ halo orbit; used as validation target for proximity operations and servicing scenarios in this paper.
