# Scintillation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A phenomenon in which radar echo signal intensity fluctuates dramatically over time. LRO's radar echo exhibited pronounced scintillation: when the solar panels were perpendicular to the radar line of sight, they efficiently reflected radar signals producing strong echoes; when parallel, the target was nearly 'invisible' with extremely weak returns. This periodic intensity variation is caused by the target's attitude motion and serves as an important characteristic signature for identifying large spacecraft in radar echoes.
