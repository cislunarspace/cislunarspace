# Orbit Determination Arc Length
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The time span covered by the observation data used for orbit determination. Longer arc lengths generally yield higher orbit determination accuracy but impose greater reliance on dynamical models.

## Application Value

The Orbit Determination Arc Length plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
