# Lunar Libration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The phenomenon allowing observation of more than half the Moon's surface due to the equality of its rotation and revolution periods combined with orbital characteristics, divided into optical and physical libration.
