# GEO Belt

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A ring-like region centered at geostationary altitude (~36,000 km) extending a few hundred kilometers above and below, where the majority of GEO satellites reside. About two-thirds of GEO satellites have altitude deviations within 100 km, eccentricities mostly below 0.002, and inclinations between 0 and 15 degrees. It is the primary surveillance region for high-orbit space situational awareness.
