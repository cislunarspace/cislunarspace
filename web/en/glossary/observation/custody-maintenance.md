# Custody Maintenance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ability to maintain continuous tracking of a space object, knowing its orbital state, position, and maneuver status at all times. Loss of custody requires costly reacquisition. Custody maintenance depends on sensor layout, sensitivity, illumination conditions, and observation geometry.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
