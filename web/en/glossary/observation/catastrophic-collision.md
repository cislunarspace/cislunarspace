# Catastrophic Collision

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A catastrophic disintegration event caused by a spacecraft colliding at high speed with another orbital object. Unlike explosion break-up, the fragment characteristics from collision break-up require separate modeling and analysis.
