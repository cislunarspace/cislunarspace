# Quantum Optical Sensor

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A sensor leveraging quantum optical principles to enhance detection sensitivity. The paper proposes combining it with anti-Rayleigh scattering algorithms to suppress lunar reflection interference, potentially enabling sensitive detection of meter-scale debris in cislunar space as a future SSA technology direction.


## Application Value

This optimization method has wide applications in cislunar space trajectory design, suitable for scenarios such as trajectory optimization and mission planning.
