# Non-Cooperative Target
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A space object that does not actively transmit cooperative signals (such as transponder signals or ranging beacons). Its orbit determination can only rely on passive observation methods (such as optical angle measurement), precluding high-precision range and velocity measurements.

## Application Value

The Non-Cooperative Target plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
