# Surface Coverage Percentage, SCP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The ratio of the lunar surface area continuously covered by at least one CN satellite (CTP = 100%) to the total lunar surface area. Computed by discretizing the lunar surface into a grid and counting the proportion of grid cells with 100% continuous coverage.

## Application Value

Surface Coverage Percentage, SCP is used to evaluate the observation performance of cislunar spacecraft, guiding mission planning and constellation deployment.
