# Coverage Time Percentage, CTP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A metric for the continuous availability of positioning service provided by a constellation to a specific lunar surface area. Defined as the ratio of the time during which the GDOP value is better than 10 to the total constellation operation time. A higher percentage means fewer service interruptions and better coverage continuity.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
