# Space-Based Surveillance System
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A surveillance system deployed on orbital platforms for space object monitoring. Compared to ground-based systems, it has inherent advantages in site selection and immunity to atmospheric interference. The US is developing systems like the Next-Gen Space Architecture, GSSAP, and CHPS, though current limitations in aperture, stray light, and constellation size mean ground-based systems remain primary with space-based systems in supporting roles.

## Application Value

The Space-Based Surveillance System plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
