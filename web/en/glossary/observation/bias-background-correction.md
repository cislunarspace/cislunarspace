# Bias/Background Correction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The first step of astronomical image preprocessing, which removes the fixed-pattern noise (bias signal) produced by CCD detectors at zero exposure time, including electronic offset and fixed components of thermal dark current. Corrected images more faithfully represent celestial signals.

## Application Value
