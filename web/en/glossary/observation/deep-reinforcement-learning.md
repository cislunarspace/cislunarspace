# Deep Reinforcement Learning

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An intelligent decision-making method combining deep neural networks with reinforcement learning. The paper proposes applying it to dynamic scheduling of cislunar observation resources, fusing optical, radar, and passive RF data to reduce observation gaps and fill blind spots behind the Moon.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
