# Radar Cross Section

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A physical quantity characterizing a target's ability to reflect radar electromagnetic waves, measured in square meters. A larger RCS produces stronger radar echoes and makes the target easier to detect. LRO has a large effective RCS due to its deployed 4.26 m x 3.2 m solar panels, yielding strong and clear echoes; Chandrayaan-1 has smaller panels and lower RCS; Ouna is the smallest with no deployed panels, producing the weakest echoes. RCS determines the difficulty of ground-based radar detection for different targets.

## Application Value

Radar cross section measures target reflectivity to radar waves.
