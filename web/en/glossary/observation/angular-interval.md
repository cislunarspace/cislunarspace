# Angular Interval

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The inverse of the relative angular rate between observer and target, measured in degrees per day. A larger angular interval means the target moves faster across the observer's field of view, providing more geometric diversity for orbit determination. A smaller angular interval means the target appears nearly stationary, reducing estimation accuracy due to insufficient geometric diversity.

## Application Value

在轨道设计和转移轨道优化中，该方法用于确定最优转移时机和轨道形状，以最小化燃料消耗或飞行时间. 通过数值仿真和迭代优化，可获得满足任务约束的可行轨道方案.
