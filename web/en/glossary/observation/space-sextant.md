# Space Sextant

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Optical instrument for measuring angular separations between celestial bodies or between a body and the horizon from a spacecraft; key equipment for autonomous celestial navigation on Apollo spacecraft.

## Application Value

支撑自主导航系统的开发，提高轨道确定精度 为目标跟踪和状态估计提供关键技术支撑。
