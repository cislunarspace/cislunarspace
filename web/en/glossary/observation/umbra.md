# Umbra

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The dark region of complete shadow during an eclipse where the light source is fully blocked.

## Application Value

The Umbra represents the dark region of complete shadow during an eclipse where the light source is fully blocked. Understanding umbra geometry is essential for thermal control and power planning of cislunar spacecraft.
