# Space Object Detection

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The technique of identifying and locating space objects in astronomical images, comprising three steps: background estimation, object detection, and brightness measurement. Cislunar objects pose significantly greater detection challenges than near-Earth objects due to their distance and weak signals.

## Application Value

Space Object Detection identifies and locates space objects in astronomical images through background estimation, object detection, and brightness measurement. Cislunar objects pose significantly greater detection challenges than near-Earth objects due to their distance and weak signals.
