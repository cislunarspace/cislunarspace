# Critical Baseline

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The baseline length that causes a 2π phase change in interferometric processing. When the baseline exceeds this critical value, interferometric correlation is completely lost. In practice, the operating baseline must be controlled to a small fraction (e.g., 5%-10%) of the critical baseline.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
