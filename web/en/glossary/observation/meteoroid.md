# Meteoroid

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Naturally occurring small meteoritic bodies in space. Similar to space debris, high-velocity meteoroid impacts on spacecraft can degrade or destroy exposed sensor surfaces and sensitive components. They are an important class of objects for cislunar space situational awareness and protection.
