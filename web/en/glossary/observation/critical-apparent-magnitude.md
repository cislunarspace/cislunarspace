# Critical Apparent Magnitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The brightness threshold corresponding to a given observability ratio α. An object is considered observable when its apparent magnitude is less than (brighter than) m_ca. This metric reflects the observation capability of a periodic orbit over a specified time interval: a smaller m_ca means the orbit can detect fainter objects during its visible windows.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
