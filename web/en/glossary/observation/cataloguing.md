# Cataloguing
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The process of orbit determination, numbering, and classification of space targets to establish an orbital database. Cataloguing is a fundamental component of space situational awareness; un-catalogued debris is difficult to track or warn against, posing hidden threats to spacecraft safety.

## Application Value

The Cataloguing plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
