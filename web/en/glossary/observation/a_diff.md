# Diffuse Reflectance, a_diff

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The proportion of incident light that is scattered uniformly in all directions by the target surface. Together with specular reflectance, it determines the overall reflective properties of the target.

## Application Value

This concept has application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
