# Apparent Magnitude Threshold
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A cutoff value for the accessibility metric that determines whether a target is observable. A target is considered visible when its apparent magnitude is below this threshold, and invisible otherwise. The paper uses magnitude 17 as the threshold.

## Application Value

The Apparent Magnitude Threshold plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
