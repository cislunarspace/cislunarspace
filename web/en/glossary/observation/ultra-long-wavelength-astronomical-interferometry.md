# Ultra-Long Wavelength Astronomical Interferometry

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique using multiple spatially distributed radio receivers to synthesize high angular resolution ultra-long wavelength (meter to decameter wavelength) radio astronomical images through signal interferometry. The two Chang'e-4 piggyback microsatellites each carry an ultra-long wavelength detector, performing formation flying interferometry with 1 to 10 km baselines in lunar orbit for all-sky ultra-long wavelength imaging, radio spectral measurement, and observation of planetary ultra-long wavelength radio activity in the solar system.

## Application Value

在态势感知系统中，该观测方法为地月空间目标检测与跟踪提供关键数据支撑，是空间交通管理的技术基础。
