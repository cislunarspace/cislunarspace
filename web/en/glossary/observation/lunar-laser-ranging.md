# Lunar Laser Ranging

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique that precisely measures the Earth-Moon distance by transmitting laser pulses from ground stations to lunar surface reflectors and measuring the round-trip travel time. Chang'e-4 carries a laser retro-reflector on the relay satellite, combined with ground-based 0.5 m transmitting and 1 m receiving telescopes, to achieve the first international pure reflective laser ranging over Earth-Moon distances, providing orbit verification data for the relay satellite.
