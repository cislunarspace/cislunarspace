# Observability Metric, Gamma

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The coverage metric defined in this paper. It represents the fraction of time over the observation campaign during which the targets in the grid are visible to the observers, quantifying the effectiveness of a cislunar SDA architecture.

## Application Value

This concept is significant for space-based optical observation and space situational awareness, used for evaluating spacecraft visibility to targets.
