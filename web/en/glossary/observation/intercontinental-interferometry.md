# Intercontinental Interferometry

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Interferometric measurement using stations located on different continents to form ultra-long baselines. Compared with domestic VLBI baselines (~1000 km), intercontinental baselines reach ~10000 km, significantly improving angular resolution. For example, the baseline formed by Jiamusi and Namibia deep space stations can be used for high-precision orbit determination of lunar and deep space targets.

## Application Value

洲际干涉测量技术在地月空间态势感知中发挥重要作用，有助于提升对空间目标的监测能力 在实际应用中，洲际干涉测量能够支持光学跟踪和目标识别任务 洲际干涉测量为地月空间安全运营提供了重要的技术保障手段 利用洲际干涉测量，可以有效提升对空间目标的探测和跟踪性能
