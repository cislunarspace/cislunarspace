# Baseline Delay

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The fundamental VLBI observable. The time difference between a signal from the same source arriving at two different stations, containing angular position information of the source. Longer baselines yield higher angular resolution. In deep space tracking, domestic VLBI baselines span about a thousand kilometers, while intercontinental baselines can reach tens of thousands of kilometers.

## Application Value

在态势感知系统中，该观测方法为低轨空间目标监视提供技术手段，是空间交通管理的数据来源。
