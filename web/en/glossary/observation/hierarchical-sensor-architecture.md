# Hierarchical Sensor Architecture

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A near-far collaborative observation scheme for cislunar space proposed by the University of Arizona. Long-range guidance uses RF radio, switching to optical telescope for close-range precision tracking. The team successfully predicted the impact parameters of the Chang'e 5 booster with a 0.5 m optical telescope, validating the architecture.


## Application Value

视觉导航不依赖地面站设备，适合地月空间的自主相对导航，通过特征点提取和滤波估计实现追踪航天器相对目标的位置姿态估计。
