# Space-Based Optical Observation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique for observing space objects using optical sensors deployed in orbit. The observables are right ascension and declination. Its advantages include wide coverage and immunity to atmospheric interference, making it an important complement to ground-based observation.

## Application Value

在编目系统中，该技术可提高对空间目标的探测、跟踪和编目能力。天基观测可弥补地基观测在远距离和观测几何上的不足。
