# Exposure Time

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The duration over which the detector continuously collects light from the target. Longer exposure times yield more photons and higher SNR, but also produce longer trailed star images. In cislunar object surveys, exposure times typically range from 15 to 120 seconds depending on the apparent brightness of the target.

## Application Value

该概念在地月空间任务设计中具有重要应用价值，涉及轨道设计、导航控制或动力学分析等多个方面，为地月空间探测任务提供理论支撑.
