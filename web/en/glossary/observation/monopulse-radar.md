# Monopulse Radar

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A tracking radar technique that obtains target angle information from a single pulse by comparing signals across multiple beams or within a beam pattern. It enables high-accuracy angular measurement in a single look, making it well-suited for continuous tracking of distant targets. In this study, a 100-m monopulse radar achieves angular accuracy of ~0.15 mrad at 100,000 km range.

## Application Value

This concept is applied in analysis and design, with important implications for mission performance.
