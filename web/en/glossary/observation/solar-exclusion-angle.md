# Solar Exclusion Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The minimum sun angle constraint imposed by optical sensors to avoid direct solar illumination interference. In cislunar SSA system design, the solar exclusion angle limits effective observation periods and spatial coverage, and is an important parameter for evaluating system coverage performance.

## Application Value

太阳排除角是避免太阳干扰通信的最小角距，通信系统设计师据此设计天线指向策略和通信窗口规划。
