# Lunar Penetrating Radar

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A scientific instrument mounted on the rover to detect regolith thickness and shallow crustal structure along the roving path by transmitting and receiving radar electromagnetic waves. After penetrating the lunar surface, differences in electromagnetic properties at different depths produce reflected echoes that can be processed to reconstruct subsurface layering information. The Chang'e-4 lunar penetrating radar is an international collaboration payload for achieving the scientific objective of shallow structure investigation in the lunar farside roving area.
