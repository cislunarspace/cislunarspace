# Observation Mechanism

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The organizational structure and operational strategy of an observation system, including the number and distribution of stations, single- or dual-station observation modes, observation duration and frequency. The design of the observation mechanism directly determines the efficiency and accuracy of cataloguing orbit determination.
