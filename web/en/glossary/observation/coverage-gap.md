# Coverage Gap

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Longest duration that a relay satellite cannot cover a region on the lunar far side; a single Halo satellite's maximum coverage gap can reach 8 days.

## Application Value

Coverage Gap is significant for spacecraft tracking, identification, and perception in cislunar space.
