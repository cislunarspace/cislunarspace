# Anomaly Awareness

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The capability to detect and identify abnormal changes in space object orbits or behavior. Unlike routine catalog tracking, anomaly awareness focuses on detecting unexpected maneuvers (e.g., orbit changes, breakups, anomalous drift), requiring high observation timeliness and low false alarm rates. The paper shows that higher double coverage rates yield lower false alarm rates and higher detection confidence.

## Application Value

在观测系统设计中，需要考虑传感器性能、观测几何和数据处理方法。该概念支撑目标检测、跟踪和识别等任务。
