> Author: [Tianjiang Says](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

This concept holds application value in spacecraft system design and mission analysis; specific application scenarios require further investigation.

## Application Value

Perception systems provide essential environmental awareness capabilities for spacecraft, supporting autonomous navigation, rendezvous and docking, and collision avoidance in cislunar space operations.
