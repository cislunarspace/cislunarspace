# Observer Constellation Configuration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The number and orbital combination of observer satellites carrying optical sensors. This paper tests nine configurations (3-5 GEO satellites combined with 0-4 HEO satellites) and finds OCC has much less impact on tracking uptime than LEA.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space research and mission design.
