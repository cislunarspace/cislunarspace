# Control Weighted Average Coverage
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A coverage metric proposed in this paper that weights the average coverage by the control history. Defined as the dot product of the control history vector and detection history vector divided by the sum of control magnitudes. This metric prioritizes coverage during high-thrust maneuvers, reflecting the SDA architecture's need to monitor spacecraft orbit changes.

## Application Value

The Control Weighted Average Coverage plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
