# Corner Cube Reflector, CCR

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A passive optical reflector composed of three mutually perpendicular reflective surfaces that retroreflects incident light back along its incident direction. Deployed on the lunar surface as target reflectors for laser ranging. Apollo 11, 14, and 15 deployed CCRs on the Moon; Chang'e-6 placed an Italian-made CCR on the lunar far side in 2024.

## Application Value

The 角锥棱镜 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
