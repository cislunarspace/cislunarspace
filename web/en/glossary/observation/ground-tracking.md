# Ground Tracking

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Traditional method using ground station equipment for radio tracking and measurement of spacecraft, limited by Earth's rotation and geometric constraints with blind spots for spacecraft on the far side of the Moon.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
