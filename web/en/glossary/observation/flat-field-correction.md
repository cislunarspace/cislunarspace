# Flat Field Correction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An astronomical image preprocessing step that compensates for non-uniform pixel response of the CCD detector to uniform illumination. By normalizing images of a uniform light source (such as twilight sky or flat-field screen), it removes pixel-to-pixel sensitivity variations and optical vignetting.

## Application Value

在观测系统设计中，需要考虑传感器性能、观测几何和数据处理方法。该概念支撑目标检测、跟踪和识别等任务。
