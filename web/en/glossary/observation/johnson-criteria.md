# Johnson Criteria
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A physics-based criterion for evaluating an imager's target recognition capability. It uses resolution as the metric: the number of pixels across the target multiplied by the sensor's highest spatial frequency must meet a threshold for 50% probability of discrimination. This study uses a threshold of 13.5 pixels, corresponding to a minimum resolution of 0.77 m/pixel.

## Application Value

The Johnson Criteria plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
