# Multistatic Radar

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A radar system comprising one transmitter site working in coordination with multiple receiver sites. In the proposed cislunar debris detection architecture, a deep space station antenna (e.g., Kashi 35m or Jiamusi 66m) equipped with a high-power transmitter handles transmission, while multiple radio telescopes in China (e.g., Tianma 65m, Wuqing 70m, Miyun 40m, Kunming 40m) independently receive the echoes. The multi-receiver configuration improves target detection probability and enhances orbit determination accuracy through combined multi-site data.
