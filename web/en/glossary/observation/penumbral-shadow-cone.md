# Penumbral Shadow Cone

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The conical partially illuminated region formed when a celestial body partially blocks the light source.

## Application Value

在态势感知系统中，该观测方法为地月空间目标检测与跟踪提供关键数据支撑，是空间交通管理的技术基础。
