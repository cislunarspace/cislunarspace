# Apparent Magnitude

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A logarithmic measure of the brightness of a celestial body as seen by an observer. Using the Sun as reference, the apparent magnitude of a space object depends on its diameter, reflectivity (specular and diffuse components), solar phase angle, and observation distance. Smaller values indicate brighter objects; it is the fundamental physical quantity for evaluating the visibility of space objects to space-based optical instruments.
