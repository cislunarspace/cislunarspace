# Persistent Detection Corridor

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A space-time corridor within which a cooperative agent can persist within the detectable region of an SDA architecture at a specific time with a given delta-v cost. The corridor width varies with time, reflecting the persistently detectable spatial extent under a specific architecture layout and solar phase angle. Architectures with multiple such corridors are advantageous for cislunar SDA.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
