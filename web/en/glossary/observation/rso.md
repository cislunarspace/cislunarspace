# Resident Space Object, RSO

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Active satellites, orbital debris, and noncooperative satellites in cislunar space. SDA systems must detect, track, and characterize these objects.

## Application Value

Resident Space Object, RSO is an important concept in cislunar orbit design, crucial for understanding spacecraft motion in the Earth-Moon space.
