# Klystron Transmitter

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A microwave transmitter using a klystron as the final power amplifier, capable of generating high-power continuous-wave signals. China independently developed 10 kW S-band and X-band klystron transmitters for deep space TT&C in 2012, and achieved a 50 kW X-band CW klystron high-power amplifier breakthrough in 2017, laying the technical foundation for developing 100 kW-class high-power transmitters. The high output power of klystron transmitters is essential hardware enabling ground-based radar to detect targets at extreme distances.

## Application Value

该概念在地月空间任务设计中具有重要应用价值，涉及轨道设计、导航控制或动力学分析等多个方面，为地月空间探测任务提供理论支撑.
