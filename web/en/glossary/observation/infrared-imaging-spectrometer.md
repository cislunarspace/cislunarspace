# Infrared Imaging Spectrometer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A scientific instrument mounted on the rover to analyze mineral composition and surface characteristics at exploration points by acquiring infrared spectral data and geometric image data of lunar surface materials. Infrared spectroscopy identifies characteristic absorption peaks of minerals such as silicates and oxides, serving as a key tool for in-situ analysis of lunar surface material composition.

## Application Value

在观测系统设计中，需要考虑传感器性能、观测几何和数据处理方法。该概念支撑目标检测、跟踪和识别等任务。
