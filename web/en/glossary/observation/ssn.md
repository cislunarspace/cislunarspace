# Space Surveillance Network

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A space target detection system composed of optical telescopes, imaging radars, tracking radars, and passive RF signal detectors. The US Space Surveillance Network is the most advanced and comprehensive such system in the world, capable of detection, tracking, orbit determination, cataloguing, and identification of various space targets including debris.

## Application Value

The Space Surveillance Network is infrastructure for space situational awareness, capable of detecting, tracking, and cataloging space debris and other objects.
