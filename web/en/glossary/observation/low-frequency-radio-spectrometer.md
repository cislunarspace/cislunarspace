# Low-Frequency Radio Spectrometer

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A scientific instrument mounted on the lander to detect solar low-frequency radio characteristics and the lunar surface radio environment in the 0.1 to 40 MHz range. Operating in the unique environment of the lunar farside free from terrestrial radio interference, it performs spectral analysis of low-frequency radio signals generated by solar activity. It is the core payload for one of Chang'e-4's three scientific objectives: lunar-based low-frequency radio astronomical observation and research.
