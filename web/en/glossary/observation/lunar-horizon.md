# Lunar Horizon

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The visible limb contour of the Moon as seen from the spacecraft's viewpoint. Horizon-based optical navigation determines the spacecraft's distance from the Moon center by detecting lunar horizon pixel positions in camera images and extracting horizon vectors. Lunar terrain relief introduces horizon measurement errors.

## Application Value

在航天器导航和定位中，该方法用于从传感器数据中提取有效信息，实现自主定轨和轨道预报. 支持地月空间探测任务的实时导航需求.
