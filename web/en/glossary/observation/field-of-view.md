# Field of View

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angular extent of the observable region of an optical sensor. In optical navigation, the field of view must be large enough to fully capture the celestial body's horizon at all times. FoV selection directly impacts navigation accuracy: too large wastes edge pixels, too small may lose the horizon.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
