# Bi-directional One-Way Ranging

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A ranging system where two spacecraft independently transmit ranging signals to each other. The sum of forward and reverse ranging values yields distance, while the difference yields clock bias, enabling decoupling of distance and clock information.

## Application Value

This technique is crucial for cislunar navigation and orbit determination, providing high-precision position and velocity information for spacecraft.
