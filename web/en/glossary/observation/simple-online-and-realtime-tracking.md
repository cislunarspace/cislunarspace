# Simple Online and Realtime Tracking

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A lightweight multi-object tracking algorithm based on Kalman filter and Hungarian algorithm. It predicts target positions with Kalman filter and associates detections to predictions using the Hungarian algorithm. Simple and real-time, but prone to frequent identity switches after occlusion due to the lack of appearance features.

## Application Value

在观测系统设计中，需要考虑传感器性能、观测几何和数据处理方法。该概念支撑目标检测、跟踪和识别等任务。
