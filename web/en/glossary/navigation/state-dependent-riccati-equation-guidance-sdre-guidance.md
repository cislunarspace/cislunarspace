# State Dependent Riccati Equation Guidance, SDRE Guidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A nonlinear optimal guidance method that parameterizes relative motion dynamics into state-dependent coefficient form and solves time-varying feedback gains via the state-dependent Riccati equation. Suitable for the terminal phase of proximity rendezvous, handling end constraints such as safety corridors.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
