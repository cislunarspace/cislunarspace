# Convergence Speed

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In an ISL-based autonomous orbit determination system, the speed at which orbit determination accuracy transitions from an inaccurate initial value to a steady state. In the paper, it is defined as the time required for the position RMSE to first fall below and consistently stay under the 100 m threshold. Increasing link quantity significantly accelerates convergence but with diminishing marginal returns.
