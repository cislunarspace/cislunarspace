# Dual One-Way Ranging

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Satellites A and B transmit and receive ranging signals at the same epoch; by adding and subtracting the dual one-way pseudorange measurements, the geometric distance or clock offset components are eliminated, enabling decoupled estimation of inter-satellite range and clock offset.

## Application Value

Dual One-Way Ranging has important applications in cislunar space navigation and orbit determination systems.
