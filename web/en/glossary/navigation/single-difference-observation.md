# Single-Difference Observation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A GNSS data processing method that obtains differences between simultaneous observations of the same satellite by two stations. Single-difference can eliminate satellite clock bias and is the basis for double-difference and triple-difference observations.

## Application Value

Single-difference observation eliminates satellite clock bias, serving as a fundamental step in GNSS data processing. Building upon this, double-difference and triple-difference processing can remove additional error sources, yielding higher-precision relative positioning results.
