# Kinematic Orbit Determination

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method of precise orbit determination based on clear understanding of forces acting on satellites and precise modeling of dynamics. By establishing satellite dynamics and kinematics equations and solving initial epoch orbital parameters and dynamic model parameters, the purpose of precise orbit determination is achieved. The more precise the mechanical model, the higher the orbit determination accuracy.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
