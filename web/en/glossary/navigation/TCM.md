# Trajectory Correction Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Multiple small delta-V maneuvers performed during transfer to correct navigation errors, control errors, and other deviations. In PRM, the TCM plan includes five maneuvers: two for Earth-Moon phase errors, two for lunar orbit phase errors, and the last targeting the NRHO perilune. By repeatedly correcting at different orbital positions, the spacecraft arrives at the target orbit with high accuracy.

## Application Value

This technique is crucial for cislunar navigation and orbit determination, providing high-precision position and velocity information for spacecraft.
