# Observation Frequency

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The time frequency of measurement updates in orbit determination, set to 1 hour in this paper.

## Application Value

Observation Frequency is the time frequency of measurement updates in orbit determination. Higher observation frequency generally improves orbit determination accuracy but places greater demands on tracking resources.
