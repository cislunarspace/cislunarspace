# Patched Conic Method

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An approximate trajectory design method that decomposes a multi-body orbital problem into several two-body problems. Outside the equilibrium region, two-body conic approximations are used; inside, linearized equations apply. The pieces are joined at the equilibrium region boundary. Conley notes that this 'refined patched conic method' provides a flexible and practical framework for Earth-Moon orbit design with modest accuracy requirements.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
