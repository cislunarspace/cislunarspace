# Star Tracker

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An optical sensor that determines spacecraft attitude by imaging star fields, identifying stars, and measuring star point positions within its field of view. The paper notes that the miniaturized star tracker on Chang'E-5 achieved 3-arcsecond measurement accuracy at only 2 kg mass. Star trackers are the core instrument for celestial angular navigation, applied in missions including Deep Space-1, Lucy, and Smart-1. Beyond attitude determination, star trackers can be used for target body acquisition and relative pointing and distance measurement during approach and flyby operations, enabling autonomous navigation.

## Application Value

Star trackers are core instruments for deep space探测器 to achieve autonomous navigation. Unlike inertial devices such as gyroscopes, star trackers use stars as reference basis, providing absolute attitude information without cumulative errors. In Earth-Moon transfers and lunar exploration missions, combining star trackers with orbital dynamics models enables autonomous orbit determination without relying on ground tracking and control. When the spacecraft enters lunar capture phase or executes rendezvous and docking, star trackers can also provide relative pointing measurements of target bodies, assisting in approach and flyby operations. Chang'e-5's miniaturized star tracker demonstrates that high-precision attitude measurement can be integrated into lightweight spacecraft, laying foundation for high-performance celestial navigation of future micro-nanoscale deep space detectors.
