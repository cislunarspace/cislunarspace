# Camera offset

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The distance between the measurement camera's mounting position and the spacecraft's center of mass. A sufficiently large offset can provide observability for angles-only relative orbit determination, improving observability through measurement geometry.

## Application Value

为目标跟踪和状态估计提供关键技术支撑 该概念为地月空间任务设计提供了重要的理论基础 在实际工程中可用于轨道设计、任务规划或控制系统分析。
