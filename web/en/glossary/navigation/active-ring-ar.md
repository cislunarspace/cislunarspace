# Active Ring

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A docking mechanism ring mounted on the chaser vehicle. During proximity operations, the attitude and position of the Active Ring are actively controlled to align with the Passive Ring on the target spacecraft, achieving docking contact.

## Application Value

This concept has important application value in cislunar space orbit design, navigation control, or mission analysis. Understanding its principles helps master core knowledge in related fields.
