# Final Approach Control

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The terminal phase of rendezvous and docking, from the hold point at approximately 500 m to initial contact. Using a PD controller for 6-DoF relative pose control, the chaser approaches the target along a pre-planned velocity profile (e.g., 7.5 cm/s) until the free-drift sequence initiates.

## Application Value

该方法可用于提高地月空间导航精度和任务安全性。
