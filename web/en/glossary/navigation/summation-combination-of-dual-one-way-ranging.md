# Summation Combination of Dual One-Way Ranging

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A combined measurement obtained by summing bidirectional pseudorange measurements and dividing by two. Since the light-travel time is very short, the clock bias terms cancel out during summation, yielding an observable that contains only geometric range and measurement noise.

## Application Value

Summation Combination of Dual One-Way Ranging is significant for spacecraft tracking, identification, and perception in cislunar space.
