# LTO Satellite Receiver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A GNSS receiver operating on a lunar transfer orbit (LTO). This receiver faces the weakest GNSS signal environment, reaching up to approximately 400,000 km from Earth at its farthest point, demanding extremely high sensitivity. With HEO constellation augmentation and main lobe signals only, average positioning accuracy of 77.77 m can be achieved.
