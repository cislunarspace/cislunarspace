# LNSS-B (LNSS-B)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An enhanced lunar navigation constellation scheme formed by adding an additional orbital plane to LNSS-A. It consists of six ELFO satellites (distributed across two orbital planes, three each) and two L2 Southern Halo satellites. The right ascension of ascending node is increased by 180° compared to LNSS-A, with evenly distributed mean anomalies. This scheme achieves over four satellites visible at the Lunar South Pole simultaneously, with PDOP below 50 for most of the time and positioning accuracy at sub-meter level.
