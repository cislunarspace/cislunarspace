# Deficient Rank

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

When a satellite orbits a single body and uses only inter-satellite range for orbit determination, the rotational symmetry of the central body's gravitational field causes the observation information matrix to be rank-deficient, making it impossible to uniquely determine all orbital parameters. In the three-body problem, strong perturbation from the third body breaks this symmetry and restores full rank.

## Application Value

This concept is of significant value in cislunar orbit design, navigation control, and mission analysis; understanding its essence helps master the core knowledge of related fields.
