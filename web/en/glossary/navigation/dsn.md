# Deep Space Network

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A global deep space tracking and communication network operated by NASA, consisting of three Deep Space Complexes located in Madrid (Spain), Canberra (Australia), and Goldstone (California, USA). DSN provides navigation support including ranging, Doppler, and angular measurement for major global deep space missions, including those of Japan, ESA, and other agencies. The paper notes DSN's high angular measurement capability and wide application in deep space navigation, with Mars orbiter orbit determination accuracy in the Mars inertial frame serving as the precision benchmark for ground-based radio navigation methods.

## Application Value

The Deep Space Network provides tracking and navigation support for global deep space missions, with high-precision angular capability serving as the accuracy benchmark.
