# Elevation Angle

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The angle between a navigation satellite and the local lunar surface tangent plane as seen from a lunar surface observation point. Below a certain elevation angle, signals are blocked by lunar terrain or have insufficient signal-to-noise ratio for positioning.

## Application Value

在态势感知系统中，该观测方法为低轨空间目标监视提供技术手段，是空间交通管理的数据来源。
