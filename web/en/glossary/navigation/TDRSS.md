# Tracking and Data Relay Satellite System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

NASA's space tracking and data relay system supporting LEO spacecraft tracking and communication.

## Application Value

NASA's relay satellite system providing tracking and data relay services for low-Earth-orbit satellites and deep space probes, important infrastructure for manned spaceflight.
