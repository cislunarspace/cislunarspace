# Swift Collision Avoidance Manoeuvre, SCAM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An emergency collision avoidance scheme inside the Safety Boundary. Uses maximum-thrust short burns to push the chaser radially away from the target. Faster response and lower delta-V than OCAM, without thrust constraints. Activated during final approach when control fails.

## Application Value

This technique is crucial for cislunar navigation and orbit determination, providing high-precision position and velocity information for spacecraft.
