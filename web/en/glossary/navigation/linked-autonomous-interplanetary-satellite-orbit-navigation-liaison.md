# Linked Autonomous Interplanetary Satellite Orbit Navigation, LiAISON

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An autonomous orbit determination method relying solely on inter-satellite ranging without ground station support. It exploits the differential gravitational constraints from different celestial bodies to resolve absolute orbits. LiAISON cannot perform real-time positioning of maneuvering objects such as lunar landers.
