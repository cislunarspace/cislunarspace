# Launch Collision Avoidance Analysis

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A collision risk analysis process mandated for commercial launch and reentry operations in the United States. For cislunar STM, LCOLA has significant limitations: it underrepresents true collision risk, is inadequate for xGEO launches, and ignores space weather and terrestrial weather factors.

## Application Value

发射碰撞避让分析技术为地月空间航天器提供精确的定位能力，是实现自主导航的关键技术之一 在实际任务中，利用发射碰撞避让分析可以有效提高定轨精度，降低对地面测控系统的依赖 发射碰撞避让分析的研究对于构建地月空间导航基础设施具有重要意义 通过发射碰撞避让分析，航天器能够在复杂的空间环境中实现精确自主定位，支撑月球及更远深空的探测任务
