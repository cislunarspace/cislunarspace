# Dual-antenna design

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A design where lunar navigation satellites are equipped with both Earth-facing and Moon-facing antennas simultaneously. The Earth-facing antenna receives BDS signals and establishes inter-satellite links for spatiotemporal reference transfer; the Moon-facing antenna provides navigation services to lunar users. This design simultaneously improves BDS geometry in cislunar space, making positioning accuracy better than 50 m for near-lunar users possible.
