# Carrier-to-Noise Ratio Threshold

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The minimum carrier-to-noise ratio at which a GNSS receiver can effectively track and demodulate a signal. Signals below this threshold are deemed invalid and excluded from orbit determination. Typical commercial receivers have a threshold around 15 dB-Hz.
