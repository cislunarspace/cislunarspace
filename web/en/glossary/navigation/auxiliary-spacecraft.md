# Auxiliary Spacecraft
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In an ISL-based autonomous orbit determination system, a spacecraft that works with the core spacecraft and provides auxiliary ranging information. The orbital type of the auxiliary spacecraft significantly impacts system observability and final orbit determination accuracy.

## Application Value

The Auxiliary Spacecraft plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
