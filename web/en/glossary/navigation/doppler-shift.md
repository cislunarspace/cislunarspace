# Doppler Shift

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The deviation of received signal frequency from the nominal carrier frequency caused by relative motion between the receiver and GNSS satellite. On cislunar transfer orbits, the shift is largest near perigee (up to ±45 kHz) and stabilizes at -20~20 kHz farther from Earth. On circumlunar orbits, the range is approximately ±10 kHz.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
