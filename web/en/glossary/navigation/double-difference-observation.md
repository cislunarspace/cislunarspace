# Double-Difference Observation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A GNSS data processing method that further obtains differences between two satellites on the basis of single-difference. Double-difference can simultaneously eliminate satellite clock bias and receiver clock bias, and is the main observation form for relative positioning and precise orbit determination.
