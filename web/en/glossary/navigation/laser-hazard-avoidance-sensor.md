# Laser Hazard Avoidance Sensor

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A device that scans lunar terrain using laser ranging principles to detect hazards (rocks, craters, etc.) during the landing approach phase, providing terrain elevation data for hazard avoidance decisions.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
