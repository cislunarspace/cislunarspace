# LVLH Reference Directions (R-bar, V-bar, H-bar)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The three axes of the LVLH frame, standard directions for describing relative motion during rendezvous approach. R-bar points toward the primary body (radial); V-bar aligns with the chaser velocity (along-track); H-bar is normal to the target's orbital plane. These directions borrow Keplerian terminology and are used approximately under three-body dynamics.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
