# Pseudo-Range Measurement Noise

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Random errors in pseudo-range observations caused by receiver signal tracking, multipath, thermal noise, and other factors. In cislunar space, measurement noise is far higher than in ground or low-Earth-orbit environments due to weak signal strength.

## Application Value

Navigation system design and implementation must account for observation geometry, error propagation, and signal transmission delay. This concept supports positioning accuracy evaluation, navigation filter design, and constellation optimization.
