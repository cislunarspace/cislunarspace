# Line-of-Sight Glide, LoS-G

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A geometric strategy where the chaser does not cross the line-of-sight but remains on the same side of the cone during approach.

## Application Value

Describing the target's glide trajectory in the field of view, an important geometric concept in tracking and guidance.
