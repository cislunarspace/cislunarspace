# Lunar Global Navigation Satellite System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A satellite constellation system providing global positioning, navigation, and timing services for users on the lunar surface and in near-lunar space. Its design philosophy follows the Earth GNSS constellation architecture, deploying navigation satellites in lunar orbits for full lunar surface coverage. Typical schemes combine distant retrograde orbits with L1 and L2 Halo orbit satellites to balance polar and equatorial coverage with stable orbits and low maintenance costs.

## Application Value

The lunar global navigation satellite system can provide positioning services for the lunar surface.
