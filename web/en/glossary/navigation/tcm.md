# Midcourse Correction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Small velocity correction maneuvers performed during Earth-Moon transfer to eliminate accumulated effects of insertion errors, navigation errors, and control errors. The paper analyzes velocity increment consumption at different correction times: earlier corrections accommodate larger semi-major axis deviations, while later corrections cost more. For Chang'e-5, the maximum available velocity increment during trans-lunar flight was approximately 173 m/s.

## Application Value

Trajectory correction maneuvers correct orbit deviations in deep space missions.
