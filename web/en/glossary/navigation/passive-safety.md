# Passive Safety

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A safety strategy that avoids collisions through intrinsically drift-away trajectories, requiring no actuators or sensors. Even with complete loss of chaser control, collision is avoided. The cost is conservative trajectories, but fuel margin is preserved for re-attempt.

## Application Value

This术语在地月空间任务的设计、分析和控制中具有important应用价值，为相关技术领域的研究和工程实践provides理论支撑。
