# Broadcast Ephemeris

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Orbital and clock difference parameters broadcast by navigation satellites to users for real-time navigation and positioning services. Broadcast ephemeris is calculated by the ground monitoring system and uploaded to satellites, with accuracy relatively lower than post-processed precise ephemeris but with real-time availability.
