# Pseudo-Range Residual

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The remaining part of pseudo-range observations after subtracting all model corrections, reflecting the magnitude of measurement noise and unmodeled errors. Residual statistics are used to assess observation quality and positioning solution reliability.

## Application Value

支撑自主导航系统的开发，提高轨道确定精度 为目标跟踪和状态估计提供关键技术支撑 用于评估导航系统的精度上限，指导滤波器设计。
