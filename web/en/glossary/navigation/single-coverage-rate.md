# Single Coverage Rate

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The fraction of time during which at least one satellite in the constellation has line-of-sight visibility to a given lunar surface point (elevation angle ≥ 10°, no lunar terrain blockage). It is the primary metric for evaluating relay communication capability.
