# Authority to Proceed, ATP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An authorization step in rendezvous and docking operations: ATP is granted only after the proximity sensor has locked onto the target and confirmed its integrity, allowing the chaser to enter the target's keep-out sphere and begin final approach. This mechanism embodies passive safety at the operational level.

## Application Value

This technique is crucial for cislunar navigation and orbit determination, providing high-precision position and velocity information for spacecraft.
