# Process noise

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In orbit estimation filters, the acceleration uncertainty caused by random disturbances such as imperfectly coupled rotational maneuvers and venting, incorporated into the state prediction equation of the Kalman filter as a process noise covariance matrix.
