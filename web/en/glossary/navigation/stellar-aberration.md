# Stellar Aberration

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An optical effect where the apparent direction of a star deviates slightly from its true direction due to the observer's velocity relative to the stellar reference frame. The aberration angle depends on the ratio of spacecraft velocity to the speed of light. The paper notes that Liu Fucheng et al. established observation models based on stellar aberration, inferring spacecraft velocity information from changes in apparent star direction, combined with orbital dynamics models and Kalman filtering for cislunar spacecraft autonomous navigation. This approach overcomes the limitation of traditional celestial angular navigation being unable to directly obtain velocity measurements.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
