# Circumlunar Orbit

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit around the Moon at typical altitudes of ~100 km. On circumlunar orbits, GNSS signal reception is affected by lunar occultation, with non-line-of-sight gaps occurring approximately every 2 hours, causing periodic variations in visible satellite count.

## Application Value

Circumlunar Orbit has important applications in cislunar space navigation and orbit determination systems.
