# GNSS Ultra-rapid ephemerides

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A type of GNSS precise ephemeris that, combined with carrier phase measurements, enables rapid LEO orbit determination with position accuracy of approximately 0.2 m. More accurate than broadcast ephemeris, suitable for missions with high orbit determination accuracy requirements.

## Application Value

精密定轨是实现地月空间导航的基础，决定了轨道预报精度。在LEO区域，GNSS可支持快速高精度定轨，超出GNSS服务区则需其他手段。
