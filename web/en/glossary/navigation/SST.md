# Satellite-to-Satellite Tracking, SST

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique for orbit determination by measuring the range and range-rate between satellites. SST provides the size, shape, and relative orientation of a constellation, but cannot determine absolute orientation alone, suffering from rank deficiency.

## Application Value

This technique is crucial for cislunar navigation and orbit determination, providing high-precision position and velocity information for spacecraft.
