# Barycentric Inertial Frame

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An inertial reference frame centered at the Sun-Earth barycenter. Formed by freezing the barycentric rotating frame at the initial epoch: z-axis along the ecliptic normal, x-axis pointing from Sun to Earth, y-axis determined by the right-hand rule. In this frame, the spacecraft position is on the order of 10^8 km, with a much larger variation range than the barycentric rotating frame, resulting in lower navigation filter accuracy.
