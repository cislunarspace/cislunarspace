# Optimal Constant-Altitude Hazard Avoidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A manual hazard avoidance strategy during the approach phase of landing. The vehicle flies laterally at constant altitude with propellant-optimal trajectories to avoid hazards, minimizing propellant consumption by solving optimal angular velocity profiles under range constraints.


## Application Value

This optimization method has wide applications in cislunar space trajectory design, suitable for scenarios such as trajectory optimization and mission planning.
