# Dual Crosslink Orbit Determination

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method of joint orbit determination using simultaneously two crosslink measurement data. In this paper, data from two crosslinks between LMO-Halo1 and LMO-Halo2 are combined to estimate the initial states of three spacecraft and range biases. Dual crosslink orbit determination enhances the positive definiteness of the normal matrix, accelerates convergence, and significantly improves overall constellation orbit accuracy.

## Application Value

在组合导航系统中，该方法能够提高定轨精度和收敛速度，增强导航系统的鲁棒性，适用于地月空间的自主导航任务。
