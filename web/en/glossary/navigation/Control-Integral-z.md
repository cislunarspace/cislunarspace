# Control Integral, z

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The integral of the estimated control policy, representing the total estimated effort applied to the system, used as classifier input.

## Application Value

This control method is suitable for attitude and orbit control of cislunar spacecraft, enabling precise maneuvers and stationkeeping.
