# International Rendezvous System Interoperability Standard, IRSIS

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An interoperability standard for rendezvous and docking published by ESA in 2019. Standardizes safety zones (RS, KOZ, SB) and safety constraints in rendezvous operations, providing normative basis for autonomous rendezvous mission design.

## Application Value

Rendezvous and docking is a key technology for crewed spaceflight and space station operations, and reliable docking mechanisms are fundamental to mission success.
