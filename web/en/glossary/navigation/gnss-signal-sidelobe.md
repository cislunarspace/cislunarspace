# GNSS Signal Sidelobe

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Signal radiated from a navigation satellite antenna in directions outside the main beam. Cislunar spacecraft, being far from Earth, have most main-lobe signals blocked by the Earth and can only receive sidelobe signals, which have significantly lower power.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
