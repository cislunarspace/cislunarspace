# Disappearance of Solutions

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Observation errors can not only cause positional errors in IOD results but may also lead to the complete disappearance of solutions, rendering the IOD method invalid.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in cislunar space mission design and orbital dynamics analysis.
