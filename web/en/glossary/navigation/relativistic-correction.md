# Relativistic Correction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The incorporation of general-relativistic effects into navigation observation equations, including proper time to coordinate time conversion, gravitational light deflection corrections, and reference frame coordinate transformations. Relativistic correction is the key feature distinguishing cislunar high-precision navigation modeling from near-Earth navigation.
