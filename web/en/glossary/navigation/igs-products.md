> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

## Definition

High-precision satellite orbit, clock difference, Earth rotation parameter and other products provided by the International GNSS Service (IGS). IGS products include ultra-rapid, rapid, and final products with accuracy ranging from decimeter to centimeter level.
