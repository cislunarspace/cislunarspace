# One-way ranging

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An inter-satellite measurement method where the signal is transmitted unidirectionally from one satellite and received by the other. Produces code pseudo-range and Doppler measurement data; the pseudo-range consists of instantaneous geometric distance, hardware delay, and thermal noise.

## Application Value

This concept plays a key role in trajectory transfer design, helping evaluate transfer costs and flight time to provide quantitative basis for mission trade studies. Combined with global search algorithms, multiple solution families and Pareto frontiers can be identified to guide orbital design decisions.
