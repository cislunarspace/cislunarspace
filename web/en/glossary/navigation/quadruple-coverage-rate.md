# Quadruple Coverage Rate

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The fraction of time during which at least four satellites in the constellation simultaneously have line-of-sight visibility to a given lunar surface point. Simultaneous visibility of four satellites is the minimum requirement for radio navigation satellite service (RNSS) positioning, making this the primary metric for lunar navigation capability.
