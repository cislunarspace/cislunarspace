# Simplified Dynamic Orbit Determination

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit determination method that relaxes and simplifies the dynamic model, relying on estimating empirical parameters to absorb errors caused by inaccurate mechanical models. The estimated dynamic parameters and empirical forces in three directions (along-track, normal, and radial) can be adjusted according to actual orbit determination conditions.
