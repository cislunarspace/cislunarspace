# Deep Space Network, DSN

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A ground station network providing tracking measurement services including range, range rate, and angular measurement for deep space exploration and cislunar missions. The Chinese Deep Space Network (CDSN) performs orbit determination for cislunar spacecraft, but due to DRO spacecraft's large distance from Earth and slow motion, navigation results typically require 2 to 3 days to converge.

## Application Value

Providing ranging, range-rate, and angle measurement services for cislunar and deep space missions. China's CDSN requires 2-3 days convergence for DRO spacecraft navigation, foundational infrastructure for long-distance TT&C.
