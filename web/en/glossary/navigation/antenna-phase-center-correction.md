# Antenna Phase Center Correction

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Models that correct deviations between the antenna phase center and geometric center of satellites or receivers. Antenna phase center correction accuracy directly affects the quality of pseudorange and carrier phase observations, and is one of the orbit determination error sources.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
