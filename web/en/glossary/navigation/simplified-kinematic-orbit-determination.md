# Simplified Kinematic Orbit Determination

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method that uses pure geometric orbit determination results and three-dimensional relative positions between different epochs generated by absolute constraints of prior orbits to reduce positioning result anomalies caused by geometric configuration differences and phase jumps, achieving stable and smooth orbit determination. The orbit determination results depend on the selection of prior orbits.
