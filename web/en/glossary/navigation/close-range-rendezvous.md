# Close-Range Rendezvous

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The second phase of rendezvous where the chaser approaches the target from 100 km to 1 km, using relative position control with impulsive or continuous thrust.

## Application Value

Close-range approach technology provides precise positioning capability for spacecraft in cislunar space and is one of the key technologies for autonomous navigation. In practical missions, it effectively improves orbit determination accuracy and reduces dependence on ground tracking systems. Research on close-range approach is of great significance for building cislunar navigation infrastructure. Through it, spacecraft can achieve precise autonomous positioning in complex space environments, supporting lunar exploration and missions to deeper space.
