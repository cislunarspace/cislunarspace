# Long-Term Management Mode

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The low-density observation mode after a deep space probe completes its primary mission and enters long-term stable operation. Deep space stations observe about 2 hours per day per station; VLBI provides 1-2 sessions per week of about 2 hours each. Observation is intensified during orbital maneuvers. Contrasts with mission mode and represents the routine operational state for deep space probes.

## Application Value

长管模式技术为地月空间航天器提供精确的定位能力，是实现自主导航的关键技术之一 在实际任务中，利用长管模式可以有效提高定轨精度，降低对地面测控系统的依赖 长管模式的研究对于构建地月空间导航基础设施具有重要意义 通过长管模式，航天器能够在复杂的空间环境中实现精确自主定位，支撑月球及更远深空的探测任务
