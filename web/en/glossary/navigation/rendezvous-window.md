# Rendezvous Window

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The time window when two spacecraft reach the same position and velocity. In space-station-based lunar missions, crew waits at the station for the lunar departure window, and after landing, waits for the return window before Earth reentry.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
