# Fake Solution

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Solutions that satisfy the polynomial equation in IOD but have large residuals compared to the true orbit; can be preliminarily distinguished by observation residuals but the criterion may fail for short-arc observations.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
