# Dual Symmetry

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The DOP distribution of a resonant constellation exhibits symmetric characteristics along both the Earth-Moon axis and the lunar orbital plane. This symmetry arises from the constellation's structural symmetry about these two reference directions.
