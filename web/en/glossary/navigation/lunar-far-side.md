# Lunar Far Side

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The hemisphere of the Moon permanently facing away from Earth due to tidal locking, unable to establish line-of-sight communication with Earth. Far-side spacecraft require relay satellites or navigation constellations for communication and positioning support.
