# Mission Mode

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The high-density observation mode for deep space probes, used during launch and critical mission phases. The ground tracking system continuously tracks the probe during observable arcs, deep space stations provide 24-hour relay observation, and the VLBI tracking subsystem performs full-arc tracking when the probe is visible from three or more stations. Contrasts with long-term management mode.

## Application Value

This concept has practical applications in cislunar space science and engineering. Related research supports the planning, implementation, and operations of cislunar missions, forming an integral part of the knowledge system in this field.
