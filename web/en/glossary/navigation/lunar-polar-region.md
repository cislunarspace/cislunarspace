# Lunar Polar Region

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Regions above approximately 75 degrees lunar latitude at both poles. With extremely low solar elevation, shadowed craters may harbor water ice, making polar regions key targets for future lunar resource development. However, they pose the greatest challenge for navigation constellation coverage.

## Application Value

Lunar Polar Region has important applications in cislunar space navigation and orbit determination systems.
