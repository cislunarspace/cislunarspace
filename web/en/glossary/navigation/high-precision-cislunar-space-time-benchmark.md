# High-Precision Cislunar Space-Time Benchmark

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A foundational framework providing unified time and space references for high-precision navigation and positioning in cislunar space. The paper indicates that constructing and maintaining this benchmark requires BCRS, with construction, traceability, and transfer via space-based infrastructure at cislunar libration points as the implementation path.
