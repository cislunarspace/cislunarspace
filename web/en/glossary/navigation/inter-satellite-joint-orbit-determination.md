# Inter-Satellite Joint Orbit Determination
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method of precise navigation satellite orbit determination that only relies on inter-satellite link measurement data, with little or no dependence on ground station data. It is the core technology for achieving autonomous navigation of navigation satellites, significantly enhancing the system's autonomous operation capability.

## Application Value

The Inter-Satellite Joint Orbit Determination plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
