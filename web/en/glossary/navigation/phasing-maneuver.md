# Phasing Maneuver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbital maneuver performed between the end of the Earth–Moon transfer phase and the beginning of proximity operations in a cislunar rendezvous mission. When the target vehicle orbits on a periodic trajectory such as an NRHO, the chaser arriving in the Moon's vicinity may have a phase offset from the target. The phasing maneuver closes this gap, enabling the chaser to enter the target's proximity. The paper proposes two implementations: a two-impulse strategy exploiting the natural dynamics of Halo parking orbits, and a three-impulse low-energy strategy exploiting invariant manifolds.
