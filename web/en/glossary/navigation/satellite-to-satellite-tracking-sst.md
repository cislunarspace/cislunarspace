# Satellite-to-Satellite Tracking, SST

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique for orbit determination by measuring the range and range-rate between satellites. SST provides the size, shape, and relative orientation of a constellation, but cannot determine absolute orientation alone, suffering from rank deficiency.

## Application Value

星间跟踪通过双向测距链路获取航天器间距离数据，是LiAISON自主导航的核心观测手段，在DRO-LLO编队飞行中实现高精度相对导航。
