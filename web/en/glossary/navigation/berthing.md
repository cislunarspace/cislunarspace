# Berthing

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The process of achieving physical connection through mechanical interfaces after the chaser approaches the target. Unlike docking in rendezvous and docking, berthing emphasizes the chaser being captured by the target and fixed in a designated position.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
