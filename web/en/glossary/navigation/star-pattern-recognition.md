# Star Pattern Recognition

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Technique for determining spacecraft attitude by matching star patterns captured by star trackers with known star catalogs, crucial for autonomous celestial navigation.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
