# Space-based Tracking Station

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A concept of treating LEO satellites as tracking stations deployed in space. Like ground stations, LEO satellites undergo periodic near-circular motion but at higher speed and shorter orbital period, yielding better visibility and faster geometric variation in inter-satellite links. A single LEO satellite equipped with inter-satellite measurement devices and a GNSS receiver can simultaneously achieve its own orbit/timing determination and support cislunar spacecraft orbit determination.
