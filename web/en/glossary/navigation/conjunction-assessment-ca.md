# Conjunction Assessment

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The technical process of computing the probability and geometry of two space objects approaching each other, determining whether an avoidance maneuver is required. It is the core technical element of Space Traffic Management (STM), typically executed periodically by control centers based on orbit prediction data.

## Application Value

With increasingly frequent cislunar space activities, collision risk assessment between spacecraft is becoming increasingly important. Conjunction assessment determines whether avoidance maneuvers need to be executed within specific windows by computing miss distance and collision probability of two objects. Assessment results directly affect spacecraft safety and control center decision-making — when collision probability exceeds the threshold, urgent orbit raising or lowering is required, otherwise potentially catastrophic consequences may result. In future scenarios with dense deployment of lunar navigation constellations and commercial satellites, conjunction assessment will become a routine operational element of cislunar space traffic management, holding critical significance for ensuring safe spacecraft operation and maintaining orbital order.
