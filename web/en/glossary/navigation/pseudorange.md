# Pseudorange

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The measured range from a navigation satellite to a user receiver, derived from signal propagation time multiplied by the speed of light, containing errors such as clock bias and atmospheric delay. Simultaneous equations from multiple satellites yield the user's 3D position and clock bias.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
