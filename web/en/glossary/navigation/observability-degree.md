# Observability Degree

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A quantitative measure of how strongly a system's states can be observed. Classical observability theory only determines whether states can be uniquely recovered from observations (yes or no), whereas observability degree further measures how well they can be recovered. The paper uses CRLB as the metric for observability degree: a smaller CRLB indicates higher observability and a better theoretical accuracy limit.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
