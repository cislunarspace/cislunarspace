# NRHO Rendezvous and Docking

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The complete process of performing rendezvous and docking with a space station operating in a near-rectilinear halo orbit. Unlike near-Earth rendezvous, the target orbit is a periodic orbit in the three-body system with non-natural stability characteristics, requiring specialized guidance schemes.
