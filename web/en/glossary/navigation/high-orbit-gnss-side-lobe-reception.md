# High-Orbit GNSS Side Lobe Reception
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique for receiving GNSS satellite antenna side lobe signals in high-orbit or cislunar space environments. Since GNSS main lobes point toward Earth's center, high-orbit users can only obtain navigation signals through side lobes. The low side lobe power (below -195 dBW) demands extremely high receiver sensitivity (typically around -190 dBW), making it a key technology for autonomous navigation in cislunar space.

## Application Value

The High-Orbit GNSS Side Lobe Reception plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
