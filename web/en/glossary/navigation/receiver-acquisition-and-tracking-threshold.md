# Receiver Acquisition and Tracking Threshold

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The minimum carrier-to-noise ratio required for a receiver to acquire and stably track GNSS signals. Ground receivers typically have thresholds of 35~40 dB-Hz, while high-sensitivity receivers can reach 24 dB-Hz or even 10~12 dB-Hz. Lowering the threshold is a key technology for improving GNSS availability in cislunar space.
