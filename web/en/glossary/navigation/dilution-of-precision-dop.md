# Dilution of Precision, DOP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter describing how satellite geometry amplifies ranging errors into position errors. Wider satellite spatial distribution yields smaller DOP and higher positioning accuracy. The DOP family includes GDOP, PDOP, and TDOP as variants.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
