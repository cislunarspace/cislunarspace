# Space-based Orbit Determination

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit determination method that uses LEO satellites as space-based tracking stations, establishing inter-satellite links with cislunar spacecraft for orbit estimation. The LEO satellite achieves its own precise orbit determination and timing via GNSS signals, then combines inter-satellite measurements with orbital dynamics models and filtering algorithms to estimate the cislunar spacecraft's orbit. Compared with ground-based methods, it benefits from shorter Earth-occlusion intervals, better measurement geometry, and immunity to atmospheric delay.

## Application Value

Space-based Orbit Determination is significant for spacecraft tracking, identification, and perception in cislunar space.
