# Autonomous Rendezvous and Docking, AR&D

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The capability for highly reliable, autonomous rendezvous, proximity operations, and capture/attachment to free-flying space objects, cooperative or non-cooperative.

## Application Value

This concept is applied in cislunar space: 交会对接是载人航天和空间站运营的关键技术，可靠的对接机构是任务成功的基础保障.
