# LiAISON

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Linked Autonomous Interplanetary Satellite Orbit Navigation; an autonomous navigation technique that uses satellite-to-satellite tracking range measurements to determine absolute orbital states in a three-body system's asymmetric gravity field, requiring at least one satellite deployed in the asymmetric region.
