# Far-Range Guidance Section

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The rendezvous and docking flight phase from the end of large-range orbit transfer (about 500 km relative distance) to the start of close-range approach (about 20 km). Uses a two-impulse guidance method based on three-body relative dynamics, optimizing impulse timing and magnitude for phase adjustment.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
