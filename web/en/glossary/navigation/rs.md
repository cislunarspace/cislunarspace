# Rendezvous Sphere

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A safety sphere of 10km radius centered on the target spacecraft. Transfer segments outside the RS must satisfy passive safety; inside the RS, active closed-loop safety takes over.

## Application Value

This term has significant practical applications in cislunar mission planning and execution.
