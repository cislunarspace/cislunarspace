# Ground-Based Radio Tracking, Telemetry and Command

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A navigation method using ground-based radio equipment for spacecraft ranging, Doppler, and angular measurement, currently the primary means of precision orbit determination for cislunar exploration spacecraft. The paper notes that China has established a deep space tracking network consisting of Kashgar, Jiamusi, and the South American Zaragosa stations, but geographic siting limitations constrain coverage to approximately a certain level. Key limitations of ground-based radio TT&C include: high communication delays inadequate for real-time requirements, growing tension between increasing spacecraft numbers and scarce ground station resources, mismatch between large data volume demands and low data transfer rates, and threats to safe operation from the complex cislunar space environment.

## Application Value

Ground-based radio tracking, telemetry and command is the primary means of precision orbit determination for cislunar spacecraft, but affected by geographic limitations and communication delays.
