# Satellite-to-Satellite Tracking, SST

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A space-based measurement technique that performs bidirectional ranging and velocity measurement between two or more spacecraft via inter-satellite links. In DRO-LLO formations, both satellites acquire inter-satellite range data through bidirectional one-way ranging, serving as the core observation for LiAISON autonomous navigation.

## Application Value

The 星间跟踪 concept is applied in cislunar space research, providing technical support or analytical methods for lunar exploration missions.
