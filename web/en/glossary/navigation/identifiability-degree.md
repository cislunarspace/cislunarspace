# Identifiability Degree
> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A quantitative metric measuring the precision level of a navigation system's ability to identify the initial state. Assessed via the condition number of the identifiability information matrix: a larger condition number implies a larger error bound for state estimation and lower identifiability capability. Unlike identifiability (which asks whether identification is possible), the identifiability degree describes the quality of identification.

## Application Value

The Identifiability Degree plays a significant role in cislunar space mission design, analysis, and control. In orbital design, it can be leveraged for transfer trajectory optimization; in navigation and control, it improves mission execution precision and reliability; in system analysis, it facilitates deeper understanding of complex multi-body dynamical behavior, guiding mission planning and risk assessment.
