# Dilution of Precision, DOP

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A parameter describing how satellite geometry amplifies ranging errors into position errors. Wider satellite spatial distribution yields smaller DOP and higher positioning accuracy. The DOP family includes GDOP, PDOP, and TDOP as variants.

## Application Value

This technique is crucial for cislunar navigation and orbit determination, providing high-precision position and velocity information for spacecraft.
