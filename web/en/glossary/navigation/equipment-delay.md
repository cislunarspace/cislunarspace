# Equipment Delay

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A fixed time bias introduced by signal propagation through on-board transmit and receive equipment, slowly varying with temperature. In dual one-way ranging, the sum combination contains the total equipment delay while the difference combination contains their difference. Equipment delay and clock offset are coupled and cannot be separated in orbit determination, requiring joint estimation as a single parameter.


## Application Value

钟差解算为星间时间同步和联合定轨提供了关键参数，是地月空间高精度导航的基础。
