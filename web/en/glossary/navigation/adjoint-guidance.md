# Adjoint Guidance

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A guidance method using adjoint equations to solve optimal two-impulse rendezvous trajectories. Adjoint variables are propagated backward from terminal state, combined with transversality conditions to determine departure and arrival burns. Provides open-loop optimal solutions for transfer segments between hold points under three-body dynamics.
