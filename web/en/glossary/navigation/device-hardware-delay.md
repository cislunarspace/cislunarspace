# Device Hardware Delay

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The additional delay produced as a signal passes through the electronic hardware at the transmitter and receiver, consisting of both transmission and reception delays. Device hardware delay is a systematic bias that must be eliminated through calibration or estimation during data processing.

## Application Value

This concept provides fundamental support in cislunar space research and mission design. In practical applications, the appropriate analysis method should be selected based on specific mission scenarios and constraints.
