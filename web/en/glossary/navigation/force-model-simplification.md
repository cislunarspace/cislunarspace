# Force Model Simplification

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Process of reducing the full force model for onboard orbit propagation to fit limited computation and storage resources. Specific measures include approximate computation of Earth and Moon J₂ perturbations, neglecting planetary gravity, and compressing ephemeris storage. This can improve computation speed by roughly one order of magnitude while maintaining prediction accuracy.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
