# Overlapping Arc Strategy

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An orbit determination accuracy assessment method that divides tracking data into adjacent overlapping arcs, performs independent orbit determination on each, and compares the ephemeris differences within the overlapping period. Longer overlaps and greater attitude variations amplify the accuracy differences between models. The strategy isolates orbit determination quality from the effects of momentum wheel unloading and station-keeping maneuvers.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
