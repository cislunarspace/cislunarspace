# Rapid Orbit Recovery

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A rapid orbit determination technology that re-determines high-precision orbits in a short time after satellite orbit maneuvers. The BeiDou system is designed to achieve orbit determination accuracy better than 30 meters within 8 hours after orbit maneuvers, which is crucial for the continuity of the navigation system's services.

## Application Value

Rapid Orbit Recovery has important applications in cislunar space navigation and orbit determination systems.
