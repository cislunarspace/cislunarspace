# Spin-Plane Maneuver Constraint

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An inherent constraint on spin-stabilized spacecraft during orbit maneuvers. The spin attitude control limits the thrust vector to the spin plane (the hemisphere below the ecliptic pole), preventing delta-V generation in the northern ecliptic direction. For ARTEMIS, radial thrusters mounted on the lower deck enforce this constraint. It is incorporated into the optimization as a nonlinear constraint requiring the delta-V direction to lie in the spin plane, with the maneuver epoch varied to achieve a radial direction.
