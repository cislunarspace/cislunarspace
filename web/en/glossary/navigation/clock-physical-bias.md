# Clock Physical Bias

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The frequency bias of an atomic clock due to manufacturing imperfections, distinct from the proper time vs. coordinate time difference caused by relativistic effects. Clock physical bias is typically modeled and predicted using polynomial models; current rubidium and hydrogen clocks achieve stability on the order of 2x10⁻¹⁴ per day.
