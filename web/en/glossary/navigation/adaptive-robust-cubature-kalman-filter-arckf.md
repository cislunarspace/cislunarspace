# Adaptive Robust Cubature Kalman Filter, ARCKF

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An enhanced CKF that simultaneously performs adaptive estimation of process noise covariance Q and measurement noise covariance R, while incorporating a robust factor weighting mechanism to suppress measurement outliers. It converges faster and is more robust than the standard CKF.
