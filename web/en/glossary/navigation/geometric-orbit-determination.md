# Geometric Orbit Determination

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A method of determining satellite orbit by using navigation satellite observation data from the spacecraft receiver to perform absolute single-point positioning by simultaneously observing 4 or more satellites to obtain the spacecraft position. This method is completely independent of the mechanical model and only relies on satellite measurement accuracy and the number and geometric configuration of observed satellites.
