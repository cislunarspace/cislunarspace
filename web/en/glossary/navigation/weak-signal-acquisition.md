# Weak Signal Acquisition

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Technology for identifying and locking onto navigation signals under extremely low signal-to-noise ratio conditions. Libration point constellation signals travel hundreds of thousands of kilometers, incurring 26 to 32 dB more path loss than GPS, requiring this technology to compensate for long-range attenuation.

## Application Value

在组合导航系统中，该方法能够提高定轨精度和收敛速度，增强导航系统的鲁棒性，适用于地月空间的自主导航任务。
