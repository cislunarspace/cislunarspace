# Dual One-Way Ranging, DOWR

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A measurement technique in which two satellites simultaneously exchange signals and each records a code pseudo-range at the same epoch. Through linear combinations (sum and difference) of the two pseudo-ranges, geometric distance and clock offset are decoupled, enabling independent orbit determination and time synchronization over a single inter-satellite link.

## Application Value

This concept is fundamental to cislunar orbital mechanics and mission analysis, providing essential theoretical support for trajectory design and operational planning.
