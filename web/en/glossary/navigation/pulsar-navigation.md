# Pulsar Navigation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An astronomical ranging navigation method that determines a spacecraft's position in the solar system barycentric frame by comparing observed X-ray pulsar signal arrival times with predicted models. Pulsar signal periods are extraordinarily stable, serving as natural cosmic clock references. The paper notes that China launched the XPNAV-1 pulsar navigation experiment satellite in 2017, NASA conducted X-ray Timing and Navigation Technology experiments on the ISS in 2018 achieving 5-10 km positioning accuracy, and China's 2019 in-orbit pulsar navigation experiment further improved accuracy. The method is applicable across all of space, but currently faces limitations including few usable pulsars, weak signals, long sampling periods, and challenges in high signal-to-noise ratio detection and rapid signal processing.

## Application Value

Pulsar navigation technology provides precise positioning capability for cislunar spacecraft and is one of the key technologies for achieving autonomous navigation. In practical missions, pulsar navigation can effectively improve orbit determination accuracy and reduce dependence on ground tracking systems. Research on pulsar navigation is of great significance for building cislunar space navigation infrastructure. Through pulsar navigation, spacecraft can achieve precise autonomous positioning in complex space environments, supporting lunar and deeper space exploration missions.
