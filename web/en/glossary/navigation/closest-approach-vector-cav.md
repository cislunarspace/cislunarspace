# Closest Approach Vector, CAV

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The relative position vector with the smallest norm between chaser and target, representing the time and direction of closest approach along a trajectory. Collision avoidance optimization aims to push the CAV outside the safety boundary.

## Application Value

The closest approach vector represents the direction of minimum relative distance between chaser and target. The optimization objective of collision avoidance maneuvers is to push the CAV outside the safety boundary, making it a core parameter of collision warning systems.
