# Rank Deficiency Problem

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

In SST autonomous orbit determination, the state transition matrices of conventional two-body orbit constellations cause multi-collinearity among columns of the extended coefficient matrix H, rendering the normal matrix rank-deficient. This manifests as the absolute orientation parameters of the constellation being unobservable from the measurements alone.

## Application Value

When designing Earth–Moon transfer schemes, exploiting this orbit characteristic reduces the total mission ΔV requirement. In cataloging systems, the technique improves the detection, tracking, and cataloging of space objects. Precise orbit determination is the foundation of cislunar space navigation and determines orbit prediction accuracy.
