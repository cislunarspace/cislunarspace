# Powered Explicit Guidance, PEG

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A bilinear tangent guidance law with constant thrust magnitude and linearly varying thrust direction, used for powered descent phases of rocket engines to control flight trajectory through real-time thrust direction adjustment.
