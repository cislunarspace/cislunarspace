# Ionospheric Delay

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)

> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The additional delay caused by changes in signal propagation speed as a radio signal passes through the ionosphere due to the presence of free electrons. Ionospheric delay is frequency-dependent: higher frequencies yield smaller delays. In cislunar ranging modeling, it is corrected as one of the propagation medium delay components.

## Application Value

This concept is essential for understanding motion characteristics in mission planning and analysis, and plays a vital role in mission success.
