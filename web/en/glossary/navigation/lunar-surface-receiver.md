# Lunar Surface Receiver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A GNSS receiver deployed on the lunar surface, approximately 380,000 km from Earth. Due to the vast distance, received GNSS main lobe signal power is about -180 dBW and side lobe signal below -195 dBW, both far weaker than terrestrial reception levels. With HEO constellation augmentation and main lobe signals only, average positioning accuracy of about 330.54 m can be achieved.

## Application Value

Navigation system design and implementation must account for observation geometry, error propagation, and signal transmission delay. This concept supports positioning accuracy evaluation, navigation filter design, and constellation optimization.
