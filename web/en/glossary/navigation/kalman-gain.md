# Kalman Gain

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Critical weighting matrix in EKF linking predicted state and measurement update, computed from predicted covariance and measurement noise covariance, determining how much the new measurement refines the state estimate.
