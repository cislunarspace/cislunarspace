# Free Space Propagation Loss

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The power attenuation of electromagnetic waves due to energy spreading during propagation in free space, dependent on propagation distance and signal wavelength, calculated as 20lg(λ/4πd). The large propagation distances in cislunar space make this a primary cause of weak signals.

## Application Value

地月空间传播距离远，传播损耗是信号微弱的主要原因之一。
