# Safety Boundary, SB

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A 100m-radius minimum safety sphere around the target, determined by the physical dimensions of the two vehicles. The chaser must enter in closed-loop control to perform docking or berthing.

## Application Value

Safety is the primary consideration in space missions, requiring multiple layers of redundancy and fault protection mechanisms in system design.
