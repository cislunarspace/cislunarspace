# Cramer-Rao Lower Bound, CRLB (CRLB)

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The inverse of the Fisher information matrix, representing the theoretical lower bound of the covariance of any unbiased estimator. In ISL-based autonomous orbit determination, it is used to evaluate the minimum achievable orbit determination accuracy given a specific observation geometry and measurement accuracy.

## Application Value

在编目系统中，该技术可提高对空间目标的探测、跟踪和编目能力。轨道预报精度是编目系统时效性的核心指标。
