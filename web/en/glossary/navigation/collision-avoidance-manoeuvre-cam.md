# Collision Avoidance Manoeuvre, CAM

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An active propulsion manoeuvre executed upon detecting collision risk, pushing the chaser away from the target's safety zones. Classified as Optimal CAM (for KOZ) and Swift CAM (for SB) by optimization level and response speed.
