# GDOP Value

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A numerical measure of Geometric Dilution of Precision, reflecting the amplification effect of visible navigation satellite spatial geometry on positioning accuracy. Smaller GDOP values indicate better satellite geometry and higher positioning accuracy. In cislunar space scenarios, GDOP without augmentation can reach thousands, reducing to the hundreds with HEO constellation augmentation.

## Application Value

This concept is applicable in cislunar space mission design, orbit optimization, and trajectory planning for spacecraft transfers and maneuver strategies.
