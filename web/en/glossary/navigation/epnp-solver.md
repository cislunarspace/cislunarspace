# EPnP Solver

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

Closed-form solver for the Perspective-n-Points problem, converting 3D-2D point correspondences into pose estimation


## Application Value

This optimization method has wide applications in cislunar space trajectory design, suitable for scenarios such as trajectory optimization and mission planning.
