# Optical Navigation

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

An autonomous navigation method that uses optical cameras to capture celestial body images and determines spacecraft relative position by measuring apparent diameter, limb contour, or pixel location. In lunar missions, it achieves positioning relative to the Moon center by detecting the lunar horizon, requiring simple and low-cost equipment.

## Application Value

This concept plays an important role in trajectory optimization and mission planning applications.
