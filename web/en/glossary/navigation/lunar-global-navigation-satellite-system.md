# Lunar Global Navigation Satellite System

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A satellite constellation system providing global positioning, navigation, and timing services for lunar surface users and cislunar space users near the Moon. Its design follows the architecture of Earth's GNSS constellation, deploying navigation satellites in lunar orbit to achieve full lunar surface coverage. Typical schemes integrate Distant Retrograde Orbits and L1/L2 Halo orbit satellites, balancing lunar polar and equatorial coverage with stable orbits and low maintenance costs.

## Application Value

During the orbital design phase, this orbital type can be used to construct candidate orbit pools, providing references for constellation deployment and mission orbit selection. During orbital operations, its orbital characteristics can be applied to orbit maintenance strategy design, reducing propellant consumption. In orbital transfer planning, its stable manifold structure can guide the search for low-energy transfer corridors.
