# Autonomous Orbit Determination

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

A technique where spacecraft determine their own orbits using only inter-satellite ranging data and orbital dynamics models, without relying on ground stations. Around a single body, rotational symmetry of the gravitational field causes deficient rank in the information matrix when only inter-satellite range is used. In the three-body problem, the strong gravitational perturbation of the third body breaks this symmetry, enabling autonomous orbit determination.

## Application Value

In orbital mechanics analysis and mission design, this concept provides a theoretical basis for evaluating orbital characteristics and comparing alternatives, helping to optimize orbital design parameters and improve mission economy.
