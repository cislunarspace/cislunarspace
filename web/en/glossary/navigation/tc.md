# Chipping Time

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The duration of a single chip of a GNSS pseudo-random code, equal to the reciprocal of the chipping rate. For the L1 C/A code, the chipping time is approximately 9.78e-7 seconds. It directly affects the pseudo-range measurement accuracy of the delay-locked loop.

## Application Value

The 码片时间 concept provides technical support and analytical methods for cislunar space research and lunar exploration missions.
