> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

NASA's space tracking and data relay system supporting LEO spacecraft tracking and communication.

## Application Value

Tracking and Data Relay Satellite System has significant application value in cislunar space missions, primarily used for autonomous positioning and orbit control of spacecraft.
