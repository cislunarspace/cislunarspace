# Pseudo-range Rate

> Author: [Tianjiang Shuo](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_33254264)
>
> Website: [https://cislunarspace.cn](https://cislunarspace.cn)

## Definition

The rate of change of pseudo-range, computed from carrier phase rate extracted by the phase-locked loop (PLL). Used alongside pseudo-range to provide both range and range-rate measurements for GNSS orbit determination.

## Application Value

Pseudo-range Rate is significant for spacecraft tracking, identification, and perception in cislunar space.
