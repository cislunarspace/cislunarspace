# NASA's Revised Moon Plan: Can Crewed Landing Happen in 2028?

**Summary:** NASA recently outlined a revised Artemis moon-landing plan: Artemis 3 will no longer attempt a direct lunar landing, but instead perform a crewed test in Earth orbit, pushing the crewed landing to Artemis 4 in late 2028. Both SpaceX's Starship HLS and Blue Origin's Blue Moon lander must complete key technology milestones by 2027.
