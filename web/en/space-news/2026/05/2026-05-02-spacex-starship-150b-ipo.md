# SpaceX IPO Filing Reveals Over $15B Invested in Starship, $1.75T Valuation

**Summary:** On May 1, 2026, Reuters cited SpaceX's confidential IPO filing revealing the company has invested over $15 billion in developing the next-generation Starship rocket, valued at $1.75 trillion, with plans to launch the first Starlink V3 satellites in H2 2026. SpaceX's space business R&D spending reached $3 billion in 2025, all directed to Starship.
