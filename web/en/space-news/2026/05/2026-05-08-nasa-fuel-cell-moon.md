# NASA Tests Regenerative Fuel Cell System for Sustainable Energy Storage on the Moon

**Summary:** NASA Glenn Research Center in Cleveland successfully tested a regenerative fuel cell system — about the size of a sedan — that generates electricity by combining hydrogen and oxygen and can recharge by splitting water back into its components. The technology is designed to provide sustainable power and energy storage for long-term lunar habitats and exploration under the Artemis program.
