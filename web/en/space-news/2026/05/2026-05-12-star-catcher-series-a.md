# Star Catcher Raises $65 Million Series A to Build World's First Space Power Grid

**Summary:** Florida-based Star Catcher Industries announced on May 12 it raised $65 million in an oversubscribed Series A funding round, bringing its total investment to $88 million, to develop the world's first in-space power grid that beams solar energy to client satellites via laser.
