# NASA, Eta Space Partner on In-Orbit Cryogenic Fluid Management Demo

**Summary:** NASA is collaborating with Eta Space of Rockledge, Florida, on the Liquid Oxygen Flight Demonstration (LOXSAT), an in-orbit test of cryogenic fluid management technologies necessary for creating in-space propellant depots — essentially gas stations in space — that could support long-term deep space exploration.
