# Blue Origin's Blue Moon Lander Mockup Assembled at NASA JSC for Artemis Training

**Summary:** A full-scale mockup of Blue Origin's Blue Moon Mark 2 (MK2) lunar lander crew cabin has been assembled inside the Space Vehicle Mockup Facility (SVMF) at NASA's Johnson Space Center (JSC) in Houston. The training mockup is ready for Artemis 3 astronauts to begin their pre-mission ground training, marking the first time a Blue Moon cabin model has been integrated into a NASA facility.
