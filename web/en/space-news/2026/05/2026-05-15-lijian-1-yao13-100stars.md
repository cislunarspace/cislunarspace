# Li Jian-1 Yao-13 Launches Five Satellites as Series Reaches 100-Orbit Milestone

**Summary:** On May 15, 2026 at 04:33 UTC (12:33 Beijing Time), CAS Space's Li Jian-1 Yao-13 rocket, also known as "China Has a Show," lifted off from the Dongfeng Commercial Space Innovation Experimental Zone, placing five satellites — including the Taijing-3 05A/05B, Tianyi-50, Tianyan-27, and Jilin-1 Gaofen-03D55 — into precise orbits via a "one rocket, five satellites" mission profile. This was the 13th flight of the Li Jian-1 rocket and the 14th launch of the broader Li Jian series, which has now delivered a cumulative 100 satellites to orbit with a total payload mass exceeding 18 tonnes, commanding the top market share in China's commercial launch services sector.

Li Jian-1 Chief Designer Shi Xiaoning described the "100-satellite milestone" as a landmark achievement signifying CAS Space's complete transition from a research-focused organization to a professional, mature commercial launch service provider, having established a comprehensive and rigorous project management and full-process quality assurance system.

The "Youxi" (有戏号) satellite — the world's first integrated space-ground interactive cultural communication satellite — will further drive commercial space applications in cultural dissemination.

## Payload Details

The "one rocket, five satellites" mission delivered the following satellites to orbit:

- **Taijing-3 05A/05B**: Optical Earth observation satellites
- **Tianyi-50**: Commercial remote sensing satellite
- **Tianyan-27**: Commercial communication/remote sensing satellite
- **Jilin-1 Gaofen-03D55**: High-resolution optical Earth observation satellite
- **"Youxi" satellite** (including "Rongcheng-1" 01/02 pair): World's first integrated space-ground interactive cultural communication satellite
- **"Dianjian-1"**: Professional application satellite

## Li Jian Series Milestone

The Li Jian-1 rocket, developed by CAS Space (中科宇航), is a solid-propellant launch vehicle with rapid response capabilities. Since its maiden flight, the Li Jian series has completed 14 successful launches, delivering 100 satellites to orbit with a cumulative payload mass exceeding 18 tonnes, holding the top market share in China's commercial launch services sector. The year 2026 is regarded as the "mass production inaugural year" for China's commercial rockets, and the Li Jian series' 100-satellite milestone marks a critical leap from single-mission verification toward high-density, low-cost, rapid-response, and scaled batch delivery.
