# Laser Detection Technique Reveals Environmental Impact of Falling Space Debris

**Summary:** Germany's Leibniz Institute of Atmospheric Physics has employed a novel triple-beam green laser detection technique to successfully identify metal vapor clouds generated by decaying satellites and rocket bodies during atmospheric reentry, quantifying their potential environmental impact on Earth's atmosphere.
