# China Unveils Shenzhou-23 Crewed Mission Badge: Circular Emblem Blends Tradition and Modernity

**Summary:** China's Manned Space Engineering Office officially released the mission badge for the upcoming Shenzhou-23 crewed spaceflight. The circular emblem features the Long March 2F launch vehicle as its central visual element, complemented by golden auspicious clouds and ruyi-pattern motifs. Three five-pointed stars represent the three crew members. The color scheme pairs technological blue as the dominant tone with Chinese red accents, symbolizing the seamless integration of traditional culture and cutting-edge space technology.
