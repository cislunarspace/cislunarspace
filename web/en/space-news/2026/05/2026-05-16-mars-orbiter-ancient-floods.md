# Mars Express Orbiter Releases Stunning Images of Ancient Flood Carved Terrain

**Summary:** The ESA-Roscosmos Mars Express orbiter releases high-resolution images revealing chaos craters and valley networks carved by ancient floods across regions like Shalbatana Vallis, offering new geological evidence that Mars was once far warmer and wetter than it is today.
