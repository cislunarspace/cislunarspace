# NASA ISS Research: How Space Bacteria That Cause Pneumonia Damage the Heart

**Summary:** Expedition 74 astronauts aboard the International Space Station are uncovering how bacteria that causes pneumonia can lead to long-term damage in the heart. In the microgravity environment, bacteria tend to be more severe and exhibit enhanced drug resistance. Researchers are leveraging these traits to observe how stem cell-derived heart tissues respond to bacterial infections, aiming to discover new methods for managing cardiovascular health and infectious diseases on Earth.
