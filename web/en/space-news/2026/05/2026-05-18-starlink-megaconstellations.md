# Scientists Warn: SpaceX Starlink and Other Satellite Megaconstellations Constitute an 'Unregulated Geoengineering Experiment'

**Summary:** Multiple scientists have jointly warned that the rapid deployment of SpaceX Starlink and other large satellite megaconstellations is negatively affecting Earth's atmosphere, constituting an "unregulated geoengineering experiment," and are calling on the international community to establish a corresponding regulatory framework.
