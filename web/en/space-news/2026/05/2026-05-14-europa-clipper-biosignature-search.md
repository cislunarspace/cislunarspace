# Scientists Propose New Approach to Alien Life Search: Europa Clipper Could Lead the Way

**Summary:** A team of researchers from the University of California, Riverside, published findings on May 14, 2026, proposing that the most effective method for searching for extraterrestrial life is not merely detecting biosignatures, but analyzing how they are organized — living matter tends to exhibit "life-like organization," while non-living matter lacks this order. The team noted that the MAss Spectrometer for Europa (MASS) aboard NASA's Europa Clipper spacecraft is equipped to make such measurements directly, potentially making it the first instrument to apply this approach when it flies by Jupiter's moon Europa in the 2030s.
