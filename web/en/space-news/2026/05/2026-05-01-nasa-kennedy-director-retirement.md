# NASA Kennedy Space Center Director Janet Petro Announces Retirement Plans

**Summary:** NASA announced on May 1, 2026, that Janet Petro, Director of NASA's Kennedy Space Center, has announced her retirement plans. During her tenure, Petro led the transformation of Kennedy from a traditional government launch facility into a modern commercial spaceport, making significant contributions to the development of commercial space in the United States.
