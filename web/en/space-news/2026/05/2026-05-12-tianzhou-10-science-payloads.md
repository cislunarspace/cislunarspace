# Tianzhou-10 Delivers 67 Scientific Payloads to Tiangong Space Station

**Summary:** Tianzhou-10 cargo spacecraft delivered 67 research products weighing approximately 770 kg to China's Tiangong Space Station on May 11, supporting 41 scientific experiments across four major domains: space life science and biotechnology, microgravity physics, new space application technologies, and astronomy & Earth science. Multiple experiments are being conducted in orbit for the first time in China.
