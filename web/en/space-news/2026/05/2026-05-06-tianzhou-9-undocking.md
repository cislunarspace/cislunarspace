# Tianzhou-9 Cargo Spacecraft Undocks from Tiangong Space Station

**Summary:** On May 6, 2026 at 16:34 Beijing Time (08:34 UTC), the Tianzhou-9 cargo spacecraft successfully undocked from the Tiangong Space Station complex, transitioning to independent flight. The spacecraft will conduct a controlled re-entry into Earth's atmosphere in the coming days, with any surviving debris set to land in a predetermined safe maritime area.

Tianzhou-9 is a cargo resupply spacecraft for China's Tiangong Space Station. Since its launch in 2023, it has completed multiple resupply missions. This undocking marks a routine operational step in the space station's ongoing operations, clearing the way for the upcoming Tianzhou-10 docking mission and subsequent crewed Shenzhou missions.
