# Cowboy Space Raises $275 Million to Deploy AI Data Centers in Earth Orbit

**Summary:** American space computing company Cowboy Space (formerly Aetherflux) has raised $275 million in a Series B funding round led by Index Ventures. The company plans to use the proceeds to build and operate AI data centers in Earth orbit, with its first launch targeted for late 2026.
