# World First: Artificial Human Embryos Enter Space Experiment Phase on China's Space Station

**Summary:** On May 13, multiple authoritative sources including Xinhua News Agency confirmed that samples of an artificial human embryo experiment — carried to space aboard the Tianzhou-10 cargo spacecraft — have been successfully installed in an experiment module aboard the China Space Station (Tiangong), with smooth progress. This marks the first time humanity has conducted an artificial embryo-related experiment in space, aiming to study the effects of microgravity on early human embryonic development and to pave the way for future long-term human space habitation.
