# NASA Perseverance Rover Snaps New Selfie at Mars' Western Frontier

**Summary:** On May 12, NASA's Perseverance rover captured a new selfie mosaic at Mars' Western Frontier, setting a new record for the number of Mars selfies, composed from multiple images taken by the WATSON camera.
