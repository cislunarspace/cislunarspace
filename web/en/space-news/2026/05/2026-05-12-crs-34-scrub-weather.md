# NASA, SpaceX Scrub CRS-34 Launch Due to Poor Weather, Target May 13

**Summary:** NASA and SpaceX called off the planned May 12 launch of the 34th Commercial Resupply Services (CRS-34) mission after poor weather conditions prevailed at Cape Canaveral. The mission is now targeted for May 13.
