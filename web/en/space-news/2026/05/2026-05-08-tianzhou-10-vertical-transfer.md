# Tianzhou-10 Cargo Spacecraft Vertical Transfer Completed, Launch Imminent

**Summary:** On May 8, 2026 (Beijing Time), the Tianzhou-10 cargo spacecraft and Long March 7 Y11 launch vehicle combination completed vertical transfer at the Wenchang Space Launch Center, officially entering final launch preparation. The mission, China's 5th cargo resupply flight for the Tiangong Space Station's application and development phase, is scheduled to launch in the coming days.
