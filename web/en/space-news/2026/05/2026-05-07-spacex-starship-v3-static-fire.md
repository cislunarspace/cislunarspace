# SpaceX Static-Fires 33-Engine Starship V3 Super Heavy Booster Ahead of May 15 Launch

**Summary:** SpaceX successfully conducted a static fire test of all 33 Raptor engines on the Version 3 Super Heavy booster at Starbase, Texas on May 7 (UTC), lasting 14 seconds. The company is targeting May 15 for Starship Flight 12, which would be the first flight of the new, more powerful V3 vehicle.
