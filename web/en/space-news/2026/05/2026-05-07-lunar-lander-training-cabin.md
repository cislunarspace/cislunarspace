# Blue Origin Blue Moon Mark 2 Crew Cabin Mockup Arrives at NASA Johnson Space Center

**Summary:** A full-scale prototype of the crew cabin for Blue Origin's Blue Moon Mark 2 crew lander has arrived at NASA's Johnson Space Center in Houston, Texas, to be used for astronaut training ahead of Artemis lunar missions.
