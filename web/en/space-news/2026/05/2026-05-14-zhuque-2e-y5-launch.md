# Landspace Zhuque-2E Y5 Launches Successfully

**Summary:** Landspace's Zhuque-2E Y5 launch vehicle successfully lifted off from the Dongfeng Commercial Space Innovation Test Zone on May 14, 2026, at 11:00 AM Beijing Time. The rocket performed nominally throughout its flight, with the second stage entering its planned orbit.
