# Malta Becomes 65th Artemis Accords Signatory, Joining NASA's Lunar Coalition

**Summary:** Malta became the 65th nation to sign the Artemis Accords during a ceremony held in Kalkara on May 4, 2026, with NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman delivering welcoming remarks via video. The signing marks Malta's formal entry into the U.S.-led international framework for lunar exploration.
