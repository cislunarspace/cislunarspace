# 80 Texas Residents Sue SpaceX Over Rocket Sonic Boom Property Damage, Seeking Millions in Compensation

**Summary:** On May 3, 2026, 80 residents of southern Texas filed a federal lawsuit against SpaceX, alleging that sonic booms from 11 rocket tests conducted between April 2023 and October 2025 caused significant damage to their homes near the Boca Chica Starbase facility. The plaintiffs allege negligence and strict liability, seeking millions in damages.

## Sources (original pages)

- [Texas Tribune: Texas residents sue SpaceX over rocket test sonic boom damage](https://www.tribtalk.org/)
- [QQ News: Texas residents sue SpaceX over massive sonic boom property damage](https://so.html5.qq.com/page/real/search_news?docid=70000021_63769f6d8fb28152)

## Details

According to the Texas Tribune, the lawsuit was filed last week in federal court. The 80 plaintiffs own properties in southern Texas near SpaceX's Starbase facility in Boca Chica, where the company develops and tests its Starship launch vehicle.

The complaint states that between April 2023 and October 2025, SpaceX conducted 11 rocket tests that generated powerful sonic booms, causing visible damage to the plaintiffs' homes, including:

- **Structural cracks**: Walls and foundations showing varying degrees of cracking
- **Broken glass**: Windows and glass doors shattered under the acoustic blast
- **Roof damage**: Roofing materials damaged by the shock wave
- **Interior damage**: Plaster falling, door frames warping

The plaintiffs' attorneys allege that SpaceX knew the sonic booms could damage surrounding structures but failed to take adequate measures to protect residents' property, constituting negligence and strict liability.

The specific damages sought have not been disclosed, but legal experts in Texas estimate the total claim could reach millions of dollars, given the scale of damage and number of affected residents.

## Background: Boca Chica and SpaceX Starbase

Boca Chica is home to SpaceX's Starship launch vehicle development and testing facility. Since 2014, the company has built out the site into America's largest private spaceport. As the Starship program has accelerated, test frequency has increased substantially.

This marks the first large-scale class-action lawsuit from Boca Chica residents against SpaceX over property damage. Previously, some residents had disputes with SpaceX over land acquisition and noise complaints, but a lawsuit of this magnitude over structural damage is unprecedented.
