# Pakistani Astronaut Candidates Debut in Chinese Feitian Spacesuits

**Summary:** In May 2026, two Pakistani astronaut candidates — Muhammad Zeeshan Ali and Khurram Daud — publicly appeared wearing the full Chinese "Feitian" intravehicular spacesuit at the China Astronaut Research and Training Center. Featuring Shenzhou mission patches and the flags of both Pakistan and China on their sleeves, the candidates were introduced as "Pioneers of Pakistan's First Crewed Spaceflight Mission." Under the China-Pakistan agreement, one of them will ultimately be selected to fly aboard China's Tiangong Space Station.

![](./figures/2026-05-03-pakistani-astronauts-chinese-spacesuits/hero.jpg)

Shenzhou-23 is scheduled to launch from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center in May 2026. The two Pakistani astronauts have been training in China for some time, and their photos in Chinese spacesuits quickly went viral on social media. Notably, while two candidates are shown in the photos, the China-Pakistan agreement specifies that only one Pakistani astronaut will ultimately execute the spaceflight mission — the Shenzhou-23 crew itself will remain primarily Chinese astronauts.

This debut marks a significant milestone in China's crewed space program international cooperation. China announced years ago that it would conduct in-depth space cooperation with Pakistan, ultimately selecting astronaut candidates for comprehensive training at Chinese facilities.
