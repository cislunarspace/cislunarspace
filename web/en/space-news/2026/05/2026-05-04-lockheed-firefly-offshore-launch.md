# Lockheed Martin Joins Firefly Aerospace and Seagate Space for Offshore Launch Collaboration

**Summary:** Lockheed Martin announced on May 4, 2026, that it is joining the offshore launch collaboration between Firefly Aerospace and Seagate Space, supporting Alpha rocket launches from the Seagate Space Gateway floating platform. Lockheed Martin will contribute its "legacy" expertise in national security alongside the "innovative spirit" of the two younger commercial space companies.
