# Solar Activity Causes Space Junk to Crash to Earth Faster

**Summary:** Scientists analyzing 36 years of orbital debris data have demonstrated for the first time that increased solar activity significantly accelerates the rate at which space debris falls into Earth's atmosphere. The finding is expected to be key for planning sustainable space operations in the future, with approximately 130 million pieces of space debris currently orbiting Earth.
