# Ireland Becomes 66th Artemis Accords Signatory, Joining NASA in Lunar Exploration Commitment

**Summary:** Ireland signed the Artemis Accords on May 4, 2026, at a ceremony hosted by NASA in Washington, D.C., becoming the 66th signatory and the final ESA member state to join the U.S.-led lunar exploration framework.
