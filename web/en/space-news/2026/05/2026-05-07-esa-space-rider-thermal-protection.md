# ESA Space Rider Thermal Protection System Passes Extreme Testing

**Summary:** ESA's Space Rider reusable spacecraft has cleared two major technical hurdles — its thermal protection system surviving extreme reentry conditions and the assembly of a full-scale drop-test model for guided landing trials — bringing Europe closer to flying its first reusable orbital vehicle later this decade.
