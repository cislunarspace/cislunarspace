# SpaceX Reportedly Exploring International Launch Sites for Starship Rocket

**Summary:** SpaceX has begun exploring potential international launch sites for its Starship megarocket, engaging with several countries about hosting launch facilities, according to multiple reports. The discussions come as Starship V3 approaches its third integrated flight test, targeting May 19 from SpaceX's Boca Chica, Texas facility.
