# SpaceX CRS-34 Cargo Dragon Successfully Docks with International Space Station

**Summary:** SpaceX's CRS-34 cargo Dragon spacecraft successfully docked with the International Space Station's Harmony module at 6:37 a.m. EDT on Sunday, May 17, 2026, delivering approximately 6,500 pounds (≈2,948 kg) of science research materials and crew supplies. The mission marks the 34th Commercial Resupply Services flight and the first Dragon cargo mission of 2026.
