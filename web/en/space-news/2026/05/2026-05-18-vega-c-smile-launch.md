# Vega C Launches European-Chinese SMILE Space Weather Mission

**Summary:** A Vega C rocket successfully launched the European-Chinese SMILE (Solar wind Magnetosphere Ionosphere Link Explorer) space weather satellite from Europe's Spaceport in Kourou, French Guiana. The spacecraft entered a 707 km near-Earth orbit to study how Earth is affected by the solar wind.
