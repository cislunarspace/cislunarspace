# Paraguay Signs Artemis Accords, Becomes 67th Signatory

**Summary:** The Republic of Paraguay signed the Artemis Accords on May 7, 2026, during a ceremony in Asunción, becoming the 67th nation to commit to the shared principles guiding civil space exploration.
