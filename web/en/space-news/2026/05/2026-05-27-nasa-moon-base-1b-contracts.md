# NASA Awards Nearly $1 Billion in Moon Base Contracts to Astrolab, Lunar Outpost, and Blue Origin

**Summary:** NASA announced nearly $1 billion in initial Moon Base contracts on May 27, 2026, during a press conference at NASA Headquarters in Washington D.C. Astrolab and Lunar Outpost each received approximately $220 million contracts to complete their lunar terrain vehicle (LTV) designs and deliver them to the lunar surface. Blue Origin was awarded a contract worth approximately $234 million per LTV delivered, using its Blue Moon Mark 1 lander. NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman said missions would begin in the back half of 2026, with the first phase extending through 2029, establishing a "hundreds of square miles" human/robotic hybrid base at the lunar south pole.

## Three-Phase Lunar Base Roadmap

NASA unveiled a three-phase plan for permanent human presence on the Moon:

**Phase 1 (through 2029):** Intensive robotic missions — 25 launches, 21 landings, delivering approximately 4 metric tons of cargo to the lunar surface. This includes the first three contracted missions (Lunar Base 1, 2, and 3) carrying rovers, scientific instruments, and technology demonstrations.

**Phase 2 (2029–2032):** Assembly of semi-permanent facilities enabling early crew habitation. Power, communications, and life support infrastructure will be deployed to support sustained operations.

**Phase 3 (from 2032):** Transition to continuous human presence on the Moon. The base may eventually cover "hundreds of square miles" with regular crew rotations and expanded scientific activities.
