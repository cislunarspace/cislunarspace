# NASA-Supported 3D Printing Tech Advances Earthly Construction

**Summary:** A 3D printing construction process originally developed to outfit NASA's lunar habitat designs has been adapted for use on Earth, making building construction more efficient and sustainable. The technology transfer showcases how NASA research creates tangible benefits beyond space exploration.
