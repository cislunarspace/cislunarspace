# SpaceX Targets May 19 for Starship V3 Debut from Launch Pad 2

**Summary:** Following a successful integrated tanking test of the full Super Heavy-Starship stack, SpaceX announced on May 12 that the third major iteration of Starship will launch no earlier than May 19 from the newly constructed Pad 2 — marking both the V3 debut and the first launch from the updated infrastructure supporting both launch and catch operations.
