# China's Tianzhou-10 Delivers New Space Treadmill to Tiangong Space Station

**Summary:** On May 11, 2026, Tianzhou-10 cargo spacecraft successfully docked with China's Tiangong space station. The cargo includes a new upgraded space treadmill, 67 pieces of scientific research materials, 6 experiment payloads, and a new extravehicular spacesuit, comprehensively upgrading living and research conditions onboard.
