# Shenzhou 21 Crew Returns to Beijing After Record-Breaking 210-Day Mission

**Summary:** China's Shenzhou 21 astronaut crew returned to Beijing by aircraft on May 30, 2026, after completing a historic 210-day mission aboard the Tiangong space station — a new record for the longest single crew duration in Chinese spaceflight history.
