# CNSA Releases Latest Commercial Space Launch Progress Report

**Summary:** On July 25, 2026, CNSA released a progress report highlighting recent commercial launch achievements by Zhuque, Lijian, and Kuaizhou rockets, affirming the momentum of Chinas private space sector.

On July 25, 2026, the China National Space Administration (CNSA) published a comprehensive progress report on commercial space launches via its official website, systematically cataloging recent domestic commercial rocket achievements. The report highlighted mission performance across the LandSpace Zhuque series, CAS Space Lijian series, and Expace Kuaizhou series, commending private space enterprises for their pace of technical iteration and mission success rates.

According to the report, the Zhuque series has continued advancing reusable rocket technology verification, the Lijian series has achieved milestones in batch production and high-frequency launches of solid-propellant launch vehicles, and the Kuaizhou series has maintained its rapid-response launch capability. CNSA noted that these commercial rockets have served a diverse range of payload requirements spanning Earth observation, communications, and technology demonstration missions, indicating that the domestic commercial space sector is transitioning from standalone launch services toward full value chain coordination.

On the policy front, CNSA reiterated its stance of encouraging the orderly and regulated development of commercial space activities. The administration signaled that it will further refine commercial launch licensing and safety management mechanisms, aiming to provide private space enterprises with a more transparent and predictable business environment. Industry observers interpreted this as a continued positive regulatory signal, which could help stabilize market expectations and attract greater private capital investment.

In recent years, the frequency of commercial space launches in China has risen steadily, with an increasingly diverse range of participants. This latest report serves as both a milestone summary and a tone-setter for the next phase of commercial space development. For the specific mission manifest and upcoming launch schedules, readers are directed to official CNSA announcements.
