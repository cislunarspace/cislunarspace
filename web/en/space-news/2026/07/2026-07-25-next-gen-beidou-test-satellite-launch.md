# Next-Generation BeiDou Navigation Test Satellite Successfully Launched, System Upgrade Enters New Phase

**Summary:** On July 25, China successfully launched a next-generation BeiDou navigation test satellite from Xichang Satellite Launch Center using a Long March 3B rocket, marking a new phase in the BeiDou systems upgrade and evolution.

On July 25, China successfully launched a next-generation BeiDou navigation test satellite from the Xichang Satellite Launch Center in Sichuan Province using a Long March 3B launch vehicle. According to CCTV News, the mission was a complete success, with the satellite entering its intended orbit and all onboard systems operating normally. The launch marks the official entry of the BeiDou Navigation Satellite System (BDS) into a new phase of upgrade and evolution.

The BeiDou Navigation Satellite System (BDS) is China independently constructed and operated global satellite navigation system, consisting of a space segment, a ground segment, and a user segment. The BeiDou-3 global navigation satellite system was officially completed and put into operation in July 2020, with more than 30 BeiDou satellites currently in orbit providing positioning, navigation, and timing (PNT) services to users worldwide. The next-generation test satellite launched this time will be primarily used to verify the technical architecture and key performance parameters of the new-generation navigation satellites, laying the technical foundation for the comprehensive future upgrade of the BeiDou system.

The Long March 3B is one of China primary workhorse launch vehicles, part of the Long March 3A family, and is mainly used for geostationary transfer orbit missions. It has previously carried out numerous BeiDou navigation satellite launches. This mission represents another successful flight for the Long March rocket family, further demonstrating the reliability and mission adaptability of this launch vehicle. According to reports, additional launches of next-generation BeiDou satellites and on-orbit technology verification campaigns are planned for the future.
