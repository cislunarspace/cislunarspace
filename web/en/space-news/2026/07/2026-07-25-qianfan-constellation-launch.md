# Yuanxin Satellite Successfully Launches Batch of Qianfan Constellation Satellites

**Summary:** On July 25, Yuanxin Satellite launched Qianfan constellation satellites from Jiuquan, advancing satellite internet development.

On July 25, 2026, Yuanxin Satellite Technology Co., Ltd. successfully launched a new batch of Qianfan constellation satellites into their designated orbits from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center. This mission marks another step forward in the Qianfan constellation networking plan, further advancing satellite internet infrastructure development.

The Qianfan constellation is a large-scale low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellite internet network led by Yuanxin Satellite, designed to provide broadband internet access services to users worldwide. Since the first batch was launched, the constellation has been deployed in sequential batches according to a predetermined schedule, gradually building a globally covering communication network.

This launch is one of the key Qianfan constellation missions for 2026. According to previously disclosed information, Yuanxin Satellite plans to carry out multiple launches throughout the year to accelerate the constellation networking process. As the number of satellites in orbit continues to grow, the service capability of the Qianfan constellation will further improve, laying the groundwork for subsequent commercial operations.

Detailed information including the exact number of satellites on board, the launch vehicle type, and orbital parameters is awaiting further disclosure from official sources.
