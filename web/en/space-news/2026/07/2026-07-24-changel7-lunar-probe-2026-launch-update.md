# Chinas Change-7 Lunar Probe on Track for 2026 Launch

**Summary:** On July 24, 2026, CNSA announced that the Change-7 lunar probe is progressing smoothly in development, on track for launch within 2026

On July 24, 2026, the China National Space Administration (CNSA) announced that the development of the Change-7 lunar probe is progressing smoothly, with the spacecraft currently entering a critical pre-launch testing phase. According to the official statement, all systems are advancing on schedule, and the mission is slated for launch within 2026.

The Change-7 mission is a key component of Chinas Lunar Exploration Program Phase IV. Its primary scientific objectives include conducting a detailed survey of the lunar south polar region and searching for evidence of water ice and other volatile substances. The probe consists of multiple components — an orbiter, lander, rover, and a flying detector — designed to work in concert to perform multi-dimensional investigations of the Moons surface morphology, mineral composition, and space environment.

Reportedly, Change-7 is targeted to land near the rim of Shackleton Crater at the lunar south pole, an area believed to host permanently shadowed regions that are prime candidates for harboring water ice. The success of this mission would lay a crucial foundation for the subsequent Change-8 mission and the planned construction of the International Lunar Research Station. Further details regarding the specific launch window and mission parameters are awaiting official confirmation.
