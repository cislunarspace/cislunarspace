# Long March 5 Y14 Rocket Arrives at Wenchang for Change-7 Mission

**Summary:** On July 13, 2026, the Long March 5 Y14 rocket, tasked with the Change-7 mission, arrived safely at the Wenchang Spacecraft Launch Site.

According to the China Manned Space Agency, the Long March 5 Y14 launch vehicle, designated for the Chang'e-7 lunar probe mission, was safely transported via sea to the Wenchang Spacecraft Launch Site in China on July 13, 2026. The multi-day transport operation was completed successfully, with all rocket components reported to be in good condition. This arrival marks a new critical phase in the launch preparations for the Chang'e-7 mission.

Following its arrival, the rocket will undergo a series of assembly, testing, and inspection procedures as scheduled. These steps are designed to ensure the vehicle is in optimal technical condition for launch. The Wenchang launch site, China's only coastal spaceport at a low latitude, offers unique geographical advantages for deep space exploration missions.

The Chang'e-7 mission is a vital component of China's Lunar Exploration Program Phase IV. Its primary objective is to conduct detailed surveys of the lunar south pole's environment and resources. The successful execution of this mission will lay a crucial foundation for the subsequent Chang'e-8 mission and the construction of the International Lunar Research Station. All preparatory work is currently progressing steadily according to plan.
