# Shenzhou-17 Astronaut Crew Working Smoothly in Orbit

**Summary:** On July 24, 2026, the Shenzhou-17 astronaut crew conducted various scientific experiments in the space station, working in good condition.

On July 24, 2026, according to CCTV reports, the three-member crew of the Shenzhou-17 crewed spacecraft continued their smooth operations aboard the China Space Station, Tiangong. All astronauts were reported to be in good physical health and high spirits. Since entering the station, the crew has been carrying out routine daily tasks including in-orbit health monitoring and cabin environment maintenance, with all station systems operating stably.

In terms of scientific research, the crew completed multiple space science experiments on that day, covering fields such as space life sciences, microgravity fluid physics, and space materials science. According to the China Manned Space Agency, experimental samples were collected and preserved in accordance with established protocols and will be returned to Earth via the re-entry capsule for further analysis on the ground.

The astronauts also conducted preparatory inspections of external equipment and performed routine checks and maintenance on various experiment racks inside the station. According to reports, the crew is expected to carry out an extravehicular activity in the near future to inspect and upgrade external components of the space station, with the exact date yet to be confirmed by the authorities.

Currently, the space station complex maintains a stable orbital trajectory, and telemetry and communication links are functioning normally. The ground control center maintains close communication with the crew to ensure the continued smooth progress of all on-orbit missions.
