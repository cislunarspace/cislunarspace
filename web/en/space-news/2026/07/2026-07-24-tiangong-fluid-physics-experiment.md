# Tiangong Station Completes Key Microgravity Fluid Physics Experiment With International Collaboration

**Summary:** On July 24, 2026, astronauts aboard Chinas Tiangong station completed a microgravity fluid dynamics experiment, with results published jointly by Chinese and international researchers.

On July 24, 2026, astronauts aboard China's Tiangong space station successfully completed a key microgravity fluid physics experiment. According to SpaceNews, the experiment is part of the station's on-orbit science mission, designed to study fluid behavior in microgravity environments. The resulting data is expected to provide valuable references for future spacecraft design and in-space manufacturing.

A notable aspect of this experiment is its international collaborative nature. The results were published jointly by researchers from China and multiple other countries, reflecting Tiangong's expanding openness to international scientific cooperation. By observing fluid dynamics phenomena under microgravity conditions, the research team obtained scientific data that would be difficult to replicate in ground-based experiments. Further details regarding the specific experiment parameters and the list of international partners are pending confirmation from the relevant agencies.

Microgravity fluid physics is a critical area of aerospace research. In the microgravity environment of space, fluid behaviors such as convection, surface tension, and capillary action differ significantly from those on Earth. These studies carry important implications for advancing key technologies including spacecraft thermal management, propellant storage, and life support systems. Tiangong continues to conduct such fundamental scientific research, building a technical foundation for crewed deep space exploration missions.
