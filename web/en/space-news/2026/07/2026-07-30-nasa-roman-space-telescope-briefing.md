# NASA to Host Media Briefing on Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope

**Summary:** NASA announced it will host a media briefing next month before the launch of the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, which was lifted to a work stand in late June.

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) has announced plans to host a media briefing next month, ahead of the scheduled launch of the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope. The briefing will focus on the latest status and launch readiness of the observatory, providing an update on this flagship astronomical mission.

A significant milestone was achieved in late June 2026 when the telescope was successfully lifted to a work stand. This operation marked substantial progress in the assembly and testing phase, laying the groundwork for the upcoming launch. The Roman Space Telescope is NASA's next-generation wide-field space observatory designed to investigate dark energy, exoplanets, and the evolution of the universe.

According to NASA, the specific date for the briefing, along with the list of participating experts and a detailed agenda, will be announced in the coming weeks. The telescope is currently scheduled for launch in August 2026 from NASA's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. This briefing will serve as a key opportunity to present the mission's launch preparations to the media before liftoff.
