# CNSA Reports on Recent Commercial Space Launch Progress

**Summary:** On July 25, 2026, CNSA reported on recent launch achievements of commercial rockets including Zhuque, Lijian, and Kuaizhou, affirming the momentum of private space development.

On July 25, 2026, the China National Space Administration (CNSA) held a press conference to report on the recent progress in China's commercial space sector. The briefing highlighted the latest launch achievements and mission performance of rockets developed by private space companies, specifically the Zhuque series, the Lijian series, and the Kuaizhou series operated under the China Aerospace Science and Industry Corporation (CASIC).

According to the report, the Zhuque series rockets have successfully completed multiple launch missions recently, further validating their technical reliability and commercial operational capabilities. The Lijian series launch vehicles have also continued to make progress in the launch services market, providing stable orbital insertion services for domestic and international customers. The Kuaizhou series rockets, known for their rapid response and flexible launch capabilities, have played a significant role in contingency launches and commercial ride-share missions.

CNSA fully affirmed the strong development momentum demonstrated by private space enterprises during the conference. Officials stated that commercial space, as a vital component of the national space program, is of great importance for driving aerospace technological innovation, reducing the cost of accessing space, and fostering new business models in the space industry. In the future, the state will continue to improve relevant policies and regulations to create a more equitable and open development environment for commercial space companies.
