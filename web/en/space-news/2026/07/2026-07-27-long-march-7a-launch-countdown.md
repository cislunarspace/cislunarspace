# Long March 7A Rocket Rolled Out to Launch Pad, Countdown Begins for July 27 Launch

**Summary:** On July 27, 2026, the Long March 7A rocket was successfully rolled out to the launch pad at Wenchang, entering a two-day countdown for its upcoming launch.

According to the China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation, the Long March 7A rocket designated for this mission was vertically transferred from the technical area to the launch zone at the Wenchang Spacecraft Launch Site in Hainan on July 27, 2026, following the completion of its tests. This milestone signifies that the critical pre-launch preparations are largely complete, officially initiating the final two-day countdown for the mission.

The Long March 7A is a new-generation medium-lift launch vehicle primarily designed to carry payloads to geosynchronous transfer orbit. The rollout process proceeded smoothly, with the rocket moving steadily along a dedicated track to the launch pad, carried by its mobile launch platform. Upon arrival, final inspections, tests, and propellant loading operations will be conducted.

The launch is currently scheduled to take place on July 29, 2026. All systems at the launch site are reported to be in good condition, and personnel are proceeding with preparations according to plan to ensure the mission's complete success. Specific launch window times and payload details are expected to be officially announced closer to the launch.
