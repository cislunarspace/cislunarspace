# BeiDou Navigation Satellite System Completes In-Orbit Upgrade

**Summary:** On July 31, 2026, Chinas BeiDou Navigation Satellite System completed a comprehensive in-orbit upgrade of its 50 satellites, achieving sub-meter positioning accuracy.

China's BeiDou Navigation Satellite System (BDS) has successfully completed a comprehensive remote software upgrade for all 50 satellites currently in orbit, the China Satellite Navigation System Office announced on July 31, 2026. This upgrade, which covered the entire constellation of the BeiDou-3 global system, marks a significant step forward in the system's technological iteration and autonomous operational capabilities.

According to official information, the core achievement of this upgrade is a major breakthrough in positioning accuracy, officially entering the sub-meter era. This means the positioning service provided by BDS to global users will now be accurate to within one meter, better serving high-precision application scenarios such as autonomous driving, precision agriculture, smart cities, and surveying and mapping. The upgrade was executed through remote command injection and software updates from ground control centers to the satellites in orbit, demonstrating the powerful in-orbit management and maintenance capabilities of the BeiDou system.

This full-scale upgrade is a crucial part of the continuous evolution and optimization of the BeiDou system. It not only enhances the system's core performance metrics but also lays a solid foundation for the future expansion of higher-precision and more reliable services. The China Satellite Navigation System Office stated that the BeiDou system will continue to adhere to innovation-driven development, constantly improve service performance, and provide global users with superior and reliable spatiotemporal information services.
