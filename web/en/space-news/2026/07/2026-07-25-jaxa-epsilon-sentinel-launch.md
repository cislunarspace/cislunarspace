# JAXAs Epsilon Rocket Launches ESAs Sentinel Satellite for Earth Observation

**Summary:** A JAXA Epsilon rocket successfully launched an ESA Sentinel satellite to monitor Earths environment, marking a key step in international space cooperation. Date: 2026-07-25.

On July 25, 2026, the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA) successfully launched an ESA Sentinel satellite using an Epsilon rocket. The satellite is designed for Earth observation and environmental monitoring.

The Epsilon rocket is a solid-fuel launch vehicle developed by JAXA, primarily used for launching small to medium-sized satellites. This launch represents a significant step forward in international space cooperation between Japan and Europe.

The Sentinel satellite series is a crucial component of ESAs Copernicus program, providing continuous Earth observation data. The satellite will support climate change research, natural disaster monitoring, and other environmental applications.

The successful mission demonstrates the reliability of the Epsilon rocket and lays the foundation for future international cooperation in space exploration.
