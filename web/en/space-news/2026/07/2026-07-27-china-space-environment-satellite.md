# China Launches New Satellite for Space Environment Exploration

**Summary:** China launched a new satellite for space environment exploration on July 27, 2026, at 1:10 p.m. Beijing Time using a Long March rocket.

China successfully launched a new space environment exploration satellite on July 27, 2026, at 1:10 p.m. Beijing Time (05:10 GMT). A Long March carrier rocket lifted off from a Chinese launch facility and placed the satellite into its预定 orbit, marking another successful mission for the Long March rocket family, according to Xinhua News Agency.

The satellite is designed to carry out in-orbit measurements of key physical parameters in near-Earth space, including high-energy particle radiation, magnetic field conditions, and plasma environments. Data gathered from such missions are essential for ensuring the safe operation of spacecraft in orbit, forecasting space weather events, and supporting crewed spaceflight programs.

In recent years, China has been steadily expanding its space environment monitoring capabilities by deploying a series of scientific and technological test satellites. These efforts are contributing to the gradual build-up of a multi-orbit space environment observation network. This latest launch adds further capacity to the country's space science instrumentation portfolio.
