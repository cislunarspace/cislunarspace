# NASA Selects SpaceX to Provide Laser Communications for Artemis III Mission

**Summary:** On July 30, NASA announced it has selected SpaceX to provide laser communications capabilities for next years Artemis III mission to deliver imagery from the Orion spacecraft.

The National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) officially announced on July 30, 2026, that it has selected SpaceX to provide critical laser communications capabilities for the Artemis III mission. Scheduled for 2027, the mission aims to return astronauts to the lunar surface. Under the agreement, SpaceX will be responsible for developing and providing a laser communications terminal to be integrated into the Orion spacecraft.

The selected laser communications technology offers significant advantages over traditional radio communications. It is capable of transmitting high-definition imagery and scientific data captured by the Orion spacecraft during the mission back to Earth at much higher data rates. According to NASA, this will greatly enhance the efficiency of data return for future deep space missions, providing more timely information for scientific research and mission decision-making.

The Artemis III mission is a pivotal flight in the Artemis program, targeting the first crewed lunar landing since the Apollo era. The contract awarded to SpaceX is a crucial component in ensuring the reliable operation of the mission's communication systems. NASA stated that the collaboration with SpaceX will help validate this advanced technology, laying the groundwork for subsequent deep space exploration missions to more distant destinations.
