# CCTV News： Shenzhou-23 Crewed Mission Declared a Complete Success

**Summary:** On July 27, 2026, CCTV News reported the Shenzhou-23 crews two-month space journey was a complete success, with all scientific experiments progressing smoothly.

According to a report by CCTV News on July 27, 2026, the Shenzhou-23 crew has completed two months of work and life in orbit. The report stated that this space journey was a complete success, with the crew in good condition and the space station complex operating stably.

During their two-month orbital stay, the Shenzhou-23 crew completed multiple space science experiments and technology tests as planned. It was reported that all scientific experiments progressed smoothly and achieved expected results, accumulating valuable experience for the application and development phase of the Chinese space station.

Currently, the Shenzhou-23 crew continues to carry out follow-up tasks inside the space station, including experiments in fields such as space life sciences and microgravity physics. The astronauts physical health and psychological status remain good, and all work is advancing in an orderly manner according to plan.
