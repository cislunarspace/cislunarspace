# CAS Space Signs Intent Contract with Starlink Data Chain for GTO Satellite Launch

**Summary:** On July 31, 2026, CAS Space announced the signing of an intent contract with Starlink Data Chain for the launch of a GTO communication satellite using the Lijian-2 rocket.

On July 31, 2026, Chinese commercial space company CAS Space announced that it has formally signed an intent contract with Starlink Data Chain for the launch of a communication satellite. Under the agreement, CAS Space will utilize its Lijian-2 liquid-fueled launch vehicle, currently under development, to deliver a communication satellite into Geosynchronous Transfer Orbit (GTO) for Starlink Data Chain.

The Lijian-2 is a new-generation medium-lift liquid rocket developed by CAS Space, designed to meet the diverse market demands of future large-scale constellation deployments and high-orbit satellite launches. The signing of this contract marks a significant milestone for the Lijian-2 in the commercial launch services market and reflects market confidence in the capabilities of domestically developed new launch vehicles. Reports indicate that the rocket is currently in its development phase, and the specific launch schedule will be determined based on subsequent negotiations between the parties and the progress of the rocket's development.

This collaboration represents a notable market linkage within China's commercial space sector. As a satellite communication and data service provider, Starlink Data Chain has an ongoing demand for efficient and reliable launch services. Through the Lijian-2 program, CAS Space is actively positioning itself in the commercial launch market, aiming to offer competitive launch solutions to domestic and international clients. The achievement of this intent contract lays a foundation for deeper cooperation between the two parties in the future.
