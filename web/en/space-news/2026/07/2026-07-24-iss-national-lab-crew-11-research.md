# ISS National Laboratory Sponsors Multiple Research Payloads on SpaceX Crew-11 Mission

**Summary:** The ISS National Laboratory announced on July 24, 2026 that it is sponsoring multiple research payloads on the upcoming SpaceX Crew-11 mission.

The ISS National Laboratory confirmed that it is funding and manifesting multiple research payloads aboard the upcoming SpaceX Crew-11 mission. Crew-11 is part of SpaceX's routine crew rotation flights to the International Space Station under its Commercial Crew Transportation Contract with NASA, expected to deliver a new contingent of astronauts for a long-duration stay aboard the orbiting laboratory.

As the primary platform operator for U.S. research in low Earth orbit, the ISS National Laboratory provides microgravity research opportunities for commercial companies, academic institutions, and government agencies. The sponsored payloads span multiple research disciplines, with the full experiment manifest and detailed research objectives to be disclosed in subsequent announcements. The laboratory has previously facilitated hundreds of research investigations through numerous commercial crew and cargo missions.

This announcement underscores the ISS National Laboratory's continued commitment to advancing commercial research in low Earth orbit. As the International Space Station transitions toward a more commercially oriented operational model, sponsored research missions like this are expected to become increasingly common, offering unique microgravity conditions for drug development, advanced materials manufacturing, and life sciences research. The specific launch date and crew composition for Crew-11 have yet to be officially confirmed by NASA and SpaceX.
