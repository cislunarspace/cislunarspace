# Satellite Internet Constellation Deployment Accelerates, Industry Awaits Commercialization

**Summary:** On July 31, 2026, industry data showed the Qianfan constellation has 238 satellites in orbit and the GW constellation has about 180 satellites operating, with both planning to complete backbone network deployment within the year.

According to the latest industry data, as of July 31, 2026, the deployment of China's two major low-Earth orbit (LEO) satellite internet constellations—Qianfan and GW—is accelerating significantly. The Qianfan constellation currently has 238 satellites in orbit, while the GW constellation has approximately 180 satellites operational. Both constellations have set clear plans to complete the deployment of their backbone networks by the end of 2026.

The rapid constellation deployment marks a solid step forward in China's efforts to build an integrated space-ground information network. LEO satellite constellations, with their potential for low latency and global coverage, are considered a key component of future communication infrastructure. As the number of satellites in orbit continues to increase, network coverage capabilities and system reliability are steadily improving.

However, the industry is widely focused on how to achieve a commercial closed-loop as quickly as possible, alongside the accelerated technical deployment. This involves the popularization of terminal devices, optimization of tariff plans, and innovation in business models for vertical industries such as maritime, aviation, and emergency communications. Only by forming a sustainable commercial ecosystem can the long-term operation and iterative upgrades of the constellations be supported.

Currently, relevant operators are actively collaborating with upstream and downstream players in the industrial chain to explore diversified application scenarios and profit models. The market expects that with the completion of the backbone networks, trial commercial services may be launched in the near future, offering users a more comprehensive satellite internet access experience.
