# Long March 5 Y14 Rocket Arrives at Wenchang for Change-7 Mission

**Summary:** On July 13, 2026, the Long March 5 Y14 rocket for the Change-7 mission arrived safely at the Wenchang Spacecraft Launch Site, marking a key milestone for the lunar exploration programs fourth phase.

According to a report from IT之家, the Long March 5 Y14 launch vehicle, designated for the Change-7 lunar exploration mission, arrived safely at the Wenchang Spacecraft Launch Site in China on July 13, 2026, via sea transport. The successful completion of this transportation marks the official entry of a key mission in China's Lunar Exploration Program Phase IV into its final pre-launch preparation stage.

The Long March 5 Y14 rocket is the launch vehicle specifically configured for the Change-7 mission. Following its arrival at the launch site, it will undergo a series of rigorous assembly, testing, and inspection procedures according to schedule to ensure its technical condition meets the launch requirements. These preparatory steps are critical to ensuring mission success.

The Change-7 mission is a vital component of China's Lunar Exploration Program Phase IV. Its primary objectives include conducting a detailed survey of the lunar south pole, with a particular focus on searching for resources such as lunar water ice. The successful execution of this mission will lay a crucial foundation for the subsequent Change-8 mission and the construction of the International Lunar Research Station. Currently, all preparatory work at the launch site is proceeding in an orderly manner.
