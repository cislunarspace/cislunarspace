# Long March 5 Y14 Rocket for Change-7 Mission Arrives at Wenchang Launch Site

**Summary:** On July 13, 2026, the Long March 5 Y14 rocket for the Change-7 mission safely arrived at the Wenchang Space Launch Site, where it will undergo preparations alongside the previously arrived Change-7 probe.

According to the China National Space Administration (CNSA), the Long March 5 Y14 launch vehicle designated for the Change-7 lunar mission safely arrived at the Wenchang Space Launch Site in China on July 13, 2026, following its long-distance transport. This milestone marks the beginning of a new phase in the mission's launch site preparations.

The arrived rocket will now join the Change-7 probe, which was transported to the site earlier. The rocket and the probe will subsequently undergo a series of tests, assembly, and joint inspections in the technical area to ensure full readiness for the final launch.

The Change-7 mission is a crucial part of China's Lunar Exploration Program Phase IV. It aims to conduct a detailed survey of the lunar south pole, with a particular focus on investigating the environment and resources in that region. The safe arrival of the Long March 5 Y14 rocket, the mission's launch vehicle, is a key step toward the successful execution of the mission. The specific launch window will be determined based on the overall mission readiness and orbital conditions.
