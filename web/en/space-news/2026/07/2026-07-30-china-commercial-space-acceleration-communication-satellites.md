# Successful Launch of Communication Tech Test Satellites Marks Acceleration in Chinas Commercial Space

**Summary:** On July 30, 2026, China successfully launched the Communication Technology Test Satellites 27A/B from the Taiyuan Satellite Launch Center, marking the 18th space launch of the year and paving the way for the Smart China wide-area satellite communication backbone network planned for 2027.

On July 30, 2026, China successfully launched the Communication Technology Test Satellites 27A and 27B into their predetermined orbits from the Taiyuan Satellite Launch Center using a Long March series launch vehicle. The mission was a complete success. This launch marks China's 18th space mission of 2026, indicating that the country's space launch activities continue at a high frequency.

According to mission briefings, the newly launched satellites are primarily intended for in-orbit verification of new communication technologies. They will test key technologies such as high-speed data transmission, inter-satellite links, and network protocols, accumulating valuable data and experience for the future construction of large-scale satellite communication constellations. These technology validations form a critical foundation for building next-generation satellite internet systems.

The technical pathway of this launch directly points towards the Smart China wide-area satellite communication backbone network, which China plans to initiate construction on in 2027. This network aims to build an integrated space-ground information network covering the nation and even the globe, providing high-speed, stable, and secure communication services for remote area connectivity, emergency disaster relief, the Internet of Things, as well as aviation and maritime sectors. The success of this mission represents a solid step forward in realizing this ambitious blueprint.

The rapid development of commercial space is key to supporting such high-frequency, high-tech launch missions. In recent years, China has continuously accelerated the commercialization process in fields such as launch vehicles, satellite manufacturing, and launch services. The coordinated progress between private aerospace enterprises and national teams has collectively driven the overall acceleration and technological innovation of China's space industry.
