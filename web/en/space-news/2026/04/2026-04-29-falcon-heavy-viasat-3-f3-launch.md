# SpaceX Falcon Heavy to Launch ViaSat-3 F3 Tonight, Returning to Flight After 18 Months

**Summary:** On April 29, 2026 (ET), SpaceX is scheduled to launch the ViaSat-3 F3 high-throughput communications satellite aboard a Falcon Heavy rocket, targeting a geostationary transfer orbit. This marks the Falcon Heavy's first flight since NASA's Europa Clipper mission in October 2024, and its 12th mission overall. The original launch attempt on April 27 was scrubbed due to weather.

![Falcon Heavy launch scene](./figures/2026-04-29-falcon-heavy-viasat-3-f3-launch/hero.jpg)
*Credit: SpaceX (file photo)*

ViaSat-3 F3 is the third and final satellite in Viasat's ViaSat-3 series of high-throughput communications satellites. The first two spacecraft cover the Americas and Europe/Africa/Middle East respectively. The ViaSat-3 constellation uses Ka-band design and is capable of delivering over 1 Tbps per satellite, aimed at providing high-speed, flexible broadband internet services globally.

The launch is scheduled from Kennedy Space Center Launch Complex 39A, with a window opening at 00:38 Beijing time on April 30 (14:17 UTC on April 29). The window lasts 85 minutes, with a backup opportunity on April 30 at 14:17 UTC. The two side boosters (B1075 and B1072) are planned to land at Landing Zones 2 and 40 respectively, while the center core (B1098) will be expended over the Atlantic due to orbital inclination constraints.
