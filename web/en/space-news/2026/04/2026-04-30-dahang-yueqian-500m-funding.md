# Dahang Yueqian Raises 500M RMB, Accelerating China's First Tower-Catch Reusable Rocket

**Summary:** On April 30, Dahang Yueqian, China's only private rocket company specializing in tower-catch recovery, announced the completion of a 500 million RMB (approximately $69 million) financing round. The round was co-led by Qianhai Ark, Houji Capital, and PuHua Capital, with participation from over ten investment institutions and strategic investor Shenhou Cable. The funds will accelerate the development and maiden flight of China's first "chopsticks-style" tower-catch reusable liquid-fueled rocket, "Yueqian-1," along with a 100-ton-class liquid oxygen methane engine. The vehicle has entered full production and testing, with final assembly targeted for the second half of 2026 and its first flight planned for 2027.

![Dahang Yueqian "Yueqian-1" rocket illustration (DoNews)](./figures/2026-04-30-dahang-yueqian-500m-funding/hero.png)

## About Yueqian-1

Yueqian-1 (codename YQ-1) is China's first liquid-fueled rocket designed for tower-catch recovery — a "chopsticks-style" system similar to SpaceX's Super Heavy booster catch mechanism. Rather than performing a vertical soft landing, the rocket's first stage is captured directly by mechanical arms on the launch tower.

Key development milestones:
- **December 2024**: Design scheme passed expert review
- **January 2025**: Auxiliary propulsion system ground hot-fire test passed
- **March 2025**: Rocket engine and control system combined test successful
- **May 2025**: Integrated auxiliary propulsion system for the liquid rocket passed coordinated test, entering mass production
- **November 2025**: Tower-catch recovery test platform completed
- **First half 2026**: Self-developed "Huoshi-1" flight control system passed full-condition qualification; key technologies for the "Qingyu-11" 100-ton-class liquid oxygen methane engine fully broken through

The core first-stage engines are developed by the Sixth Institute of the China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation (CASC-6). With this new funding, engine reliability verification has been completed, placing the vehicle on track for flight testing.

## Investors and Fund Allocation

This round was co-led by Qianhai Ark, Houji Capital, and PuHua Capital, with participation from Junling Capital, Haiyuan Capital, Huifengda, Junchuan Capital, Chongqing Chuanging Technology, Zhongzhong Technology, Yuanchuang Capital, Hanli Capital, Shanghai Angel Association, and strategic investor Shenhou Cable. Some existing shareholders also increased their stakes. Huaxing Capital served as the long-term financial advisor.

The funds will prioritize:
1. Engineering development and maiden flight preparations for the Yueqian-1 reusable launch vehicle
2. R&D and ground testing of a 100-ton-class liquid oxygen methane engine
3. Continued development and optimization of the tower-catch recovery test platform

## China's Commercial Rocket Recovery Race

2026 is shaping up as a pivotal year for reusable rocket technology verification in China's commercial sector. Alongside Yueqian-1, multiple vehicles are competing in the recovery technology race:

- **Long March 10Y**: Targeted for maiden flight in Q2 2026, planned to验证海上柔性网系捕获回收 (ocean-based flexible net capture recovery), designed for at least 10 reuses with a 72-hour launch interval
- **Zhuque-3 Yauri-2**: LandSpace vehicle, has completed all problem-resolving rectifications and entered factory departure stage, targeting Q2 launch for first-stage vertical recovery attempt
- **Lijian-2**: CAS Space vehicle, planned recoverable launch project for 2026
- **Tianlong-3**: Space Pioneer vehicle

Dahang Yueqian is currently the only private rocket company in China focused on the tower-catch approach. Its "chopsticks" system parallels SpaceX's tower-mounted mechanical arm catch, and success would fill a critical gap in China's commercial rocket recovery technology landscape.
