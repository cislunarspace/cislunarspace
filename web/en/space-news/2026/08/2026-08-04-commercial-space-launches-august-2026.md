# Outlook for Key Global and Chinese Commercial Space Launches in August 2026

**Summary:** A report published on August 4, 2026, outlines key global and Chinese commercial space launches for August, including the Zhuque-3 Y2 reusable rocket verification and LEO constellation networking.

According to an industry outlook report released on August 4, 2026, global commercial space launch activity will remain robust throughout the month. The report notes that in the international market, several companies will continue deploying satellite constellations. SpaceX is expected to conduct multiple launches for its Starlink constellation to maintain its massive low-Earth orbit internet service network. Additionally, other emerging space companies plan to execute technology verification or commercial payload launch missions.

Within the Chinese commercial space sector, one of the most anticipated missions this month is the Zhuque-3 Y2 launch vehicle by LandSpace. The primary objective of this mission is to verify the reusability of the rocket's first stage, a critical step toward achieving breakthroughs in rocket recovery and reuse technology. Success would significantly reduce future launch costs and enhance the market competitiveness of China's commercial space industry. Meanwhile, other domestic commercial firms are also preparing networking launches for low-Earth orbit communication and remote sensing constellations, aiming to accelerate the establishment of an independent and controllable satellite internet system.

The report also mentions that the success rate and pace of global launch missions are influenced by multiple factors, including technical readiness, weather conditions, and supply chains. Specific launch windows and final mission manifests are subject to official announcements from each company. Overall, the commercial space launch activities in August 2026 reflect a clear industry trend toward reusability and the large-scale deployment of constellations.
