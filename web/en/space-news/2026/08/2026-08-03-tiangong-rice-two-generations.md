# Chinas Space Station Successfully Cultivates Two Generations of Rice in Orbit for First Time

**Summary:** On August 3, CCTV reported that the Shenzhou-23 crew has been conducting multiple space science experiments for over two months, with Chinas space station successfully cultivating two generations of rice in orbit for the first time.

According to a report from CCTV on August 3, the Shenzhou-23 crew has been in orbit for over two months since entering China's space station. During this period, the crew has been conducting a series of space science experiments and technological tests in an orderly manner. Notably, significant progress has been made in a key botanical experiment: China's space station has successfully achieved the continuous cultivation of two generations of rice in orbit for the first time.

This experiment aims to study the effects of the microgravity environment on rice growth, life cycle, and genetic characteristics. The report indicates that the rice plants completed their entire life cycle within the space station, from seed germination and growth to flowering and seed production, successfully yielding the first generation of seeds. Currently, the cultivation of the second generation of rice, using seeds produced in space, has been successfully initiated and is growing well. This breakthrough marks a crucial step forward for China in space plant cultivation technology, providing valuable scientific data and technical reserves for achieving sustainable food production during future long-duration deep space exploration missions.
