# Landspaces Zhuque-3 Y2 Rocket Scheduled for August 11 Launch

**Summary:** Landspaces Zhuque-3 Y2 large reusable liquid oxygen-methane rocket is scheduled to launch on the morning of August 11, 2026, from Jiuquan.

According to a post on the Eastmoney stock forum, LandSpace's Zhuque-3 Y2 rocket is scheduled to launch on the morning of August 11, 2026, from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center. The Zhuque-3 is a large, reusable liquid oxygen-methane launch vehicle, and this mission marks its second flight.

The Zhuque-3 rocket is designed to achieve vertical landing and reuse of its first stage, aiming to significantly reduce launch costs. The successful development and subsequent verification of this rocket model is crucial for China's commercial space industry to achieve low-cost, high-frequency access to space. Specific details regarding the payload and the precise launch window for the Y2 mission await further confirmation from LandSpace.
