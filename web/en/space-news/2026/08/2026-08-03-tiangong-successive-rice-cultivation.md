# First Successive Generation Rice Cultivation Experiment on Chinas Space Station Progressing Smoothly

**Summary:** On August 3, 2026, Chinas space station reported smooth progress in its first in-orbit experiment cultivating two successive generations of rice, marking a new breakthrough in space life science research.

According to a report from CCTV on August 3, 2026, the first in-orbit experiment cultivating two successive generations of rice aboard China's space station is progressing smoothly. This experiment aims to study the effects of the microgravity environment on the entire life cycle of rice, from seed germination, growth, and flowering to seed production, with a particular focus on exploring the feasibility of crop generational reproduction in space.

The experiment is being conducted inside the Life and Ecology Experiment Cabinet in the Wentian laboratory module. Researchers have provided optimal conditions for the rice plants through a sophisticated environmental control system, managing light, temperature, humidity, and nutrients. Currently, the first-generation rice plants have successfully completed their growth cycle and produced seeds. The second-generation rice plants have already germinated from these space-produced seeds and entered their growth phase, marking a critical milestone for the experiment.

The successful cultivation of two successive generations of rice in orbit represents a significant breakthrough for China's space life science research. It not only validates the technical feasibility of completing the full rice life cycle under the microgravity conditions of the space station but also provides valuable scientific data and an experimental foundation for establishing controlled ecological life support systems and enabling food production in space during future long-duration deep space exploration missions.
