# Chinas Space Industry Enters Intensive Launch Period in H2, Satellite Sector Reaches Scale Inflection Point

**Summary:** On August 3, 2026, analysis indicated that Chinas space launch schedule for the second half of the year is intensive, featuring state teams leading, private companies taking charge, and reusable breakthroughs, with companies like GalaxySpace achieving batch production capacity of hundreds of satellites.

Industry analysis indicates that China's space launch activities will be exceptionally intensive in the second half of 2026. This wave of launches displays a clear tiered structure: "State teams," represented by entities like CASC and CASIC, will continue to handle major national missions and strategic projects, ensuring their steady progress. Meanwhile, private companies such as GalaxySpace and LandSpace are playing an increasingly crucial role in commercial satellite launches and technological innovation, becoming significant executors of launch missions.

Particularly noteworthy is the key breakthrough in reusable rocket technology, which is expected to be further validated and applied in launch missions during the latter half of the year. This advancement is anticipated to significantly reduce launch costs and increase launch frequency. Analysis suggests this marks an accelerated transition for China's space industry from the technology verification phase to a phase of scaled and commercialized application.

The development of the satellite sector is a core manifestation of this transformation. Leading companies like GalaxySpace have achieved an annual batch production capacity of hundreds of satellites, establishing a solid production foundation for large-scale constellation deployment and satellite internet applications. The formation of this scaled production capacity is viewed as a critical inflection point for China's satellite industry entering a phase of scaled development, which will strongly support the future construction of an integrated space-ground information network.
