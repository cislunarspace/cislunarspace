# Commercial Space Concept Stocks Surge on August 3, Shengjian Shares and Chengchang Technology Hit Daily Limit

**Summary:** On August 3, 2026, commercial space concept stocks surged, with Shengjian Shares and Chengchang Technology hitting the daily limit, followed by Western Materials, Tongyu Communication, and Aerospace Huanyu.

On August 3, 2026, commercial space concept stocks in the A-share market showed strong performance, with the sector experiencing a significant intraday surge. According to a report from Securities Times, Shengjian Shares and Chengchang Technology led the rally, both hitting their daily price limits. Other related stocks, including Western Materials, Tongyu Communication, and Aerospace Huanyu, also followed suit with notable gains, indicating a sharp increase in market capital attention towards the commercial space sector.

The sector's movement is reportedly linked to recent policy tailwinds or advancements within the commercial space industry. In recent years, China's commercial space sector has developed rapidly, achieving breakthroughs in key areas such as satellite internet, reusable launch vehicles, and low-cost access to space. This progress has attracted significant investment and corporate engagement. Market analysts suggest that the maturation of these technologies and the expansion of application scenarios are continuously driving a revaluation of companies across the industrial chain.

It is important to note that short-term stock market fluctuations are influenced by multiple factors, including market sentiment, capital flows, and industry news. While investors may focus on sector hotspots, they should also view market volatility rationally and make prudent judgments based on company fundamentals and the long-term development trends of the industry.
