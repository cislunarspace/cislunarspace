# Shenzhou-23 Crew Completes Two Months in Orbit, Space Station Science Experiments Progress Steadily

**Summary:** As of August 1, 2026, the Shenzhou-23 crew, consisting of astronauts Zhu Yangzhu, Zhang Zhiyuan, and Li Jiaying, has completed two months in orbit, with over a hundred scientific experiments progressing intensively aboard the space station.

According to Space Launching, the Shenzhou-23 crew has been residing and working aboard the China Space Station for two full months since their arrival on June 1, 2026. During this period, the three astronauts have maintained good physical condition, and all mission tasks have been executed smoothly as planned. The space station, serving as the national space laboratory, continues to operate its scientific experiment platforms efficiently.

Throughout their stay, the crew has conducted over a hundred scientific experiments and technological trials spanning multiple fields, including space life sciences, microgravity physics, space materials science, space astronomy, and Earth science. The astronauts have completed the collection, processing, and storage of numerous experimental samples, while also performing routine tasks such as in-orbit equipment maintenance and health management. Preliminary statistics indicate that some experiments have yielded initial results, with relevant data being downlinked to the ground for in-depth analysis as scheduled.

Looking ahead, the Shenzhou-23 crew will continue to carry out subsequent space science experiments and technological trials according to the established plan and prepare for the upcoming visit of the Tianzhou cargo spacecraft. The routine operation of the China Space Station is continuously generating valuable scientific data and application outcomes.
