# Commercial Space Online： Two Reusable Rockets to Conduct Flight Tests This Month

**Summary:** On August 4, 2026, Commercial Space Online reported that LandSpaces Zhuque-3 Y2 reusable LOX-methane rocket is scheduled for launch on August 11 from Jiuquan, marking new progress in Chinas commercial reusable rocket development.

According to a report by Commercial Space Online on August 4, 2026, China's commercial space sector is set to witness two significant reusable rocket flight tests this month. LandSpace's Zhuque-3 Y2 (ZQ-3 Y2) reusable LOX-methane launch vehicle has confirmed its launch window, with the mission scheduled for August 11, 2026, from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center. This flight marks the second launch of the Zhuque-3 rocket and represents a critical step in validating its reusability technology.

The report highlights that the Zhuque-3 is a medium-lift liquid launch vehicle designed for future large-scale constellation deployment and low-cost space transportation. Its first stage is engineered for vertical landing and reuse. The success of this Y2 mission will directly influence the model's subsequent development and commercialization. As one of the leading enterprises in China's commercial space industry, LandSpace's continuous investment and progress in reusable rocket technology are propelling China's space transportation systems toward greater economy and efficiency.

In addition to the Zhuque-3, another reusable rocket model is expected to conduct related tests this month, although specific details regarding the model and timeline have not been officially announced. Industry analysts note that the intensive testing of multiple reusable rockets within a single month indicates that China's commercial space sector has entered an accelerated phase in developing this key technology, with significantly enhanced market competition and innovation vitality.
