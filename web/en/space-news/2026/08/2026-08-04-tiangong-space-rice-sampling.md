# China Space Station Update： Space Rice Sampling Conducted

**Summary:** On August 4, 2026, sampling of space rice was conducted aboard the China Space Station, marking new progress in in-orbit life science experiments.

On August 4, 2026, according to the China Manned Space Agency, the Shenzhou-18 astronaut crew conducted in-orbit sampling of "space rice" within the China Space Station, as part of an ongoing space life science experiment. This sampling represents a critical step in the in-orbit experimental phase of the project, aimed at obtaining growth samples of rice under special conditions such as microgravity and space radiation.

The "space rice" seeds for this experiment were previously transported to the space station by a cargo spacecraft. Inside the station's experiment module, these seeds germinated and grew under artificially controlled lighting, temperature, and humidity conditions. The astronauts are required to regularly observe and record the plants, and collect samples at specific growth stages to study the effects of the space environment on rice growth, gene expression, and metabolic processes.

The sampling of "space rice" is an integral part of a series of space life science research projects conducted on the China Space Station. These studies not only help reveal the adaptation mechanisms of plants in the space environment but also provide valuable scientific data and technical reserves for achieving sustainable food production during future long-term crewed deep space exploration missions. The relevant samples will later be returned to Earth via a re-entry capsule for in-depth analysis by scientists.
