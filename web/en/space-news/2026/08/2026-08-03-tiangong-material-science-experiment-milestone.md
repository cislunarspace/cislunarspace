# Chinas Space Station High-Temperature Material Science Experiment Device Completes 35 Experiments After Over 1200 Days in Orbit

**Summary:** On August 3, 2026, it was revealed at the China Materials Conference 2026 that the high-temperature material science experiment device on Chinas space station has been operating stably in orbit for over 1200 days, completing 35 space material experiments.

According to information disclosed at the China Materials Conference 2026 on August 3, 2026, the high-temperature material science experiment device installed in the Mengtian laboratory module of China's space station has been operating stably in orbit for over 1,200 days. Since its commissioning, the facility has successfully completed 35 space material science experiments, marking a significant advancement for China in the field of space materials research.

The device is capable of conducting high-temperature processing and performance studies on various materials under the unique space environment characterized by microgravity and high vacuum. Its long-term stable operation in orbit provides scientists with a valuable experimental platform, facilitating in-depth exploration of material formation mechanisms and property changes in space. The outcomes of this research are expected to have future applications in advanced manufacturing, energy, and aerospace technologies.

The data released demonstrates the sustained output capability of China's space station as a national space laboratory. The completion of 35 experiments over more than 1,200 days of stable operation validates the device's reliability and efficiency, laying a solid foundation for more complex space science experiments in the future.
