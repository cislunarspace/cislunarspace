# Chinas First On-Orbit Continuous Two-Generation Rice Cultivation Experiment Progresses Smoothly

**Summary:** On August 3, 2026, CCTV reported that the Shenzhou-23 crew successfully completed Chinas first on-orbit continuous two-generation rice cultivation experiment aboard the China Space Station.

According to a CCTV report on August 3, 2026, the Shenzhou-23 astronaut crew aboard the China Space Station recently completed China's first full life-cycle rice cultivation experiment in orbit, successfully growing rice from seed to seed and achieving continuous two-generation breeding. This experiment marks a significant breakthrough in China's space plant cultivation technology.

The report indicates that the astronauts meticulously cared for the rice plants within the plant cultivation facility on the space station, guiding them through the entire process of germination, growth, flowering, and seed setting in a microgravity environment. After the first generation of rice seeds matured, the crew promptly sowed and cultivated the second generation. The experiment is currently progressing smoothly, with the plants growing well.

This continuous two-generation rice cultivation experiment is of great significance for establishing controlled ecological life support systems for future long-duration deep space exploration missions. It verifies the feasibility of continuous crop production in the space station environment, providing crucial technical reserves and scientific data for future astronauts to achieve food self-sufficiency at lunar or Martian bases.
