# SpaceX Falcon 9 Rocket Scheduled for Two Starlink Missions from Vandenberg in First Week of August

**Summary:** On August 3, 2026, SpaceXs Falcon 9 rocket is scheduled to launch two Starlink satellite deployment missions from Vandenberg Space Force Base in the first week of August.

According to the Ventura County Star, SpaceX is planning to launch two Falcon 9 rockets from Vandenberg Space Force Base in California during the first week of August 2026. Both missions are intended to deploy new batches of Starlink satellites into orbit, further expanding and reinforcing the company's global satellite internet constellation.

Specific launch dates and window times have not yet been finalized. Reportedly, mission details and the exact launch schedule will be announced by SpaceX and Vandenberg Space Force Base closer to the launch dates. The Vandenberg base serves as SpaceX's primary launch site on the West Coast, handling numerous Starlink missions to polar and sun-synchronous orbits.

The Starlink project is SpaceX's ambitious low-Earth orbit satellite internet initiative, designed to provide high-speed, low-latency broadband internet service globally, particularly to remote and underserved areas. Continuous launches are critical to maintaining and expanding the constellation's coverage capability.
