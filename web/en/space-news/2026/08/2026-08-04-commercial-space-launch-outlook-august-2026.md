# Global and China Commercial Space Launch Outlook for August 2026

**Summary:** On August 4, 2026, an outlook article was published, outlining multiple important commercial launch missions for mid-August, including the LandSpace Zhuque-3 Y2 reusable rocket.

According to an outlook article published by Eastmoney on August 4, 2026, global commercial space activity is set to remain robust in mid-August 2026. One of the most anticipated missions is the planned launch of LandSpace's Zhuque-3 Y2 (ZQ-3 Y2) launch vehicle from China. This mission marks the second flight of the reusable Zhuque-3 rocket, and its success will be crucial for validating the vehicle's reusability capabilities and advancing its future commercial operations.

In addition to the LandSpace mission, the article also mentioned launch plans from several other commercial space companies. These missions encompass various activities, including satellite constellation deployments and technology demonstrations, indicating the continued growth and competitive dynamics of the global commercial space market. For the most accurate information on specific launch dates and mission details, it is recommended to follow the official announcements from the respective companies.
