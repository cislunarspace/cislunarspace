# Jingan Dream Star Youth Science Experiment Satellite Scheduled for August 6 Launch

**Summary:** The Jingan Dream Star youth science experiment satellite, developed by the Jingan education team, is scheduled to depart Shanghai on August 6, 2026, for launch from Taiyuan, Shanxi.

According to a report on the Eastmoney stock forum, the launch schedule for the Jingan Dream Star youth science experiment satellite, developed by the Jingan education team, has been confirmed. The satellite is set to depart from Shanghai on August 6, 2026, and will subsequently be transported to the launch site in Taiyuan, Shanxi Province, for liftoff.

The Jingan Dream Star is a satellite dedicated to youth science experiments. The project aims to provide a platform for young people to engage in practical aerospace technology activities and stimulate their interest in scientific exploration. Specific technical parameters of the satellite, the experimental payloads it carries, and details of its subsequent on-orbit mission have not yet been officially disclosed in greater detail and await confirmation from the relevant authorities.

This launch plan represents an attempt to integrate youth aerospace science education with actual aerospace engineering. If successful, the Jingan Dream Star will provide valuable space experiment data for the participating students, supporting their scientific inquiry-based learning.
