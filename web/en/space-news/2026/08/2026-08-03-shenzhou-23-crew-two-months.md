# Shenzhou-23 Crew Has Been in Orbit for Over Two Months

**Summary:** On August 3, 2026, CCTV reported that the Shenzhou-23 astronaut crew has been living and working in orbit for over two months, with the space station operating in good condition.

According to a CCTV report on August 3, 2026, the three-person crew of the Shenzhou-23 manned spacecraft has been in orbit for over two months. The astronauts are reported to be in good physical condition, with all mission tasks progressing as planned. The China Space Station complex is operating stably, with all functional parameters normal.

During their stay, the crew has conducted various space science experiments and technology tests. They have also completed platform maintenance, equipment installation, and commissioning work for the space station. CCTV footage showed the astronauts in high spirits, maintaining smooth communication with ground control and undergoing regular health monitoring and physical exercise.

As China's national space laboratory, the space station continues to generate scientific data and application results. The long-duration stay of the Shenzhou-23 crew further validates the reliability of the station's life support system and operational management, accumulating valuable experience for future missions.
