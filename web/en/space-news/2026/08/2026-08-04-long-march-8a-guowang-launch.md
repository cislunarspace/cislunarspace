# Long March 8A Rocket to Launch Guowang LEO Satellites from Hainan Commercial Space Launch Site on August 4

**Summary:** On August 4, 2026, the Long March 8A rocket is scheduled to launch the Guowang LEO 23A-I satellites from the Hainan Commercial Space Launch Site.

According to the China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation, the Long March 8A launch vehicle is scheduled to perform a mission on August 4, 2026, from the Hainan Commercial Space Launch Site. The rocket will deliver the Guowang LEO 23A-I series of satellites into their intended orbits. This launch represents another important flight for the Long March 8A rocket and highlights the continued commercial launch services provided by the Hainan facility.

The Long March 8A is an improved medium-lift launch vehicle based on the Long March 8 rocket, offering enhanced payload capacity and greater mission flexibility, primarily targeting the medium and low Earth orbit satellite launch market. As China's first dedicated commercial space launch site, the Hainan Commercial Space Launch Site aims to provide efficient and reliable launch services for both domestic and international customers.

Specific details regarding the launch window, the exact number of satellites on board, and further technical parameters for this mission are expected to be announced by the launch provider as the date approaches. The deployment of the Guowang LEO satellite constellation is a key component in China's efforts to build an integrated space-ground information network.
