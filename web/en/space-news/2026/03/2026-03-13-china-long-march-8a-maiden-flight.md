# Long March 8A launches 20th group of internet satellites

**Summary:** According to **China National Space Administration (CNSA)**, the **Long March 8A** rocket successfully launched on **March 13, 2026**, at **03:48 Beijing Time** from the **Hainan Commercial Space Launch Site**, deploying the **20th group of satellite internet LEO satellites** to orbit. Long March 8A is developed by **China Academy of Aerospace Launch Vehicle Technology (CALT)** and is a two-and-a-half stage liquid rocket. Since its maiden flight on February 11, 2025, Long March 8A has completed multiple successful missions.
