# Gravity-1 Rocket Completes Second Flight Successfully

**Summary:** On October 11, 2025 (Beijing Time), the Gravity-1 carrier rocket developed by Orienspace successfully completed its second flight (Y2) from a sea-based platform near Haiyang, Shandong Province, placing 3 satellites into their planned orbits. With a low Earth orbit payload capacity of 6 tons, Gravity-1 is China's largest private launch vehicle. Following the maiden flight on January 11, 2024, this second mission further validated the maturity of solid-propellant sea-based launch technology.
