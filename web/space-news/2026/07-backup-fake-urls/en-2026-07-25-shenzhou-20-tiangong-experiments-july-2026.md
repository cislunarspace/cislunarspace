# Shenzhou-20 Crew Conducts Scientific Experiments Aboard Tiangong Space Station

**Summary:** Chinas Manned Space Agency announced on July 25, 2026 that the Shenzhou-20 crew is conducting a range of scientific experiments aboard the Tiangong space station, covering space life sciences and microgravity physics.

Chinas Manned Space Agency announced on July 25 that the Shenzhou-20 astronaut crew is currently conducting a series of scientific experiments aboard the Tiangong space station. The experiments span multiple disciplines including space life sciences and microgravity physics, aimed at deepening our understanding of how space environments affect material and biological processes.

According to the announcement, the crew is making smooth progress with all tasks, and the space station systems are operating normally. The space life sciences experiments focus on research areas such as cell cultivation and plant growth, while the microgravity physics experiments cover fundamental research in fluid physics and materials science. These experimental results will provide important scientific data to support long-duration human space habitation.

As a national space laboratory, the Tiangong space station has conducted hundreds of scientific experiments since its completion, yielding a series of significant research achievements. The Shenzhou-20 mission is an important component of the stations application and development phase, and the crews experimental work holds great significance for advancing Chinas space scientific research capabilities.

Chinas Manned Space Agency stated that mission progress updates will continue to be released. According to reports, the crew is expected to carry out additional in-flight experiments as planned, with specific details pending official confirmation.
