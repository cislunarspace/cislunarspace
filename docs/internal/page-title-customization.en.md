# Page title customization plan

## Design principles

1. **Uniqueness**: every page gets a unique title reflecting its core content
2. **Keyword optimization**: include primary search keywords
3. **Length control**: 50–60 characters (including spaces)
4. **User intent**: consider what searchers intend
5. **Action orientation**: encourage the click
